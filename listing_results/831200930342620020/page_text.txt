Step into luxury at this brand-new 4-bedroom Nashville retreat, where modern design meets Music City charm. Spacious gathering areas, stylish décor, and thoughtful finishes throughout make it an ideal home base for a memorable stay with family or friends.

The space
Don't miss the private rooftop deck — complete with a hot tub and sweeping skyline views. It's the perfect spot to sip your morning coffee, catch the sunset, or wind down after a night out on Broadway.

Just 20 minutes from Nashville International Airport and a short rideshare from downtown, you'll have easy access to the honky-tonks, live music, top restaurants, and nightlife that make this city famous. Spend your days out exploring, then retreat to the privacy and comfort of your own upscale hideaway.

About Us:
At King Rentals, we’re committed to making your stay as comfortable, easy, and enjoyable as possible. As a locally owned and operated business, our friendly team is available 24/7 to help with any questions or needs during your stay. We’re here to help you relax, enjoy your time together, and create wonderful memories with your family and friends.

Other things to note
IMPORTANT – PLEASE READ BEFORE BOOKING:
• Maximum Occupancy: Maximum of 12 guests, including adults, children, and infants. No additional guests are permitted due to fire safety and insurance requirements.
• Age Requirement: All guests must be 18 or older, except children staying with their parents or guardians.
• No Parties: Parties and unauthorized gatherings are strictly prohibited. Noise complaints or law enforcement calls may result in immediate termination of the stay without a refund.
• No Smoking: Smoking is strictly prohibited.
• No Pets: This property has a strict no-pet policy.
• Check-In/Check-Out: Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available for an additional fee starting at $60. Please message us in advance to check availability.
• Cancellations & Travel Insurance: We highly recommend purchasing travel insurance for protection against unexpected cancellations, weather, natural disasters, power outages, and other unforeseen events. We’re unable to offer refunds or rescheduled stays outside Airbnb’s Extenuating Circumstances Policy.
• Starter Supplies: We provide a starter supply of essential items. Please bring your personal toiletries and plan to purchase additional supplies if needed.
• Trash: Please place all trash in trash bags and place them in the trash can outside by garage.
• Stay Connected: Follow us at @kingrentalspropertymgmt for updates.
