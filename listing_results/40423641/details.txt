=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
ONE MILE FROM DOWNTOWN BROADWAY! Situated between beautiful Germantown and Downtown Nashville, this lovely 2 Bedroom/ 2 Bath condo is an inexpensive ride-share from Nashville best attractions. The condo is minutes from Nissan Stadium, Bridgestone Arena, Ascend Amphitheater, and The Ryman Auditorium. This is truly a prime location for the Nashville experience.

The space
- The Charlesville is located 1 mile from Downtown Lower Broadway, and 9.5 miles from Nashville International Airport (BNA). 
- The 2 bedroom, 2 bath unit has 1 queen sized bed in each bedroom as well as a queen sized sleeper sofa in the living room. 
- The kitchen is fully outfitted with stove and oven, microwave, regular coffee pot & toaster. We do not provide pantry items or coffee. 
- There is a washer and dryer in the condo for your convenience. 
- Each unit includes one free parking space in our parking garage. 
- There is a nearby active commercial rail line with noise abatement regulations in place.

Guest access
*** Early Check in is not able to be guaranteed. You may send a message the night prior or morning of your arrival to see if early check in or luggage drop off is a possibility. The time allotted in between stays allows the cleaners to perform their duties prior to your arrival. There are multiple luggage stow companies in Nashville that you may utilize while exploring the city. If early check in is a necessity for your group, we ask that you book the night prior to your arrival. This ensures that you may check in anytime on your arrival date.

Guests will have full Access to the entire condo. 2 Bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, a fully equipped kitchen and Living room with queen size pull out sofa. One parking spot is permitted per rental in the parking garage. Parking passes are provided upon arrival. Only one permit will be provided, there are no additional spots, and there are no exceptions. Please plan accordingly. An elevator is on site and two stairwells are accessible. An elevator code, and a keyless door entry code will be provided to you 2 days prior to arrival.

Other things to note
There is an active train track very near to the property. The train should not blow its whistle, but the tracks do make noise. These are commercial trains and we do not have schedules.
