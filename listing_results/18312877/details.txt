=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome! This cozy 2 bedroom / 1 bath home is in a quiet neighborhood plotted nicely between Nashville Intl Airport (BNA) and Downtown Nashville. Just 4 miles away from the Ryman Auditorium, Honky Tonks on Broadway, trendy East Nashville, Nissan Stadium, Country Music Hall of Fame, Bridgestone Arena & lots of great restaurants. Or, hop on Briley Pkwy to head over to the Grand Ole Opry, Gaylord Opryland Hotel & Convention Center, and Opry Mills Mall all of which are less than 5 miles away.

The space
This home is kept professionally cleaned and equipped with everything needed to enjoy your stay! We try to fit everyone's tastes and needs the best we can. There are two brand new Ashley Sierra sleep mattresses (one soft and one firm) as well as a super comfy sectional couch and an additional full size couch as well. We understand that travel can be challenging so we have an electronic door lock on the front door in case we can't be there so our guests can check in whenever needed. It is only a short / inexpensive Uber or Taxi ride from the airport to the house then anywhere else you want to go.
