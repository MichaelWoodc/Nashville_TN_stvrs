=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Escape to a peaceful guest suite nestled in-home on a beautifully wooded property just 10-16 mins from downtown Nashville (7 mi), BNA Airport, Grand Ole Opry, & East Nashville. This spacious retreat features a private entrance, covered outdoor living, fire pit, wood-burning fireplace, kitchenette, washer/dryer, dedicated parking, and a serene backyard surrounded by mature trees and lush potted plants. Enjoy the perfect blend of quiet relaxation & easy access to Music City's best attractions.

The space
The ~800 sq. ft. in-home guest suite offers a thoughtfully designed space and feels more like an apartment than a traditional guest suite. Zen Woodland Oasis is most comfortable for two adults (perfect for a couples getaway!), though the space can accommodate four with the use of a convertible sleeper sofa.

Features include:
•	Private exterior entrance with self check-in
•	Comfy queen bed
•	Queen sleeper sofa
•	Full bathroom (shower only, no tub)
•	Kitchenette with essential items
•	Full-size washer and dryer
•	Wood-burning fireplace
•	Two Smart TVs (log into your favorite streaming services)
•	Dedicated parking for up to two vehicles

There is no stove cooktop or oven. Starter K-cup coffee pods or ground coffee and an assortment of teas is provided, along with a Brita pitcher for filtered water.

You'll enjoy the quiet, charming feel of an established neighborhood with tree-lined streets while being close to Nashville's top destinations.
