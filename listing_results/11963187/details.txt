Just 3 minutes from downtown, you will find a large white house with a wrap around porch awaiting your arrival. This brand new home faces the gorgeous Shelby Park & connects to Nashville's Greenway! Luxurious, private home in hip East Nashville! Heated in-ground Pool!

The space
Located in the artistic East Nashville neighborhood, this brand new home overlooks Shelby Park & a gorgeous view of downtown. With quick access to downtown, the Greenway bike trail, Shelby Park, and East Nashville's 5 Points, the location couldn't be better for anyone taking a trip to Nashville or passing through town! Heated in-ground Pool for your enjoyment! Open May-September (weather dependent). 

Our home is brand new and designed for the ultimate Airbnb experience. The space includes 4 bedrooms (1 King bed and 3 queen beds, 1 pull out bed), 2 queen air mattresses and a second pull out sofa. In total you can sleep 10 people comfortably!
