Brand New $60k Renovation designed for next level comfort & entertainment!

All just a short walk to Broadway Honky Tonks within minutes!

That’s right, you can organize a great trip to Nashville with all of your friends & stay together in one location without the expense of Hotel rates.

Join us today at 'Southern Velvet'.

The space
Featuring an Incredible games room with a pool table, stunning bar, leather sleeper-sofa & flatscreen TV to watch your movies!

This property enjoys 3 spacious re-designed bedrooms with either a King or Queen bed as their primary and additional twins for our larger groups. Each bedroom has it's own en-suite bathroom too!

We find it beneficial to break down the bed set up, so here is a detailed layout:

Bedroom #1 = 1 Queen size bed & 2 Twin size beds in Bunk formation. It has an en-suite bathroom with tub shower combo. (3 Beds - sleeps up to 4 people).

Bedroom #2 = 1 Queen size bed & 2 Twin size beds in Bunk formation. It has an en-suite bathroom with tub shower combo. (3 Beds - sleeps up to 4 people).

Bedroom #3 = 1 King size bed & 2 Twin size beds in a Day Bed and Trundle formation. It has an en-suite bathroom with walk-in shower and also features a stylish make-up station. (3 Beds - sleeps up to 4 people).

The Games Room = 1 Queen Sleeper Sofa. (1 Bed - sleeps up to 2 people).

The whole house has been wonderfully renovated, featuring a variety of hardwood, tile and stained concrete flooring. The kitchen is tremendously well equipped with everything you need to eat in and entertain.
An island with barstool seating and a spacious dining area allows your entire group to hang out and have food and drinks together.
When you are ready to wind down and play some games, head down to the converted garage that has been finished out as a brilliant games room. In this great hang out space, you can relax on the sleeper sofa in-front of the flat screen tv, challenge each other to a game of pool, set the ping pong table topper for a round of beer-pong or pour yourself a drink from the full size fridge and bar.

We hope you choose to stay with us and come back to visit time and time again.
