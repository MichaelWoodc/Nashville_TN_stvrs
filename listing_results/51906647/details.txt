The space
Before you even set down a bag, the living room makes its case: a piano, a blue guitar, an Elvis mural, and a neon sign reading 'Thank You Very Much' glowing over the sofa. Downstairs, the game room is the other anchor of this Airbnb: ping pong, a disco ball, a bar with a life-size cutout, and a TV set up for karaoke that runs late. Spend the day out around Nashville, come home to trade ping pong shots and karaoke turns, then peel off to four separate bedrooms once the group is ready to crash.
