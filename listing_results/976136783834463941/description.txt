Enchanted Garden Studio is a private, standalone-feeling guest suite tucked beneath a character-rich historic home on Belmont Boulevard. Surrounded by an abundant garden and designed as an inspiring sanctuary, it’s a perfect fit for musicians, couples, and professionals seeking a furnished monthly stay—especially traveling nurses and teachers. Enjoy in-unit laundry, private parking, and a secluded hot tub oasis just minutes from 12 South, Belmont, Vanderbilt, and Music Row.

The space
Set beneath a beautifully, preserved historic home (owner-occupied upstairs), Enchanted Garden Studio offers a peaceful, private retreat with its own separate entrance and a true residential Nashville feel. Inside is one spacious studio-style room thoughtfully arranged to feel like a small home: a sleeping area with a queen bed and smart TV, a comfortable living room setup, and an open kitchen with a small dining area for easy meals and longer stays. A modern bathroom and in-unit laundry make the space especially convenient for extended visits, work travel, and furnished monthly rentals.

Step outside and you’ll find one of the studio’s best features—an abundant, luxuriant garden that creates a calm, inspiring atmosphere that musicians and creatives love, while still being practical and comfortable for couples or traveling professionals. Guests also have access to an indoor-outdoor dining area just outside the studio, plus a hot tub for a relaxing soak after a long shift or a day exploring Nashville.

Parking is simple and secure: one complimentary space is available in the alley for a single vehicle.

⭐ About Humming Host
Humming Host is a local, boutique property management company curating beautiful homes in prime locations for sustainable, unforgettable stays.
At the heart of how we operate is our Thoughtful Stay Program — a collection of intentional decisions, some visible to guests and some behind the scenes, that come together to create a stay that feels genuinely looked after. The program focuses on six key areas: local sourcing, waste reduction, sustainable amenities, community giveback, curated local culture, and guest convenience.
We implement these standards across every property we manage as part of our commitment to thoughtful hospitality. Our local hosting team ensures each home is fully prepared before arrival, and our guest services team is available daily from 7:00 AM–11:00 PM CST to support you before, during, and after your stay.

Guest access
Check-in is simple with smart locks. Before your arrival, we’ll send detailed instructions with your door codes, directions to the unit, and any access info for shared amenities or parking. Please review these notes ahead of time to ensure a smooth arrival. Guest may access the unit only after their $500 security deposit is collected and confirmed.

Other things to note
★Shared Outdoor Amenities★
The indoor-outdoor dining area and hot tub are located in a shared garden space with the homeowners. The homeowners are often traveling and guests typically enjoy a private-feeling stay, but these outdoor amenities may be shared when the homeowners are on-site.

★Smoke-Free Home & Community★
In accordance with building rules, smoking is not permitted inside the unit or any common areas. Guests who violate this policy may be subject to fines or removal per HOA regulations.

★Supplies & Essentials★
We provide a generous starter supply of essentials, including trash bags, toilet paper (at least two rolls per bathroom), and a full roll of paper towels. If you run out during your stay or require more than what is provided, you are welcome to purchase additional items as needed.

★Families & Baby Gear★
Traveling with little ones? A pack-n-play and baby gate (in homes with stairs) available upon request, subject to availability. Please message your host in advance if you would like a baby package prepared for your stay.

★Maintenance & Repairs★	
Each property is thoroughly inspected before arrival to ensure systems and appliances are functioning properly. If you experience any maintenance issue during your stay, please contact your host as soon as possible. We will always do our best to resolve it promptly; however, repair timelines may depend on vendor and contractor availability.
