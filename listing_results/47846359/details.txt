=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Parking is not included.

This is a historic 3 story building on the 5th Avenue of the Arts in downtown Nashville.  In the early 1900s it was the Armstrong Corset Shop.  Over the last several years I have renovated the 3rd floor into 3 unique lofts.

The space
It has 20' ceilings, 3 large historic windows that look over 5th Ave, & 2 skylights.  There is a private rooftop deck with copper & cedar shake siding.  Custom welded handrails & spiral stairs with wood treads.  Original heart pine beams from the building were repurposed to make shelves.  Two 65" tvs, one 50" tv, and a 12' projector screen.  Stained concrete floors downstairs with red oak flooring upstairs, vintage Turkish rugs throughout, antique buffets, a record player, 2 radios with bluetooth capability.  Reclaimed barn wood wall in bedroom that is over Silent FX drywall for added sound insulation.  50" round Ralph Lauren chandelier in the main area with a Visual Comfort Chandelier in the bedroom.  Custom welded farm table on the roof deck.
