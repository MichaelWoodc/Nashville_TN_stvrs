=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
You will love my place because it has easy access to downtown, only a 5-7 minute drive to  Broadway, Nissan Stadium, Amphitheater and Bridgestone, yet located on a quiet street in the heart of East Nashville. 
Close to Nashville's most renowned music venues, 5 Spot, and The Basement East. Other favorite go-to places are: Buds and Brews, Bongo Java, Margo, 5 Points Pizza, Edley’s BBQ, and Bad Idea Nashville.
Elegantly styled, Nashville chic, a relaxing retreat a home away from home.

The Pineapple Room has a well-lit private entryway for guests who may be arriving later in the evening or returning from downtown festivities. There is a keypad doorknob where guests type a code to enter. 
Once inside the room is filled with elegant pineapple decor, a TV with fire stick to watch your favorite streaming services, a large dresser, mirror, two end tables on either side of the Queen Beauty-rest bed, and pineapple nightlight/oil diffuser.
