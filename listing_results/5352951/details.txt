=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Villa Place, an incredible Craftsman, immaculately decorated home gently tucked in Edgehill Village, in the heart of Music Row. Our beautifully restored home offers you and your group a comfortable place to hang your hat and enjoy our favorite city.  While we did major upgrades on the house, we still managed to leave the charm of a true Nashville craftsman. We are so excited to play a special part in the Nashville vacation of your dreams.

The space
We have combined our decade of experience as Airbnb hosts with our design team's thoughtfulness and craft to create a home we feel is perfect for entertaining, hosting, and comfort while staying true to the home's original charm and character. We want our guests to experience our Nashville craftsman as if it's their home away from home.

Our downstairs features a formal living room and den, as well as a spacious dining area and an open chef's kitchen & butler's pantry.  It features an office with a single bed, and two bedrooms -- one with a queen-size bed and the other with a king-size bed.  There is a full-size bathroom with a walk-in shower and a half bathroom tucked behind the den. 

Just up the stairs you'll find we have a landing with an additional single bed, two bedrooms, and the Master Suite.  One bedroom features a queen-sized bed, while the other has two twin-size beds.  These bedrooms have a shared bathroom and closet space. The spacious Master Suite has a California king-size bed and a private bathroom with a soaking tub and a large walk-in shower.

Please note that prior to renovations, we had a hot tub.  We no longer have the hot tub.
