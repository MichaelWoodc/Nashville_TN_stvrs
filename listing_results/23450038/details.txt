Guest access
The house is the back house of a 2-house lot. The two houses share a walkway that is next to the front house. As to not bother the family that lives in that house, guests access to the property is from the back through the garage. (Codes to the garage door will be given the day of arrival.) 

DO NOT ARRIVE BY THE FRONT DOOR. It is very very easy to go to the front house which is not our house. That neighbor gets annoyed by this intrusion, so please do not use the front door and walkway.

Front gate and door are emergency exits ONLY.

Guests have access to the entire home and a one-car bay in the attached garage except for supply and utility closets. Additional free parking is available in the driveway and on the street. Entry access codes will be sent via text early in the morning of the first day of your stay. Check-in and check-out are a breeze!

Other things to note
We want to make your vacation time in Nashville memorable!

We do not charge for early and/or late check-in. We try to make your stay here in Nashville as stress-free as possible and that starts and ends with check-in and check-out. We know that it is not always possible to coordinate air travel schedules with check-in and check-out times of a vacation or weekend rental. If possible, we allow guests to have an earlier check-in and later check-out if the property is not occupied before or after the guest's stay.  We are one of only a few houses in the Nashville area that do not charge for this flexibility. However, we can never  guarantee early check-in or late check-out.
