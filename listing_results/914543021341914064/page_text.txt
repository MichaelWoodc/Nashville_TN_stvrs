The space
Welcome to the "Ring of Fire Retreat" – A Johnny Cash-Themed Getaway

This unique 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath home spans over 2,700 sq. ft. and is fully inspired by the legendary Johnny Cash. From themed decor to memorabilia throughout, this space offers a one-of-a-kind stay for fans and travelers alike.

🛏 Bedrooms
Bedroom 1 (first floor): Three full beds – great for families or groups.

Bedroom 2 (second floor): Queen bed, walk-in closet, and private bathroom.

Bedroom 3 (second floor): Full-size bunk beds + optional trundle, with a full bathroom just across the hall.

Bedroom 4 (second floor): King bed with private bathroom – perfect for relaxing.

Bedroom 5 (Third Floor): Bonus living space with air hockey and games – great for kids or game nights.

🍳 Kitchen & Living
Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen and a Johnny Cash-themed living room. Keurig coffee included to start your day right.

🌆 Rooftop Terrace
The fourth-floor rooftop features Cash-themed decor and stunning city views – ideal for sunsets, drinks, or stargazing.

🎶 Entertainment
Flat-screen TVs in every room, plus plenty of music and memorabilia to celebrate the Man in Black.

Whether you're a lifelong fan or just looking for a fun and comfortable stay, the Ring of Fire Retreat delivers a memorable experience.
