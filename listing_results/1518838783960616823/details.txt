The space
This Airbnb pulls the whole group into one space fast. The open living room and kitchen keep everyone together instead of scattered across separate rooms, blue sofa and pink armchairs for after a day out, kitchen island within reach for whoever's on snack duty.

Everyone can cook at once thanks to the kitchen island, gas stove, and full-size fridge, and the open layout means the cook never gets cut off from the conversation. Four bedrooms plus a pull-out sofa give the group room to spread out while still sharing the run of the house.

A built-in makeup and getting-ready area means nobody's fighting over a bathroom mirror before a night out, and out back, the hot tub deck is the real gathering spot, string lights on, gas grill going, outdoor dining table set for the group. Spend the day exploring Broadway or East Nashville, grill out back as the sun goes down, soak in the hot tub, then head to bold, statement-wallpapered bedrooms to recharge.
