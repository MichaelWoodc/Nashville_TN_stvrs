=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Yeehaw! Saddle up and stay in true Nashville style at our cowboy-inspired townhome, located in the heart of Music Row and Midtown. Whether you're here to kick up your heels and catch live music or take a ride down the country music trail, this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath retreat offers the perfect blend of comfort, charm, and a touch of southern hospitality.

The space
🎸 Location, Location, Location!:

Nestled in the historic and lively neighborhoods of Music Row and Midtown, you’re just minutes from the best honky-tonks, country music bars, and iconic venues that Nashville is famous for. Enjoy a stroll to The Gulch, Broadway Street, or the Ryman Auditorium—your country music adventure starts right at your doorstep.

You are only a few blocks from Vanderbilt University, great restaurants like Catbird Seat, Halls Chophouse, Henley, Ichigo Ichie and Giovanni’s. A 5-minute walk to Loser’s Bar, where artists like Riley Green and Post Malone consistently stop in for a drink and impromptu concert, and the Patterson House for an upscale cocktail.

The Rodeo Room: A brand new bunk bed with twin trundle decked out in cowboy chic linens—perfect for a restful night after a night of honky-tonk fun! Take a step outside on your private porch for a late night drink or cigar!

The Honky-Tonk Hideaway: A cozy queen bedroom with a rustic, barn wood headboard and large walk in closet.

The Country Star Suite: A bright and airy room with 4 twin bunk beds, perfect for the little cowboys and cowgirls.

Master Bath: Soak in a tub fit for a cowboy after a long day of sightseeing or boot-scootin’!

Half Bath: Convenient for guests, with southwestern charm and plenty of space.
