=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to The Look Out & Turtle Bay…

This is a brand new construction home as of 2022 and is the newest addition to our collection. Please be sure to see the 1000’s of reviews on our other properties.

This stunning waterfront development overlooks Old Hickory Lake and Turtle Bay Marina and is just 15 mins from the airport.
Enjoy an evening beer or glass of wine on the 'Look Out' rooftop soaking in the sunset over the water and then head into downtown Nashville and be on broadway in under 30 mins in an Uber!

This 4 bedroom, 3 bathroom townhome features not only a spacious rooftop with bar area, but multiple balconies and a covered front porch to take in the outdoors. There is a fully equipped kitchen for all your cooking needs and bar top seating for 8 that flows openly into the living room where a flat screen Smart TV is there for your entertainment.
The garage has been converted into a stunning games room with activities such as ping pong, darts & ringtoss to enjoy your time with friends. We've conveniently added a fridge in the games room to keep your drinks in too!
A short 1 minute walk will have you sitting at the Marina Bar & Restaurant enjoying a wonderful ‘lake life’ setting in one of Nashville’s waterfront favorite hang outs. Live music is performed on most days and the atmosphere is truly Nashville. Boat rentals and other waterfront activities are available at the Marina.

Other amenities such as Grocery stores and multiple golf courses are located just minutes away including 2 public championship quality courses located at The Hermitage.

The sleeping arrangements in the home are as follows:

Bedroom #1 - King Bed with en-suite bathroom. TV

Bedroom #3 - Queen Bed. TV

Bedroom #4 - Full Sized Bunk Beds. TV
