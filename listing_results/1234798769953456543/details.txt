=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Vandy Haven, a 1 bedroom, 1 bath apartment filled with the spirit of a Commodore in the heart of Sylvan Park! Just 1 mile from Vanderbilt University and 2 miles from Lower Broadway, this charismatic flat offers the best of Nashville at your doorstep. Pet-friendly with a fenced yard for your furry companions, Vandy Haven also boasts a relaxing outdoor space featuring Adirondack chairs and café lighting. Whether you're here for work or play, you'll feel right at home.

The space
Welcome to Vandy Haven, a stylish one-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment filled with plenty of Vanderbilt spirit. The kitchen showcases custom Vanderbilt Commodore wallpaper, complemented by handsome black cabinets and gold accents. In the bedroom, you'll find two queen beds, sound and light-reducing curtains, and a ceiling fan for a peaceful night's rest. The bathroom features a tub/shower combo for relaxation or a quick refresh. Enjoy meals in the chic dining area, and unwind in the bright living room with a sofa that converts to a full-size bed. Step outside to a community space with Adirondack chairs, café lighting, and beautiful landscaping. The Richland Greenway, just at the end of the street, offers an easy bike ride to Vanderbilt or a scenic walk to Centennial Park.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the private apartment and shared outdoor spaces.

Other things to note
Please be aware that a freight train runs behind the apartment complex, operating irregularly, including during the night. While some guests may not be bothered by the noise, others may find it disruptive. If you are sensitive to noise, we recommend not renting this property.

The train travels from Memphis to Nashville, but it does not blow its horn near the building, so you will mostly hear it as background noise. It is mostly obscured from view by vegetation. To help minimize disturbances, sound and light-reducing curtains and a sound machine are provided.

Stairs are required to access the apartment on the 2nd floor.
