The space
This home is located on a quiet street in the Nations, just a half mile away from Fat Bottom Brewing and one and a half miles from the shops of the Nations; a cutesy neighborhood of Nashville featuring local restaurants and coffee (frothy monkey!). Just minutes to downtown Broadway!

The home sleeps up to 10 people in total with the tenth either on a couch or air mattress; there is a newly added twin sofa bed in the main bedroom. On the main floor is a bedroom with a queen bed and TV. Upstairs features a primary suite with a king bed, flat screen TV and an ensuite luxury bathroom and private balcony. A third bedroom upstairs is down the hall with a twin over twin bunk and a twin over full bunk. There is a shared third half bathroom in the hallway upstairs. 

Our well equipped kitchen has featured kitchen items such as an Air fryer, Insta-pot, and Juicer. If you're looking to cook or barbecue, you will be able to at our home. Inquire for any specific tool and we can ensure if we have it! We have recently added a sous vide and a ninja blender!

Traveling with little ones or pets? Let us know! We offer amenities such as high chairs and pack n plays. We love pups! There is a $75 fee per pet. Let us know if you plan to bring the furry ones and we will leave some coveted doggy beds out just for them. Sorry folks, no cats are allowed!

Looking to hot tub, grill out, and hang around a fire pit...yes you can do that ALL at our home. Enjoy your brand new shuffleboard and PACMAN inside the house as well!  We cannot host your party but we can host your mellow group looking to have a cozy haven to return to and enjoy after exploring Music City.

Guest access
You will have the entire home and backyard to yourself during your stay! There is private parking for up to two cars behind the home and additional street parking at the front of the home. No more than 4 cars are allotted please.
