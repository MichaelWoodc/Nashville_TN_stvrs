=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Keep it simple at this peaceful and centrally-located place. Walkable to all the best restaurants in East Nashville and only a few blocks from Basement East and the Titans Stadium, this brand new studio apartment is the perfect location for those who like to do it all. At just under 1 mile to Broadway, walkability is primo!

The space
This brand new studio apartment offers ample space for 2. Queen bed with fresh linens, large bathroom with walk-in shower and nearly full kitchen(no stove) will give you all you need for a long weekend in Nashville. This historic Nashville home was built in the early 1900's and is on the National Registry of Historic Places. It has been owned by the same family since the 1970's and much work as been done too keep it as close to the original as possible. You will notice some of the bare brick walls in this studio-at 3 ft thick, this helps keep the apartment very cool in the summer. The AC is kept at 70°. If you get chilly you are welcome to prop open the door or use the space heater.
