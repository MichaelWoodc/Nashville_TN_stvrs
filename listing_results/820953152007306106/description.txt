Guest access
You’ll have 100% private access to the entire house during your stay (with the exception of a few locked supply/storage closets), including the front and back yard, back deck, and washer/dryer in the laundry room.

A unique, electronic key-code will be provided to you prior to your arrival for an easy self check-in.

High-speed WiFi is available throughout the entire space.

You will have access to smart TV’s in the dining room and the bonus room (as well as the two downstairs bedrooms) during your stay so that you can use your favorite streaming services (such as Netflix, Hulu, and Amazon Prime) from your own account.

There is plenty of free street parking available right outside the house. As a simple precaution for a stay in a large urban area, we recommend that you don’t leave valuables in your vehicles and always keep your car doors locked.

Ubers/Lyfts are abundant and mostly inexpensive in this area. If you choose to drive your own car or rent a car to get around town, just know that parking downtown will typically cost you a pretty penny!
