Free private parking is located in the attached garage for one vehicle. 

Street parking on the main street is available during certain hours and pay parking is available off the alley.

The space
Lots of room! Enjoy a whole space on the first floor with attached one-car garage.  Large fully equipped kitchen with coffee maker and laundry room with washer and dryer. The family room includes a faux fireplace and Smart TV (Roku and other free apps installed, bring your own favorite paid streaming channel).

We do have a pull-out couch in the living room for overflow but be aware the mattress is not very thick and may not be comfortable for adults.

Guest access
Self-check-in with electric lock codes, to ensure safety for all guests. Full access to the entire unit, no shared spaces.

Other things to note
