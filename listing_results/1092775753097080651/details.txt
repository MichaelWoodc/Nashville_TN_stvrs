=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Close to the calamity but private enough for a calm evening - this location alone is a must. A mere 10 minute drive to Broadway, with similar & shorter travels to other wonderful must-see's around town. Ride share miles stay low, and the free parking on premises makes bringing your own car or rental even easier. The interior itself offers modern arrangements & decor with a spacious, open layout with enough natural light to never need to hit a switch during the day. We can't wait to welcome you!

The space
This spacious condo is both charming and gorgeous. Your first steps up the stairs will lead you into the kitchen and living areas, where you'll find up-to-date appliances and amenities, including a full sized washer & dryer, a dining table that can accommodate six, as well as a 55" smart TV, and a pull out couch that includes a queen sized, premium mattress topper. Around the corner you'll find the stairs that lead up to the primary bedroom as well as our patio lined with string lights and wicker patio furniture! In that bedroom you'll find another smart TV,  as well as two queen mattresses, and some of the best lighting in the entire home. You'll also find a master bath with a dual sink, and a closet big enough to live in.
