The space
Whether you're planning a family/friend trip to the city, bachelorette/bachelor get-together, or a couple's retreat, this home provides a spacious layout that's perfect for any group!

This four-story home offers 3 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and a half bath that can sleep up to 10 guests with 5 beds. Guest access
This home is equipped with a smart lock keyless entry system as well as Ring Video Doorbell for added security & easier access. A passcode will be sent to you prior to your stay! 

Full access to the homes private garage during your stay. To ensure vehicle safety, please park in the garage. One large vehicle/two small vehicles will fit comfortably in the garage. The closet in the primary bedroom is locked for storage please use the dresser provided for your clothing. Thank you!
