=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Renovated historic Craftsman built in 1901 with modern updates throughout. Warm, inviting, and spacious, it offers the perfect home base in East Nashville. Walk to Nissan Stadium for TN Titans games or major events, walk to Five Points for local shops, coffee, and restaurants, or take a quick 5-minute drive to downtown. Enjoy easy city access while returning to a quiet neighborhood for a restful night’s sleep away- the perfect balance of character, convenience, and authentic East Nashville charm

The space
This historic house was built in 1901, and still has the original floors with an updated layout and modern kitchen. There are two fully renovated bathrooms,  vaulted ceilings throughout the entire house and plenty of large windows to make the space feel open and inviting. 

The kitchen is equipped with all the essentials and an island with seating. Keurig and coffee pot included. Local artist have played an important role in decorating- the kitchen island and coffee table were hand built by a local artist, headboard in the master bedroom and the large wall in the living room were designed by friends.  Many prints throughout the home are originals. Decor through out the home is a mix of new and old, with lots of finds through the years from local thrift stores and flea markets. 

The master bedroom is spacious with a king size bed, large window with lots of natural light, plenty of furniture to organize your trip, and a bathroom with large walkin shower. We provide all amenities (shampoos, conditioners, body wash, towels etc), have high speed internet and netflix/smart TV.

The second bedroom is equally spacious, with a queen size bed, two large windows, and its own bathroom with shower/tub. 

We also provide our guests with a guidebook of places we personally recommend in Nashville, from bars to restaurants to thrift stores; so our guests can have a firsthand experience of everything Nashville has to offer.

The house is located in Historic Edgefield (right in 5 points) - approx 5 mins/2 miles from downtown, 5 blocks from five points (the heart of east Nashville), 4 miles from Vanderbilt,  and 10 miles from Gaylord Opryland Resort & Opry Mills Mall, and 1.5 miles/5 mins from Nissan Stadium. Very centrally located.

Guest access
Guests have access to the entire house both bedrooms, living room, both bathrooms, large dine in kitchen, fully fenced in backyard with private deck and large wrap around front porch. All these spaces have vaulted ceilings and huge windows. There is indoor and outdoor seating. 

The supply closet in the kitchen is locked off limits to guests.
