Welcome to your perfect Nashville getaway! This beautiful home in the heart of Berry Hill/Nashville comfortably sleeps up to 16 guests, making it ideal for large groups. Nestled in a safe gated community.
Located within walking distance to Geodis Park, minutes from 12 South, 8 South, The Gulch and Downtown.

The space
You will have a picket fenced in small front yard, and tall-more private fenced in small backyard with plenty of seating. There is a fire pit and grill that both run on gas, not propane. We ask that you turn the gasline on before use, and off after use so there can be no mistake of gas still emitting after use. 

There are 3 bedrooms upstairs. 2 average sized rooms each with a queen bed and a bigger "bonus room" bedroom with a day bed where the bottom rolls out to create 2 twin beds. There is also a futon with a good quality mattress that folds down for another sleeping space.  There is a landing with seating connecting these 2 rooms with a full bathroom. There is a TV in each bedroom.  One is a smart TV and the other has Apple TV.  There is NO CABLE but you can log into Netflix, HBO, Showtime and most streaming services that you have a personal account for.

Downstairs you will have a laundry room, full kitchen, half bathroom, living area connected in an open floor plan with a dining area, and a master bedroom with a king sized bed connected to it's own private master bathroom with double sinks, a shower and tub. In the living room is a 65 inch television which does get some standard channels through an antenna and is a smart TV for signing into streaming services you have a personal account for.
