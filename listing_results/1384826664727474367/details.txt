=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Experience Nashville in style! This luxury 3-BR condo sleeps 10 and is just .7 miles from Broadway. Enjoy a bright, modern space with a private balcony and resort-style amenities, including a pool, gym, and co-working lobby. Perfect for groups looking to explore the vibrant heart of Music City. Walk to iconic venues, restaurants, and bars from Layla's Nashville Retreat - the perfect home base for your trip.

The space
Step into a chic, comfortable retreat designed for your group's comfort. The open-concept living area features designer furnishings, Smart TV, electric fireplace, a sleeper sofa, and opens onto a private balcony. Enjoy meals at the six-person dining table overlooking the stunning pool and cabanas. The fully stocked kitchen has everything you need to prepare meals before a night out on the town (please note, cooking oil is not provided.) Workspace on the kitchen island with accessible outlets. Washer/dryer available with complimentary laundry detergent, and starter kit of coffee pods/sugar/creamer, and fresh linens including pool towels are also provided.

Rest and recharge in one of three beautifully appointed bedrooms:
* Primary Bedroom: A plush king-size bed with an ensuite bathroom featuring a double vanity and a shower/tub combination.
* Secondary Bedroom: A comfortable king-size bed, a spacious walk-in closet, and access to a shared bathroom.
* Third Bedroom: A cozy room with a queen-over-queen bunk bed, perfect for light sleepers seeking a quiet space.
* For additional guests, the living room includes a sleeper sofa with a full-size pull-out mattress, complete with a mattress topper for added comfort and extra linens stored in the bedroom closets.
* Traveling with little ones? A pack and play crib is available upon request.
