WestWood Shed • Vacation & Work Stay: a unique and relaxed entertaining space. Four Private Apartments—each with a front living room & open kitchen for your visitors, with a keyed separate bedroom & laundry—all within your private Suite. The City of Berry Hill in Nashville has over 60 music agencies & recording studios, and an efficient police department.

The space
Each Suite has a private entrance, private deck, office-living room & open kitchen. All three lower Suites have "Visitable" spaces, which means your visitors with handicapped limitations can access your front office-living room area—zero-step entrance & walkways, 36" doors, & wheelchair-accessible restroom. There is a dedicated van-accessible parking spot, a wheelchair ramp that leads to the 1st-floor walkway & an accessible shared patio in front. Also, for those who stay in Suite #1, additional railing supports are in the shower/tub and a shower stool. Suite #3 has larger 5-foot turning areas with a wheelchair accessible kitchen, and roll-in shower. Still, Suite #1 is perfect for staying guests with low mobility issues.

Lock away your private area—each Suite has a keypad on the front office-living room door, with interior keyed doors to lock off the bedroom and shower/laundry area. This allows you to share your office-living room with your visitors and co-workers while blocking access to your personal space. You may deadbolt lock the front door at night for added security and block access to the keypad if you've shared your private code with a co-worker or visitor.

A Shared Patio in front of the building extends beside the Tree Boxes along the string of lights. If smoking bothers others on your private deck, the space by the Tree Boxes is a nice area to get away from the building and smoke freely. Please do not smoke inside.
