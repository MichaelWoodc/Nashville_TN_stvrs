Smooth as Tennessee whiskey. A ground-floor suite with its own cocktail bar, two queen bedrooms, and a living room built for the whole crew. Dining for 8, a queen sleeper, and a TV over a decorative fireplace. Sleeps 6 on historic Music Row, a short walk from RCA Studio B.`

Book with the Swiftie Suite next door to unlock the entire first floor for 12 people.

The space
Chris Stapleton's suite is where the evening winds down. Between the galley kitchen and the spacious living room sits a proper cocktail bar - mix a round, then settle in.

The 'Drink You Away' Room and the 'Whiskey & You' Room each have a queen bed and flat-screen TVs. The living room seats everyone, featuring a dining table with 8 stools, a queen sleeper sofa, and a TV above a decorative fireplace. Sleeps 6 across 3 beds, with one full bathroom.

The galley kitchen includes a full-size fridge/freezer, dishwasher, oven, cooktop, microwave, coffee station, cookware, and cutlery.

Out front, the covered porch has outdoor lounge seating plus a table and chairs for 6 - first-row seats for a Music Row evening.

Traveling deeper than 6? This suite connects to the Swiftie Suite through a lockout door. Book both and the entire first floor is yours: 4 bedrooms, 3 baths, sleeping 12.

**ACCESS**

The full suite is yours, plus the covered front porch, one allocated parking space behind the home, and the shared basement laundry.

**NOTES**

You're on 17th Avenue South - the real Music Row, where the labels, publishers, and studios still work. RCA Studio B, where Elvis recorded over 200 songs, is a short walk from the front door. Belmont University and Belmont Mansion are just up the hill; Vanderbilt is less than a mile north.

Hillsboro Village is a 5 minute walk for coffee, brunch, and the Belcourt Theatre. Edgehill Village and 12South are minutes away. Lower Broadway's honky-tonks are about a 5-7 minute drive - close enough for a night out, far enough that you'll actually sleep. Nashville International Airport is roughly 15 minutes by car.

This is one suite within a 98 year-old home divided into five individually themed rentals, so other guests may be staying in neighboring suites. One rear parking space is allocated per suite; free street parking on 17th Ave is first-come, first-served and easiest after business hours. Plan ahead if you're bringing more than one vehicle.

The basement laundry (two full-size washers and dryers, pods and dryer sheets included) is shared with guests in other suites and accessed from outside. Luxury linens, towels, shampoo, conditioner, and shower gel are provided.
