=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Stay where Nashville happens—steps from the music, energy, and unforgettable nights—then come home to relax in comfort downtown.

Whether you’re in town for a weekend of live music, a bachelorette trip, a concert, or a Titans/Preds game, this condo is the ideal Nashville home base. The open-concept living area is great for pre-game hangs before hitting Broadway, and the fully equipped kitchen makes mornings easy after a big night out.

The space
🎸 Downtown Nashville Condo with Views • Garage Parking • Walkable Location
Welcome to this tall-and-skinny, multi-level condo in the heart of downtown Nashville—perfect for groups looking to be close to the music, nightlife, and iconic Broadway energy 🎶.

🏡 Layout & Sleeping Arrangements
The entry level features an open-concept kitchen, living room, dining area, and half bath, ideal for gathering and relaxing. Upstairs are two bedrooms and a Jack-and-Jill full bathroom with dual vanities. One bedroom has two queen bed and some of the best views of Nashville 🌆, while the second offers a king bed.
