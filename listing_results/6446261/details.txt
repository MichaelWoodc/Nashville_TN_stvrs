RECYCLING/TRASH 
For stays of a week or longer, it will be necessary to take the trash and recycling out and prepare it for the city to pick up. For shorter stays of a week or less this may be less necessary. We have compiled the following information regarding trash and recycling bins and pick-up:

Trash day is every Tuesday, and the recycling is picked up once per month, on the first Tuesday of the month. We have two trash bins and one recycling bin that we store outside at the back of the house. The trash bins are brown/tan color, and the recycling is dark green. All trash must be bagged, and all recyclables must be un-bagged. The bins have to be rolled down to the street to be picked up by the city. The recycling and trash trucks can come as early as 6 am, so we’d recommend pulling them down the night before. We're also happy to come by and pull them down if needed, please just let us know your preference in advance.

The black container by the back door is just for glass recycling. That bin does not get picked up by the city. Please let us know if it becomes full during your stay, and we will pick it up to take it to be emptied.

UTILITY USAGE
Guests are expected to use reasonable care in conservation of utilities not chargeable to the guest. Reasonable maximums on utilities based on previous year’s usage during peak in summer months: Metro Water Services = ~$35/month, Nashville Electric Service = ~$120/month, Piedmont Natural Gas = ~$20/month.

ALLERGENS
