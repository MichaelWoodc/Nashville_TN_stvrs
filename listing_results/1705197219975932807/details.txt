=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Located at the Nashville KOA Resort, you can enjoy all of the fantastic campground amenities including two outdoor pools, onsite cafe, live music performances, yard games, dog parks and more.  This location offers a perfect balance of easy access to downtown Nashville with a Downtown Shuttle, but far enough from the heart of the city to enjoy a quiet evening around the campfire.  "Camping Cabins" are an affordable option and do not have a bathroom but are within a short walk to the bath house.

The space
- Sleeps 4: Our rustic, pet-friendly camping cabin offers the perfect blend of comfort and outdoor camping, complete with a full bed and bunk bed. Linens are included. No bathroom inside the cabin but close walk to bath house. 

- Modern Amenities: This cabin is equipped with an air conditioning and heating unit, as well as a ceiling fan. Guests will also have access to their own mini-fridge and WIFI. 

- Outdoor Living Space: Step outside to your porch, relax in the swing, cook dinner on your charcoal grill, and unwind at your fire ring. 

- There is NO Kitchen and NO bathroom in our camping cabin. However, the bathhouse is conveniently located just a short walk from these cabins. If you need a private bathroom, please explore our other cabin listings.

- You are reserving an accommodation type: The amenities and sleeping arrangements will be as described but the actual unit and location within the campground will vary.

Guest access
Guests will have dedicated access to the cabin, front porch with swing, fire pit and BBQ grill. The campground offers many amenities like pools, a fitness center, general store and laundry which are shared facilities. As a registered guest you will have shared access to all the campground amenities.

Other things to note
Our cabins are pet friendly with an additional fee of $25 per pet. No aggressive pets. If left unattended, they must be in a crate. Max of two pets per unit. 

- Plan to have visitors during your stay?  These cabins are limited to 4 people, including guests & visitors. If the number of overnight guests on your reservation allows for day visitors, those day visitors must check in at the registration building upon arrival. Your visitors are welcome for $10 per additional adult. Visiting guests are permitted until Quiet Hours and must adhere to campground rules.

-Please note that there is a maximum of two vehicles allowed per cabin.
