Walk to everything you want in Nashville! The hottest bars, best restaurants, and grocery stores are right outside your door! Or just stay at the condo and enjoy the historic guitar shaped pool. The space
Fully renovated condo with all the charm of old school Nashville!  The Spence Manor condo is quiet and cozy in the heart of Nashville.  There is a free parking garage on the first floor of Spence Manor.  The condo overlooks Buddy Killen Circle, Tailgate Brewery, Owen Bradley Park, and the iconic guitar shaped pool built for Elvis Presley!  The pool is open year round!  

Situated approximately one mile from the bustling Broadway district, Spence Manor provides easy access to Nashville's lively music scene, dining, and shopping options.  The area boasts a Walk Score of 82, indicating it's very walkable.  

At Spence Manor, you’re staying in a piece of history, where some of the most famous singers and songwriters have stayed such as Elvis Presley, Willie Nelson, Kenny Rogers, Frank Sinatra and the list goes on!  Whether you're visiting Nashville for its rich musical heritage or simple seeking a comfortable place to stay, this Spence Manor condo offers a distinctive and convenient lodging experience!
