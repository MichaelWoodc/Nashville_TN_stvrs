The space
Welcome to your premium Nashville stay by NASHBNBRENTALS.
Discover luxury living in the heart of The Gulch, Nashville’s most vibrant, walkable neighborhood. This stunning 1-bedroom, 1-bath condo places you steps from trendy boutiques, world-class dining, live music venues, iconic murals, and the best of urban Music City energy—while offering a peaceful, sophisticated retreat just a short stroll (about 15-25 minutes) or quick 5-minute drive to the legendary buzz of Downtown Broadway.

Inside, enjoy modern elegance with cozy touches: a fully equipped chef’s kitchen for easy meals, plush king-size bed for ultimate comfort, smart TV, fast Wi-Fi, and a private patio perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation.

Elevate your stay with resort-style amenities: a seasonal sparkling pool, gym, free parking, in-unit washer/dryer, and convenient transportation options in this safe, upscale area.

Ideal for romantic escapes, solo adventures, or catching Broadway shows—this chic haven comfortably sleeps 4 (1 king bed + sleeper sofa).

Dog-friendly too—bring your furry friend to join the fun!

Walk to the Gulch Directions:
Head west on South St toward 12th st.
Turn right onto 12th st.
You reach the bridge – you are in the Gulch!

Music Row is just a 10-minute walk from the house.
Music Row
