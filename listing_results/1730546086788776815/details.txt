=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
One Building, Five suites, A 98-year-old home on historic Music Row. Every floor honors a legend: Taylor, Elvis, Dolly, Johnny Cash, & Chris Stapleton. Sleeps 26, 17 beds, 8 Bedrooms, 6 baths, 5 kitchens, 5 free parking spots, balcony with fire pits & Belmont Mansion views. RCA Studio B is a short walk away.

Built for reunions, bachelorette weekends, corporate retreats, & game weekends at Vanderbilt & Belmont. Each suite has its own kitchen, so five families can gather without sharing a fridge.

The space
**THE SPACE**

You're booking all five suites of a 98-year-old home on Music Row, each one designed around a music legend. Every suite has its own entrance, kitchen, and bathroom — so your group gets the togetherness of one house with the privacy of five apartments.

GROUND FLOOR

The Swiftie Suite (sleeps up to 6): 2 bedrooms, 2 full baths with walk-in showers. Bedroom #1 - 'The Showgirl Room' has a queen bed and TV. Bedroom #2 - 'The Meet Me At Midnight Room' has a queen plus twin bunks under a custom starry-night ceiling with a full moon chandelier, TV and a walk-in closet designed to double as a custom makeup station. Full kitchen with dining table for 6.

The Chris Stapleton Tennessee Whiskey Lounge (sleeps up to 6): 2 bedrooms, 1 Full bathroom. Bedroom #1 - 'Drink You Away' and Bedroom #2 'Whiskey & You', each have a queen bed and TV. A spacious living room with a queen sleeper sofa, dining table for 8, cocktail bar, and a decorative fireplace. The covered front porch seats your whole crew - lounge seating plus a table for 6.

These two suites connect through a lockout door, turning the entire first floor into one large space.

SECOND FLOOR

The Elvis Presley Heartbreak Hotel (sleeps up to 6): 1 bedroom with a queen bed and queen sleeper sofa and TV, plus a flex room that converts from a dining room (seating 6) to a third queen bed, also with a TV. Full kitchen with gas range, fridge/freezer, microwave, dishwasher etc. 1 Full bathroom is located in this unit with a walk-in shower.

The Dolly Parton Backwoods Barbie Suite (sleeps up to 10): The biggest suite in the house. Bedroom #1 'The Butterfly Room' has a queen bed. Bedroom #2 'The What Would Dolly Do Room' has a king, two singles in bunk formation, and a walk-in closet that doubles as a custom makeup station. 10 people can get ready for Broadway without fighting over a mirror. The living room adds two queen sleeper sofas, a dining table for 8, a coffee station, and a cocktail stand. 1 Full bathroom serves this unit with a tub/shower combo.

The Johnny Cash Walk The Line Studio (sleeps up to 2): A private queen studio with kitchenette and full bathroom featuring a walk-in-shower. Perfect for the grandparents, the newlyweds, or whoever in your group wants a quiet corner. Pack 'n' Play stored in the closet.

The second-story balcony has outdoor furniture seating for 12, 2 gas fire pits, evening lighting, and a straight view of the Belmont Mansion. One half of the balcony is accessed from The Johnny Cash Suite and the other half from the Backwoods Dolly Suite.

THE NUMBERS

17 beds. 8 Bedrooms. 6 bathrooms. 5 kitchens. 5 parking spaces in the private parking lot. 2 outdoor spaces. Sleeps up to 26 across all suites. Basement laundry (separate exterior access) with 2 full-size washers and dryers, pods and dryer sheets included. Luxury linens, towels, and bath products in every suite.

**NEIGHBORHOOD OVERVIEW**

You're on 17th Avenue South - the real Music Row, where the labels, publishers, and studios still work. RCA Studio B, where Elvis recorded over 200 songs, is a short walk from the front door. Belmont University and Belmont Mansion are just up the hill; Vanderbilt is less than a mile away. Walk to Vandy & Belmont games, be it Baseball, Football, Lacrosse, Tennis or Soccer.

Hillsboro Village is a 5-minute walk for coffee, brunch, and the Belcourt Theatre. Edgehill Village, The Gulch and 12South (Draper James, Five Daughters doughnuts & countless restaurants) are minutes away. Lower Broadway's honky-tonks are about a 5-7 minute drive - close enough for a night out, far enough that you'll actually sleep. Nashville International Airport is roughly 15 minutes by car.
