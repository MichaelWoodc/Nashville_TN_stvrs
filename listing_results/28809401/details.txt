Step into this charming and stylish 2-story modern home right in the heart of East Nashville’s lively historic district, just 3 miles from downtown. The space
This brand new beautifully designed bungalow is fully stocked with every amenity you could ever wish for. Open living room and kitchen boast tons of natural light. Relax around the indoor gas fireplace with an over sized sectional providing plenty of space for large groups, or play some tunes on an antique piano. Head outside and sit in the front porch swing to witness some of the most breathtaking sunsets you have ever seen! The outdoor space in the backyard is designed with entertaining in mind! Have dinner outside or lay on the sofa bed to look at the stars. 

The master bedroom with a beautiful king bed and en-suite bathroom are located on the main level. There are three additional bedrooms upstairs to accommodate additional guests. The bedrooms contain blackout curtains, and plenty of storage for your belongings. This room has been updated with two additional twin beds replacing the desk to accommodate a more comfortable sleeping quarters. We also have a queen blow up mattress to use for additional guests to sleep in this area.
