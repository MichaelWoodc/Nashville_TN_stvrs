HISTORY IN THE MAKING - A MUSIC CITY STORY:  

H&O, (now “The Crescendo”) is one of the first purposefully built new construction developments on Music Row. Seeking to reflect the history of its avenue, H&O pays a special honor to brothers Harold and Owen Bradley who are often credited with creating that Nashville sound that began in a Quonset Hut attached to a house they owned on 16th Avenue which eventually became known as “Music Row.”
