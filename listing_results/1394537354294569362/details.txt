=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Rustic Cabin at Pilot Knob is loaded with historic charm! This one bedroom cabin sleeps up to 6 guests and is one of three properties on a quiet, private estate.

The space
On the main floor, you and your guests can gather in the living room for great conversation or game night, play music on the record player, and there's plenty of room for everyone to enjoy a meal around the table and kitchen bar.

The Cabin sits on the front of the property and features a large covered front porch with a swing and lots of seating to enjoy the wildlife as they come through.

At Pilot Knob Estate, we are proud to offer communal lake access for all the cabins. This means that every guest staying at our estate has the wonderful opportunity to enjoy and make unforgettable memories by the lake. Whether you're looking to swim, fish, or simply relax by the water, our communal lake access ensures that everyone can indulge in the natural beauty and serenity of the lake during their stay.
