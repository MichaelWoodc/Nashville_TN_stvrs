The space
Working remotely in Nashville? We’d love to host you! Reach out to inquire about extended-stay options—we’d be happy to accommodate your work trip!

Located in the heart of the Gulch in downtown Nashville, Mercury View Lofts is exactly where you want to be to take advantage of all that the Music City has to offer. 

Situated directly across the street from Nashville’s iconic Station Inn, the lofts are walking distance to a variety of restaurants and nightlife and just six blocks to the Music City Center. Continue just two blocks further to enjoy the nightlife on Broadway. The loft’s convenient location will allow you to leave your car at home and explore the city on foot. 

The 970 SF loft features 15ft ceilings and huge windows looking out onto Nashville and the vibrant Gulch neighborhood. The loft offers a king-size bed, as well as a sleeper sofa with a memory foam mattress in the living room that provides a second sleeping spot if it is needed.

We offer a SmartTV with all streaming capabilities (NO cable), high-speed wifi, and a washing machine and dryer in your unit! The space is a true loft with an open layout - there is no door between the bedroom and living room.

Gym & pool access by request - OFFSITE at our sister property just 0.2 miles away. Please request from the hours of 8:30am - 5pm. All requests after 5pm will be handled the next business day! 

Please note, the unit has an industrial open piping structure, which has the potential for more noise when the AC/Heat kicks on. If you are sensitive to that type of noise, let our team know! We do provide a sound machines in the bedroom, but are happy to provide ear plugs if needed as well.
