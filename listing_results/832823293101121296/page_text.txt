Enjoy your beautiful stay in the heart of Nashville, with living-like-a-local vibe... yet <1mi from Broadway! 
If you're looking for the perfect centralized location for your stay in Nash-Vegas, this modern styled condo is close to Nashville's best attractions (Nissan Stadium, Bridgestone Arena, First Horizon Park, Country Music Hall of Fame, The Ryman Auditorium,  Nashville's Farmers Market and Broadway). So, if you want to experience what Nashville has to offer, this is your place!!

The space
This is a 1bedroom/1bathroom condo on the 3rd floor and just under 900 st ft in Germantown. It's the perfect location and walkable to just about everything Nashville. 

The bedroom has a king size bed.  I have 2 blow up mattresses if needed. 

The kitchen is fully equipped, new stainless steal appliances, kureig coffee maker, toaster.  It has a courtyard balcony with seating for 2.

Guest access
The whole condo minus the entry coat closet which is my owners closet.

There are over door hangers for your convenience.

Other things to note
I can not stress enough what a perfect location this is!!!!!
