⭐️⭐️ Welcome to Mimosas at Tiffany’s! ⭐️⭐️
📍Nashville, TN 

Located less than 5 minutes from the heart of downtown, this stylish condo is designed with groups in mind! Step into a bright, modern space featuring chic, on-trend decor—perfect for those IG-worthy group shots. Whether you're mixing mimosas or preparing a group dinner, the fully stocked kitchen has everything you need to cook, sip, and celebrate in style.

The space
Enhance your Nashville getaway with exclusive savings on top attractions and experiences. Through our partnership with Tripster, Halara Hosting guests enjoy special discounts on shows, tours, museums, and local favorites—available through our private booking link after reservation.

To access the discounted offers, simply search “Halara Hosting Nashville Tripster” in your browser or message us during your stay and we’ll be happy to share the booking link with you!

We stock the home with starter supplies to get you comfortably through your first day or two — toilet paper, hand soap, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, dish soap, dishwasher and laundry pods, paper towels, and trash bags. East Nashville, one of Nashville's most diverse and emerging neighborhoods, has a quirky vibe and a buzzing food and nightlife scene. The Five Points area is known for its street art, upbeat bars, trendy fine-dining restaurants and vibrant independent music scene (the 5 Spot or Basement East).
