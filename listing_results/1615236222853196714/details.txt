Elevated Midtown Retreat 

Design-forward, Midtown condo in historic building that blends architectural character with modern comfort offering a stay that feels more like a boutique hotel than a typical short-term rental. The space
Step into a thoughtfully designed Midtown residence where historic character meets modern comfort. With soaring ceilings, exposed beams, and warm layered lighting throughout, the home offers an inviting, upscale atmosphere that feels more like a private boutique suite than a typical rental.

The spacious living room provides plenty of space to unwind, featuring comfortable seating, a pull-out sofa for additional sleeping space, a Smart TV, and high-speed Wi-Fi—perfect for relaxing evenings, streaming your favorite shows, or catching up on work.

The primary bedroom offers a calm, restful retreat with a king-size bed, quality linens, and a dedicated workspace, making it ideal for both relaxation and productivity. Whether you’re visiting Nashville for business, medical travel, or a weekend getaway, the bedroom is designed to provide both comfort and quiet.

The modern kitchen is fully equipped with updated appliances, generous counter space, and everything needed to prepare anything from morning coffee to full meals. Whether you’re in town for a quick stay or a longer visit, the kitchen is designed to make dining in easy and convenient.
