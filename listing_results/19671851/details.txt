Guest apartment, located on the 1st floor of our home. 2 full size beds, a bathroom w/ walk-in shower, small full kitchen, & living room w/ dining space. Hidden away on 2 wooded acres, just up the road from Nashville - but even closer to the best hiking, canoeing, scenic drives & country cooking in Middle Tennessee. Garage bays for guests, & your own private patio space.  UBER & LYFT available and used by many guests.

The space
You're in the middle of the woods but close to everything. The famous Loveless Cafe is 4 miles away from our house - right next to the entrance of Natchez Trace Scenic Parkway.
