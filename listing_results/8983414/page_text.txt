REDUCED PRICE FOR THE WEEKEND! Beautiful designer home in Sylvan Park, just 1 mile from downtown! Enjoy bright, stylish interiors, a modern kitchen, cozy living spaces, and an upstairs game room perfect for groups. Relax on the covered patio or stroll to nearby restaurants, coffee shops, trails, and parks. Close to Nashville’s coolest neighborhoods, this charming retreat offers comfort, convenience, and an authentic Music City stay with plenty of space to unwind.

The space
This spacious home offers four comfortable bedrooms, including two king beds, one queen bed, and two twin beds, along with three full bathrooms. Enjoy a gorgeous dining area, a cozy sitting room off the modern, fully equipped kitchen, and an upstairs game room and den complete with board games, a multi-game table (pool, air hockey, and more), playing cards, a big-screen TV, and a DVD player with iconic movies. Outside, relax on the backyard patio with a grill and outdoor dining area—perfect for unwinding after exploring Nashville.
The home was thoughtfully designed for short-term rentals and includes everything needed for a comfortable, memorable stay. It accommodates 8 guests, with two additional twin air mattresses available for parties of up to 10. ($40 per person per night after 8 guests.) The listed price also includes taxes, so there are no hidden fees.
If the home is still available two weeks before your desired dates, we’re happy to consider a two-night minimum stay—just ask!
