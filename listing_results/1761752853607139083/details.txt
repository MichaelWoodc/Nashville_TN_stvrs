=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Enjoy Music City from my favorite part of Nashville—East Nashville. This newly remodeled private guest suite in quiet Maplewood Heights has a queen bed, full bath, sitting area, fast Wi-Fi, Netflix, and its own climate control. You’ll also have a mini fridge, microwave, Keurig, and dedicated parking. Just minutes from Five Points, 10 minutes to the Opry, and about 15 minutes to downtown and the airport. A cozy place to relax after a day or night exploring Nashville.

The space
The guest suite is a newly remodeled section of our 1970s ranch-style home with its own private exterior entrance.
Inside you’ll have a queen bed, private full bathroom with a walk-in shower, sitting area, dining table for two, TV, mini fridge, microwave, Keurig, and fast Wi-Fi. The suite also has its own climate control, so you can set the temperature however you like.
Netflix is included on the TV—just use the “Guest” or “Kids” profile during your stay.
The suite is connected to our home, but there are no shared indoor spaces. It’s set up to give you your own place to relax and come and go independently.
There is no cleaning fee.

Guest access
Check-in is completely contactless. Before your stay, you’ll receive a unique access code for the guest suite’s exterior door.
The parking pad directly in front of the house is your dedicated parking spot during your stay.
Guests have private access to the guest suite and its bathroom. The main house and backyard are not included with the rental.
Wi-Fi information will be available in the room.

During your stay
We live on the property, but you’ll be able to check in and enjoy the space without any in-person interaction. If you need anything during your stay, just send me a message through Airbnb.
