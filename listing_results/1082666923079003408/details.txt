The Retreats at South Water Manor are a one of kind destination. Originally built in the 1860s, this building was used as servants quarters, while the second was a summer kitchen for the FitzGerald Manor, which remains on the property. Essentially untouched for the past 100 years, they underwent a complete, exhaustive renovation from the ground up, converting them into the upscale guesthouses they are today.
