Guest access
This condo is yours to enjoy! Guests have full, private access to the entire space, including two bedrooms, two bathrooms, the open kitchen and living area, the glam get-ready vanity room, and the extra-long private balcony overlooking the courtyard pool—perfect for morning coffee or pre-night-out hangs.

Check-in is seamless with secure key-code access to both the building and the condo. You’ll receive easy step-by-step instructions before arrival, so getting settled is stress-free.

Arriving early? Guests who arrive before check-in are welcome to store their luggage at the Green Light Bar next door, so you can drop your bags and start enjoying the neighborhood right away.

During your stay, you’ll also have access to the resort-style courtyard pool and modern fitness center during posted hours—ideal for recovery days between nights out.

Parking: Guests have access to one reserved parking space in the secure garage, plus additional community garage parking (first-come, first-served) and free street parking.

Please note: A small owner’s closet is locked and not part of guest access. All other spaces are private and exclusively yours.

If you have any questions about access or amenities before booking, feel free to reach out.

Other things to note
This condo is in a secure residential building and is ideal for girls trips and celebrations, but it is not a “party house.” Paying guests only, and we ask that you are respectful of neighbors.

The condo sleeps up to 8 guests, including a king-size sofa sleeper. There is extra storage for luggage under queen bunk beds. 

Early check-in and late check-out cannot be guaranteed, but early arrivals may store luggage at the Green Light Bar next door and start enjoying the neighborhood.

Here’s how it works: pay for a pair of drinks at this bar/restaurant/music venue and they’ll hold your luggage for free. This spot is also the transportation hub for JoyRide. They can hook you up for a sightseeing tour or group transportation via their diverse fleet including golf carts, monster trucks, and tour buses.

The pool and fitness center are shared amenities and subject to posted hours and seasonal availability.

As the condo is centrally located, some normal city noise may be heard. Complimentary earplugs are provided for light sleepers—just in case other guests are enjoying their stay too.
