=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Golden Hour has everything you want for a Nashville getaway.  Freshly renovated townhouse with luxury furnishings, fully-stocked kitchen, and comfortable beds to melt into after a long day of sightseeing.  Take a step outside into the fenced-in back yard and enjoy an evening by the fire.  High-end body wash, shampoo, and conditioner are provided for you to rejuvenate yourself for another day.

The space
The kitchen, living room, and half bath are all located downstairs.  The two bedrooms and the full bath are upstairs.  Both bedrooms are equipped with really comfortable beds.  One bedroom has a Casper mattress and the other bedroom has a Nectar mattress.  The couch downstairs has a bedding kit that turns the couch into a comfortable sleeping arrangement for a 5th guest.

Guest access
If you are driving in, we have space in the driveway for a maximum of 2 vehicles. There is additional parking on the side of the lot, but please communicate if you have more than 2 vehicles.

Other things to note
We have sleeping arrangements for a maximum of five people.  Two people in each bedroom upstairs, and one person on the Burrow couch that coverts to a single person bed with the Nomad sleep kit.  We also have a pack ‘n play available for a small toddler or infant as well.  The neighbors are very sweet people, so we ask that you respect that they live in close proximity by limiting your noise levels.  If you need help with anything, don't hesitate to reach out to us!
