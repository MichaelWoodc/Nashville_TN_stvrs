▌TRUE WALKABILITY: THE 12 SOUTH LIFESTYLE

This is the kind of neighborhood where you start the morning with a 100-layer donut from Five Daughters Bakery or a latte on the porch at Frothy Monkey, then spend the afternoon browsing Nashville's best independent boutiques. Everything below is within walking distance.

★ CAN'T-MISS CULINARY DELIGHTS

⊙ Buttermilk Ranch (BMR): Arguably the crown jewel of the 12 South brunch scene. High-end, pastry-focused brunch just steps away. Expect a wait on weekends, but with their world-class counter service and Cube Croissants, it's worth every minute.

⊙ Bar Taco: The ultimate social hub with what is arguably the most coveted outdoor patio in the city. Sip a signature Bar Taco Margarita (fresh-squeezed lime is the secret) and enjoy an afternoon of people-watching. Don't miss the Duck Birria or Ahi Tuna Tataki, paired with hand-mashed guacamole.

⊙ Frothy Monkey: Neighborhood coffee shop perfection. A rosemary latte to start the morning, or a glass of wine to end the evening.

⊙ Postino: The hip new wine bar that replaced Taqueria del Sol. Perfect for late-afternoon bruschetta boards and a chilled glass of rosé.

⊙ Urban Grub: Farm-to-table dining with incredible oysters, steaks, and craft cocktails. A true neighborhood gem.

⊙ Five Daughters Bakery: Famous for their 100-layer donuts. A must-stop for anyone with a sweet tooth.

★ BOUTIQUE SHOPPING

12 South is arguably Nashville's best shopping destination for independent and high-end brands.

⊙ Draper James: Reese Witherspoon's flagship brand, right around the corner.

⊙ Imogene + Willie: Famous for custom denim, housed in a beautifully converted gas station.

⊙ White's Mercantile: A modern general store with a curated, sophisticated feel.

⊙ Emerson Grace & Judith Bright: Thoughtfully curated fashion and jewelry you won't find downtown.

⊙ The 12 South Collection: A newer development featuring Rag & Bone, Alice + Olivia, and more. A luxury retail layer on top of the local favorites.

★ THE NEIGHBORHOOD

⊙ Sevier Park: The neighborhood's "front lawn." Perfectly manicured and home to a wonderful farmers market on Tuesdays (May through October).

⊙ Vanderbilt & Belmont University: Beautiful campuses perfect for an afternoon walk just minutes away.

⊙ The Vibe: Polished. Walkable. Curated. 12 South is where restored historic bungalows meet independent boutiques and world-class dining. 

It's an energy you can feel the moment you step outside.
