"We spent most of our time on the rooftop, from morning coffee to late night chats." That is what a guest said about AllSaints House, our four-suite townhouse in Nashville, 3/4 mile from Broadway. Your group gets ready in four en-suite baths, each stocked with an iron, steamer, and straightener. Evenings move up to our rooftop, fire pit going, string lights on, dining for twelve. Three quarters of a mile from Broadway, our townhouse has hosted over fifty groups, most of them ten or twelve people who wanted one house instead of four hotel rooms. Four floors give everyone somewhere to be, the rooftop carries the fire pit and the dining table, and four and a half baths mean the whole group gets ready at once. Each ensuite bedroom has its own ironing board, iron, steamer, blow dryer, hair straightener and curler, ideal for bachelorette groups wanting to depart on time to Music City hotspots. Coffee up top or on the front terrace off the living room in the morning and a short ride to Broadway at night is the shape of most stays here.

Our three floors stack the gathering spaces and the bedrooms on separate levels, so the group can be together or apart without anyone tiptoeing. The bunk room sits on the entry floor, built as its own hangout with a bar counter, stools, makeup mirrors, and a mounted 70" TV. The kitchen and living room sit above, with a king-ensuite bedroom, tv, workstation desk, and walk-in shower off the kitchen. Off the living room with 75" tv is a covered terrace that holds a wicker sectional and an egg chair for coffee, a private chat, or a smoke. A king and queen site on the third-floor, each with a full bath and both have a desks, tvs, mirrors, and chair for anyone working.

Our rooftop is the reason most guests book. It runs the length of the building under a pergola strung with mood-setting cafe lights, with dining for twelve at one end, a bar counter and stools facing the downtown skyline at the other, and a fire pit in the lounge seating between them for the hour after everyone gets back from Broadway. A grill is set up there for group dinners. Two floors down, the covered terrace off the living room is the quiet counterpart, allowing for private spaces while still perfect for large groups.
