Welcome to our cozy retreat! Unwind in the charming living room, whip up meals in the well-equipped kitchen, and sleep soundly in comfy beds. You'll love the serene backyard for morning coffee and evening BBQs. Situated in a vibrant neighborhood, you'll be close to top attractions, music venues, and a variety of dining options. Whether you're here for a weekend or an extended stay, our house offers the perfect blend of comfort and convenience, making your Nashville experience truly memorable!

The space
The property consists of a large backyard with an area equipped with a firepit. The two bedrooms in the house have plenty of space, with one having a queen sized bed and the other having a double bed. The dining room/kitchen has a 5 person breakfast nook for meals while the living room comfortably seats 5. 
For one or two additional guests, there is sleeping space on the couch or an air mattress provided in the bedroom closet.

Guest access
Guests will be able to access any and all parts of the house and property with the exception of an owners closet in the second bedroom and a couple of owners cabinets. Everything else is open and available.

Other things to note
The house can comfortably fit 5 guests, as long as one is okay sleeping on the couch or an air mattress (provided). 

I would also like guests to know that the neighborhood is very charming and there tends to be a lot of out of towners for the weekend. To the left and right of the house, however, are full time home residents and would like to remind guests to be respectful of those neightbors.

From the host
The neighborhood is the ultimate blend of laid-back charm and artistic vibes. East Nashville is the hippest section of Music City. Steeped in history, this neighborhood is a vibrant hub of local music, arts, award-winning dining, and craft beer delights. Located just across the river from downtown, East Nashville beckons with its eclectic mix of local tunes, artisanal brews, unique boutiques, and mouthwatering culinary experiences.

Listed are some of the best local attractions in the neighborhood:

- The Dive Motel: A trendy adult pool, bar, and motel open to guests. It is a gem stuck in time that is a huge summer attractive to visitors and locals alike.

- Red Headed Stranger: A popular neighborhood taco shop, Red Headed Stranger is a trendy restaurant inspired by southeastern style foods.

- Rosemary-Beauty Queen: This low key bar is a hot spot in the popular 5-Points area in East Nashville. Two combined bars, Rosemary lends itself well to the bar-goer who wants to be discreet. Beauty Queen is the garage bar in the back alley that doubles as a small dance club with a kitchen for food.
