The space
The property consists of a large backyard with an area equipped with a firepit. The two bedrooms in the house have plenty of space, with one having a queen sized bed and the other having a double bed. The dining room/kitchen has a 5 person breakfast nook for meals while the living room comfortably seats 5. 
For one or two additional guests, there is sleeping space on the couch or an air mattress provided in the bedroom closet.

Guest access
Guests will be able to access any and all parts of the house and property with the exception of an owners closet in the second bedroom and a couple of owners cabinets. Everything else is open and available.

Other things to note
The house can comfortably fit 5 guests, as long as one is okay sleeping on the couch or an air mattress (provided). 

I would also like guests to know that the neighborhood is very charming and there tends to be a lot of out of towners for the weekend. To the left and right of the house, however, are full time home residents and would like to remind guests to be respectful of those neightbors.
