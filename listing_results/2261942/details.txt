=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This house just had its 100th birthday, but has had tons of updates to keep it fresh.   Recently featured in Apartment Therapy, it's on a quiet street, but is an easy walk to Five Points and just 2 miles from downtown.  The hot tub on the back deck is just the way to finish out the evening.

The space
This bedroom has a comfy queen-sized bed that rests in an alcove between closets.  It has a private entrance off the front porch where you can have a morning coffee.  There is plenty of room to hang clothes or to put them in the antique chest of drawers.  The original hardwood floors and high ceilings will remind you that they really don't make them like this anymore.  The guest bathroom takes a fresh twist to vintage styling and comes with towels, soaps and bath robes.  The bathroom features a clawfoot tub that's just the place for a long soak or shower.  (This rental is a shared space, and the bedroom and bath are separated by the dining room and kitchen.  Midnight runs to the restroom in your skivvies are only for the daring.)

Also, this space is better-suited to people 18 and up.  Nashville law requires that the primary renter be over 21.

Guest access
Guests are welcome to use the whole house (aside from the master suite and basement).  You're free to use the WiFi.  There is no cable, but the TV has Roku, so you can log into your favorite streaming platforms. Make yourself a cup of coffee, or head to the end of the block for a gourmet coffee at Barista Parlor.  Try out the deck, where a terrific hot tub awaits.  You're welcome to pop some clothes in the washer/dryer.

During your stay
I will be present when guests are staying over and am glad to go with the flow of what you need.  If you want to get the lay of the land, I'm your man.  Also happy to give you your space.

I've travelled all over the world and am excited to be able to host some people visiting my town.  This home and East Nashville are LGBT friendly.

Other things to note
* During December, I put up a real Christmas tree, so if you have allergies to fir, this might not be a good choice for you.

* Nashville codes do not allow me to serve food, so I guess this is better described as "Airb."
