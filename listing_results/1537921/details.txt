=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to my charming cottage, nestled just minutes away from the vibrant energy of lower Broadway's famous Honky Tonks and the iconic Ryman Auditorium. Whether you're here for a game and Nissan Stadium is your destination, or you're eager to explore the trendy restaurants, coffee shops, and local watering holes near the historic "5 Points" neighborhood, our location is truly central.
Come and immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of old and new Nashville.

The space
Welcome to your private retreat in the heart of East Nashville! The cottage offers a unique and separate alley entrance behind the main home, ensuring your privacy during your stay. Step inside and discover a cozy haven with all the comforts you need.

The fully equipped kitchen invites you to whip up your favorite meals, and the convenience of a washer and dryer ensures you can pack light for your trip. Enjoy a restful night's sleep on the Queen size bed and stay entertained when streaming your favorite shows on the internet access.

The newly remodeled bathroom boasts a spacious tiled shower, adding a touch of luxury to your stay. Hardwood floors throughout the cottage provide a warm and inviting atmosphere.

Take advantage of the front porch in the courtyard, the perfect spot to savor your morning coffee, enjoy a quiet lunch, or unwind with an evening beverage. Also the cottage is located a half block to Shelby Bottoms Park where you can take a morning walk or run through acres of nature trails. As you plan your next adventure, let the peaceful ambiance of this space inspire you.

After exploring the vibrant sights and sounds here in "Nashvegas," return to the cottage for a quiet and comfortable retreat. We invite you to be our guest and experience the harmonious blend of old and new Nashville. Your relaxing and memorable stay awaits!
