=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Escape to the tranquility of a 96-acre working farm — complete with rolling pastures, peaceful ponds, and even a hidden waterfall. Just 30 minutes from downtown Nashville, this agritourism retreat offers a serene country experience without sacrificing convenience.

The space
Relax in your private apartment featuring rustic barn-wood details, modern amenities, and a steam shower for the perfect end to your day. Step out onto the back deck to catch breathtaking sunsets, or gather around the fire pit to roast s’mores under a canopy of stars.
Inside, you’ll find a farm map highlighting the best walking trails, fishing spots, and scenic viewpoints. You’ll also have access to the entire apartment and balcony — completely private and all yours to enjoy.

Guest access
The entire apartment and balcony is yours to enjoy. In the apartment you will find a map with information as to where you can explore . There are several ponds (including one pond you can fish) on the farm and when we are not in a dry spell, a small waterfall. Feel free to gather some wood and roast s'mores or hotdogs over the fire pit.

Other things to note
You may encounter other folks who live and work on the farm. Feel free to ask questions about our farm mission to help feed the hungry. 
The apartment is completely yours so if you want privacy that is totally available as well.
