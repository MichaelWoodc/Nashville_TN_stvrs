=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
At Ironwood Grove we have created an unprecedented tiny house hospitality experience nestled in the bustling, eclectic epicenter of East Nashville. Our tiny homes let you explore the curiosity of tiny living while indulging in our special selection of local amenities, our clever and efficient uses of small spaces, and stunning finishes. We hope Ironwood Grove feels like your cozy (tiny) home away from home as you explore the local music, art, and food of the great Music City.

The space
This tiny house has our most luxurious kitchen space, complete with a full range, larger refrigerator, combination washer/dryer, dishwasher, and dining area. Guests are welcome to enjoy our complimentary beverage bar with our exclusive locally roasted coffee and assortment of teas. Open the garage door for all the fresh air and natural light, and access to a quaint patio lounge space. A cozy main bedroom sleeps our guests on a queen-sized memory foam mattress and features built in cabinetry for luggage storage. A full bathroom and lux tiled shower are complete with the Good Fill's clean, zero waste soaps, and bath & body products. Maximum occupancy: 2

Guest access

Also, please note we have fast WiFi access, but no TVs. You are welcome to bring streaming devices if you need this kind of entertainment!
