=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Visit Music City with a comfortable stayover at the Cowboy Copas Room. Located in the park-like neighborhood of Inglewood in East Nashville. Conveniently situated near several bars, delicious restaurants and easy access to Briley Parkway & the airport. Complete with Western vibes, a plush queen bed, a sleeper futon couch, work/dining station, wifi, TV, fridge & a stocked kitchenette. Enjoy your own private entrance, shower, bathroom, patio & off street parking. Minutes from 5 Points & downtown.

The space
Our house is a 1950s single family home nestled in the Inglewood area of East Nashville.   The area has a nice mix of longtime residents and younger families, giving it a true community feel. 

You'll enjoy the quiet streets while still being just minutes from some of Nashville’s best spots.

We're about a 15-20 min drive/uber from downtown Nashville and about 12-15 min from BNA Airport, so it's a very convenient spot to be.  Plus, we're about 7 min from the Grand Ole Opry.
