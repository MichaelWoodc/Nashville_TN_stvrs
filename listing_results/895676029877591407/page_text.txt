Escape to a luxurious historic loft in Nashville’s iconic Printers Alley, just minutes from Broadway, Downtown, and top music venues. Perfect for large groups, wedding parties, bachelorette trips, and special events, this spacious retreat features 14-ft ceilings, upscale modern finishes, and smart home technology. Walk to bars, live music, and restaurants, then get event-ready at our full-service salon located directly below the units—ideal for hair, makeup, and group bookings.

The space
Welcome to your exquisite Nashville retreat in historic Printers Alley, where timeless character meets modern luxury. Soaring 14-foot ceilings and exposed architectural details create a grand, spacious ambiance—perfect for group stays, wedding parties, and special occasions just minutes from Broadway and downtown nightlife.

Living Space: Relax in the open-concept living area with rich dark green paneled walls, a plush sofa, and stylish velvet armchairs—ideal for entertaining or unwinding.
Gourmet Kitchen: Cook and gather around the marble island with premium stainless steel appliances and sleek cabinetry.
Bedroom & Bathroom: Enjoy a serene bedroom and a spa-like bathroom with floor-to-ceiling black tile and a glass-enclosed shower.
Smart Features: Voice-controlled tech and a 75-inch TV elevate your stay.
Extras: Wireless chargers in every room for convenience.

Plus, get event-ready at our full-service salon located just below the units—perfect for hair, makeup, and group bookings.

Guest access
You will have private access to the entire loft during your stay. Make yourself at home and enjoy all the comfort and style it has to offer.

Other things to note
We are committed to providing a five-star experience. Please let us know if there is anything we can do to make your stay more comfortable.
