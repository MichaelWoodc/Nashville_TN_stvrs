=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Hachland Hill's Main Inn is a cabin/lodge with logs over 200 years old. Situated on 90 acres of stunning natural beauty (flowers, shrubs, spring fed creek, abundant wildlife) + hiking trails for your enjoyment. The Main Inn is perfect for solo-travelers, couples looking for a serene escape or a family getaway. You'll be at home in the country, but just a short drive from downtown Nashville and the airport.

The space
Poplar #2 bedroom has 1 king sized bed, and 1 twin bed. This bedroom has a private bathroom. The common areas of the Main Inn includes a living room,  a dining room with a beautiful view. and an outdoor terrace with plenty of seating for your enjoyment. There is also a common Rec room with ping pong/pool. The inn is appointed with primitive antiques and handmade quilts, yet offers modern conveniences and comfort.

Guest access
The inn has over 10,000 sq. feet.  Make yourselves at home in the cozy living room,  the lovely terrace overlooking the woods, the comfortable porches, and the wooded hills and open meadows.

Other things to note
Designed and appointed with love by Tennessee Treasure, Phila Hach (chef, author, TV personality). Pictures just don't do the setting justice. eight other private rooms are available as well as our small inn, Spring Creek (5BR/5BA) in its entirety for groups of up to 18 pp..
