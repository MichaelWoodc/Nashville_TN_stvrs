=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The ultimate Nashville group stay. Stay side-by-side in our stylish Monroe Homes — perfect for bachelorettes, wedding weekends, girls’ trips, family getaways, and group retreats. Enjoy the fun of staying together with the comfort, privacy, and elevated design of a boutique stay — not your typical party house.

This listing includes Monroe Homes 9 and 10, offering plenty of space for your group, plus private car garages and convenient parking for a stress-free stay.

The space
Welcome to Monroe House #9 and 10, Two of 16 custom-built homes arranged around a shared social courtyard on the Buena Vista / Germantown edge. This is not a random standalone rental with beds packed in. The whole community was designed for groups who want their own private house, real bedrooms, full kitchens, and a central place to gather.

Inside, the house is a three-story townhome built in 2020 with high ceilings, big windows, modern finishes, and an open main level. The kitchen has a full-size fridge, dishwasher, gas range, coffee bar, cookware, dishes, and the basics for eating in before a night out. The dining area seats 8, and the living room gives the group a comfortable place to land between plans.

Each House:
The sleeping setup is simple and group-friendly: 4 bedrooms, 5 beds, and 3 full baths across three floors. You also have fast Wi-Fi, central AC, smart TV, and in-unit washer/dryer. Plus a  private 2 car garage 

Out front, the house opens straight into the shared Monroe courtyard with two pickleball courts, fire pit seating, BBQ grills, outdoor dining tables, cornhole, foosball, and giant Jenga.

Guest access
Each house Guests will have private access to the entire home, including the kitchen, living room, bedrooms, bathrooms, laundry area, and your own private attached two-car garage.
Private Two-Car Garage
Your stay includes access to a private attached two-car garage, giving you the convenience of secure, dedicated parking right at the home.
Please note:
Garage space may vary depending on vehicle size. Additional free street parking is available on Monroe Street on a first-come, first-served basis.
Shared Courtyard
Step outside your front door and you'll be right in the heart of the Monroe community—our shared social courtyard, designed for groups to gather, play, and enjoy time together.
Enjoy two pickleball courts, fire pit seating, BBQ grills, outdoor dining tables, cornhole, foosball, and giant Jenga.
The courtyard is shared with guests staying in the neighboring Monroe Houses, creating a fun and social atmosphere while you still enjoy the privacy of your own home.
