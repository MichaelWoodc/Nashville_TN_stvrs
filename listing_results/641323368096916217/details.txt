The space
Once the sun drops, this group lives on the back patio: a sectional, string lights, a glowing 'Nashville' neon sign, and a giant four-in-a-row game that turns into a running tournament. Inside, the open kitchen and living room keep everyone in one conversation. Leather sofas face a fireplace and TV while the island and its foosball table pull cooks and game-watchers into the same room.

Rock House earns its name through 4 themed bedrooms, plus a built-in photo-op corner with pink and white balloons, a silver tinsel backdrop, and a disco ball for the group shot everyone wants from the trip. The standout bedroom is the Bunk Room, 6 stacked twin beds under a vaulted ceiling and a pink neon sign, alongside rooms honoring Dolly, Johnny Cash, and Elvis.

Spend the day exploring downtown Nashville, come back to the Airbnb for patio drinks as the string lights click on, snap a few shots at the tinsel backdrop, then split off to whichever themed room calls your name.
