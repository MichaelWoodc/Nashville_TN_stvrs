=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Stay in the Heart of East Nashville’s Creative Soul

Steps from 5 Points, this light-filled retreat blends art, comfort, and walkable charm. Enjoy original ceramic installations by Jim Sherraden of Hatch Show Print and a peaceful street just moments from the action.

The space features a modern kitchen, dual-sink bath, large shower, and private water closet — all designed for a relaxed, stylish stay.

A songwriter studio may be available depending on dates — perfect for creative getaway!

The space
Step inside to a bright, open living and kitchen space — perfect for relaxing or entertaining. A comfy queen sleeper sofa, sculpted leather chair, and ottoman make it easy to unwind, while high windows fill the room with light and treetop views. Local art, including original ceramics by Jim Sherraden of Hatch Show Print, adds a creative Nashville touch.

The modern kitchen has everything you need — a full range, roomy fridge, microwave, dishwasher, and island with bar seating.

The quiet bedroom features a plush queen bed, blackout curtains, and an oversized closet with plenty of shelves and hanging space for longer stays.

In the spa-style bathroom, you’ll find a large rain shower, double-sink vanity, and separate water closet, plus a convenient stacked washer and dryer.

Street parking is usually easy to find, right outside the door — just three small steps up from the sidewalk.
