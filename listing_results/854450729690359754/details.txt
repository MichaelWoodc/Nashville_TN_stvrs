=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Spread out across three stories built for groups. Four bedrooms and 3.5 baths, one bedroom per level for real privacy, Smart TVs in every room, and a 70" screen in the living room for game nights. Morning coffee on one balcony, evening wind-down on the other. Sleeps up to 10.

The space
The Layout

Most Nashville rentals stop at three bedrooms. This one gives you four, spread across three levels so a full house of 10 still has quiet corners.

Level 1, The Garden Suite. Two queen beds and a half bath on the entry level. No stairs required, which grandparents and early sleepers will appreciate.

Level 2, The Social Hub. One sprawling open floor where everyone ends up: a chef's kitchen ready for a group dinner, a 70-inch cinematic Smart TV, a balcony made for dining outside, and a full bath with shower so nobody has to run upstairs.

Level 3, The Sleeping Quarters. The primary suite pairs a queen bed with its own ensuite bath and a private balcony that catches the sunset. Bedrooms three and four each have a full bed and a Smart TV, and share a large hallway bath.

Three floors, four bedrooms, and room for the whole crew to be together when you want and apart when you need.
