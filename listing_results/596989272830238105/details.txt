=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Step into a light filled, stylish home in Nashville’s most sought-after neighborhood, historic Lockeland Springs. Mere steps away from the best restaurants, bars and coffee shops that East Nashville has to offer, this peaceful retreat will give you the city at your fingertips. The location doesn't get any more prime than this.

The space
Lockeland Landing is a 1920’s craftsman home that has been completely refurbished and decked out to suit creatures of both comfort and style. Tucked into a bucolic, residential street in the heart of East Nashville, the space can comfortably sleep 7, with a primary suite on the main floor, an upstairs loft with its own en suite bathroom, and 2 bedrooms downstairs with a full bath and its own living room and kitchenette. The main kitchen is equipped with brand new, state of the art appliances, and is fully stocked with the essentials for cooking a big meal with family and friends. You’ll have direct access to both a ground floor patio with a beautifully manicured gravel garden and an elevated deck with outdoor furniture, where you can sip your morning coffee and take in the fresh air. The house is across the street from the best sandwiches in town at Bill’s Sandwich Palace, and a cute gift shop called Freshie and Zero. Two blocks from the ultra hip Urban Cowboy bar, and arguably the finest restaurant in Nashville, Lockeland Table. Walk 5-10 minutes in any direction, and you’ll find yourself with plenty of shopping, eating, and coffee sipping to indulge in. 5 minute Uber/Lyft to downtown Broadway bars and Titan Stadium.

With the guests in mind, we have thoughtfully equipped our house with brand new mattresses & crisp clean linens on each bed with plenty of amenities to make your stay as comfortable & enjoyable as possible.
