A Dolly-inspired 1BR in a new boutique building at 815 Main Street, heart of East Nashville. Hand-painted palm murals, pink and green, velvet and brass — bold by design, and it reads intentional in person. Floor-to-ceiling windows and a bright corner sunroom keep it full of light. Sleeps six: a king plus a murphy bed and sofa sleeper. Step out to the bars and restaurants of Main Street and Five Points; downtown is about seven minutes by car. Full kitchen, one bath, building lounge.

The space
The condo is just minutes away from all of Music City’s most famous sites. Step out the front door and find shopping, restaurants, bars, and live music venues right outside, or hop in the car and head to the honky tonks on Broadway, visit the Johnny Cash Museum, Ryman Auditorium, Country Music Hall of Fame, Music Row, the state capitol and- all just a few miles away.

The brand new lux building offers a one of a kind, spacious lounge, perfect for morning coffee while checking out all the Dolly inspired design or sipping cocktails with friends before your night out in Nashville.  Spend your downtime streaming your favorite shows on one of the smart tv's, and taking in the stunning city skyline views from the swanky rooftop deck.
