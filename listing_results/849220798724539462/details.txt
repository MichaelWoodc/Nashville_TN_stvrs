=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Stay inside Nashville history — literally. This brick-and-beam loft lives in an 1865 factory building, now Marathon Village: craft coffee, whiskey tastings & live music steps from your door. Sleeps 8 across 2 bedrooms + sofa beds, with a sunny pool for hot days. 5 min to The Gulch, 7 to Broadway — close to everything, quieter than downtown. Original brick, huge windows, 160 years of character & free parking. The loft your group remembers.

The space
Indulge your inner chef in the sleek kitchen, equipped with a full suite of stainless steel appliances. 2 bedrooms one with extra large closet and queen size bed. The other bedroom has bunk beds with large closet. The second bedroom also has a vanity with sink and mirror. This is perfect for multiple people getting ready at the same time. Extra perks include central AC and a private washer/dryer.
