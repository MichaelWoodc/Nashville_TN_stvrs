=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Best-kept secret in Nashville! Stay in a place of country music history.  This historic cabin on five spacious acres - previously owned by a Grand Ole Opry Hall of Fame member - is just a mile from the Grand Ole Opry and 10 minutes from downtown. Enjoy local wildlife in their natural habitat with a secluded feel, yet only minutes from EVERYTHING Nashville has to offer. You will not be disappointed!

The space
This cabin gives you a private escape from the hustle and bustle, while still being within Nashville city limits. Stay on a street that is full of Opry history while enjoying five acres of forest and wildlife. Less than a mile away you’ll find the Opry Mills shopping, dining, and entertainment district.  A quick 15-minute Uber ride away is downtown Nashville: historic honky tonks, live music venues, top-rated restaurants, bars, museums, and more. Funky East Nashville and the international airport are also a 15-minute Uber ride away. This location gives you a Music City experience second to none!

Guest access
Enjoy five acres of natural fields and woods around the cabin. Put feed out for the family of deer that live in our woods and watch them eat at dusk.  If the weather is right, light a fire in the small fire pit or take turns on your personal par 3 golf hole on the tee box next to your private wrap-around porch. Maybe it’s just relaxing with a coffee or a glass of wine enjoying quiet before heading out to enjoy Nashville … you can have it all here.

Other things to note
Stay on the back 5 acres of a historic Opry star’s property.  Enjoy coffee and the sunrise on the deck or have happy hour & practice your par 3 skills from the tee box!
