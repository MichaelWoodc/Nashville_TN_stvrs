=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Stay in the heart of Music City at Bluebird By Broadway just 0.7 miles from Broadway! This stylish downtown condo features a queen bed, queen sleeper sofa, private balcony, fully equipped kitchen, Keurig coffee bar, and Smart TVs. Enjoy on-site parking, a heated seasonal pool, cabanas, fire pits, courtyard, gym with Peloton equipment and more- all within walking distance of Nashville’s top attractions.

The space
Welcome to Bluebird By Broadway! The condo features a queen bed plus a queen sleeper sofa, giving your group a comfortable place to recharge after exploring Nashville. Both the bedroom and living room feature Smart TVs, so you can keep up with your favorite shows during your stay. The fully equipped kitchen has everything you need to prepare meals at home, including a blender, toaster, kettle, wine glasses, plates, utensils and more. Cooking oil is not provided. You’ll also find a stocked Keurig coffee bar with K-Cups to make mornings easy. Step outside onto your private furnished balcony to relax and take in the atmosphere overlooking the pool area.

In-Unit Conveniences:
The condo is stocked with thoughtful extras to make your stay as comfortable as possible, including a full-sized washer and dryer with detergent provided, Keurig coffee maker and K-Cups, fully equipped kitchen, shampoo, conditioner and body wash, hairdryer, iron and ironing board, ping pong paddles and balls, pool bag and pool towels, and a walk-in closet with extra bedding.
Self check-in and check-out make arrival and departure easy, and on-site security is available for added peace of mind throughout your stay.

Everything You Want in a Nashville Stay:
Bluebird By Broadway checks all the boxes: a prime downtown location just 0.7 miles from Broadway, free on-site parking, Insta-worthy decor, a newer building, private balcony, full-sized in-unit washer and dryer, self check-in and check-out, and access to an impressive collection of building amenities. The heated outdoor pool is open March through October from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. and features cabanas and a sunbathing area. The outdoor courtyard also offers plenty of seating, fire pits, a gas grill and outdoor kitchen area, plus a ping pong table near the pool deck. Keep up with your routine in the state-of-the-art fitness center featuring Peloton equipment. If you need to work during your visit, the building also offers workspace and a community lounge with a TV, vending machine, couches and tables—perfect for remote work or casual meetings.

On-site security provides additional peace of mind throughout your stay.

Walk to the Best of Nashville:
Leave the car parked and explore Music City on foot. Bluebird By Broadway is within walking distance of City Winery, Bad Axe Throwing, Fogg Street Lawn Club, Iris Tattoo, Third Man Records & The Blue Room, Yee-Haw Brewing Co., Ole Smoky Distillery, Tennessee Brew Works, Music City Center, Country Music Hall of Fame, Bridgestone Arena and the Ryman Auditorium.

You’ll also be close to party bus and Pedal Tavern pickup locations, plus tons of restaurants, bars and shopping in The Gulch. And, of course, Broadway is only 0.7 miles away—approximately a 15-minute walk, quick Uber ride or scooter trip.

Explore Upper Broadway favorites including Tootsie’s, The Stage, Robert’s Western World and Assembly Food Hall, or head farther down Broadway for Hard Rock Cafe, Jason Aldean’s, Luke Bryan’s, Kid Rock’s, Ole Red, Morgan Wallen’s This Bar, Posty’s, Miranda Lambert’s Casa Rosa and much more.

A Quick Ride to More Nashville Favorites:
When you’re ready to venture beyond downtown, Nissan Stadium, the Grand Ole Opry and Opry Mills Outlet Mall are all just a short Uber ride away. Whether you’re coming to Nashville for live music, a concert, a girls’ trip, a weekend getaway or simply to experience everything Music City has to offer, Bluebird By Broadway puts you close to the action while giving you a comfortable place to come home to.
