The space
Nestled in an enviable location, the Summit in the City offers easy access to an array of Nashville's most sought-after attractions. Situated close to 12 South (1.3 miles), The Gulch (1.4 miles), and Broadway (1.9 miles), you'll find yourself immersed in the vibrant pulse of Music City. Experience the soulful rhythms of live music, embrace the iconic country music scene, and enjoy the diverse selection of nearby restaurants, bars, and coffee shops. For those seeking outdoor recreation, local parks and farmer’s markets are just a short distance away, ensuring that every aspect of your Nashville adventure is within reach from this prime location.
