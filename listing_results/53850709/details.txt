=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
If safety, comfort and convenience are priorities, then Good as Gold Rowhouse is an ideal Nashville vacation rental.  The layout works well for groups who value privacy + spacious gathering areas.  Walk to trendy food, shopping, Vanderbilt University + Medical Center, Belmont University. Downtown Nashville (Broadway, Bridgestone Arena, Nissan Stadium) is within a 10-minute drive.  Read our reviews of Nashville birthday trips, college reunions, co-working meetups, girl's getaways + family trips.

The space
Good as Gold Rowhouse was built in 2021 and was designed with comfort and luxury for large groups in mind. Most guests take full advantage of the location and spend their time experiencing the nightlife and restaurants nearby. However, if you enjoy cooking and staying in, there's a fully equipped kitchen, huge couches and a top floor lounge plus outdoor deck to enjoy sunsets.  Or maybe even a enjoy musician jam session with our bright yellow piano, massive murals and musical inspiration around every corner.  

All bedrooms feature high-end Helix mattresses, high thread count linens, Smart TV's and secure, independent check-in and check-out.  There are four bedrooms (3 king sized beds and 1 queen bed) as well as two luxury American Leather king sized sleeper sofas.  These aren't your grandma's squeaky sofas!  The layout is ideal for guests desiring a common gathering place, but preferring separation and privacy.  Bedrooms are on different floors and each has a dedicated bathroom.

House layout:
Floor 1: Bedroom 1 - King sized bed and ensuite bathroom
Floor 2: Kitchen, living room with king sized pullout couch and half bath
Floor 3: Bedroom 2 - King sized bed and ensuite bathroom
Floor 3: Bedroom 3 - Queen sized bed and hall bathroom
Floor 4: Bedroom 4 - King sized bed and hall bathroom
Floor 4: 4th floor music lounge and deck with king sized pull-out sofa

Things to note:

This tall, narrow rowhouse is four stories. So, expect stairs.
There is one rear dedicated carport parking spot.  There are additional building and street parking options that are typically available but are first come first served.
Each TV is a Smart TV where you can log into personal programs and access local channels.  But, there is no cable provided.
The layout may not be ideal for small children.  Bedrooms are separate on different floors, there are no baby gates, baby proofing, etc.
