=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Family-friendly lakefront escape with 2 paddle boards (with seats!), two kayaks, plenty of outdoor toys and games, swing set and firepit - just 30 minutes from Nashville.

Welcome to our 1960’s charming lake house, Old Hickory Haven. Nestled on the serene shores of Old Hickory Lake, our cozy and inviting home offers breathtaking views!

Bring the entire family, plan the perfect girls trip, or just come here to enjoy the peace and quiet. Deer will roam all year long!

- Bathrooms: 3 full bathrooms stocked with fresh towels, toiletries, and essentials. Forgot something? Don’t worry, we have your essentials like Q-tips, disposable tooth brushes, makeup wipes and more.
