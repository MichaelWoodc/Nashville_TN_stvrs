=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
At Ironwood Grove we have created an unprecedented tiny house hospitality experience nestled in the bustling, eclectic epicenter of East Nashville. Our tiny homes let you explore the curiosity of tiny living while indulging in our special selection of local amenities, our clever and efficient uses of small spaces, and stunning finishes. We hope Ironwood Grove feels like your cozy (tiny) home away from home as you explore the local music, art, and food of the great Music City.

The space
This tiny house has been designed with striking floor to ceiling wood paneled walls. Featuring a main bedroom with its own balcony lounge, guests rest comfortably on a queen-sized memory foam bed. A full bathroom and lux tiled shower are complete with the Good Fill's clean, zero waste soaps and bath & body products. A small kitchenette features a mini fridge, microwave, and hot plate. Guests are welcome to enjoy our complimentary beverage bar with our exclusive locally roasted coffee and assortment of teas. Maximum occupancy: 2
