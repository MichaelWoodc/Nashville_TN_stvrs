=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to the Flats on Acklen!  Located in Sylvan Park, our community is perfectly situated just one mile from Vanderbilt University and two miles from the vibrant nightlife of Broadway, Bridgestone Arena, The Ryman, and Nissan Stadium, you'll be at the center of it all. Walk or jog  to Centennial Park via the Greenway. Our pet-friendly space features a fenced pet area and a community outdoor space with dining and seating.

The space
Experience comfort and convenience in our one-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment. The bedroom has two queen beds, room-darkening curtains, and a ceiling fan for a restful night's sleep, while the bathroom features a combo tub/shower for a relaxing soak or quick refresh. Cook up your favorite meals in the full kitchen and enjoy them in the cozy dining area. The spacious and bright living area is perfect for unwinding after a day of exploring, working, or studying. Step outside to the community shared outdoor area, where you can relax in Adirondack chairs under cafe lighting, surrounded by tasteful landscaping.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the private apartment and shared outdoor spaces.

Other things to note
Please be aware that a freight train runs behind the apartment complex, operating irregularly, including during the night. While some guests may not be bothered by the noise, others may find it disruptive. If you are sensitive to noise, we recommend not renting this property.

The train travels from Memphis to Nashville, but it does not blow its horn near the building, so you will mostly hear it as background noise. It is mostly obscured from view by vegetation. To help minimize disturbances, sound and light-reducing curtains and a sound machine are provided.
