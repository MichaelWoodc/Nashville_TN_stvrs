The space
This one's for the group that wants a stage of its own, not a hotel bar mic night. Gulch Glam is a themed Nashville house built around karaoke, glam, and getting ready together. Four bedrooms and four full bathrooms give everyone room to spread out, and the open kitchen and living room (fireplace, cowhide rug, big screen) is where the group regroups between outings. Smart TVs run through almost every room.

Upstairs is the whole point: a themed lounge with a music-icon mural, wet bar, and private karaoke stage, right next to a 4-station Hollywood-style vanity room for hair, makeup, and photos before Broadway. Days go to the Gulch and Broadway, evenings to the balcony as the skyline turns pink, nights to the karaoke stage until the group calls it.
