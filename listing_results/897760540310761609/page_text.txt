Welcome to our newly renovated trendy town-home. We're 2 miles from the Gulch area, from the streets of Downtown Broadway and Mid-Town. Our space is very inviting, tastefully furnished with contemporary furnishings, and new appliances. Street and private parking is available. The town-home is equipped with key-less entry and privacy fence. Welcome to our home and to Nashville.

The space
Our town-home has a privacy fence for your comfort. There is s a small deck on the back for sitting, as well as a separate fenced area for gathering located on the property. Two bedrooms are furnished with televisions and one television in the living room area. Each bed comes with sheets, a blanket and comforter as well as four pillows. There are two full bathrooms; both full bathrooms are furnished with four sets of towels, size shampoo, conditioner, bath soap and toilet paper. There is an additional half bathroom. The kitchen is furnished with dishes, glasses, coffee cups, pots/pans, cutlery, and a Keurig.

Guest have the entire townhome for use.

Please feel free to text or call during your stay with any questions or concerns.
