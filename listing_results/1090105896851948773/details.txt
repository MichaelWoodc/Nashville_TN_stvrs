=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Dancing in the Moonlight at the Hyve! Featuring unique design and luxurious furnishings, this downtown condo is the perfect getaway. Sleeping 6, it includes a queen bed, a convertible sofa that transforms into a full-size bed, and queen air mattress. The fully equipped kitchen has everything you need to whip up gourmet meals or cocktails, complete with a stocked Keurig coffee bar. The building offers parking, gym, pool and cabanas, fire pits, and more! Just .7 miles to Broadway!
