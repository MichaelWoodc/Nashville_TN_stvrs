The renowned City of Berry Hill (in Nashville) has over 60 music agencies & recording studios, and an efficient police department.

The space
For your visitors, Suite #4 has a 12' x 12' private deck, an office-living room with an accessible restroom for your visitors, & the kitchen is open to the front visitable space. Suite #4 has a "Visitable" home office—your visitors with handicap limitations can easily access your Suite—zero-step entrance & walkways; 36" doors throughout; & a wheelchair-accessible restroom. For those who stay in Suite #4, additional railing supports are in the step-in shower and a shower stool. Suite #3 has larger 5-foot turning areas with an accessible kitchen. Still, Suite #4 is perfect for individuals with low mobility and can accommodate a wheelchair. Also, there are 2 parking slots per Suite with more available upon request. There is a van-accessible parking spot with a wheelchair ramp that leads to the 1st-floor boardwalk leading to your Suite.

Lock away your private area—each Suite has a keypad on the front office-living room door, with interior keyed doors to lock off the bedroom and shower/laundry area. This allows you to share your office-living room with your visitors and co-workers while blocking access to your personal space.
