The space
The Nash Lights Condo offers all the amenities of home while embracing the modern, urban style of Nashville condo living.  Step into a luxurious 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom condo with lots of natural lighting on Nashville’s eastside—home to the Hot Chicken Festival and Tomato Arts Festival—centrally located to be minutes to Broadway, Five Points, West End, The Gulch, 12South, or Opry Mills.  This condo was designed with clean urban lines to showcase Nashville’s country music scene and over the top nightlife. 
 
Relax knowing that you don’t have to fret about parking or the cost of parking because this condo comes with 2 free, dedicated parking spaces [1 full size, 1 compact].  Although this condo is conveniently located within Nashville’s urban core you can also enjoy the peace and comforts of being situated just far enough away from the hustle and bustle of Broadway that you can kick back on a spacious sectional sofa and enjoy the game or a day full of games before hitting the town later that night. 
 
Enjoy views of the downtown skyline or one of Nashville’s famous fireworks displays from not 1, but 2 rooftop decks.  Or if you prefer a more private experience spend that time on your own private balcony.  Grab a drink at East Nashville’s Urban Cowboy where you might run into celebrities such as Aaron Rodgers or maybe you’ll be lucky enough to be at The Red Door Salon when Lady Gaga makes a special appearance or maybe you’ll sit next to Jillian Michaels at Butcher & Bee….you never know [all true events].  The Nash Lights Condo comes equipped with kitchen essentials [pots + pans + bakeware], a washer & dryer, 3 Samsung smart TVs, a Keurig, and Wifi.
 
Kick the night off getting ready in your super chic condo before enjoying a beer or glass of wine on the rooftop overlooking the Batman building in the distance.  Then catch a quick Uber or Lyft ride to 12South where you’ll eat boutique tacos and drink the famous Smoke on the Water at Bar Taco before heading for a nightcap at LA Jackson in The Gulch.  And if you’re feeling kinda frisky maybe you’ll end the night at one of Nashville’s many honky tonks.
