=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Experience Nashville with a "Casa Concert"; a private living room concert performed by some of the best singer/songwriters in Nashville, in your AirBnb! This beautiful 2,400 square foot townhouse w/4 bedrooms, bonus room w/queen bed, 3 baths is in a quiet & safe Edgehill neighborhood only 2 blocks from Music Row & walking distance to EVERYTHING! It's centrally located & a 1/2 mile to Midtown, Demonbruen Hill, the Gulch, & Vanderbilt. Belmont is only .9 miles & downtown on Broadway is 1.2 miles.

The space
Our newly constructed 4BR/3BA (+Bonus Room w/queen bed) townhouse is right in the heart of Nashville: perfect for whatever your vacation has in store!  It doesn't get any more conveniently located than this!  Free street parking & 2 additional parking spots in the alley.  All furniture is brand new, has a fully stocked kitchen & fast wifi.

INSIDE THE HOUSE

Enter into the open-concept dining/kitchen/living space. The elegant dining area is first, with room for 6-8 to sit at the table. From there, you’ll find a chef’s kitchen full of luxury touches, including granite countertops, gas range, all new stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar that seats 3.

The kitchen also features a cozy office area beneath the staircase: perfect for getting some last-minute work done (or working on your novel!).

At the back of the house, you’ll find the sunken living room with elegant furnishings, a large 65" flat-screen TV, conversation corner and access to the patio via French doors.

Our house sleeps up to 12 guests between four bedrooms, an upstairs bonus room with a queen bed (sliding barn door), and 2 twin xl 4 inch memory foam mattresses in the living room.  All beds are brand new BeautyRest Mattresses.  The master has a king-size bed and 36" TV, plus an elegant en-suite private bathroom with a dual quartz vanity and tile walk-in shower with a rain shower-head.

The other 2 upstairs bedrooms each have queen beds, 36" TVs and share a double sink bathroom with a shower and bathtub. The fourth bedroom, on the main level, also has a queen-size bed, a 36" TV and a single sink bathroom with a walk-in shower across the hallway.  This is also the common bathroom on the main floor.  

We’ve also got lots of outdoor space for you to relax in, too! Have a cookout on the grill and spend some time in the conversation space under the shaded pergola on the patio.
