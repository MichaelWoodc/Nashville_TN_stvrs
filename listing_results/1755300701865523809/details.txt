The space
This Airbnb sits close enough to lower Broadway and downtown Nashville that you're only a quick rideshare from the honky-tonks, but far enough to actually sleep when you get home. Four stories give TOPSIDE room to breathe that most downtown rentals can't offer. Everyone lands together on the main level, where the kitchen opens straight onto the living room and dining table.

From there, the group peels off floor by floor when it's time to wind down for the night. The rooftop deck is where this house earns its name: outdoor seating, string lights, faux ivy walls, and a gas grill against an open skyline view. Grill up top as the sun drops over downtown Nashville, then head down for a night out on Broadway.

Picture the arc of a weekend here: an afternoon downtown or at Geodis Park, sunset drinks on the rooftop, then everyone splitting off to their own floor to actually sleep.
