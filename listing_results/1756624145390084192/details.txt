The space
Twin Skyline puts your group as close to downtown Nashville as a rideshare ride gets, with two full homes to yourselves for when the night winds down. Two front doors, two kitchens, two rooftop decks, zero walls shared between families or friend groups. Home 1 holds four bedrooms plus a bonus room with three single beds, its kitchen open to the living and dining area so the cook never misses the conversation.

Its rooftop deck looks out over the skyline against an artificial grass wall, strung with lights for a sunset drink before the group heads toward Broadway. Home 2 mirrors it almost exactly: four more bedrooms, its own bonus room with three single beds, a second kitchen and living room, and a second rooftop deck with skyline views and bar seating.

Catch a set at Zanies Comedy Night Club or spend the day at Geodis Park, grab a plate at the grill, then ride into lower Broadway for the bars before regrouping on the rooftops as downtown's lights come on.
