=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Enjoy comfort and convenience in our cozy basement apartment, just 6 minutes from the airport and 14 minutes from downtown. Located in a peaceful neighborhood on an acre of land, this suite offers new flooring, a fully stocked kitchen, and a 55" Smart TV. With a private entrance and plenty of parking, it's perfect for relaxation. Quick access to major freeways makes exploring easy. Ideal for business or leisure travelers seeking a quiet yet central location!

The space
Enjoy a fully private stay in our cozy basement apartment, complete with a separate entrance and contactless check-in via a personalized smart lock code. The space features a living area with a couch and dining table, a bedroom with a queen memory foam mattress and dimmable bedside lamps, a second sleeping area with two twin beds, a kitchenette, and a full bathroom

Please note, some areas have lower ceilings (as low as 5'9"), particularly between the living area and bedroom, and the first portion of the twin bed area.

The apartment has heating and air conditioning. If you need the temperature adjusted, simply send us a message, and we’ll happily update it remotely. For entertainment, enjoy our Smart TV with Netflix provided (no cable).

This inviting space is ideal for travelers seeking comfort, privacy, and thoughtful amenities.

Outdoor enthusiasts will love being within walking distance of Seven Oaks Park, home to Tennessee’s best disc golf course, as well as brand-new pickleball and basketball courts. Whether you're looking to explore Nashville or relax in a welcoming environment, our home offers the best of both worlds!

Guest access
Our home features a spacious backyard with a small playset and a charming treehouse, perfect for outdoor fun. Feel free to share the space with our family, including our friendly dog who loves playing with the kids. Parking is easy, with a large driveway offering plenty of free space.

Located in a quiet neighborhood, our home offers quick access to major freeways. We’re just 6 minutes from the airport, 14 minutes from downtown Nashville, 10 minutes from the Grand Ole Opry and Opry Mills Mall, and 12 minutes from the Nashville Zoo.
