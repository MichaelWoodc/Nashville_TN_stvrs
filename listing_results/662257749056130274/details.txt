=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to the Pink Palm Retreat. A one-of-a-kind Nashville escape designed for unforgettable group getaways. Located in the urban trendy Hendrix building, adjacent to the 2 story atrium café of Forevermore Coffee & Goods, this vibrant townhome features a glam-ready beauty bar, karaoke stage, rooftop lounge, and Insta-worthy design throughout. Walk to East Nashville’s hottest bars & restaurants or be on Broadway in minutes. Perfect for bachelorettes, celebrations, and memory-making in style!

The space
🌴 Your Nashville Experience Starts Here Tucked within an exclusive collection of eight custom-built townhomes, the Pink Palm Retreat offers a rare blend of privacy, personality, and proximity. At the heart of it all is Forevermore, a breathtaking two-story atrium coffeehouse just steps from your door— an inviting space to sip, gather, and ease into your day or wind down at night.
Positioned in East Nashville’s Cleveland Park neighborhood, you’re surrounded by some of the city’s most celebrated dining and nightlife. Whether you’re strolling to dinner or grabbing a quick Uber, everything you came to Nashville for is effortlessly within reach.

📍 Unbeatable Location Enjoy walkable access to standout local favorites like Audrey, Folk, Lyra, Redheaded Stranger, Pharmacy Burger Parlor, Mac Tacos Por Favor, Xiao Bao Biscuit, and Wilburn Street Tavern, along with coffee shops, cocktail bars, and late-night bites.

💄 Ground Floor – The Ultimate Glam Lounge Step into a space unlike anything else in Nashville. This transformed 40’ garage is your private pre-game paradise featuring a full wall of illuminated makeup stations (10 mirrors!), plush seating, custom counters, and dedicated outlets for all your styling tools.
Turn up the energy with your own karaoke stage, complete with microphones and karaoke machine, or kick back at the built-in bar while your favorite movie or slideshow plays on the big screen. Wrapped in vibrant floral textures and lush greenery, this space sets the tone for unforgettable nights.

Share meals around the custom dining table (seats 8) or gather at the island with additional seating for 8. The adjacent living room offers comfortable seating, a large TV, and a sleeper sofa.
Just beyond, discover the signature Wild Roots photo alcove complete with a swing, stand ring light and lush backdrop, perfect for capturing the spirit of your stay.
