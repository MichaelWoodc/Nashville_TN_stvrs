=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Located just steps off Nashville's popular 12 Avenue South foodie and shopping district, this is the perfect spot for those looking to enjoy all the neighborhood has to offer!  Stocked with all the essentials, thoughtfully designed and hosted by long-time locals, "The Elmwood" won't just be your place to stay, it might just be your favorite part of your trip to Nashville.

The space
This bright apartment has a fully-equipped kitchen, full bath with stunning walk-in shower, laundry closet, bedroom with a cozy queen bed, and living room with a Crate and Barrel queen sleeper sofa.  A perfect space to relax between sightseeing excursions or unwind after a day of business meetings.  

If you're looking to veg-out, the entertainment center in the living room has a 4K TV.  You can access your personal Netflix, Hulu and other streaming accounts via Apple TV.

There's also a blue-tooth record player and collection of vinyl so you can stream music or spin records while you're relaxing or getting ready for your big night out.  

Park in your personal parking space, plug into our complimentary EV charger if needed, then head inside, up the stairs, and make yourself at home.  We're willing to bet you won't want to leave.

Guest access
Guests have private access to the entire "The Elmwood 12 South" interior space.   Guests have a private entrance with a keypad entry.  We will program a personal entry code for you to use during your visit.  This code is unique to you and will only be active during your stay, adding a level of security and convenience for every guest.

**Please note: guests do not have access to the garage space located below the apartment.  We do park one vehicle below but are mindful of not disturbing guests.  Guests also do not have access to the backyard space of the main house, however this space is completely separated from guest space via a privacy fence.

Other things to note
PARKING: 
There is a one-car parking space located next The Elmwood 12 South that is designated specifically for our guests. If you are traveling in an electric vehicle, we have a ChargePoint Homeflex Level 2 EV charger in the parking space. You are welcome to charge your vehicle during your stay! This is a complimentary service for our guests only. Please do not park in the driveway or block the large garage door as we do park in the space below and need to have access to this door.

STAIRS & GARAGE:
Please note that this is a second floor unit, so it is not suitable for guests who cannot walk up 1 flight of stairs.  Stairs are interior and do have a banister. The space below the unit is a garage that is used to park one personal vehicle so you may occasionally hear the homeowner below although they are mindful when guests are present. We do have a quiet garage door opener. The garage is not available for guest access/use.

PRIVACY/SOFA BED:
Guests who wish to use the sofa bed as well as the queen bed should note that while there is a door between the bedroom and living space, access to the bathroom is located in the bedroom area, so guests sleeping on the sofa bed would need to walk through the bedroom to access the bathroom.  Please consider if this fits the privacy preferences of your group before booking.
