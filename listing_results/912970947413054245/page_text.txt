Welcome to the Game Night Getaway!
Step into a world of entertainment and relaxation at our unique Airbnb apartment. Designed with your enjoyment in mind, our space offers a variety of gaming options to add a touch of fun to your stay.  Our apartment is located in a quiet neighborhood, steps to Vandy and Centennial, & under 10 minutes by car to the hustle and bustle of Broadway.

The space
As you enter, you'll find a cozy and inviting living area where you can kick back and relax. In the living room, we have set up a shuffleboard table, and a scrabble board. Upstairs in the loft, you'll find a foosball and poker table.  Challenge your friends or family to friendly matches and create lasting memories together. The kitchen is fully equipped with everything you need to prepare meals or snacks during your stay. Feel free to whip up some appetizers for game nights or enjoy a leisurely breakfast before starting your day. When it's time to rest, our bedrooms provide a comfortable retreat. Sink into the cozy beds and enjoy a peaceful night's sleep. We also have clean and modern bathrooms for your convenience.  Don't forget to take a picture in front of one of the 3 murals from Nashville's famous artist before you depart.

Enjoy the freedom and privacy of this spacious apartment without sacrificing the quality, make yourself at home in this fully furnished loft, equipped with smart locks, all natural eco-friendly toiletries. 

We want to ensure your stay is seamless and enjoyable, so please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need assistance. We want your experience to be exceptional and leave you with fond memories.
