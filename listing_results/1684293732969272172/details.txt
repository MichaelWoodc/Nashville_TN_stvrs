=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
￼Experience Downtown Nashville like a local at Big River! Inspired by classic Nashville, this unique condo blends vintage record covers, Opryland memorabilia, and retro charm with modern comforts like convenient charging stations, a pool, and photo-worthy backdrops. Fantastic views of the river and wildlife from the cozy private balcony. Walk to Broadway, Germantown, First Horizon Park, restaurants, bars, and shops—right in the heart of the action.

The space
Experience comfort and charm in this cozy riverside condo. Located on the second floor for optimal close river views. This spacious bedroom features a luxurious king bed, ambient lighting, bedside charging stations, a walk-in closet, luggage racks, and plenty of space to unpack and settle in. Multiple mirrors and a bathroom just off the bedroom make getting ready easy and convenient.

Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen with a stove, refrigerator, microwave, coffee maker, blender, and adorable vintage dishes. The inviting living area includes a comfy full-size pullout sofa, retro dining table for four, and a plush armchair with footstool overlooking beautiful river views.

Step out onto the private balcony and enjoy the peaceful sounds of the river just feet away, where you can often spot wildlife and watch pontoon parties and river cruise boats pass by. 

The bathroom is stocked with shampoo, conditioner, body wash, and a hair dryer — plus a fun sequin shower curtain perfect for selfies!

Guest access
The entire condo is yours to enjoy, with the exception of any containers labeled “Owner Supplies.” Your stay also includes a key fob for access to the pool and workout room. Guests can enjoy community seating areas with a gas fire pit, barbecue grills, and convenient pay-per-use laundry facilities nearby.

Other things to note
Owning a little place downtown has been a long-held dream, and we’ve thoughtfully selected every piece in the condo with care and meaning—from retro dishware and 1950s-inspired lamps to vintage record album covers featuring some of Nashville’s greatest musicians.

We hope you feel right at home here. Please treat the space with respect, kick off your boots, and when you head to Broadway, kick up your boots and dance the night away!
