The space
Welcome to Belle Air Mansion, a beautifully restored 1800s Greek Revival estate offering a one-of-a-kind bed and breakfast experience in Nashville. Set on 6 peaceful acres just minutes from downtown, BNA Airport, and Opryland, Belle Air blends historic charm with modern comfort for a truly memorable stay.

As a boutique inn, Belle Air offers individually designed guest rooms, each with its own character, along with inviting shared spaces throughout the mansion and grounds. Guests are encouraged to relax on the veranda, stroll the property, or unwind in our elegant common areas.

Each morning, enjoy a complimentary full plated breakfast prepared fresh and served on-site—one of the highlights of the Belle Air experience.

Please note this is a bed and breakfast setting, not a full home rental. While your room is private, common areas may be shared with other guests unless the property is reserved in full for a private event or buyout.

Whether you're visiting Nashville for a romantic getaway, wedding, retreat, or weekend escape, Belle Air offers a unique blend of Southern hospitality, history, and comfort.

Guest access
Guests have access to a variety of shared spaces throughout Belle Air Mansion and the surrounding grounds. You’re welcome to relax on the veranda, enjoy our elegant common areas, and explore the property during your stay.

Please note that these spaces are shared with other guests, as Belle Air operates as a boutique bed and breakfast. On occasion, certain areas may be reserved for private events or property buyouts, which may limit access during those times.
