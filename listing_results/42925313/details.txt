=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===

The space
Two blocks from Broadway's neon strip and half a block from the storied corridor of Printer's Alley, this single-level downtown condo puts twelve people at the center of Nashville without any of the chaos of fighting for the elevator or splitting across floors. City skyline fills the windows from every angle, and on a warm September evening, the faint pulse of live music rising from the street below sets the tone before you've even decided where the night goes.

Sleeping arrangements
Eight beds across four bedrooms and the living area give a group of twelve room to breathe. Three Queen Master Suites offer their own private corners of the condo - ideal for couples or anyone who wants a retreat from the group energy. A Double Twin Bunk Suite rounds out the bedroom count for those comfortable sharing. A King Memory Foam Sleeper in the living room covers the remaining guests without crowding the space. Extra pillows, blankets, and clothing storage throughout make settling in easy, whether you're here for a long weekend or a longer stretch.

Living space
The open living area is built for a group that actually intends to use it. Cushioned seating, a flat-screen TV, a record player, and a full sound system give you a complete evening in without stepping outside. Board games are on hand when the mood calls for something lower-key. The single-level layout keeps the whole group on one floor - no stairs, no split levels, everyone moving through the same space.

Kitchen & dining
A full kitchen handles a crew of twelve with ease. You have a refrigerator, freezer, stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, coffee maker, toaster, blender, baking sheet, wine glasses, cooking basics, and a complete set of dishes and silverware at your disposal. The dining table brings everyone together for a proper sit-down meal - a group breakfast before hitting Broadway, or a late dinner when the night winds down back at the condo.

Workspace & comfort
A dedicated workspace - no private door, but a clear, functional area separate from the group - gives anyone on a working stay a place to focus. Central air conditioning, ceiling fan, and heating keep the space comfortable through Nashville's warm September days and cooler evenings. The in-unit washer and dryer are a practical asset for longer visits.

Location & access
Printer's Alley is half a block on foot. Broadway - live music, honky-tonks, dining, and Nashville nightlife - is two blocks away. Self check-in via smart lock means you arrive on your own schedule. A private entrance keeps your group independent from the building. Paid parking is available off premises. Exterior security cameras are on the property and in the building hallway outside the condo, alongside smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher, and a first aid kit.

This condo is pet-friendly and open to long-term stays of 28 days or more.
