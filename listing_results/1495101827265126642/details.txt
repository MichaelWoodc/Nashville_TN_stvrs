The space
There is a main living room next to the kitchen, with the main entry point to the home being in the backyard. To the left is the kitchen and living room area, to the right is the staircase to the upstairs space and bathroom! The upstairs sleeps roughly 5 people with 1 single bed, 1 full-sized bed, and 1 queen-sized bed with it's own dedicated bathroom!
