The space
Step into a stay with character—this historic building is known for its 1865 Club speakeasy in the building, blending Nashville’s past with a modern, elevated living experience in the heart of Marathon Village.

This thoughtfully designed 1-bedroom condo flat features one of the most desirable layouts in the building, offering a comfortable and functional space for both short and extended stays. 

The open-concept living area provides a relaxed environment to unwind, complete with a smart TV, natural light, and seamless flow into a fully equipped kitchen. Whether you're starting your morning with coffee or winding down after a night out, the space is designed to feel effortless.

The bedroom includes a queensize bed with quality linens, while additional sleeping arrangements allow for extra guests while maintaining a clean, uncluttered feel. 

Guests also enjoy access to premium building amenities, including a seasonal dipping pool and a fully equipped fitness center. The building itself is one of Nashville’s most recognizable historic properties, offering secure access, elevator entry, and a unique atmosphere that stands out from typical rentals.
