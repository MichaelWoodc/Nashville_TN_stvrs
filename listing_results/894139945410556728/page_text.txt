The space
This newly completely renovated shotgun style home boast a beautiful interior.  

The master bedroom is tastefully decorated with a minimalist style and is furnished with a comfortable King size bed with a Medium Firm Nectar Mattress, luxurious linens and a 42 inch Smart TV.

The main bathroom has a shower/tub combo and a LED mirror that has Bluetooth capabilities as well.  

The 2nd bedroom is perfect for hanging out in addition to sleeping.  It is furnished with a Sealy Futon Sleeper and a 42 inch Smart TV.  An air mattress is available as well. There is a half bathroom located inside this room.  This room also has an additional door that makes it accessible from the outside as well as inside.

The kitchen is well stocked for your cooking needs.

In the living room you will find a 65 inch Smart TV with a Bluetooth sound-bar.  The sofa converts to a queen size sofa bed with a Sealy Mattress.

Guest access
Guest have access to entire property.
