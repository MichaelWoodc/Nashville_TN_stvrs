=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Schoolhouse, with logs over 200 years old, is situated on 90 acres of stunning, natural beauty (flowers, shrubs, spring fed creek, abundant wildlife) +hiking trails for your enjoyment. It is a completely private cabin with its own entrance. Main floor (king bed, bath, TV, fireplace), and lower level offers kitchenette, Queen (luxury) sleeper sofa and additional full bath, TV and fireplace.

The space
The Schoolhouse cabin/bedroom has a king size bed, a private bathroom, and a wood burning fireplace. There is a downstairs great room with a kitchenette, wood burning fireplace, full bath and Queen luxury sleeper sofa. There is a small, private patio on the lower level with access to the large terrace patio.

Guest access
The main inn has over 10,000 square feet. Make yourselves at home inside and out on our beautiful terrace and porches, our recreation room with pool and ping pong, and explore our 90 acres of wooded hills and open meadows. Your breakfast is served in the main inn dining room from 8am-9am.
