Across from Eastland Park and just minutes from downtown, you'll be close to Nashville's best restaurants, bars, coffee shops, and live music while enjoying one of the city's most vibrant neighborhoods.

The space
The open main level features a spacious living room, dining area and fully stocked kitchen, making it easy to cook, dine and spend time together. Upstairs, a second large living room gives everyone extra space to relax, play games or enjoy some downtime.

Sleeping arrangements include a king bedroom, queen bedroom and a third bedroom with two full-size beds. With three full bathrooms, two living areas and plenty of separate spaces throughout the home, groups can comfortably spend time together without feeling crowded.

The outdoor spaces are one of the highlights. Start your morning with coffee on the expansive covered front porch, enjoy dinner and drinks on the deck with outdoor dining and a grill, then wind down around the fire pit in the private fenced backyard under the string lights.
