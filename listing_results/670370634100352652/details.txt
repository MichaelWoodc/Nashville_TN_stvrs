GERMANTOWN:

Named for the European immigrants who first settled here in the mid-19th century, Germantown is a historic community on the National Register of Historic Places located just a few blocks northwest of downtown Nashville.

BUCHANAN ARTS DISTRICT:

Centered around Buchanan Street just northwest of Germantown, you’ll find the Buchanan Arts District - one of Nashville’s up-and-coming areas. It has quickly become a haven for artists and designer businesses and is one of Nashville's hidden and eclectic gems.
