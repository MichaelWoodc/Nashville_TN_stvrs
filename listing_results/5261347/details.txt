Our unique, private guesthouse is in one of the most vibrant and walkable neighborhoods. We are one block from Sevier Park and located between the 12 South District and 8th Ave South/Melrose, and only 10 minutes from downtown. Bathroom has shower (but no tub), open shelves, fluffy white towels, extra toiletries and hair dryer.
