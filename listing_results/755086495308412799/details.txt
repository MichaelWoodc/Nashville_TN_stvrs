This isn’t your picture-perfect, cookie-cutter stay… and that’s exactly what makes it special. Our home is a little quirky, a little lived-in, and full of character, just like any great piece of history should be.

Come stay in a home that’s more than just a place to sleep, it’s a piece of a legend’s story 🪕
