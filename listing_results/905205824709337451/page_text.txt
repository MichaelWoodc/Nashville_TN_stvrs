East Nashville + it's outrageous charm is beautifully reflected in our pink cowgirl-inspired decor. This isn't just a place to stay; it's an experience. Nashville TN is all about the country theme + we love to bring it to you! Bachlorette groups, friends coming together, this suite is perfect to come back to after a night on Broadway! 

6 minute Uber/Lyft drive to Broadway, walkable to everything East including Basement East, Love & Exile, Fatherland, this location is amazing + affordable!

The space
The Suite:

Giddy Up Cowgirls (and boys)! We love to keep it country. Come take selfies on the saddle, enjoy the vibrant colors + bright space in all 4 rooms!

->Bedroom 1 "Cowboys + Tequila Please" : twin bunk beds | 1 single bunk

->Bedroom 2 "Giddy Up Cowgirl": Queen memory foam bed

->Bedroom 3 "Cowgirl Up" : Queen memory foam bed

->Bedroom 4 "Disco Cowgirl in Pink" : Queen memory foam bed

*all bedrooms have smart tvs, alarm clocks (who needs em tho, right??), noise machines for that extra comfortable sleep
*living area and select bedrooms have cable tv 
*kitchenette's have the perfect mini kitchen for your stay including; an oven, stovetop, dishwasher, fridge + most importantly.. a coffee maker:)
*both bathrooms are shower only, no tubs
*linens, towels, its all provided



Guest access
check in via a keypad

*please note our check in hours are 3pm-8pm, this is when we are officially avaliable for you. you're welcome to check in later as it’s self check in, but we may not be avaliable.

Other things to note
Absolutely, positively… NO PARTIES OR LOUD GATHERINGS. We have neighbors, we appreciate our neighbors + they appreciate you being respectful of them as they will be of you:)
