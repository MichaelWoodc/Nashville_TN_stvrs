=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Private room for 2 - comfortable furnishings & separate from the main living space.  Quiet neighborhood, walkable, booming East Nashville:  1/2 mi. or less to Aldi & Kroger grocery stores,  restaurants, cafes, Shelby Park & Greenway.  1 mi. from Historic 5 Points (restaurants , boutiques, live music venues),  2.5 miles from downtown, Interstates and Germantown neighborhood. The space
This private space has a queen-sized platform bed, recliner chair, cafe table/chairs, a seating area outside, in addition to a roomy bathroom with tub.  There are also a closet and chest of  drawers for storage and Wifi access.

We request that you please recycle (cans, plastic bottles & cardboard goes into the green bin outside your door.   For any glass, please leave them outside your door so we can take to be recycled.

For longer term visitors, if you need, a vacuum & cleaning supplies are contained in the closet.
