The space
You're not stuck in a hotel room ordering room service. Music Chic is one big open room where the cooking, the drinks, and the conversation all happen together, so nobody's left alone at the stove while the group hangs on the couch. The kitchen island runs the show: gas range, pot filler, open shelving, and bar stool seating that spills right into the living room.

A bar cart with a world map and glassware waits for cocktail hour, and leather sofas circle a fireplace under a mounted TV for the wind-down part of the night. When the group wants air, the backyard's outdoor lounge gives you a second hangout spot with room for a group meal outside.

Upstairs, the primary suite holds a king bed with its own bathroom, a full-length mirror, and a barn-door bath entry, basically the built-in getting-ready spot before a night out. Spend the day at Belmont Mansion or wandering Hillsboro Village, cook a group dinner at the island, then settle by the fire once Nashville quiets down.
