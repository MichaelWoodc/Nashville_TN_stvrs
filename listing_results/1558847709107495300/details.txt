The space
The Rooftop Record is this house's name, and its rooftop deck is why groups keep coming back. The marble island seats four on stools, stainless appliances handle the cooking, and the grey sectional with yellow armchairs makes the living room an easy hangout after a day out.

Smart TVs are mounted in the kitchen, living room, and every bedroom, so nobody misses the game. Upstairs, string lights, a dining table set for six, and lounge seating turn the rooftop into the group's evening headquarters.
