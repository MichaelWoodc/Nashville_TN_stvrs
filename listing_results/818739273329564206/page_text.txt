Getting Around (Hospitals & Medical Centers)

This location is ideal for travel nurses and medical professionals, with quick access to many of Nashville’s major hospitals:

• ~5–10 mins to TriStar Southern Hills Medical Center
• ~10–12 mins to Vanderbilt University Medical Center
• ~10–12 mins to TriStar Centennial Medical Center
• ~10 mins to Ascension Saint Thomas Midtown
• ~12–15 mins to Ascension Saint Thomas West
• ~10 mins to Nashville General Hospital
• ~15 mins to TriStar Skyline Medical Center

Most major hospitals in Nashville are within roughly **2–7 miles of downtown**, making this location highly convenient for commuting healthcare professionals.
