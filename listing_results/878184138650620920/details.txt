=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
100 year old historic charm meets fun city vibes! Great place to begin and end your day. 

Gather around the backyard fire-pit in the evenings, warm up with the sun on the porch and enjoy the chefs kitchen! Epic eats nearby at: The Pharmacy, Mas Tacos por Favor, and Lyra less than a block away :) 

The house is located in East Nashville that has tons of tasty eats and grocery stores within walking distance.

The space
The house was one of the original homes in East Nashville, built in 1907. Most of the home has been updated but there are still certain quirks and characteristics alive throughout the house for you to experience. 

There are two bedrooms, each with comfortable queen beds and bathrooms. The primary bath has the most beautiful walk in shower (with a rain head) and the guest bath has a tub big enough to soak in. We also have a dedicated office space for any wfh or business needs.

There is a third multi-purpose room upstairs that has full bathroom, futon, and relax/meditation corner. It has its own cooling/heating unit for comfort.

Guest access
You will have full access to the house and backyard for your privacy, but absolutely no parties will be allowed.

Other things to note
As noted, it is an old home and we appreciate gentle care to help us preserve the qualities and characteristics of its age and beauty.
