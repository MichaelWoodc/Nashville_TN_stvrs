The space
Centrally located less than a mile from Lower Broadway, “A Condo Named Blue” is the perfect spot for your next Nashville getaway. This eccentric, stylish condo comfortably sleeps 6, and has unparalleled views.

The fully-stocked kitchen is great for cooking meals at home; we'll provide all the pots, pans, and bakeware you need. The smart TVs in the living room and bedroom allow you to log in to your streaming services.

The bright, colorful bedroom for your belongings. The sofa in the living room pulls out to a queen-sized bed, as well.

An outdoor patio provides you a relaxing retreat from the Honky Tonks of Broadway and makes the perfect spot for a morning cup of coffee or an evening glass of wine. In the winter, the gas fire pit provides the same escape.
