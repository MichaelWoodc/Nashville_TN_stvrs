From the host
Situated just south of Broadway in the heart of Nashville, Pie Town is celebrated for its up-and-coming atmosphere and proximity to iconic attractions. This neighborhood blends a laid-back vibe with the lively pulse of downtown Nashville, making it the perfect destination for those seeking both entertainment and authentic local experiences.


Attractions:

Dive into the rich history of Nashville by visiting nearby landmarks such as the Country Music Hall of Fame and the Ryman Auditorium. Take a stroll through Music City Center or enjoy the green spaces at Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park, perfect for outdoor enthusiasts and history buffs alike.


Food & Retail:

Indulge in the flavors of Southern comfort food at trendy eateries and explore the vibrant culinary scene of The Gulch just a few blocks away. Whether you’re shopping at stylish boutiques or exploring local artisanal shops, there’s something for every taste and style.


Vibrant Community:

Immerse yourself in the live music scene and connect with Nashville’s friendly, music-loving locals. From spontaneous street performances to lively bars on Broadway, Pie Town offers a dynamic experience that enriches your stay.

This neighborhood is perfect for travelers who love music, culture, and local adventures. Whether you're here for a weekend getaway or an extended stay, Pie Town serves as your gateway to the best that Nashville has to offer!

As with any large metropolitan city, use caution in areas that are unfamiliar or off the beaten path, and use your best judgment. You can easily walk to all main attractions including Broadway, Music City Center, Bridgestone, Country Music Hall of Fame, and much more. However, after dark or when headed away from downtown, such as in The Gulch area (west), Lyft or Uber is recommended.
