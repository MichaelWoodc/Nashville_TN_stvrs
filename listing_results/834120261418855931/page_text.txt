Dog-friendly two-bedroom East Nashville condo with a shared rooftop deck, full kitchen, in-unit laundry, and fast WiFi. Sleeps up to eight with five verified beds. Nissan Stadium is just over a mile away.

The space
Stay in the heart of East Nashville, steps from neighborhood bars, restaurants, cafés, shops, and music venues. Nissan Stadium is just over a mile away, and downtown Nashville is about two miles away across the Cumberland River.

This dog-friendly condo sleeps up to eight guests:
• Bedroom 1: two double beds and one twin bed
• Bedroom 2: one queen bed
• Living room: one sofa bed

The open-plan home includes a full kitchen, dining area, central air conditioning, fast WiFi, TV, and an in-unit washer and dryer. Head upstairs to the shared rooftop deck for fresh air and sunset views. The building has elevator access and garage parking.

Streaming services are available with guests’ own accounts.

Guest access
As our guest, you'll have full access to the entire property, except for a few areas reserved for house supplies.
