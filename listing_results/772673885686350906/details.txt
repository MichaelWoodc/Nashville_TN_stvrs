Wake up to the Cumberland River right outside your window. This downtown condo sits on the riverfront with a private balcony over the water and skyline — a view most Nashville rentals don't have. Bright and full of character, with a king bed and sleeper sofa that sleeps 4. Broadway is about a mile away (a short walk or quick ride), First Horizon Park is a 5-minute walk, and Germantown is a few blocks over. Free parking, gym, laundry, and a summer pool. Great for couples or a small group downtown

The space
The kitchen is fully stocked — pots, pans, bakeware, and the basics for cooking a real meal in. There are smart TVs in both the living room and bedroom, so you can log into your own streaming accounts.

The private balcony over the Cumberland River is the spot for morning coffee or an evening drink, away from the noise of Broadway. In the cooler months, the fireplace gives you the same quiet retreat indoors.
