Welcome to our newly built 4-bedroom home in East Nashville! This spacious retreat features a fully stocked kitchen, plush beds, and a spa-like primary bath with heated floors. Explore local shops, trendy restaurants, and the lively Five Points area—all a short walk away. Plus, we’re just 2 miles from Broadway and Nissan Stadium, making it the perfect base for your Nashville adventure. Book your East Nashville escape today!

The space
Step into our stunning 2021-built Nashville sanctuary, thoughtfully curated by a renowned Miami designer. This spacious, elegant home is perfectly situated just steps from Nashville's top restaurants and attractions, making it ideal for both relaxing and gathering with friends. Enjoy proximity to Shelby Park, tennis courts, and two public golf courses, with complimentary bicycles available for exploring the neighborhood.

This expansive retreat offers two luxurious primary suites—one on each floor—with the second-floor suite featuring a spa-like bathroom complete with heated floors, a soaking tub, and a walk-in shower. The first floor boasts a fully equipped chef's kitchen, a dining room, a cozy sunken living room, and more. Double glass doors lead to a charming back porch, large yard, and a fire pit for outdoor relaxation.

With four bedrooms, including two king-sized suites, a queen guest room, and a room with twin beds and a crib, there’s plenty of room for everyone. A bonus room with a 72” TV and comfortable seating ensures entertainment for all. Three full bathrooms, each with unique touches, add to the convenience and comfort.

The chef’s kitchen is designed for culinary excellence, featuring stainless steel appliances, a gas range, pot-filler, and more. Outside, enjoy the fire pit, porch, and fun yard games in the fenced backyard.

Guests have full access to the home, with the exception of a few closets, and ample free street parking is available. Essentials such as plush towels and toiletries are provided for your comfort. Make lasting memories in this luxurious Nashville retreat!

Guest access
Guests have full access to the entire home, excluding a few closets, the pantry, and the attic.

Other things to note
Discover Nashville's hidden gems with ease! Just a short 5-10 minute walk brings you to trendy spots like Frothy Monkey, Lockeland Table, and Urban Cowboy. Need BBQ or essentials? Roy's Meat Service, only one block away, offers freshly smoked meats, beer, water, cereal, and fresh produce.

Downtown is just a quick 5-8 minute Uber ride or a short bike trip away. Convenient bike rentals are available two blocks over at Shelby Ave Bicycle. Outdoor lovers will appreciate Shelby Park, a 300-acre green space just two blocks away, featuring jogging and biking trails, kayaking, tennis, pickleball courts, E-bike rentals, playgrounds, and a nature center.

Everything you need for the perfect Nashville getaway is just outside your door!
