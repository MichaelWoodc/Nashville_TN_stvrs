=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Tucked away on the top floor of a historic building in downtown Nashville, this spacious 2024 buildout includes 4+ bedrooms, 6.5 baths, an open living room & kitchen & dining space overlooking Nissan Stadium. There is an additional upstairs mezzanine with arcade games and another living space!

Each bedroom is a suite with its own bathroom, brand new plush mattress and bedding, and a Smart TV with 85 streaming channels to make you feel right at home.

Metro Nashville NOO STR Permit #2025005506

The space
This 2025 new-build penthouse loft is located on the 6th floor of a beautiful, historic Printers Alley property. Enjoy incredible views of the heart of Nashville and Nissan Stadium from inside your loft and on your own private rooftop deck! 

The loft itself is two floors.
The main floor includes a spacious open floor plan with a kitchen, dining and living spaces with a full bath. There are two king bedroom suites with their own bathrooms on this main floor. 

The second floor offers a fun mezzanine that overlooks the main living space and has great outdoor views as well. This floor offers an additional living area; including a half bathroom, full wet bar with an ice machine and mini fridge and arcade games.  You can also find two more king bedroom suites on this floor with their own full bathrooms!

Additional sleeping can be found off of the main floor (back by the two bedrooms). Follow the back staircase to your own private loft furnished with a king bed and private bathroom! 
The mezzanine couch is also a queen-size sleeper sofa!

Guest access
Guest's will have access to attached private rooftop deck, as well as the communal 6th floor balcony that overlooks Printers Alley (located on the Alley side of the property).
