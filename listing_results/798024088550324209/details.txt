=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Slagledipityville Lake House where Music meets the Lake. Situated on the beautiful Percy Priest Lake this lakefront home has it all. 
Conveniently located 12 miles from Downtown/Broadway, it is the perfect place to retreat in a quiet space and only a 2 minute walk to the lake. Percy Priest Lake has awesome and fun activities, boat rentals, jet ski rentals, zip-line adventures, waterpark (Nashville Shores), marinas and restaurants. 
Driveway 20ft x 50 ft. No street parking please!

The space
We have 3 spacious bedrooms, 2 full baths, a nicely appointed kitchen and dining room overlooking our serene backyard. The master bedroom has a king sized bed, walk-in closet, is tastefully furnished with it's own en-suite full bathroom with stand up shower. The second bedroom is the Americana styled room, has a queen sized bed and also a walk-in closet. The third bedroom is lake-themed with a splash of Nashville's beautiful skyline, where the music meets the lake. It has a queen sized bed with a roomy closet. Both 2nd and 3rd bedrooms are across from a full bathroom. 
The living room has a huge comfy couch with a music/country vibe, where you will enjoy relaxation, listen to the radio, watch TV, read a book and just live.
The kitchen is spacious and has all the amenities to make a full home cooked meal, bake, etc. Crock-pot, pots and pans, coffeemaker, rice maker/insta pot all provided. Open to the dining room which sits 6 people and overlooks the backyard and trail to the lake. Only a 2 minute stroll.
On the back deck, there is a screened room furnished with patio furniture to chill and take in the beautiful surroundings.
The home sits on a cul-de-sac on a peninsula, which provides peace and tranquility.
