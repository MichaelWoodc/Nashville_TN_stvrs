=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Three entertainment spaces. One Nashville townhouse. An 800sqft rooftop with skyline views, gas firepit, grill, and TV. Converted garage games room with pool table, karaoke, and lounge. Living room with board games. 4 bedrooms, 12 beds, 4 baths — everyone gets their own.

10 minutes from Broadway. 5 minutes from Nissan Stadium and Top Golf. EV charger on site. Guest Favorite with 4.92★ across 86 reviews. Nashville's go-to for bachelorette weekends, bachelor groups, and sports fan trips.

The space
The English Garden was built in 2021 as a purpose-designed group house — three dedicated entertainment spaces, 12 real beds, four bathrooms, and an East Nashville address that puts Broadway 10 minutes away.

Rooftop

The reason most groups book twice. Over 800 square feet of rooftop terrace overlooking the Nashville skyline. There's a gas firepit with a large sectional couch, a gas grill, a mounted flat screen TV, and an outdoor dining table with extra chairs. The artificial grass area has a croquet course, giant checkers, and space to lay out in the sun. Strung with hanging lights and illuminated signage, it's one of the best rooftop spaces in the city after dark. The sunsets up here are genuinely something.

Games Room (Ground Floor — Converted Garage)

The garage has been fully converted into a dedicated entertainment space: a full-size pool table, karaoke machine, flat screen TV, bar stools, and a couple of couches. When the rooftop winds down, the gathering moves here.

Main Living + Kitchen (Second Floor)

A kitchen built for a group — island seating for four, gas cooktop and griddle, stainless steel appliances, tiled backsplash, and a well-stocked pantry. The dining area opens into a large living room with a flat screen TV and a collection of board games and after-dinner games.

Sleeping Layout

Ground Floor — Bedroom 4: Two full beds in bunk formation plus a twin trundle underneath. Full bathroom with tub/shower combo on this floor. Sleeps up to 5.
Second Floor — Bedroom 3: Two queen beds in bunk formation with a flat screen TV. Full bathroom with shower on this floor. Sleeps up to 4.
Third Floor — Primary Bedroom: King bed, rollaway twin, en-suite bathroom with double vanities and a large walk-in shower, flat screen TV, large walk-in closet. Sleeps up to 3.
Third Floor — Bedroom 2: Two queen beds in bunk formation plus a rollaway twin. Full bathroom with tub/shower combo accessed from the landing. Sleeps up to 5.
Third Floor Landing: Twin daybed with twin trundle beneath, adjacent to a hand-painted English Garden mural — a great photo spot. Sleeps up to 2.
Every guest sleeps in a real bed. No air mattresses, no couch assignments.

Guest access
Guests have full private access to the entire property: all four bedrooms, all four bathrooms, the games room, the living and dining areas, the kitchen, and the full rooftop. 24-hour self check-in via keypad — arrive any time after 4:00 PM.

An EV charger (Level 2) is available on-site.

Other things to note
Check-in: 4:00 PM. Check-out: 10:00 AM. Maximum occupancy: 12 guests.

Parking: With the garage converted to the games room, limited parking is available in the designated guest spots on the property and on Cherokee Ave (street parking). Do not park in front of the garage doors — violators may be towed.
