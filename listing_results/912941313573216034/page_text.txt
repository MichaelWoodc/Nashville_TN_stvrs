The space
✦ Stay in luxury and comfort at this beautifully designed downtown home nestled in Wedgewood Houston, one of Nashville’s most desirable neighborhoods. 

✦ Whether you’re gathering your gang for a bachelor/bachelorette bash, planning a fabulous friends' trip, or bringing the family together for a music-filled escape, this is your ideal destination! 

✦ Prime Location - Centrally located and just a quick ride to Broadway, The Gulch, Ryman Auditorium, Bridgestone Arena, Nissan Stadium & all the other hotspots

✦ Community Perks - Guests enjoy access to a gas fire pit and BBQ grills, perfect for gathering after a day of enjoying the city
