=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Cute, historic home, straddling 12 South & Melrose neighborhoods (walkable to both). 4 bed/2 bath, charming old house that was just fully renovated for modern comfort.

The space
This renovated 1930's bungalow is in the thick of it all in 12South and Melrose. Set on a quiet street with wide sidewalks, historic homes, and large shade trees, you'd never know that you can walk or scooter over to 12 South or 8th Ave for coffee, dining, and nightlife so quickly! Downtown is 2 miles away, Vanderbilt 1.5 mile, and Belmont 1 mile away. Very convenient to anywhere in the city! 

Our home has been remodeled in 2018. It retains the character of the original house and neighborhood, but with modern comfort. There is plenty of room for a large group to hang out in the living/dining space and open kitchen, plus 4 separate bedrooms and a master bedroom sitting room set up as another sleeping space if you need. 

Downstairs has 3 bedrooms with a queen sized bed in each. Upstairs is a master suite with a king bed, and a sitting room with 2 daybeds with trundles (4 twin beds total). 
There are two full bathrooms. The downstairs bathroom has a clawfoot tub, and the upstairs master bath has a large walk in shower with two shower heads. 
The kitchen is fully-equipped with stainless appliances, gas range & oven, dishes and utensils, and a drip coffee maker. 

There is outdoor living space on the wide front porch and the back deck with dining space. 

Parking for two cars is available in the driveway. You could also fit another car or two on the street in front of the house if needed.

Guest access
Guests will be renting the house entirely- you will have access to everything in the house.
