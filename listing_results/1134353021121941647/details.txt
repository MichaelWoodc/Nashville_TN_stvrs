=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Bob's Deer Den!  Meet Bob, our Den Proprietor, who is here to greet you and say howdy-do. We hope you'll enjoy our stylish retreat that blends vintage urban cowboy with mid-century design. Perfect for relaxing after a night on Broadway or a day exploring Music City. Minutes from downtown, great eats, and entertainment — your Nashville adventure starts here!

The space
LOCATION
Nestled in a quiet residential community, we have the benefit of safety and comfort with the convenience of a great location:

*5 min drive to Nissan Stadium
*10 min drive to Broadway
*10 min drive to Ascend Amphitheater
*12 min drive to Opryland
*20 min drive to Nashville Zoo
*17 min drive to Nashville International Airport (BNA)

There's also lots of shops and restaurants right here in East Nashville.  You'll be close to historic 5 Points, vintage clothing shops, craft beer joints, even the famous Redheaded Stranger (featured on Diners, Drive-ins & Dives, Season 36). Plus -- just around the corner -- you'll find Uptown Grocers, food trucks, a local Mexican restaurant, and day-pass access to swimming and hot tubbing at the Dive Motel pool and lounge.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS
You'll find a private bedroom with a comfy queen bed, plus a queen vertical Murphy bed with the same high quality mattress in the living room.  One handy feature: the Murphy bed can stay made with bedding – just tuck the bedding in and lift it into its wall cabinet during the day, and pull it down at night.  Next to the Murphy bed is a cabinet with storage and pull-out nightstand for added convenience.  There is LED strip lighting running along the inside frame of the Murphy bed with several color settings and is controlled with a remote.

All the sheets are a luxurious 100% long staple cotton, and all mattresses and pillows are fully enclosed with zippered waterproof cases for cleanliness and protection.

The bedroom also offers a huge closet with plenty of space for clothing and luggage. There are also room darkening shades on the bedroom windows.

LIVING ROOM
The living room sports a green velvet sofa and vintage leather lounge chairs over a cowhide rug.  In the center of the seating area is a one-of-a-kind coffee table made from a live-edge piece of wood, and its cracks and crevices are filled with green epoxy resin. The TV is a (2025) 55" HDTV with all the hook-ups you would expect. Room darkening shades are provided on the windows.

*Wifi is 3g Google fiber, btw!

The kitchen is full-sized and well stocked for real cooking: pots, pans, cooking utensils, oven mitts, stylish dishes, glassware, place mats, oil and vinegar, a selection of cooking spices, and even a few tasty snacks to tide you over.  In addition to the full-sized stove, microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher, and garbage disposer, you'll also find a wide-slot toaster, blender, Keurig coffee maker, water pitcher, electric tea kettle, coffee pods, classic drip coffee maker, creamer, sweeteners, and an assortment of teas.
