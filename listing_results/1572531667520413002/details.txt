Outdoors you’ll find a 700-square-foot patio with a covered deck, perfect for coffee at sunrise, grilling, & nights spent around the gas fire pit. Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, or a holiday with family, the cottage is your perfect stay!

The space
The Cottage is central to Nashville and all it offers as it is directly off of HWY 24. Just a 25 minute drive to BNA Airport. The neighborhood is extremely safe and although it's only 20 min from Nashville, it feels like you're in TN countryside.
