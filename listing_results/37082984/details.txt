=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Treat yourself to serenity in a 400 sq. ft cabin nestled in the woods of beautiful Nashville. Modern amenities include a brand new 2 lounger hot tub in a picturesque setting. Experience the wonders of a cabin in the woods without the long drive to East TN or Blue Ridge, GA. Only 15 minutes to Broadway & minutes to Sylvan Park & the Nations restaurants bars shops & cafes. 
Wind down in complete solace with the sight of wildlife and the sound of birds after exploring all Nashville has to offer!

The space
Pecan Valley Cabin is a newly renovated 400 sq ft. modern cabin nestled in the trees on a quiet street between Bells Bend Outdoor center and Beaman Park Nature reserve. You will experience the feeling of being 1000 miles away while being only 15 minutes to Downtown Nashville. Perfect for a romantic getaway or for a songwriters retreat close enough to the heart of music city but far enough away to think, reflect, and create. 

The main floor features a full kitchen with a farmhouse sink, brand new stainless steel appliances, and butcher block counter tops and pull out sofa bed and flat screen tv. French doors lead out onto a partial wraparound deck with a 2 person hot tub, a table for 2, propane grill, and patio furniture. Up the stairs you will find a bedroom fitted with a queen bed and full bathroom. Simply white Ship Lap is adorned throughout.
The cabin has 2 parking spaces directly out front. If additional parking spaces are required, please inquire. 
Please note, this is a 400 sq. ft. space that is very comfortable for a party of 2, but can accommodate up to 4 with a pull out sofa in the living room. 

Pets welcome! $40 pet fee. Let us know if your furry friend(s) will be joining you and we will be sure to leave a special treat!

Please let us know prior to arrival if you will be pulling a trailer so that proper accommodations can be made.

Guest access
Entire cabin is private and for guests only.

Other things to note
Upon arrival, you will cross over a bridge and head up a long/steep driveway. 
The driveway is T shaped with two cabins at the top. Your cabin is to the left. We live in the adjacent cabin on the right with our small dog and free range chickens. 
We want your experience to be as private and convenient as possible. Therefore you will enter via lockbox. 
We will leave all guests to enjoy their stay peacefully and privately. However if you need us we are a text or phone call away.

There is a ring motion floodlight above the garage door, lighting the driveway for incoming guests. 

We routinely empty and deep clean the hot tub to ensure its cleanliness and your comfort. Please note that this may be done on the morning of your check in date and therefore the hot tub may not be at full temperature upon arrival.
