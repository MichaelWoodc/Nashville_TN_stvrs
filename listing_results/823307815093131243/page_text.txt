The space
This large 1 bedroom apartment is laid out to accommodate those who want to connect but also retreat to your own private space for sleep. The bedroom is perfect for the vampire who doesn't want to see the light of day until they are ready.

Guest access
The full apartment is 2 bedrooms but for this listing, you'll be the only occupants for the entire apartment and only be booking for 1 bedroom. No one else will be able to book the other bedroom. My son lives in Nashville so I visit often.  The Nations is a great place to stay..great bars, restaurants and a real neighborhood feel. I don't go into Broadway on these trips, but it is a close Uber ride away but staying in a more local area. Only note that if you plan to cook there are no condiments etc. This is almost never an issue in Nashville with so many places to eat but just an FYI. He has coffee, pots, pans etc. 
Communication was excellent but as noted several times this is a place where locals live not a party place. I would definitely stay again as a single or couple.

Where you’ll be
West Nashville, Nashville
Converted warehouses, craft breweries, and rolling hills shape this laid-back district, with music spots, casual eats, and leafy streets.
From the host
It's the Nations. It's on the fringe of the chaos that is downtown. This neighborhood is as hot as it gets for local eateries and drinks. Tons of restaurants from dive bars to fine dining are easily in walking distance. Get your fix morning, noon, and night for food and beverage without paying a dime for transportation. Hop over to other areas so easily with the basically instant interstate access. You'll be hopping around Broadway, wandering restaurants in Germantown, shopping in The Gulch or Green Hills, catching a show or game at Nissan, or many other things in less than 15 minutes drive from here.
