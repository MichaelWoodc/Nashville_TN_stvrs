The space
This Airbnb leans into color: bold artwork, patterned wallpaper, and statement lighting turn nearly every room into its own scene, from the geometric-tiled kitchen backsplash to a bedroom wrapped in fawnskin-print pillows and another lit by a neon sign. The chef's kitchen opens straight into the dining area, where a table seats up to 14 so the whole crew eats together instead of splitting between rooms.

Smart TVs and Wi-Fi keep the living room the natural spot to regroup once everyone's back, while the Skyline Deck gives the group an outdoor perch above the street for coffee or evening drinks.

Each bedroom takes its own direction: one wears a light green upholstered bed against a bookshelf-print wall, another goes moody with a wooden slat wall and blue upholstery, and a third pairs an orange bed with a vibrant patterned wall and neon sign.

Spend the day out around Germantown and East Nashville, come back to soak in the hot tub as the evening cools, take in the skyline from the deck, then settle into the living room for a movie before everyone splits off to recharge.
