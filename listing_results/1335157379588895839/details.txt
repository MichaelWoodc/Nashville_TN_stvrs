Historic condo on Music Row in midtown Nashville. Spence Manor was Nashville's first 'Diamond Hotel', that attracted celebrities from across the world in the 70s. Most notably, Spence Manor was Elvis Presley's location of choice when recording at historic RCA studio B right across the street. Artists like Alabama, Johnny Cash, Waylon Jennings, Merle Haggard, Willie Nelson, and many more have all spent time staying at this building whether it was a layover between tour dates, or working on music together.
