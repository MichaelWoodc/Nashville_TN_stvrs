=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Perfect for bachelor/bachelorette parties, family reunions, and gatherings with friends. Bring the whole crew to this great place with lots of room for fun.

With plenty of seating for everyone, along with 6 queen beds for a good night's sleep, you'll love this townhouse for your trip to Nashville. 

Located in the trendy Inglewood neighborhood of East Nashville, this townhouse is a 10-min drive to Broadway and Downtown as well as to the Grand Old Opry and Opryland.

The space
Enter this property through either the front door entrance or the garage. The garage will very easily fit one car - two if they're not too big!

The first floor of the unit holds a bedroom with a queen over queen bunk bed and a full bath with shower/tub combo. 

Up the steps to second floor, you will walk into the very spacious living room and kitchen. The living room has a huge sectional sofa plus several other chairs so that all your guests have a place to sit and gather around the 80-in TV. The TV can also be viewed from the full kitchen that has a fridge with beverage station, Keurig coffee maker, stove/oven, microwave, and toaster oven along with all the cooking utensils needed for putting together a great meal.

Also on the second floor, down the hall from the living room and kitchen, is a full bath with shower/tub combo and a bedroom with a queen bed and 70-in TV.

One more set of steps will take you up to the third floor, two additional bedrooms and bathrooms, as well as the laundry room and the balcony with an outdoor seating area. 

The laundry room contains one of our favorite features - an all-in-one washer/dryer! So, you can wash and dry your clothes while you're out on the town and you don't have to worry about switching your clothes over.

The 3rd floor bunk room contains a queen over queen bunk bed. This bedroom leads to the master bath with a beautifully tiled shower with glass door.

The other 3rd floor bedroom contains a queen bed with a 65-in TV and leads to the last of the 4 bathrooms with a shower/tub combo.

All of the bathrooms have shampoo/conditioner/body wash dispensers, so no need to bring your own along! 

Finally, the balcony is off of the 3rd floor landing and is a great place to sit, relax, and enjoy the Nashville weather!
