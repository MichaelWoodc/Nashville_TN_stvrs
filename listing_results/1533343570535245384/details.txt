The space
Two front doors, two rooftops, zero shared walls. This is your Airbnb base for a bachelorette weekend, family reunion, or two friend groups who each want their own house but still want to gather at night. Bubbles is the calmer of the two homes, built around a Rooftop Deck with a hot tub lit up under the skyline after dark.

Inside, an open living room flows into the kitchen island, with a workspace tucked off to the side for anyone catching up on email before heading out. Sparks brings the energy up top with its own Rooftop Deck featuring a fire pit, gas grill, and patio furniture for a second gathering spot once the sun goes down.

One bedroom here leans into a comic-inspired theme with mural wallpaper and its own doorway out to the balcony. Spend the day exploring East Nashville and Germantown, watch the skyline turn pink from the Rooftop Deck, then split between the fire pit and hot tub after dark before everyone retreats to two full homes to actually sleep.
