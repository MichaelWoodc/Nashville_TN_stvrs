Welcome to The Beverly Nash- as listed in Nashville Forbes top 10 Airbnb's!

Newly built in 2023 and ideally located in walkable Downtown Nashville, Beverly Nash is perched upon the penthouse floor of The Hyve building. Our condo has stunning downtown skyline views with 20ft ceilings, providing the ultimate in luxurious living right in the very heart of Music City, as well as an on site gym, pool, free parking, and so much more. Walking distance (.7 miles)  to World Famous Lower Broadway!

The space
Experience the best of Nashville at **The Beverly Nash**, a stunning two-story condo designed to offer comfort, convenience, and unforgettable city views. Radiating with beautiful natural light, this modern space is perfect for small groups, business travelers, or a romantic getaway. As seen on Nashville Forbes top 10 Airbnb's list!

Step into the spacious living area on the first floor with two queen size Murphy beds for ample sleeping space. The open-concept layout extends to a fully equipped kitchen, featuring sleek stainless steel appliances, a stocked Keurig coffee bar, and kitchen island (please note cooking oil is not provided). The highlight of this floor is the oversized balcony, complete with comfortable seating an incredible view of the Batman Building and the rest of the Nashville skyline—an idyllic spot to unwind and enjoy breathtaking skyline views. A full-sized bathroom is also conveniently located on this level. We can comfortably accommodate up to 6 guests. 

Upstairs, retreat to the cozy master suite, which boasts a plush king-sized bed, a private full bathroom, and more stunning city views from the comfort of your room.
