=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Nine to Vibe is a studio stay for guests who want a little more color, a little more charm, and a whole lot of ease. With bold tones, soft textures, and a few subtle nods to Nashville’s queen of country, this space invites you to unplug and settle in—whatever your rhythm.

The space
This 474 sq ft studio features a king-size bed, a cozy lounge area, and a layout designed for rest and recharge. A Crosley record player sets the tone, while the fully equipped kitchen makes simple meals easy. Soft lighting, smart TV, and thoughtful touches throughout create a comfortable retreat in the heart of SoBro—ideal for solo travelers or couples looking to slow down between city adventures.

Guest access
You’ll have access to the entire apartment, including the in-unit washer/dryer and stocked kitchen.
