=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This cute, cozy cottage is located in the heart of Nashville's Historic Sylvan Park. A retreat where you can relax, whip up a nice meal, or explore on foot many unique restaurants and shops.

The space
With two bedrooms and one bathroom, this cottage comfortably sleeps 4. The living area and kitchen have an open layout. The patio off of the kitchen provides a quaint space to sip your morning coffee, read a good book or enjoy a yummy meal.

We provide starter amenities at the beginning of your stay. We hope you find them useful.

Some of the furniture in the pics might be different -- we've been hosting for many years! Things get broken, and we replace! The good news is, it’s always an upgrade!

Guest access
You will have access to the entire cottage. The cottage has wifi, a smart tv, an outdoor dining area, and two free parking spaces. There is no washer & dryer, but extra towels are provided and a laundromat is just down the street!

Other things to note
Paying guests in the house only, please. We do not allow guests of guests, photography sessions, parties or events unless you have contacted us and have prior approval.

We aren't always able to accommodate early checkins or late checkouts, but please feel free to ask if your travel plans dictate the need for either.
