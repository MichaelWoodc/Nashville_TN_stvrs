=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
🏡 Settle into the Yellow Room in a colorful Craftsman bungalow in East Nashville’s historic Inglewood neighborhood. The home is full of character, with handmade pottery, friendly resident kitties, and a lush wraparound garden that makes it feel like a genuine home away from home.

📍 Explore neighborhood restaurants, coffee shops, and bakeries nearby, with easy access to the rest of East Nashville, downtown, BNA, and Nashville’s major attractions.

The space
🏡 MY PROPERTY
Welcome to my 1928 brick Craftsman bungalow in East Nashville’s historic Inglewood neighborhood. I’m a local potter and graphic designer, and I love welcoming people into my home. If you like bungalows, magical cottagecore vibes, pottery, friendly pets, gardens, and a home with personality, you’ll probably feel right at home here.

📍 Inglewood is a convenient Nashville home base, with easy interstate access to Five Points and the rest of East Nashville, downtown and Music City Center, Gaylord Opryland, and TriStar Skyline Medical Center.

🛏️ This listing is for the Yellow Room, a private second-floor bedroom. The upstairs guest bathroom is on the same floor and may be shared with another guest. The upstairs guest area is quiet and separate from the main living spaces downstairs.

🌼 THE YELLOW ROOM
The Yellow Room was inspired by the yellow daylillies and bright colors from my garden.

The room includes a queen bed, dresser, small refrigerator, sitting area, and a large window-side desk  with a monitor (HDMI) with plenty of natural light and side lamps. Warm yellow details throughout give the room its cheerful character.

You’ll also have fluffy white towels, extra blankets, and plenty of pillows.

🛋️ THE HOUSE
My home is filled with things I’ve made, collected, and chosen over the years. As a potter and designer, I care about spaces being functional, beautiful, comfortable, and a little unexpected.

You’ll find handmade pottery throughout the house, along with artwork, textiles, plants, books, and collected objects. Many of the dishes and mugs you’ll use were made right here in my pottery studio.

The bungalow extends around the back and upward, with expanded living spaces and a large wraparound garden.

💻 WORKING FROM HOME
I work from home during the day as a PhD student, graphic designer, and potter—and, apparently, as a full-time stay-at-home cat parent.

👋🏻 Because I’m usually around, I can help when needed while giving you as much privacy and independence as you prefer. Some guests enjoy talking over coffee; others come and go on their own. Both are completely welcome.

🐾 THE PETS
I’m an avid animal rescuer and advocate, and several rescue cats call the house home, as well as my service dog, Onyx.

🐱 🦮 They’re friendly, affectionate, and very much part of the personality of the house. Please keep the pets out of the upstairs guest area—and from going outside (they lie).
