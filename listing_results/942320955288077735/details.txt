Zero compromises on style or comfort. Nestled in the trendy East Nashville area, you are minutes to downtown, shopping, restaurants, & more. The space
URBN Ryder is a newly built three-story townhome in East Nashville featuring three private bedroom suites, 10-foot ceilings on every level, floor-to-ceiling windows, and an open floor plan designed for groups who want their own space without sacrificing time together. At just under 1,700 sq ft, it comfortably sleeps 10 across 8 beds — ideal for bachelorette parties, family reunions, corporate retreats, or a couple who wants an entire Nashville home to themselves.

»THE LAYOUT«

First Floor | Honky Tonk Hideaway

A private suite right off the rear and front entry with its own walk-in closet and full bathroom featuring a walk-in tiled shower. Concrete floors, brick accent wall, and rope lighting set a moody Music City industrial tone over a memory foam full-sized bed. Perfect for guests who want ground-level privacy.

Second Floor | Main Living + Kitchen

The heart of the home. An open living area with wooden shiplap walls, expansive windows, leather and velvet accent seating, and a hidden King Murphy Bed with memory foam mattress tucked beneath the Smart Roku TV — so your living room doubles as a bedroom when you need it. The fully equipped kitchen and stocked coffee bar make mornings easy, with ample counter space and storage for multi-night stays. Games are provided. This cowboy chic home is not shy of any insta-worthy photos! 

Third Floor | Music City Bunkhouse + URBN Loft

Two distinct retreats share the top floor. 

The Bunkhouse features a twin-over-twin bunk and a full-over-full bunk with trundle — all memory foam — with its own full bathroom, herringbone ceiling, and full-length mirror to make sure Nashville looks good on you before you head out for some fun. 

The URBN Loft is the primary suite: shiplap walls, bohemian décor, a walk-in closet, spa-like bathroom with double sinks and an oversized walk-in shower, Smart Roku TV, and a king memory foam mattress with premium pillows. A modern farmhouse escape that feels equal parts stylish, cozy, and unmistakably Nashville.

In-unit washer and dryer are located on this floor for your convenience.
