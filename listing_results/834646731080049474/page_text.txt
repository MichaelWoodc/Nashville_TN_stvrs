Plus Park | Easy, Affordable, and Close to It All

The space
Compact Comfort Near Nashville's Hotspots


This stylish 1-bedroom, 1-bath condo is ideal for couples, small families, or a few close friends who want a quiet, affordable place to call home while exploring the best of Nashville. Located just minutes from the airport and a quick drive to downtown, Plus Park blends budget-friendly comfort with convenience. Enjoy high ceilings, an open industrial vibe, and easy access to great local eats, vintage finds, and nightlife gems.


Why You'll Love It Here

✓	620 sq ft of efficient, modern living space

✓	Fast Wi-Fi + Smart TV for streaming
