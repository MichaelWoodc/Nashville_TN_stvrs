=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
BOOKING FOR FOURTH OF JULY. 
This relaxing Riverside unit has great views of the Titans Stadium and the Cumberland River. A great place to stay for the CMA Fest, Fourth of July festivities and New Year's Eve. Come enjoy the many relaxing elements of the Riverside unit. Located downtown, Historic 2nd Ave North, overlooking Riverfront Park. Beautiful, newly renovated Historic Pilcher Building. Open living/gathering space with fully equipped kitchen, pool table, and entertainment space for groups.

The space
The Riverside unit offers an artful approach of even the smallest detail with each room being designed with an individual identity. You are welcomed inside the entrance of this unit by one of Nashville’s local artisan, Jonathan Malphrus, with his beautifully modern designed entrance table. Come enjoy a master bath that has been designed for your pampering with an edgy Victorian style feel. In the unfortunate event that you must work, you can find seclusion in the rock n roll themed office, while your friends can hang out in the living area shooting pool and enjoying the view out the windows of the Titan’s Stadium.

The Riverside unit has 4 bedrooms with 1 king and 3 queen sized beds and the hall office has a queen sized murphy bed.

Other things to note
The Riverside Unit overlooks the Cumberland River, which is the location for the July 4th fireworks display and the River Stage for the CMA Music Festival.  Stay out of the crowd and watch the show from your private open living area, while entertaining your friends and guests.
