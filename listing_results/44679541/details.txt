=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to your downtown Nashville retreat - two adjoining condos steps from Broadway, designed for groups who want comfort, space & the energy of the city right outside the door.

- Sleeps 16+ | 4 bedrooms | 9 beds | 5 baths
- 2 adjoining condos w/ full kitchens, living & parlor areas
- City skyline view | private entrance & self check-in
- TV, Wi-Fi & workspace in both units
- Air conditioning, ceiling fans & all bathroom/kitchen essentials
- Pet-friendly | long-term stays

The space
Nashville's Broadway district doesn't just surround you here - it pulls you in. Two blocks from the World Famous Honky Tonks and the electric stretch of live music venues, this downtown address puts the city's heartbeat right at your doorstep, while two adjoining condos give your group the breathing room to unwind on your own terms. Printer's Alley is just across the street, and the nights here have a way of going longer than planned.

The space
Two adjoining 2-bedroom condos connected by a shared hallway, each spanning two levels. Sleeping areas are located on the lower level with 100% blackout conditions for full rest after a long night out. The setup gives larger groups both shared common areas and private retreats - ideal for families, multi-generational travel, or groups who want their own space without being in separate hotels.

Sleeping arrangements
The Dolly features two king beds and two twin beds, along with two ensuite baths and a half bath. The Parton offers two queen beds, two twin beds, and one sofa bed, also with two ensuite baths and a half bath - sleeping 16 or more guests comfortably across both units.

Living spaces
Upper levels in each condo open into living and parlor areas built for gathering. Plush seating invites long conversations and easy evenings in, and the TV gives you a way to wind down when the city has done its job. The layout balances shared social space with enough privacy that everyone gets room to breathe.

Kitchen & dining
Both kitchens are fully equipped for everything from a quick morning coffee to a full group breakfast before heading out. Each includes a refrigerator, freezer, stove, oven, dishwasher, microwave, coffee maker, toaster, and blender. Cooking basics, dishes and silverware, a baking sheet, and wine glasses are all provided - and a dining table in each unit gives the group a proper place to gather. Coffee is stocked and ready.

Bathroom
All five bathrooms are stocked with shampoo, conditioner, body soap, cleaning products, and hot water. A bathtub and hair dryer are also available for guests.

Amenities & essentials
Bed linens, towels, extra pillows and blankets, hangers, an iron, and clothing storage are all included. Air conditioning, heating, and ceiling fans keep the space comfortable year-round. Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace are available for anyone needing to stay connected - note that the workspace does not have a dedicated door. Home safety features include smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher, and exterior security cameras in common hallway areas only - not inside the units.

Access & parking
Self check-in via smart lock and a private entrance make arrival effortless at any hour. Paid parking is available off premises for guests bringing a vehicle.

Location
Situated on 3rd Avenue North between Church and Union Streets, the condos sit directly across from Printer's Alley and within easy walking distance of Broadway, Bridgestone Arena, Music City Center, the Titans Stadium, and the Waterfront. East Nashville, West Nashville, and nearby universities are all just a short ride away.

Good to know
Both condos are pet-friendly. Long-term stays of 28 days or more are also welcome. Please note that a washer is not available on-site.
