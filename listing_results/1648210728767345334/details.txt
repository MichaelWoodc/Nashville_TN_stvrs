=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Monroe House #13 is built for Nashville group trips: your own 4BR house with a private 2-car garage and front door opening straight into a shared courtyard made for hanging out. Play on two pickleball courts, grill dinner, sit by the fire pit, run cornhole or foosball, then walk back into your own house when the night winds down. Sleeps 12 with 5 beds and 3 baths. Germantown is essentially across the street; Broadway and the Ryman are about 10 minutes by car. Hosted by the owners who built it.

The space
Welcome to Monroe House #13, one of 16 custom-built homes arranged around a shared social courtyard on the Buena Vista / Germantown edge. This is not a random standalone rental with beds packed in. The whole community was designed for groups who want their own private house, real bedrooms, full kitchens, and a central place to gather.

Inside, the house is a three-story townhome built in 2020 with high ceilings, big windows, modern finishes, and an open main level. The kitchen has a full-size fridge, dishwasher, gas range, coffee bar, cookware, dishes, and the basics for eating in before a night out. The dining area seats 8, and the living room gives the group a comfortable place to land between plans.

The sleeping setup is simple and group-friendly: 4 bedrooms, 5 beds, and 3 full baths across three floors. You also have fast Wi-Fi, central AC, smart TV, and in-unit washer/dryer.

You have a private attached 2-car garage for parking. The garage opens onto the courtyard side, so arrivals are easy: pull in, unload, and step right into the shared courtyard.

Your front door opens straight into the shared Monroe courtyard with two pickleball courts, fire pit seating, BBQ grills, outdoor dining tables, cornhole, foosball, and giant Jenga.
