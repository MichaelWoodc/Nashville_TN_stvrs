Enjoy a stylish experience at this spacious and conveniently located condo! Located in the hip & trendy East Nashville neighborhood. Walk to local coffee shops, restaurants & a brewery. Only 10 minutes to Downtown Broadway bars, Nissan Stadium, Country Music Hall of Fame, The Ryman + more! 13 min to the Grand Ole Opry & 20 minutes to the Airport. Hair salon in the building if you need a cut + an Elevator. Fully equipped kitchen, Smart TV and washer/dryer in the condo. Free parking is available.

There are plenty of Ubers, Lyft and taxis available if you choose not to bring a car. This condo is in a great location to enjoy all the things that Nashville has to offer!
