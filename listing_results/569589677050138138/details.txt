=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Keep it simple at this peaceful and centrally-located place.

The space
This over-garage apartment is in the heart of the Germantown neighborhood. Germantown is the most walkable neighborhood in Nashville proper, and is one of the most sought after neighborhoods to live in. It also has many of the best restaurants and bars that Nashville has to offer. Just a short walk to Broadway and most other parts of Nashville, it’s likely you’ll never need a car. But just in case, this space comes with 1 off-street parking spot, and alternative forms of travel like e-bikes and scooters.

Large and comfortable, the apartment provides lots of space to spread out and lounge. The queen size bed has a brand new mattress and sleeps like a dream. The tv has all the apps you could need to watch your favorite show and our little book nook is a fabulous place to spend a rainy day.

The book nook has a dedicated work space desk that can be used to catch up on email, etc. or even to do some work!  There's also a make-up mirror in case you want to use the desk to comfortably apply your make-up.

There are two charging stations conveniently located, one by the desk and the other by the table in the large living space.  Both have two USB ports, so you can charge up to 4 devices at once!

The kitchen has plenty of storage and is stocked with plates, flatware, bowls, coffee supplies and even a few snacks. There is a microwave, mini fridge and a growler to fill up at your favorite brewery. Please note that this kitchen does not have an oven/stove. We highly encourage visiting the local coffee shops, bars and restaurants while you are here.

This apartment has its own private entrance, deck and patio set for you to enjoy the weather.

Guest access
We have one dedicated parking spot for this apartment. Street parking is free but hard to come by at certain times of day. 
So close to downtown, I would suggest walking, taking the free bus or jumping on a scooter.

Other things to note
While there is quite a bit of cabinetry, there is no stove or cooking surface. We highly encourage trying out all the fabulous restaurants nearby
