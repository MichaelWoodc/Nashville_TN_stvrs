=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
A lovingly styled, first-floor, private apartment in our 1960's home. Fully-equipped kitchen, bedroom, full bath, dining room, and living room with sofa bed. Separate entrances and parking. Covered porch and garden. Family-friendly. We gladly welcome guests of all ages, backgrounds, and orientations. Lots of windows and plants! Close to downtown!

The space
The apartment is a dedicated guest space. We have lovingly rehabbed and decorated it with the intention of having a comfortable and memorable home for guests to enjoy their time in East Nashville to the fullest. Furnishings and decor are a mix of flea market finds and modern comfort. Above all, we strive to offer an impeccably clean and safe space to our guests. Check out our IG for more photos and up to date info. @eastnashvillebnb

Guest access
You will have access to the entire apartment (bedroom, living room, dining room, kitchen and full bath) with two separate entrances to the outside. 

In warmer months, there is an herb garden and porch for you to enjoy. Smoking is also permitted on the porch and in the yard. 

Parking is available in the driveway on the backside of the house.

Other things to note
We don't have cable, but the TV in the living room gets local broadcast channels and is equipped with Netflix.
