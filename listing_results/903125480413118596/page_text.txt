Experience modern comfort at Casa Dora, a beautifully designed 3BR/3BA Nashville retreat just 10 minutes from downtown. Enjoy a private backyard with a grill, upper deck with firepit, and stylish interiors featuring stainless-steel kitchen appliances, two dining areas, and a 65" Smart TV. Perfect for families or groups, this pet-friendly home offers parking, workspace, and quick access to top attractions, dining, and music venues.

Other things to note
Primary renter must be at least 25 years of age. Guests may be asked to present their IDs upon arrival or anytime during their stay.



Casa Dora is situated in a perfect location near all of the best attractions, nightlife, restaurants, shopping, and museums that this vibrant city is known for. Yet, you can always kick back and enjoy some summer sunshine right at home base with the backyard featuring a gas grill that is more than ready to assist with cookouts in the fresh air along with a house-wide second deck with seating and a firepit.



When you finally head inside, the comfort and fun will continue with a stunning interior with contemporary touches, high-end furnishings, and a spacious layout offering plenty of room for everyone to spread out. When mealtimes come along, your best recipes will be a breeze to prepare in the full kitchen using the stainless-steel appliances, beautiful countertops, and two dining tables to gather at. Not to mention, the living room has plenty of seating for all to gather at the end of the day and stream favorite movies on the 65-inch smart TV. A desk awaits in the sunroom as well for those that need to stay connected with remote work or school.



A Nashville retreat has never looked so good, so book your stay while this city gem lasts!

THINGS TO KNOW:

Parking notes: There is free parking for vehicles.

Guest entry instructions: This rental utilizes an E-lock, a digital lock that requires a unique code to enter. This code is reset after each guest's stay.



Upon booking, you’ll receive timely communications about your trip. Access to your professionally cleaned and sanitized home is also convenient with our custom keyless locks. And, if you need anything before, during or after your stay, our local team is ready to help!



Please be aware that there will be NO refunds for Wi-Fi issues as it’s not guaranteed. If reliable Wi-Fi is a must, you are welcome to bring your own internet hotspot.



Non-negotiable rules: No Smoking or Vaping, No Pets, No Events or Parties, No Firearms. Quiet hours are from 10pm to 7am (unless posted signage indicates otherwise).
