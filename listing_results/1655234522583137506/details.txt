The space
Glass doors swing open onto your deck, and twilight turns the skyline pink while you're still standing in your socks. This open-plan studio keeps your whole group together in one easy space. A queen bed sits in the main room, and the built-in Bunk Nook tucks 2 stacked twin beds just steps from a pink sofa and TV.

The kitchen comes stocked with stainless steel appliances for a home-cooked meal or a round of cocktails before you head out. Nashville touches run through every corner: an Elvis print over the kitchen counter, a wolf-under-moon piece above the bed, gold accents throughout.

Beyond a glass door by the Bunk Nook, two private decks give you room to spread out, each set up with an Outdoor Lounge and a table for six under open sky. Spend the day wandering the Country Music Hall of Fame and the honky-tonks of Lower Broadway, then come back for sunset drinks on the deck before the skyline lights up for the night.

This is your Airbnb base for seeing Music City without ever fighting for elbow room.
