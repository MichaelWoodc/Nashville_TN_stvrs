Enjoy the best of city and country living in our SEPARATE Guest  House . Swim in our saltwater pool and warm up in the hot tub.

The space
This  lovely (SEPARATE) Guest  House is a perfect get-away.   Swim in the pool, walk to the lake and fish for dinner.  The 750 sq. ft. upstairs bedroom has 2 queen beds, and a day bed to sleep 4 guests comfortably. There is also a private dressing area.

The open plan in the downstairs makes entertaining easy.  The kitchen contains full-size appliances, all small appliances, and cooking necessities.  
The dining room seats 6.

Need to do laundry?  No problem.

Stream the two tvs for your favorite shows.  House is equipped with Separate private wi-fi, adult and childrens' books and games.  A playpen and baby high chair are available. 

Guests may use the pool and pool toys after signing a liability waiver.  

Guests may also use the fire pit or request to use the 2 bicycles, and croquet set.

Guest access
