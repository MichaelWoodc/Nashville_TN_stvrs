=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Located just one mile from the Grand Ole Opry, Club Wyndham Nashville provides a great base to explore all the middle Tennessee has to offer. Conveniently located near popular destinations like the Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum and the Nashville Zoo, you can easily explore all that Nashville has to offer. Plus, take a scenic drive out to the Jack Daniel’s Distillery or explore the shops and restaurants of Green Hills.

The space
This spacious one-bedroom resort suite measures approximately 900 - 978 square feet. You will enjoy a king bed in the master bedroom and a queen sleeper sofa in the living room. Additional amenities include a full kitchen and dining area, whirlpool tub and washer/dryer. Maximum occupancy is four. Private sleeping area sleeps two.

Not far from Club Wyndham Nashville, you’ll find key destinations like the Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum and the Nashville Zoo. Hillsboro Village is a Nashville neighborhood of shops and restaurants that’s full of character, too. Spend a day taking a beautiful scenic drive out to Lynchburg, home of the Jack Daniel’s Distillery, or visit the historic Belle Meade Plantation, where Tennessee’s past comes to life.

Nashville Resort Accommodations and Amenities
At Club Wyndham Nashville, enjoy convenient access to Music City, Broadway and local sporting events. There are onsite activities offered, if you don’t feel like leaving, just check the Experiences Guide for updates. Children will enjoy table tennis and basketball. What’s a resort vacation without swimming? There are both indoor and outdoor pools, hot tubs, plus a fully equipped fitness center. When it’s time to relax, guests are welcome to borrow a DVD from the resort library and take it back to their condo suite. All this and more is part of the philosophy of southern hospitality at Club Wyndham Nashville.
