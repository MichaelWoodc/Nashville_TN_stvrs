Come stay awhile in this bright and sunny condo overlooking the Cumberland River just minutes from the Heart of Music City. Walkable to the trendy Germantown neighborhood. Less than a 5-minute drive to Broadway where the music stages are rockin’ 7 days a week. This suite is the perfect spot to unwind after a long day of exploring all that Nashville has to offer. Free parking included!
