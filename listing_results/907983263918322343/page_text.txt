"The Dolly House on Music Row" is the Ultimate Group Destination in Nashville!

This unique Dolly inspired property, consists of 4 bedrooms and sleeps up to 12 guests! It's located directly on MUSIC ROW, surrounded by Country Music's top recording studios and walking distance to dozens of bars and restaurants!

Experience Nashville in an unforgettable way!!

Gated Parking + Mimosa Bar + Walkable and Historic Location + 3 Makeup Stations + More!

The space
Step off of Music Row into your perfect Bachelorette / Group Retreat!

Enter the front door where you'll find "Dolly's Bubbly Bus and Champagne Bar."  Snag a group photo under the "Broadway Girls" neon sign before walking into "Dolly's Disco Room," equipped with a fully operating disco ball to set the tone for your memorable experience in Music City!

Each bedroom has a story inspired by The Queen of Country herself.  You're going to have a hard time deciding which room you want to claim!

Layout:

"The Dolly Suite" • King bed with makeup station
"The Wildflower" • Three Queen beds with double makeup station
"The 9 to 5" • Two Queen beds
"The Jolene" • Queen bed with custom mural/neon sign

Follow the rainbow through the first floor and you'll find two full bathrooms and small but unique kitchen equipped with everything that you'll need to cook a quick meal!

Exit out of the back door to the gated parking area.
