The space
The surrounding neighborhood is transitioning into newer construction. It's an eclectic mix of home owner occupied homes, college rentals, commercial offices, a nursing home and one church. There are lots of new construction projects occurring in the neighborhood. Parking and access to the private entrance is easier from the rear parking lot accessed via the service alley off Ridley Blvd. Landscape lighting and motion lights on all sides of the building. Guest access
Go up the outside stairs to side door. Entirety of the upstairs apartment with no contact with the downstairs business offices. Only one access door between the two floors and it is locked and alarmed during stays.

Other things to note
Parking lot and sidewalk access to the apartment is from behind the home off the service alley. The easier way to access the alley is off of Ridley Blvd but it can also be accessed off of the street behind the businesses on 8th Avenue South. The alley pavement has seen better days lately so that is why we tell everyone to come in on the Eastern side. Please park diagonally in the back following the sidewalk suggestion so that there is room for 6-7 cars to park. (Do not park in the dead middle of the parking lot)
