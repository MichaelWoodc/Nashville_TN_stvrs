=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Edison Condo is located in a unique and trendy East Nashville neighborhood, just 5 miles from downtown and walking distance to Elegy Coffee, The Fox Cocktail Club, Micky's Tavern, Grimey's Record Store, Living Waters Brewery, and Nicoletto's Pasta. This condo is perfect for a group trip, family trip, or remote-work getaway. Easy access to Ellington and Briley Parkways get you to all your favorite tourist spots such as downtown Broadway and Music Row, where they make the music!

The space
Two bedroom / two bathroom spacious condo (1230 sq ft) with parking for 2 cars in the parking lot behind the building. Nearby street parking is also available. 

Open concept living room, with modern fully stocked kitchen, and kitchen island dining area. Living room with smart tv so you don't miss any of your favorite shows. Access to the Amenities Room / Conference Room for gatherings or remote work. 

1st Bedroom: The massive master bedroom holds a king-sized bed, with an additional smart tv. Master bedroom also features a walk-in closet and a private en suite bathroom.

2nd Bedroom: This bedroom has a comfortable full-sized and an additional twin-sized bed and double closets. Access to a full bathroom in the next room over.

Guest access
Access to full property and Amenities Room / Conference Room except for locked cleaning supply closet.
