=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
See yourself living your best life in the Penthouse at the Pilcher Lofts. Step back in time and celebrate in your own VIP luxury retro arcade room and invite your friends to enjoy the pinnacle of Nashville hospitality.

The space
The Penthouse unit is a sweeping 4,245 sf luxury space designed to make you and your guests feel like a star. The towering 18’ ceilings with the oversized windows that showcase views of Nashville. Relax in the Penthouse luxury master bathroom featuring a Crosswater London tub, neon lights and rain showers.

The Penthouse has 4 bedrooms with 1 king and 3 queen sized beds, the office  has a queen sized murphy bed and the arcade also has a queen sized sleeper sofa.

Have fun relaxing in the retro arcade with your friends and family as you watch the big screen TV, play foosball, arcade games and play the retro nintendo gaming system. If you enjoy board games, we have a game cabinet full of games geared to all ages. The arcade is also equipped with a wet bar with mini refrigerator, sink, microwave and ice machine.

Other things to note
The Penthouse Unit is one block from the Historic 2nd & Broadway Music Scene, which is the location for the July 4th fireworks display and multiple concert stages for the CMA Music Festival. Enjoy everything the city has to offer for these great events, while your group stays in a private suite just steps away from all the food, drinks and live music.
