=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Neon Nights — two downtown Nashville homes under one address, built for groups that take over the weekend. 36 beds, 8 bedrooms, and two private rooftops with skyline views make it a great fit for bachelorette weekends, bachelor parties, birthdays, and family trips. Kick back in the open-concept living space, head up to the rooftop, and walk to Broadway in minutes. Steps from the Ryman, Bridgestone, and every downtown hotspot.

The space
Neon Nights is two downtown Nashville homes under one roof — plenty of room for the whole group without splitting up. The setup works for bachelor/bachelorette combos (one side for the bride tribe, one for the groom squad), wedding parties, family trips, or any group that needs more room than a single rental offers.

Both sides feature modern decor, open layouts, and plenty of photo-ready spots. Each home has a comfortable living area that connects to a fully equipped kitchen with an island — a natural hangout spot for group meals, movie nights, or pregaming before a night on Broadway.

Upstairs, each side opens to its own private rooftop with lounge seating and skyline views — a great spot for sunsets, group photos, or gathering the crew before heading out.

You can book one side for a smaller group or the whole property for a larger one.
