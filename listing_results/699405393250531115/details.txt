East Centric is located in a colorful and lively part of the city - East Nashville. While we can't guarantee that you won't hear noise from any pop-up events next door, other guests in the neighborhood, or the busy corridor we're located on, we can tell you that sensitive sleepers are welcome to use the supplied disposable ear plugs as needed or ask Alexa to play sleep sounds via Echo Dot (available in each bedroom).
