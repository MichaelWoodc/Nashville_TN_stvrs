=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The perfect mix of nature, comfort and fun! This one-room studio cabin is perfect for a couple's getaway or small family.  Located at Nashville KOA Resort, enjoy campground amenities including heated outdoor pool, cafe, live music, dog park, pickleball,  lawn bowling and more. Enjoy easy access to downtown Nashville with a low cost daily Downtown Shuttle, but far enough from the heart of the city to enjoy a quiet evening around the campfire.

The space
This cabin is a cozy studio with the queen bed and kitchenette together in one room, a full bathroom and small bunk bed alcove. If you need a bedroom with separate living room, please view our one bedroom cabins also on Airbnb. 

-	Sleeps 4: (300 sq.ft.) Best for couples or small families, these one room studio cabins offer one queen bed and an alcove with one set of twin bunk beds and bathroom. 
-	Kitchenette: In same room as bed, equipped with the essentials for your stay including a refrigerator, cooktop, microwave, toaster, basic cookware, dishware and utensils. There is also a Keurig Coffee Maker!
-	Modern Amenities: Watch TV on the flat screen and stay connected with fast, modern Wi-Fi (installed 2024).  Enjoy the convenience of a full bathroom with hot water and fresh towels and linens provided.
-	Outdoor Living: Step outside to your private patio with a propane grill and outdoor seating, perfect for enjoying meals or relaxing under the stars. There's also a fire pit nearby for s'mores and storytelling around the fire.
-	Campground Recreation: There’s a LOT to do at Nashville KOA Resort! We have live music nearly every night, plus an awesome heated pool, hot tub, pickleball, lawn chess & bowling, play areas, pet yard and more. You can easily explore the whole campground with golf cart and bicycle rentals. 
-	Campground Services: Need anything? Our General Store has snacks, camping gear, souvenirs, basic groceries and more. Our friendly staff is available to assist you, and the onsite Greyline Concierge can help you plan visits to local attractions.
-      Neighborhood: Nashville KOA Resort is conveniently located near several of Music City's most iconic attractions. The Grand Ole Opry is just 3 miles away where you can enjoy legendary performances and take guided backstage tours to delve into the venue's rich history. Nightlife options are plenty including live music within 2 miles at local favorites including Scoreboard Bar & Grill, Music City Bar & Grill and Nashville Palace Honky Tonk. Opry Mills is also within 3 miles and offers an outlet mall with popular name brand shops, Regal Cinema, The Escape Game and numerous restaurants including Dave & Busters & Rainforest Café.  Outdoor enthusiasts enjoy nearby Two Rivers Park offering a wave action pool, historic mansion, disc golf, skate park, dog park and Stones River Greenway. 

The neighborhood is 6 miles, less than 15 minutes to downtown Nashville and an all-day low cost shuttle picks up daily at the campground with easy transportation to and from the Broadway area.

Guest access
Guests will have dedicated access to the cabin and the private outdoor patio, fire pit and BBQ grill. The campground offers many amenities like outdoor pool, hot tub, general store and laundry which are shared facilities. Outdoor activities are abundant with pickleball, bocce ball, horse shoes, bowling, chess, play areas, pet park and more. As a registered guest you will have shared access to all the campground amenities including special paid activity options when available.

Other things to note
- Our cabins are pet friendly with an additional fee of $25 per pet. No aggressive pets. If left unattended, they must be in a crate.

- Plan to have visitors during your stay?  These cabins are limited to 6 people, including guests & visitors. If the number of overnight guests on your reservation allows for day visitors, those day visitors must check in at the registration building upon arrival. Your visitors are welcome for $10 per additional adult. Visiting guests are permitted until Quiet Hours and must adhere to campground rules.

-Please note that there is a maximum of two vehicles allowed per cabin.
