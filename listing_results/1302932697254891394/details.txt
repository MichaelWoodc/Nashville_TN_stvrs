=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Discover your perfect Nashville trip nestled in the hills. Here, you can unwind amidst nature with hiking, kayaking, and fishing on the Cumberland River, all while being just a short drive from Nashville's renowned dining and entertainment scene. Our property offers the ideal setting for cozy evenings: grill up a feast on the BBQ smoker or griddle, gather around the fire pit, set the mood with outdoor music, and enjoy the creek flowing through the backyard. We also have kayaks available for rent

The space
This cozy 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom space features its own back entrance and a lovely covered patio. We've set up the outdoor area with patio lights and surround sound so you can fully enjoy the peace and quiet of our 2-acre property. Upstairs, resides a young family with two kids and two friendly dogs, so you'll likely see them around the property. While we aim to be mindful, please expect to hear the normal, happy sounds of a young family living overhead at times.
