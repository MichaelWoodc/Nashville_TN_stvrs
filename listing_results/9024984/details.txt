=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Super Host Status - This cozy & private Nashville upstairs apartment is located in the beautiful upscale Green Hills neighborhood with private parking. Walking distance to Lipscomb University and a two minute drive to the trendy 12South District, home to shops, restaurants and the iconic "I Believe in Nashville" mural. Approximately 5 miles away from Downtown Nashville, Bridgestone Arena, & Nissan Stadium. 2 miles from The world-famous Bluebird Cafe,  Nashville’s premiere songwriter music venue.

The space
The Lyric Loft is a private one bedroom apartment on the second floor of our house. There is plenty of parking, in our driveway and across the street. The apartment is newly painted and newly furnished, with Pottery Barn and Restoration Hardware furniture. It is a relaxing, cozy space to unwind, write, read and enjoy your trip! There is even a guitar if you feel inspired by the muse of Music City! The bedroom has a queen bed and also an ottoman that has a pull-out twin bed. We can provide a twin bed air mattress upon request to be used in the living area. Or if you like, the large sectional sofa in the living area is big enough to sleep on as well. Twin sheets, blankets, and pillows are provided for these in the bedroom closet. There is a Nest pod in the living area with high speed wi-fi. The living area has a 43" HD Smart TV with YouTube TV and Amazon Prime. The bathroom is accessed from the bedroom and has been updated with tile floors and a subway tiled shower. The apartment also has central heat and air conditioning. It can comfortably fit 2-3 people and up to 4 if needed. 
Kitchen is fully stocked with plates, glasses, silverware, bowls, pots, pans, toaster, wine opener, bottle opener, etc. It also has a Keurig (K-cup) coffee maker with a variety of coffee pods included. On the bottom of the night stand in the bedroom, there is a multi-outlet power strip to charge your phone or other electronic device. An iron, ironing board, hair dryer, towels, and robes are in bedroom closet. There is a water filter for the kitchen faucet.

Guest access
This apartment is completely separate and private with outside staircase. It has its own separate outside entrance/exit, along with it's own stackable washer/dryer and 40" Smart cable t.v. Wi-fi internet is also available. Please use the right side of the driveway to park and we will use the left side. If you need additional parking, there is an open parking lot across the street that can be used for free. There is a walking/jogging trail across the street and a quick walk to a great coffee shop, local restaurant, bookstore and a Subway shop.

Other things to note
