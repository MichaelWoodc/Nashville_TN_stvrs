=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Enjoy your vacation in this fully furnished and newly renovated 4 bedroom home with 8 beds and 2.5 baths just 4 miles from downtown Nashville!  This property is the perfect gem with close proximity to downtown Nashville, The Grand Ole Opry, and the Airport all within 5 miles or less.  The perfect location for all Nashville has to offer nestled in a quiet neighborhood with a large courtyard, a hot tub, game room and fire pit to enjoy the outdoors with family and friends.

The space
This home features an open floor plan that accommodates both dining and lounge space, making it great for couples, groups, and families looking to spend time together.  It comes with a fully equipped kitchen with brand new appliances and cooking/kitchenware equipment perfect for that gathering you were planning. All the bedrooms, kitchen, living room and bathrooms are on one level. The additional game room is accessible with a few steps or a small ramp. 

There are plenty of beds for up to 10 guests in these 4 spacious bedrooms which are located on the same floor as the living space.  2 of the bedrooms have ensuite access to a half bath with an additional full bath in the hallway.  The 2 other bedrooms have access to a large ensuite bathroom set up with double vanity and additional make-up counter. 

If lounging and relaxing with friends is what you have in mind, you will enjoy our “Game Room” where a game of darts or foosball can take place or just sit and relax on a bar stool or futon to watch TV. The futon converts to a bed if alternative sleeping arrangements are desired. 

This home features a large backyard with a private fenced courtyard featuring a large covered hot tub, fire pit, Adirondack chairs & Gas grill. This is the perfect place to enjoy a quiet evening with friends playing a game of cornhole after a busy day of sightseeing.

Guest access
Guests will have access to the house, deck and large yard.

Other things to note
The maximum number of guests at any time at the house is 10 people.

The house is not only cleaned after every guest stay but the towels, bed sheets, duvet cover and pillow cases you come into contact with are cleaned after each stay.  Extra blankets and sheets are stored in zipped bags in the bedroom closets for you to use and are cleaned after each guest use.  The hot tub is drained after every guest. Cleanliness is very important to me and this is something that I have 
always done as I manage my own property.

I strictly adhere to Airbnb cancellation policies and rules when it comes to refunds. I recommend that all guests purchase travel insurance for life’s unforeseen circumstances.
