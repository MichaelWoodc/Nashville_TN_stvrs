The space
A fresh and stylish getaway awaits when you stay at this gorgeous one-bedroom condo at Illume, a brand-new community that fuses Southern California design with the unique flair of the Music City. Set in Gulch View - a prime locale near downtown and The Gulch - this urban escape is minutes from the city’s incredible music scene, fantastic dining, local shopping, and nightlife hotspots. Luxe amenities include a beautifully landscaped courtyard, a fitness center, and a seasonal pool in the heart of the building. Pairing contemporary style with a sustainable design, this forward-thinking retreat includes features like energy-efficient systems and a green roof. Enjoy a beverage on the balcony overlooking the tree-lined street or plan activities at the convenient desk setup.

A convenient desk setup is ideal for solidifying your itinerary. This condo sleeps four guests between one bedroom and a queen sofa bed in the living area. The bedroom offers an adjustable queen bed, while the en suite bathroom is configured with a tub/shower combination. 

Greet the morning with a cup of coffee on the furnished balcony.
