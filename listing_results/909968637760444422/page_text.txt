The space

• Bedroom 1 — Queen bed

🍳 Kitchen & Dining
• Full kitchen with stainless appliances and basic cookware/serveware
• Coffee bar
• Bar seating for casual dining

🏖️ What's Included
• Private washer and dryer
• High-speed WiFi
• Smart TV

📍 Location Highlights
• Located at The 1865 near Marathon Village
• Minutes to Centennial Park and The Parthenon
• Short drive to Downtown Nashville, Broadway, and Music Row
• Close to Capitol View, Nashville Farmers' Market, and Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park
• Easy access to Watkins Park, First Horizon Park, the Tennessee State Museum, and the Cumberland River Greenway

🚗 Parking, Safety & Notes
• This home is not pet friendly. If there is found evidence of an animal's presence during your stay or post check-out, guests will be charged an additional cleaning fee.
• Parking is unassigned in the lot and first-come, first-served; there is typically plenty of space.
• Pool: The communal plunge pool is open seasonally from Memorial Day to Labor Day. Hours and availability are managed by the building.
• Communal amenities: Guests may use the shared amenities at The 1865, including the pool, fitness center, and lobby areas. Amenity availability and maintenance are handled by the building.
• Elevator: There is an elevator in the building, operated and maintained by building management. If it’s unavailable due to maintenance, stairs will be required. In case of emergency, please use the stairs.
• Check in is at 4:00 PM and checkout is at 10:00 AM.
