Funky East Nashville Bungalo, upstairs "brother-in-law" apt.  Separate entrance.  Parking for one vehicle.  1 block from bus route, 5 minutes from 5 points, 10 min from Downtown,  staggering distance from the Village Pub, Underdog and Kroger...perfect for visiting couples, songwriters, tourists, and travelling hipsters.

The space
This space was formerly a "Brother in law" apt.   It has been occupied by songwriters and some music biz people and by a travelling band or two.   Grammy winning songwriters/artists  have drank beer on these very premises.

From the host
East Nashville (East Nasty to its locals) its a funky gentrified neighborhood. There are still a few rough patches and yeah, Gallatin Rd looks a lil scary in places, but there are lots of creative people living here and its not unusual to see a rock star at the Kroger. We are spoiled by the restaurants and bars that have been sprouting up in the last few years. An "attitude" map of Nashvillle put our address smack-dab between "Hipsters without trust funds" and "Too Many places to Drink."
