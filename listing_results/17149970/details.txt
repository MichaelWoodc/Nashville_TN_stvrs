=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to our home in East Nashville! Our two bedroom/one bath home is great for anyone visiting Nashville. Enjoy the large back deck or head out to explore the city.

Within one-two miles, you can reach many East Nashville hot spots. Downtown (4 miles) & beyond are easily accessible. While having a car is best, Uber/Lyft make exploring easy.

Please note the main entrance to the house is on the back deck, up a set of stairs.

The space
This brick cottage was built in in 1952. It has gone through a few owners and even provided temporary housing for some Hurricane Katrina evacuees before my husband purchased the house in 2006.  We've done some updates in the past year and will upload new photos soon! 

You'll enter the home through the back door which is up a set of steps and access via key code. The main floor has a fully stocked kitchen with eat-in dining. Just down the hall is the bathroom (with free shampoo, conditioner and body wash),  first bedroom (queen) and living room (cable tv, DVD player and roku TV for your own streaming services.) At the top of the stairs, you'll find a desk and chaise lounge for another sitting option. The larger bedroom (queen)  is on this floor. The large space looks over the backyard. Please note this room does not have a door though it will feel very private being down the hall from the stairs. 

The upstairs bedroom has a mini split AC/Heater controlled by remote for additional comfort. 

Baby gates for the stairs, a pack n play with sheets, a toddler cot and a high chair are always available.

*This home has stairs.
