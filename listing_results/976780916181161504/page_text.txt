Bright, music-inspired Nashville townhouse designed for group stays, with 5 queen beds, 2 twin beds, and a full-size pullout sofa. Enjoy a rooftop TV and fire pit, two additional balconies, a grill, garage game room, fast 598 Mbps Wi-Fi, free in-unit laundry, and even pet-friendly accommodations. Downtown/Broadway and major venues are less than 10 minutes away. More details below!

The space
Thoughtfully designed by a hospitality expert, this stylish three-level townhouse pairs abundant natural light with Nashville-inspired details, including a colorful Music City guitar mural and a record collection for your stay.

- ROOFTOP with a TV, a fire pit and a golf game. 
- 2 other balconies, one with a grill. 
- GAME ROOM with a ping pong table, darts, NFL Blitz arcade game, ring toss and more. 
- 5 queen beds, 2 twin beds, and a full size pullout sofa. 


- 1st floor bedroom: Queen bed, walk-in closet, and private bath.
- 2nd floor: open-concept living, dining, and fully appointed kitchen. Make craft cocktails with high-end bar tools, gather around the dining table, cook a meal, or settle in with a plush blanket and a show after a night out.
- 3rd floor: The primary sleeping space. One bedroom offers queen bunk beds, twin bunk beds, the primary bath, a large walk-in closet, and a dedicated water closet. The 2nd bedroom offers 2 queen beds and a private bathroom.

Guest access
The entire townhouse is yours to enjoy, with a private entrance and self check-in via keypad. You will also have access to the garage game room, rooftop, two additional balconies, and the shared backyard.

The shared backyard has room for cornhole, ladder ball, and cold drinks. It is not fully fenced.
