The space
Prepare for a luxury retreat at the Offstage Oasis, a stunning three-story property designed to be your ultimate Nashville home base. Every detail has been curated for an unforgettable group experience, just minutes from the iconic Broadway strip.

First Floor: 
The Heart of the Home.
Enter to a spacious and welcoming layout. The main level features a great gathering room, perfect for reconnecting with your friends and group. A large, well-appointed chef's kitchen is a culinary dream, complete with a professional gas range, a sprawling island for gathering, and a convenient BBQ grill for outdoor cooking. The adjacent living room will take your breath away with its 24-foot ceilings, a cozy fireplace, TV, and the ultimate group entertainment: karaoke fun! This floor also features a large, private bedroom with a king bed and a luxurious en-suite bathroom.

Second Floor: 
The Private Quarters.
Ascend to the second floor, where you'll find two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom. The master bedroom is a true sanctuary, boasting a plush king bed and a unique double twin bed that can be converted into a queen to accommodate your needs. The master's grand, spa-like bathroom offers a full rain shower and a separate soaker tub for ultimate relaxation. A spacious walk-in closet includes dedicated get-ready stations, making it easy for everyone to prepare for a night on the town. A second bedroom on this floor is equipped with two comfortable queen beds.

Third Floor: 
Fun & City Views.
The top floor is dedicated to entertainment and incredible views. A stylish wet bar is ready for mixing your favorite cocktails. The game room offers a vibrant space to hang out and includes two twin beds for extra sleeping arrangements. Step outside onto the private patio, the crown jewel of this property. Here, you'll be treated to stunning Nashville skyline views, while you relax on comfortable seating, enjoy a game of foosball or gather around the fire place and TV.

Outdoor Amenities & Parking
The fun continues outside! The backyard is a private oasis with gated parking for your peace of mind. A large deck is the perfect hangout spot for playing a round of mini golf or a friendly game of cornhole. And, of course, the BBQ grill is always ready for a cookout. The Offstage Oasis is more than a place to stay—it's your private, Nashville experience waiting for you.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the house and the backyard! What's ours is yours for the weekend, but we do keep a closet or two locked for maintenance and cleaning items.
