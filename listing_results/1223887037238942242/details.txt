The space
Half the group hits Broadway, the other half stays back for a mini golf tournament in the yard, then everyone reconvenes on the balcony once the sky turns pink. That's the rhythm this Airbnb is made for, big enough to spread out, stocked enough that nobody has to leave to have fun.

Inside, the open kitchen and living room keep the group together around a large island, dark hardwood floors, and a dining table with bench seating for the whole crew. A staircase connects the main floor to the bedrooms above, and the white sofa and armchairs give the living room its own hangout spot away from the party downstairs.

Downstairs is where 'PLUNGE CITY' earns its name: a game room with a pool table, backyard mini golf, and a private Balboa hot tub under an open sky with an umbrella overhead. The covered balcony adds a fourth hangout, with a neon 'sip back and relax' sign glowing over a skyline view built for evening drinks.

Spend the afternoon at mini golf, soak in the hot tub as the sun drops, then head inside for pool table rematches before the group turns in.
