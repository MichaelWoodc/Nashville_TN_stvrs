=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Stay in the heart of Nashville in this bright, charming apartment right on Broadway! Featuring original hardwood floors, oversized windows, tons of natural light, a spacious living area, cozy dining space, comfortable bedroom, and full bath. Step outside and enjoy easy access to Nashville’s best restaurants, bars, live music, shopping, and entertainment. The perfect home base for couples, friends, business travelers, or a Music City getaway.

The space
The apartment is bright and airy while still feeling warm, cozy, and inviting. Oversized windows fill the space with natural light, while the historic details give it plenty of character and that comfortable, lived-in Nashville charm.
The apartment features a small but functional kitchen with a working stovetop (please note, the oven is not operational), a cozy dining room, a spacious living room, a comfortable bedroom, and a compact but functional bathroom. A separate hallway allows guests to move through the apartment without having to pass through the bedroom, offering a little extra privacy when traveling with multiple guests.
It’s an easygoing, comfortable space with everything you need to settle in and feel at home while exploring Nashville.

Guest access
You’ll have full access to the entire apartment during your stay, with the exception of a couple of locked storage closets reserved for cleaning supplies and restocking.

Other things to note
Our Broadway apartment is located in a 100+ year-old historic building, and we love the character that comes with it! While the apartment is professionally cleaned and well cared for, you may notice some quirks of an older home like uneven floors and walls, small cracks, paint chips, and other perfectly imperfect details. We think they’re part of what makes the space special, but we want our guests to know what to expect.
And because you’re staying right on Broadway in the heart of Nashville, city noise comes with the territory! You may hear traffic, music, sirens, and the occasional enthusiastic Nashvillian, especially on weekends. 
If you’re looking for brand-new construction and total silence, we may not be the perfect fit. But if you love historic character, an unbeatable location, and being right in the middle of Music City, we think you’ll feel right at home!
