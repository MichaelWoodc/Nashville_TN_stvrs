=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This lovely four-bedroom updated ranch is located in Donelson, just minutes from the Nashville Intl Airport, Gaylord Opryland Hotel, the Grand Ole Opry and vibrant downtown Nashville. It's also very convenient to other areas of Middle Tennessee.

In this space you'll find three queen beds, one full bed, and two folding twin beds. All linens are furnished for you, as well as kitchen stocked with the implements needed to cook a fantastic meal or just brew some coffee to make your mornings great!

The space
This beautiful "renovated ranch" is located in the heart of Hip Donelson, a treasured area of Nashville that is convenient to the airport and a mere 10 to 15 minutes to the hopping downtown area. The kitchen features Corian countertops and stainless steel appliances including a gas stove, and opens to the light-filled dining area and living room. Nestle in front of the remote-controlled gas fireplace on a chilly evening or spend a relaxing time in the hot tub on the back patio. Enjoy the expansive back yard where you'll most likely spot deer in the distance nibbling the grass.

Make yourself at home in the comfortable beds and take time to savor a nice, hot bath in the clawfoot tub in the upstairs bath or wash off the remains of the day in the updated shower in the downstairs bath.

The hot tub off the back patio can offer a relaxing time as well.

Guest access
Enjoy all of the comforts of this amazing home including the downstairs full-size washer and dryer and the hot tub.
