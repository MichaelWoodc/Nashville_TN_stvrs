=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Two side-by-side homes just minutes from Broadway, perfect for groups who want to stay together without feeling cramped. Each home has multiple bedrooms & bathrooms, stocked kitchens, TVs throughout, and space to spread out or gather up. Step outside to double the fun: private putting greens, grills, firepits, and stunning Nashville skyline views from two rooftops. Secure garage parking included. Perfect for bachelorettes, birthdays & reunions - book this and make it a trip they'll never forget!

The space
Level One – Arrival & Hangout
The first floor features a one-car garage, a fully stocked kitchen, comfortable living room, and half bath set the tone for the trip - perfect for pregames, grocery runs, and late-night snacks. Each home's living room comes with a king-sized sleeper sofa and TV, giving overflow guests a comfortable place to crash.

Level Two – Where the Crew Sleeps
All three bedrooms live on the second floor, so the whole group rests on the same level - no one trekking between floors at 3 AM. The primary suite is the room everyone fights over: king-sized bed, private balcony, and en-suite bathroom. One guest bedroom sleeps four with two queen beds, built for friends sharing a room, and the other has a king - ideal for a couple wanting their own space. Every bedroom has its own TV, bathroom essentials are stocked, and the in-unit washer/dryer with Tide Pods provided makes it easy to refresh between nights out.

Level Three – Lounge & Rooftop
The top floor is the trip's main event. A wet bar lounge with TV and seating gives the group a space to regroup between adventures - and pulls double duty as flexible sleeping with a king-sized sleeper sofa, futon, and two twin beds for the overflow crew. Push open the sliding doors and you're on the rooftop: putting green, gas grill, gas firepit, ample seating, and the kind of skyline views that end up in every group photo.

Stay Essentials:
Free high-speed WiFi throughout. Smart lock entry with a unique code for your stay. Bag drop, early check-in, and late check-out available on request.

Guest access
Guests have access to the entire home except for a locked owner's closet. Before arrival, you'll receive a four-digit code to enter through our Schlage Encode smart lock. There's also an on-site lockbox as a backup in case of any smart lock issues, plus 24/7 customer support to make sure you're never locked out.
