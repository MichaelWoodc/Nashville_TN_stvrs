=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Located in the heart of historic East Nashville (coffee shops, restaurants, bars and local shops right down the block) this walkable 3 bedroom, upstairs apartment is the perfect stay for weekend getaways, a fun trip with friends or anyone looking to enjoy all Nashville has to offer. This cozy and convenient space is located only 10 minutes from Broadway and only a 15 minute ride from the airport. The perfect and relaxing place to stay while you explore the town.

The space
This three bedroom apartment is the entire top floor of a two-story multi-use building located right off of bustling Eastland Ave. This space has it's own private entrance and balcony that will make you feel right at home.

Expect a clean and comfortable place that's convenient to anywhere in Nashville you plan to go.

Updated stainless steel appliances throughout the kitchen, high-speed internet, 65" Smart 4K HDTV with AppleTV located in the living room.

You are welcome to use the Peloton bike but you'll need your own cycling shoes.

Guest access
