The Etta | Historic Loft | Walkable Downtown

Experience the timeless charm of The Etta Loft inside the historic Cohen Building—just steps from Broadway. This lavish 5-bedroom, 4-bath retreat offers 3,000+ sq ft of thoughtfully restored living space with modern comforts, upscale furnishings, and unbeatable access to Nashville’s top destinations. Perfect for weddings, corporate stays, or weekend getaways.
