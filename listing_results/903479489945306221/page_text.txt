The space
★ Welcome to Your Music City Oasis! ★

》Located in the vibrant heart of downtown Nashville, this stylish one-bedroom condo offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and music-inspired charm. Whether you're in town to explore the city’s iconic attractions or relax, this condo is the perfect retreat for your stay.

✦ Location Advantages:
- Broadway: Just a 10-minute walk to the live music scene and legendary honky-tonks.
- Bridgestone Arena: Only a 5-minute walk to catch a game or concert.
- Country Music Hall of Fame: A 2-minute drive away for a dose of Nashville history.
- The Gulch: Trendy restaurants and boutiques are just a 12-minute walk from your doorstep.
- Vanderbilt University: Easily reachable in 15 minutes by car, perfect for visiting students or catching a game.

✦ Bedroom & Bathroom: This cozy condo features one bedroom with a plush queen-size bed outfitted with soft linens and a peaceful courtyard view. A queen sofa sleeper and a queen air mattress in the living room allow up to 6 guests to sleep comfortably. The spacious bathroom boasts a dual vanity, large mirrors, and a full bathtub/shower combo, providing a relaxing, refreshing space.

✦ Kitchen & Dining: The fully-equipped kitchen has modern stainless-steel appliances, including a Keurig coffee maker and a milkshake maker—perfect for indulging in a homemade treat! The island with bar seating for two adds a casual dining option.

✦ Comfort & Amenities: With central air conditioning and heating, this condo is perfectly equipped for any season. Guests can enjoy high-speed Wi-Fi, a 65-inch Smart TV, and a 40-inch bedroom TV for entertainment. A full washer and dryer in the unit make more extended stays convenient, while extra pillows, blankets, and room-darkening shades ensure a restful night.

✦ Living Space: The spacious living room is decorated with warm tones and stylish details, including a comfortable sofa, a record player with a selection of vinyl, and musical decor to embrace the Nashville vibe. Large windows let in plenty of natural light, offering a lovely courtyard view.

✦ Workspace & Entertainment: The living area has a dedicated workspace with a desk and chair, perfect for remote work. The living room also offers a board and card game collection for some indoor fun.

✦ Outdoor & Extra Features: Step out and relax on your private patio with direct access to the fantastic courtyard. The building provides access to shared amenities like a BBQ grill, outdoor dining areas, and pavilions that can be reserved for special gatherings. For fitness enthusiasts, the building’s shared gym includes ellipticals, free weights, and more.

NOTE:  The availability of amenities may vary and is not guaranteed. Property management reserves the right to alter, amend, modify, or temporarily close amenities as deemed necessary, in its sole and reasonable discretion.

Other things to note
▶ To avoid insects and mold, please keep all windows and doors closed. Leaving them open while the AC is on could result in a fee up to $500. For your comfort, we recommend setting the AC between 72°F, but please adjust as needed.

▶ This house is privately owned and does not include maid service or daily cleanings. The cleaning fee covers a final cleaning (after your checkout).

▶ Unregistered guests are not permitted.

▶ No pets are allowed. Violations will result in a $1000 fine.

▶ No smoking is permitted within the unit. Guests must be at least 15 feet away from the building when smoking. Violations will result in a $250 fine for each occurrence.

▶ No glass is allowed at the pool or on balconies surrounding the pool. Violations may result in fines up to $50,000.

▶ Trash cannot be left outside the door. Please keep it within the unit or use the garbage chute near apartment unit x56 on your floor. A $25 fine will be applied per incident.

▶ Guests in violation of community rules will be asked to leave immediately and charged a $1000 violation fee. This includes, but is not limited to, nuisance complaints, illegal activity, and behaviors that interfere with the safety or enjoyment of other residents.

▶ No parties are allowed. Significant noise or playing loud music after 9:00 PM will result in you being asked to leave without a refund for the remaining time of your reservation.

▶ Check-in is at/after 4:00 PM, and check-out time is no later than 11:00 AM. Late check-out without prior approval will result in a $100 per hour fee for each hour after 11:00 AM.

▶ During check-in, you will find towels, linens, shampoo, conditioner, soap, and toilet paper provided for your convenience. Other amenities are also available in the unit. Please contact us with any questions. 

▶ Moving furniture within the apartment is not permitted except to access the sofa sleeper. Violations of this rule may result in a $250 penalty.

From the host
Located in the heart of downtown Nashville, this stylish apartment offers guests a prime location to experience the city's vibrant culture and music scene. Located in a modern building in The Gulch area, you'll find yourself steps away from some of the best restaurants, boutiques, and nightlife Nashville offers. Visitors can walk to Broadway’s iconic honky-tonks, live music venues, and the renowned Country Music Hall of Fame. For food lovers, options abound with award-winning dining spots nearby, including Biscuit Love, Hattie B’s Hot Chicken, and The 404 Kitchen.

Beyond its food and entertainment, the neighborhood offers easy access to green spaces such as Riverfront Park and Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park, ideal for a morning jog or a leisurely afternoon. The area is also close to local markets and coffee shops, perfect for a relaxing start to the day. With its rich cultural history, lively atmosphere, and convenience, this location truly captures what Nashville offers.
