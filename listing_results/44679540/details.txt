=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to XENOS - your loft-style downtown Nashville home steps from Broadway, Printer's Alley & Music City's best live entertainment.

- Sleeps 8 | 2 bedrooms | 6 beds | 2.5 baths
- City skyline views & private entrance
- Full kitchen w/ coffee maker, blender & wine glasses
- 100% blackout bedrooms w/ en suite baths
- Self check-in via smart lock - arrive anytime
- Pet-friendly & walking distance to the Ryman

The space
Step off the elevator and into the pulse of Music City - this loft-style condo on 3rd Avenue North puts you squarely between Broadway's neon glow and the storied shimmer of Printer's Alley. Honky tonks, rooftop bars, and iconic venues are all within walking distance, while the condo itself gives you the breathing room to come home, decompress, and do it all over again.

The space
Spread across two levels, the condo offers an airy, open feel that belies its downtown address. Upstairs, the parlor and living area are furnished with recently upgraded pieces, soft lighting, and a TV - the kind of space where the group naturally gathers after a night out on Broadway. Downstairs, the sleeping areas offer quiet retreat with 100% blackout for deep, uninterrupted rest. A dedicated workspace is available for anyone who needs to stay connected, and ceiling fans, air conditioning, and heating keep every season comfortable.

Sleeping arrangements
The condo sleeps 8 across 2 bedrooms with 6 beds. Both bedrooms feature en suite bathrooms for restful privacy, and the fully blacked-out rooms ensure you can sleep in as late as Nashville lets you.

Kitchen & dining
The kitchen is stocked and ready - refrigerator, freezer, stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, toaster, blender, baking sheet, and cooking basics are all on hand. A coffee maker keeps mornings easy, and wine glasses are there for evenings in. Pull up a chair at the dining table for a home-cooked meal or a late-night snack before the next round.

Bathrooms
Two en suite baths keep the bedrooms self-contained, and a half bath sits conveniently off the main living area. Towels, shampoo, conditioner, body soap, and cleaning products are provided.

Amenities & comfort
Essentials, bed linens, extra pillows and blankets, hangers, clothing storage, an iron, and a hair dryer are all included. The condo has Wi-Fi throughout, a dedicated workspace, a TV, and a private entrance - no shared lobbies, just straight in from the street. Smoke alarm, carbon monoxide alarm, and a fire extinguisher are in place, and exterior security cameras cover common areas only (not inside units). Paid parking is available off premises.

Check-in
Self check-in is handled via a smart lock - no coordination needed. Arrive on your own schedule, drop your bags, and head straight to Broadway.

Location
From 3rd Avenue North, the walk to Broadway's music venues takes minutes. The Ryman Auditorium, Bridgestone Arena, and the Cumberland River waterfront are all nearby on foot. Printer's Alley is practically at your door. East Nashville, the Gulch, and West Nashville are a short ride when you're ready to venture further.

Good to know
This is a pet-friendly condo - assistance animals are always welcome. Long-term stays of 28 days or more are also available. Parking is paid and located off premises.

Note
Parking is NOT provided. There are several parking garages and lots located on the block for convenient parking. ($20-40+ per 24 hour period). We recommend using the city's public transportation and ride sharing with visiting.
