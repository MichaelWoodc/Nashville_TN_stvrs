=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Relax in this elegant Victorian style, home away from home, in the Historic Greenwood Neighborhood in East Nashville, located a block away from popular local restaurants like The Pharmacy Burger Parlor & Beer Garden, "Food Network's Best Restaurants In Nashville" Lyra Modern Middle Eastern Cuisine and "Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives" featured Mexican restaurant: Mas Tacos and ONLY 3 miles from downtown. The best part, each bedroom has its own full bathroom and separate temperature controls!

The space
Located in Historic Greenwood Neighborhood in East Nashville, drive up to this charming home with plenty of street parking available for guests. Ascend the stairs to the front porch where a bistro table for two and a bench provide a spot to enjoy your morning coffee.

Enter the home through the front door into the foyer where French doors to the left lead to the living room with comfortable white couches surrounding a 55” Roku flat screen TV with YouTube TV.

Adjacent to the living room is an office, with a foosball table and workstation for those who need to conduct business!

Backtrack through the living room, and you’ll find the kitchen, with a bar-height table that seats eight. Prepare a home cooked meal on stainless steel appliances including an electric stove/oven and microwave. Out of eye sight but right around the corner from the kitchen is the washer and dryer.

Return to the foyer and across from the front door you will enter the only first floor bedroom. Just to the right of the bedroom door is the staircase leading up to the other three spacious bedrooms. Each bedroom suite has a similar layout and amenities, so no need to squabble over who gets the master!

Every bedroom in the house is furnished with a queen-size bed, individual temperature controls, and a full bathroom with a stand-up shower. An added bonus is each room has a vanity area with an extra sink for your convenience.
