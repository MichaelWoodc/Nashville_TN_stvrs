=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Located in East Nashville - Inglewood on a 10 MPH street, this 1930s-era cottage includes 3 true bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Our area was hailed “a new food star” by the NYtimes. You can bike or walk to Riverside Village, for coffee at Dose, enjoy a sandwich at Mitchell’s Deli, or have drinks at the Village Pub. The cottage has a front yard and porch, with off-street parking for two vehicles, maximum.

The space
This cottage built in the 1930s has three true bedrooms and two bathrooms. It sleeps up to 6. The kitchen and gathering room are open plan. One bathroom is en suite and the 2nd bathroom is shared with the open plan space and two bedrooms.
Quirks to note: The cottage shares its longest wall with a private suite where up to two other guests may be staying. Expect noise. 
Parking is shared on a grassy, gravel drive. If it’s raining you may encounter wet grass.
It’s furnished in a bohemian style, with vintage and antique pieces, and low to the floor beds. 
Each bedroom has a low metal queen bed frame, firm memory foam mattress, vintage chair, closet with hangers and a luggage rack.
In the gathering room, you’ll find an original, non-functional fireplace with a 40-inch TV mounted on the vintage mantle. This is the only TV in the cottage.

Guest access
Guests can enjoy the front yard and front porch, which includes a bench, two chairs, and a bistro table. 
One bedroom includes an en suite bathroom with a shower, while the other two bedrooms share a second bathroom with a shower/tub combo. Both bathrooms come stocked with bath products for your convenience. 
A Nespresso machine and coffee pods are available to help you start your day. 
Parking for up to two standard vehicles is available on the grassy gravel driveway, shared with suite guests. There is no street parking.

Other things to note
Parking is available on a grassy, gravel driveway on the property. Cottage guests are limited to a maximum of two standard-sized vehicles, while suite guests may have one vehicle. Please note there is no street parking available.
The kitchen is equipped with essentials, including pots, pans, baking sheets, a small slow cooker, and more. The range and oven are electric.
We provide towels for outdoor use. Additionally, there are melamine plates, a picnic basket, and two picnic blankets available for outdoor activities. 
A Baby Bjorn portable crib and crib sheets are also available upon request.
