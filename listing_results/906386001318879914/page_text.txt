Enjoy a stylish experience at this centrally-located condo. This unit is located in the refurbished 1865 Building only 1.5 miles from Broadway! The 1865 features a private speakeasy club on the basement level of the main building. As our guest, you will have full access to the 1865 Club which features a full bar, craft cocktails, light food & music! 25% off for staying with us! The 1865 also features a pool & grill area, rooftop patio, private gym and ample parking all within the gated property.

The space
This 2 bedroom 1 bathroom unit is large enough to sleep 6 guests comfortable. Each bedroom has a Queen size bed and the Living Room has a super comfortable pull out sofa. The open floor plan of the condo allows for guests to spread out during their stay.

Guest access
Guests have access to the entire 1865 property. The 4th floor rooftop deck, gym & pool/grill area are shared with other guests in the building. In the basement you will find a stylish, yet cozy, speakeasy that is open to our guests during their stay.
