=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to The Dogwood Cottage—a charming 1899 folk Victorian home in the heart of East Nashville’s Greenwood neighborhood. With vintage charm, artistic flair, and a walkable location surrounded by restaurants, bars, and live music venues, this is the ideal retreat for couples looking to explore Nashville beyond Broadway.

The space
🏡 The Space

Dark wood tones, gold accents, and warm lighting create a cozy and inviting atmosphere. You’ll find beautiful hardwood floors in the bedroom and living room, a decorative fireplace, and dogwood-themed décor throughout—an homage to our own love story and way of living in community.

🌆 Location & Local Flavor
The house is great and all, but it's all about the location. 
East Nashville is where locals live and creatives thrive. While just a 12 minute drive from downtown and Broadway, this side of the city offers a more sophisticated, artistic vibe. Within walking distance, you'll find:

- Coffee Shops: Hanna Bee Coffee, Barista Parlor, Surefire Coffee, etc.

- Brunch: Hearts, The Nashville Biscuit House, etc.

- Restaurants: Mas Tacos (2 minutes walk), The Pharmacy, Lyra, Peninsula, etc. 

- Cocktail Bars: The Coral Club, The Amsterdamian, Chopper, etc.

- Breweries: Tailgate Brewery, Smith & Lentz, etc.

- Shopping: Relik Vintage (vintage clothes), The Groove (record store), The Bookshop (...book store), etc

- Live Music Venues: The Basement East, the 5 Spot, Jane’s Hideaway, Skinny Dennis

- Five Points: Huge variety of vintage shops, music venues, bars (15-minute walk)

- Groceries: Kroger and Aldi (10 minute walk), Publix (15 minute walk)

- Parks & Recreation: Framework Sauna and Cold Plunge (3 minutes), Cleveland Park (20 minutes), YMCA (30 minute walk), Shelby Park (45 minutes), etc.

Our house is also located in one of the few places in Nashville where taking public transportation is actually realistic. There is a bus stop less than 5 minutes walk from the house, and the city is investing in more bus infrastructure every day.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the house, yard, and patio—except for a small locked owner’s closet in the bedroom and a shed in the backyard. This is our permanent residence when guests are not on-site. 

Perfect for couples, solo travelers, or friends seeking a peaceful and inspiring space in a vibrant, walkable neighborhood. Ideal for food lovers, music fans, or creatives exploring Nashville’s local culture.

Other things to note
Decorative Fireplace Only: The fireplace is original to the home and adds a lovely ambiance, but it is non-functioning and for decoration only.

Historic Home Quirks: Built in 1899, the Dogwood Cottage is full of character. You may notice some charming creaks in the floors and occasional asymmetry—part of the historic charm!

Compact Comfort: At 600 sq ft, our home is cozy and thoughtfully arranged, best suited for 1–2 guests or couples, with the option of 2 additional guests using the pull-out couch.

Owner’s Areas: The shed in the backyard and a small locked closet in the bedroom are for owner use only. All other spaces are fully accessible.

Laundry Note: The in-unit washer/dryer is a European-style combo unit. It’s perfect for light loads during your stay but may take longer than traditional machines.

No Pets: We’re unable to accommodate pets at The Dogwood Cottage due to a severe dog allergy. To protect future guests and maintain a safe environment, a $500 deep-cleaning fee will be charged if any pets are brought into the home. Thanks so much for understanding and helping us keep the space allergy-safe!

Quiet Neighborhood: Our home sits on a peaceful, residential block in a mostly older community. We kindly ask that guests be respectful of quiet hours, especially in the evenings.

Radon Safety: Radon is a common problem in Nashville. We’ve had our home tested for radon and results came back within safe levels. Reports are available upon request.

Security Cameras: For the safety and peace of mind of our guests and property, we have three outdoor security cameras—one at the front entrance, one at the back entrance, and one covering the shed. These are in place for exterior security only and do not monitor any interior or private guest spaces.

We’ve poured love and intention into this space and hope it brings you as much joy as it brings us. Welcome to your East Nashville home away from home.
