=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This 2300sqft house is our pride and joy! We spent over a year remodeling and piecing together all the beautiful details of this 4 bed, 3 bath home. All mattresses are luxury memory foam, high end linens, granite countertops, soft close kitchen cabinets and lots of space to lounge, cook out and enjoy time with friends or family.

Only four miles from the heart of downtown Nashville yet nestled into a quiet cul de sac in a safe neighborhood. Uber/Lyft to town approx $8-13! We are Super Hosts!

The space
Our house is very accessible friendly. There is only one step from the driveway to the front door and no steps on the first floor where there are 3 bedrooms and 2 of the full bathrooms each with a bathtub.

The house is a non-smoking house but cigarette butt receptacles are located on the porch outside. This is a pet free home. 

The main floor has beautiful stamped tile floors and original hardwoods in the front part of the house, new hardwoods in the living room and new carpet in the back bedroom.

The second floor is accessible through the main house or via the second entrance on the side of the house. The second floor is an efficiency apartment with sink, dishwasher, microwave, electric tea kettle and french press coffee maker.  There is a daybed with a trundle (two twins) in the living area, a table with four chairs for dining and a queen bed in the bedroom. The 3rd full bath with large tiled shower is in the apartment. The apartment has hardwood floors and new carpet in the bedroom.

Guest access
Guests are renting the whole home. There is one room and a couple small closets with personal items stored which we ask that you not bother.

Other things to note
Our property is properly permitted through the Nashville Codes department.

We do want to make you aware that we have seasonal issues with insects here in Nashville. We try to stay ahead of the bugs but if you may see lady bugs, box elder bugs, ants, etc depending on the season. Please reach out and let us know if insects are an issue inside the house.
