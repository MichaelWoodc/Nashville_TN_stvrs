=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Wonderwood, your Nashville retro-dreamscape! We've curated a very stylish and special home, where every room is uniquely themed to transport you to another exciting dimension.

This artfully designed home is frequently used for music video shoots. Your stay will put you in the middle of the Nashville music scene!

*Seven minutes by car to Broadway
*Ten minutes by car to the Gulch, Music Row, and Nissan Stadium
*Fifteen minutes by car to BNA airport

The space
We are located in Nashville's historic Jefferson Street music district, once home to Jimi Hendrix, who lived and played a few blocks away. We have tried to honor the musical spirit of the neighborhood by filling this house with instruments, including an upright piano, acoustic guitar, and record player with a small vintage record collection. Feel free to make some music during your stay - you're in Nashville, after all!

Our midcentury-style living room offers plenty of seating around an electric fireplace, as well as a smart TV. We've also supplied a bluetooth karaoke machine, so that you can warm up for your Nashville debut performance on Broadway.

This house features a spacious, 50's diner-style kitchen, with all updated appliances, milkshake maker, bar seating, mini jukebox, and remote controlled party lights (this kitchen is for dancing!).

Our queen bedroom, the “Peace & Love Room”, is 1960’s inspired, and features a cozy queen with romantic lighting. Enjoy the large closet, makeup vanity desk, and wall-mounted smart TV. This room also hosts an 8-track player with original 8-track cassettes for all you retro music geeks. This room also offers access to the large, fenced backyard.

In our double bedroom, “That ‘70s Room”, you will find two full-size beds, along with an assortment of vintage items including a lava lamp, disco ball, shag rugs, bead curtain, and a working VCR player, with a collection of 70's era VHS tapes (Grease fans??).

Both bedrooms are outfitted with stylish blackout curtains, in case you need to sleep in.

The bathroom is equipped with a bathtub/shower, fully stocked with shampoo, body wash, and conditioner. We also provide a hairdryer, as well as various hygiene products. In the hallway, there is a washer/dryer, an iron, and an ironing board. Every room has a charging port, with cables for all devices.

This home is equipped with three outdoor security cameras, a keyless entry system, and a driveway for off-street parking.

Guest access
Guests have access to the entire house, as well as the back yard.
