=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to 'The Grand Duke'. This gated townhome community named ‘The Duke’ is just minutes from Downtown Nashville! It’s a spacious 2 bedroom, end unit featuring a open living room that flows into a stunning kitchen & island equipped with all the cooking utensils & appliances you could need. 
On the 2nd and 3rd floors you will find 2 spacious en-suite bedrooms & bathrooms plus a laundry room. There is also a great balcony to sit out and enjoy a morning coffee or an evening glass of wine.

The space
Step inside to a warm and inviting living room with plenty of natural light and comfortable seating. A few steps away, you’ll find the kitchen with modern appliances and a large island with plenty of space to prep your meals and hang out. The master bedroom is located on the second floor, with two queen sized beds, a large bathroom with a dual sink, and a walk-in closet. Up the stairs just a little further you’ll find a secondary bedroom featuring a smart TV, two additional queen beds, a walk-in closet & another full bathroom. Just around the corner is a full sized balcony with plenty of seating to enjoy your morning, afternoon and evenings outside. Don't forget that you can grill on the front porch if you desire too!!
