Off the living room is the bedroom that offers a queen size bed with a walk-in closet and full bathroom. There are hardwood floors throughout.

The open floor plan in the kitchen and living room allows for utility and comfort. The kitchen has all the appliances you'll need - sink, range, refrigerator (with ice maker), dishwasher and microwave. The space is perfect for 2 guests (queen size bed in the bedroom) but with the queen air mattress, an additional 1 or 2 guests can be accommodated.
