Welcome to Moore! Experience modern comfort in this 3BR, 2.5BA Nashville Weho home for 10. Walk to great coffee, the fairgrounds, Geodis Park - or take a quick Uber to Broadway, just 7 min away. A great deck with city views awaits plus open living and dining concept and huge windows for natural light throughout the home! Great for bachelor / bachelorette trips, group hangs, or family gatherings.

The space
Discover the pinnacle of modern comfort in this inviting 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom Nashville haven, where up to 10 guests can bask in luxury. With upscale amenities, including a stylish kitchen, elegant bedrooms, and a scenic deck, your stay promises an unforgettable experience. Stroll to the Fairgrounds, explore galleries, and savor delectable cuisine at nearby restaurants. Situated just a stone's throw from downtown, local universities, and renowned music venues, your urban adventure awaits. Enjoy hassle-free parking, and make this charming retreat your Nashville home-away-from-home.



⭐Highlights -  

- Awesome proximity to Broadway - 7 min uber ride, 2 miles away. Plus, local faves like The Gulch, 12 South, and Music Row are all less than 10 min away 

 - Cool rooftop deck with shade canopy  

- Great open living and dining concept.  

- Private fenced in yard  

- Fully equipped kitchen with complimentary coffee from Nashville favorite, The Well.  

- All bathrooms are equipped with premium full-sized body wash, hand soap, and shampoo and conditioner

- 3 spacious bedrooms - 1 queen bedroom, 1 queen over queen bunk bed, and a 2 queen bedroom. Sleeps 10!  



- Neighborhood: In the Wedgewood-Houston area, you're just a 10-minute walk to the Nashville Flea Market and Fairgrounds and 5 minutes to a series of art galleries, restaurants, and bars. Try a fancy cocktail at Bastion and pick up some delicious treats at Dozen Bakery. Brush up on your history and drive a few miles to see a replica of the Parthenon. To catch some live music, it's only 2 miles to downtown, 6 miles to the famous Bluebird Café, 2 to the Ryman Auditorium, and 10 to the infamous Grand Ole Opry.
