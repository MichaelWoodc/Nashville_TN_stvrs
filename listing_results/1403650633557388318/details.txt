=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Located in the heart of historic Lockeland Springs, our private guesthouse is a peaceful urban retreat and a perfect base for exploring wonderful, walkable East Nashville and the rest of the city. Stay just two blocks from 5 Points and only a few minutes on foot from some of the best restaurants, bars, and shops this side of the river. Soak up the neighborhood vibes and live like a local for a few days, knowing all the happenings downtown are less than a 10-minute ride away.

The space
The guesthouse is above our detached garage and behind our main residence. It has a separate ground level entrance with bench seating, storage for shoes and coats, and a laundry closet with a washer and dryer for guest use. Upstairs is the main living area, featuring a full kitchen with modern appliances, a primary bedroom with a California king size bed, a Casper memory foam mattress and organic Avocado topper, a bathroom with heated floors and a soaking tub, and an additional nook with a small desk, a swing chair, and a double bed, offering a little extra space for those traveling with kids.

We think our place is a great fit for a couple or a family with a kid or two. It can accommodate up to six people who really like each other, but know that it will be a very cozy stay!

And most importantly...

We LOVE our neighborhood and want you to love it too. We ask that you enjoy our little piece of East Nashville while also respecting our family, our property, and especially our neighbors. We cannot host parties or events. If you want to have visitors who are not on your reservation, please reach out to chat with us about it beforehand. Be sure to read and follow all posted rules about noise and quiet hours. 

Lastly, and this is a big one for us, please be aware that we do not allow smoking of anything, anywhere on the property, including the yard. No judgment, but if smoking is important to you, please do book elsewhere.

Guest access
Guests have exclusive use of the guesthouse and are also welcome to enjoy the backyard and deck, which is shared with our family. Guests do not have access to the garage below the guesthouse.
