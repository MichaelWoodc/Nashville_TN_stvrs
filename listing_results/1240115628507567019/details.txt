=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to The Smokeshow in Downtown Nashville!

Step into modern luxury at The Smokeshow, a brand-new 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom gem, located directly on Music Row, nestled in the heart of vibrant Nashville. This contemporary retreat offers the perfect blend of style and comfort, ensuring a memorable stay in Music City. Just minutes to Lower Broadway and all the amazing fun Nashville has to offer. Free on-site parking, community gym, and pool open during spring/summer. You’re going to love it here!

The space
The Smokeshow is an Art Deco–inspired condo designed with the glamour of a vintage cocktail lounge in mind. Rich textures, bold accents, and moody lighting create a stylish retreat that feels equal parts upscale and playful. Whether you’re mixing drinks before a night out or winding down after exploring the city in front of the fireplace, it’s not just a place to stay… it’s part of the experience.

Highlights:
- Spacious Bedrooms: Unwind in the plush King bed in the primary bedroom, and let the Queen over Queen bunk in the second bedroom provide a cozy yet spacious sleeping arrangement for family or friends. Additional sleeping space available in the living room, which offers a sleeper sofa. We can comfortably sleep up to 8 guests. 
- Elegant Bathrooms: Enjoy the convenience of two beautifully appointed bathrooms, designed with modern fixtures and finishes. 
- Modern Kitchen: Cook in the fully equipped kitchen featuring sleek appliances and all the essentials needed for preparing meals at home (no cooking oil provided)
- Outdoor Oasis: Relax and take in the fresh air on your private deck, an ideal spot for morning coffee or evening cocktails. Enjoy a shared rooftop in the Crescendo Building with views of the skyline, indoor dining spaces, pool tables, shared kitchen, gym, and more.
- Onsite pool open daily from 8am-8pm May-Oct 31st.

Additional Perks:
- New Construction: Be among the first to experience the stylish design and contemporary amenities of this newly built home. 
- Prime Location: Situated directly on Music Row in a vibrant neighborhood, you're just moments away from Nashville’s best dining, entertainment, and cultural attractions. Please note as Nashville continues to grow, nearby construction is common and may be present during your stay.
- Parking: You’ll have access to free onsite parking for one car in the gated garage, available on a first-come, first-served basis. If the garage is full, there’s additional reduced-fee parking just behind the building.

The Smokeshow is conveniently located on Music Row, in the heart of all of the recording studios, providing easy access to a variety of top attractions & a real possibility of seeing your favorite country artist.
