Welcome to The Painted Muse, a vibrant 3 bedroom, 3.5 bathroom retreat in East Nashville near Inglewood, the city's most exciting neighborhood for food, nightlife, and local art. 

This creative, modern home features a private rooftop hot tub, spacious entertaining areas, and custom murals throughout from acclaimed local artist, Folek, that honor feminine power, beauty, and resilience. 

Perfect for bachelorettes and group getaways, it offers comfort, inspiration, and style in the Music City.

The space
Step inside a home designed to spark connection and creativity. Two of the three bedrooms feature en suite full bathrooms, providing privacy and convenience for your group. The open layout includes a fully equipped kitchen, sunny dining area, and a cozy living room perfect for lounging or getting ready for a night out. Throughout the home, custom murals by Folek bring bold color and meaning, creating a space that feels both stylish and empowering.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the home, including the private rooftop deck and hot tub, all indoor spaces, and a shared community garden area. Free parking is available for two vehicles in the attached garage, with additional free street parking directly in front of the home. Entry is self check in via smart lock for ease and privacy.

Other things to note
The property spans multiple levels and requires stair access. 

Parties are not allowed on the premises. We want you to enjoy yourself and have a great time, but we ask that you limit total occupancy to 15 people and respect our neighbors by keeping noise levels down, especially during quiet hours between 10 PM and 8 AM. Smoking and pets are also prohibited on the premises.

The home is equipped with exterior security cameras for guest safety and Minut noise monitoring devices (no audio recording) to support a peaceful environment. 

Come make memories, celebrate in style, and experience Nashville like a local with all the comfort, creativity, and charm of The Painted Muse.

From the host
Just a stone's throw from Downtown Nashville, East Nashville is a vibrant neighborhood that pulses with unique energy. Its artistic ambiance is evident in colorful murals, local boutiques, and numerous galleries. This creative spirit extends to the diverse culinary scene, offering everything from food trucks to upscale dining.

Live music is ingrained in East Nashville's identity, with venues showcasing an array of genres. Experience the local music scene that defines Nashville's soul. The neighborhood's proximity to Downtown provides easy access to iconic attractions like Broadway's honky-tonks and the Country Music Hall of Fame.

Returning to East Nashville after a day downtown feels like a retreat into a welcoming community. Whether you're a traveler seeking authenticity or a local looking to rediscover the city, East Nashville offers a blend of culture, character, and a true slice of Music City's essence.
