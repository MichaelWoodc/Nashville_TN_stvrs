Where you’ll be
East Nashville, Nashville
Songwriter shows, colorful murals, and renovated bungalows shape this creative district, with casual bars, cafes, and local shops.
From the host
Our home is located in the highly desirable Eastwood neighborhood of East Nashville, known for its walkability, trendy restaurants, local coffee shops, breweries, and nightlife. No car needed—we walk everywhere! Downtown Nashville and Broadway honky tonks are just a 7–10 minute Uber or Lyft ride away.

Morning coffee & cafes (walkable): Ugly Mugs, Frothy Monkey, Cafe Roze, Barista Parlor, Surefire, Elegy, OSA

Restaurants & local eats nearby:

Cafe Roze – all-day spot for breakfast, lunch, and dinner

Fat Belly Deli – casual sandwiches and lunch

East Side Banh Mi – popular Vietnamese banh mi sandwiches

Hearts – Aussie-inspired breakfast & lunch

Five Points Pizza – East Nashville’s favorite pizza shop

Albrieje– authentic tacos

Maiz de La Vida – taco truck outside Chopper Tiki Bar

Dreamburger – burger truck at Vinyl Tap

Joyland – elevated fast food, burgers, and fries

Two Ten Jack – ramen and Japanese pub food

Bars & nightlife within walking distance:

Vinyl Tap – bar with records and live music vibes

Schulman’s- our local “cheers”

Tailgate Brewery – local craft beer

Chopper Tiki Bar – tropical cocktails with a taco truck outside

Skinny Dennis: Honky Tonk

Sweets & treats: Jeni’s Ice Cream 1/5 mile away

Groceries: Publix and Kroger, both less than 0.6 miles
