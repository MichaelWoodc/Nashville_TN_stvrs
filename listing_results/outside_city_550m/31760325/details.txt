=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
A quiet country retreat awaits you! A reasonable distance to Nashville, malls, historic sites. Middle Tennessee offers many beautiful hiking, waterfall day trips as well.  Come to relax, come to see sites, come to enjoy the outdoors. We look forward to meeting you!

The space
We are 10 minutes from Providence Mall in Mt. Juliet, and 30 - 40 minutes to Broadway in Nashville. PLEASE NOTE we live in the country... it’s country roads to our place, some of which are narrow and winding. We live on a VERY quiet road that would be great for a walk or jog, but we are NOT close enough to shops/restaurants to walk... you must drive or Uber. If you’re looking for city life and convenience, this might not be the place for you. 

The ABNB itself sits on a quiet 14 acres. A patio facing a wooded backyard is right outside your door for you to enjoy. 

We are an easy 15 minute drive to Baker’s School of Aeronautics in Lebanon, and many guests who have attended the school say they love our place because it’s so peaceful and quiet for their studies. 

AGAIN, please note that we are in the country, yet quite close to Mt Juliet (4 miles) and all it offers. You will need transportation for any place you wish to go. 

This is a private suite above another rental unit; this is a second floor unit. There is a MAXIMUM of 2 people in this unit. You have a queen bed, living area with tv, private bathroom and spacious closet all within the private entrance unit. Parking right outside the unit is in our driveway and very safe.
