=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This charming farmhouse-style tiny home is nestled on 12 secluded acres, with a serene lake directly across the street. Ideal for hunting and fishing, it offers the perfect escape into nature. Located just 30 minutes from downtown Nashville, and only 15-20 minutes from nearby cities, you’ll have easy access to all the essentials.

The space
Escape to a charming tiny farmhouse nestled on 12 secluded acres, offering peace and tranquility just steps away from a serene lake. Surrounded by lush wildlife, this tranquil retreat invites you to unwind, whether you’re enjoying the view of the lake, fishing, or simply soaking in the natural beauty around you.

Ideal for those seeking a peaceful getaway, the property offers ample space to explore, relax, and recharge. The cozy farmhouse is designed for comfort, blending southern charm with modern amenities. With nature at your doorstep, it’s the perfect setting to disconnect from the world and embrace the calming presence of the outdoors.

Guest access
Guests at your Airbnb have convenient access to the Lamar Hill Access Area, located just down the road. This area offers excellent opportunities for outdoor activities, such as hiking, fishing, and enjoying the scenic views. It’s a short drive away, providing a peaceful spot to immerse yourself in nature while staying close to the comfort of the farmhouse.
