=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===

Rest & relax among the trees, on the spacious deck, equipped with a fire pit, or under the treehouse where you can enjoy outdoor dining or just chill in the hammock. Spacious shower & heavenly mattress!

The space
NO SHARED SPACES! NOT in a neighborhood & SECLUDED!! This treehouse doesn’t seem very tiny @ all! The furnishings (vanity, cabinets, tables, shelves & kitchenette cabinet) are all custom-made. This labor of love took 7 months & we’re so excited to offer this unique treehouse so others can enjoy the peace & tranquility....closest neighbors are a 1/2 a mile down the gravel drive! Nectar mattress on the queen bed is heavenly! There is a pull-out sofa so 4 people can sleep comfortably. Full-sized shampoo, conditioner & body washes are provided....the high quality stuff too, not the off-brands! SO many toiletries are made available to our guests, ie: disposable razors, body puffs, lotions, toothbrush & other oral hygiene needs. There will be a variety of snacks including breakfast items for guests’ enjoyment as well as a Keurig coffee pot with lots of varieties of K-cups, sweeteners and creamer! Kitchenette includes 2 burner stove top, microwave, InstantPot, Air Fryer & toaster. Don’t be surprised if you see wild turkey, deer & you’ll definitely hear coyotes howling in the evening! It’s a perfect writer’s retreat, couple’s get-away or family backwoods adventure!
