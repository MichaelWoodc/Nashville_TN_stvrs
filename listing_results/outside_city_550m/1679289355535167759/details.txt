The space
Tucked away on a peaceful acre in Mt. Juliet, just east of Nashville, this private guesthouse offers the perfect blend of relaxation, space, and convenience. Ideally situated between the lakes and only minutes from the Nashville International airport, you’ll enjoy easy access to local restaurants, shopping, entertainment, and the best of lake life—while still having a quiet retreat to come home to.

Step outside and enjoy your own fully fenced backyard oasis. The saltwater pool—complete with an automatic cover and pool toys—is perfect for sunny afternoons or quiet evening swims. Surrounded by mature greenery and set in a quiet neighborhood, the property feels secluded without sacrificing accessibility.

Inside, the home is thoughtfully designed for both comfort and connection. The spacious primary bedroom features a king bed and connects to a large Jack-and-Jill bathroom. The open-concept living area flows seamlessly into a versatile game space, where you can enjoy a pool table that easily converts into air hockey or ping pong—perfect for all ages.

You’ll also find a cozy under-the-stairs nook with a twin bed—ideal for reading, relaxing, or a fun sleeping spot for kids (and kids at heart). A separate workout room off the kitchen provides space to stay active during your stay and can accommodate an additional twin mattress if needed.

The full kitchen is stocked and ready for everything from morning coffee to evening cocktails, making it easy to settle in and feel at home.

Whether you’re here for a weekend getaway, a family trip, or a Nashville adventure, this Mt. Juliet guesthouse offers a quiet, spacious place to land—with just the right mix of comfort, fun, and location.
