Our Lake house is tucked into a quiet cove in the esteemed Cages Bend area of Hendersonville. TN. Wake up to the natural beauty of life on Old Hickory Lake each morning and spend your time enjoying all this property has to offer.

The space
We are very interested in renting to Families on Vacation, Groups of mature couples and friends who are respectful and responsible and/or anyone else who will be respectful of our house rules. 

This is not a Party House! We do not allow bachelor or bachelorette parties. No loud music, excessive noise or unruly behavior. This is not the rental for those activities. 

We wish to cater to mature, respectful and responsible groups of guests. Please inquire and tell us about your yourself, the other people in your group and your purpose for renting our Lake House.
