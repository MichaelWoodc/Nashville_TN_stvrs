The space
Step into a beautiful, peaceful home where guests say they “felt right at home the moment they walked through the door.” This charming and spacious farmhouse-style retreat is designed for families, group trips, sports tournaments, and anyone who values comfort, space, and a quiet setting—without sacrificing convenience.

Out back, unwind in a large fenced yard with peaceful views of horses grazing, 2 rear porch swings, 4 rocking chairs on a covered wrap around country porch, and fresh Tennessee air. Many guests call the backyard a “definite plus” and one of their favorite parts of the stay—especially for kids and you're gathering for a family trip, a friend's retreat, or a relaxing escape with your pets, this farmhouse offers everything you need for a comfortable and memorable stay! Relax in our peaceful, clean, comfortable farmhouse while enjoying the views of a stable and horses in your backyard! 

It has a full kitchen stocked with everything you'll need to create home cooked meals, including a crock pot! Dining seating is bountiful, with a formal dining room that seats 6-8 comfortably, and second less formal table to seat 4 at the breakfast nook, and 2 bar stools to dine at the kitchen bar!

The large living room has a 60 inch Roku TV, sofa, 2 leather lazy-boy recliners, and a (seasonal) gas fireplace! There are a few live plants sprinkled around the home, and a photo area. Also downstairs is a half bath and one of the twin roll away beds, bedding included. The other living space is upstairs in the open loft area between both upstairs bedrooms, and features a twin daybed with a trundle for an additional guest. 

The primary bedroom downstairs features a king bed, a personal tv, and with plenty of storage space found in 2 dressers and bedside tables. The spacious primary bath has a jacuzzi tub, separate shower, his and hers sinks, and a private walk in closet that accommodates 2 stackable LG washers/dryers, with space to spare! 

Upstairs you'll find the open loft space described above, and two bedrooms. The other bedroom features two closets (one is locked for airbnb storage, the other has the twin rollaway bed with bedding) a full bed, another tv with dvd player and a few kids movies, 2 dressers, a baby crib, and a swivel accent chair for relaxing mothers.
