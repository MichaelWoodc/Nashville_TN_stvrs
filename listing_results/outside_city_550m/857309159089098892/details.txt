=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Nested in a protected watershed valley, this ranch house (4BR, 3bath) is surrounded by forested hiking trails, meadows and creeks to explore. Next to an old fashioned working well! Optional outside hot tub ($50 fee). No close neighbors. Drive to nearby lake for picnic. Canoes available. Organic farm next door. NO INSIDE PETS. Fenced area for outside pets.

The base price is 4 PERSONS. $40/person/night charge for additional.

The space
Large living area, kitchen, dining area.
Modest house in a very, very large setting. An outdoor spa need not be heated and serves as a pool. 150- and 300- gallon stock tanks may also be used to cool off and refresh during warmer weather. Big East Fork Creek is wonderful to explore and runs across the entrance to the property. Miles of trails begin just outside your door !  Unlimited blackberries during season (Late June to Early July).
Walk to organic farm and the nearby. Bike to Lake Gratitude. Canaan Valley Farms is just across the driveway. Ask Ryan and Chané for a tour.

Guest access
Access to livingroom, dining room, front and back patios, hundreds of acres of meadow and forest. 

Three magnificent trails pass by the Southern Hospitality Ranch. The Short Meadow Trail goes through a pasture then crosses a creek, goes through a hardwood forest and ends at The Fork Inn. The Log Meadow Trail winds through a meadow, crosses a small creek, goes through a young forest parallel to Big East Fork Rd to the four-pole barn. One half mile along the Farm Trail brings you to Center for Sustainable Stewardship's Farm Store. The Governor's Trail starts right behind the Ranch. Trails are maintained by CSS. Donations gladly accepted!
