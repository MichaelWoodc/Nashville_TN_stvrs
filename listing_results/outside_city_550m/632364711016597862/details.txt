Welcome to Northside Cottage

This is a brand new listing for us in mid 2022. Experienced host with thousands of reviews on other properties.

This 1940’s classic Tennessee cottage, nestled on over an 1 acre, was renovated in 2022 and is just 25 mins to downtown Nashville or the airport. Easily grab an Uber to Broadway, enjoy a day out on Old Hickory Lake (just 10 mins away), or visit one of your favorite sporting teams in town while having a great place to call home.

It features beautiful indoor and outdoor spaces for relaxing with family and friends and includes a great selection of sleeping arrangements.

The main level is a large open area including a spacious living room that flows seamlessly into the dining area and stunning kitchen. To the rear there is access to a great sized covered back porch that includes a large sectional couch and additional table seating for 4. To the front is your quintessential Tennessee covered front porch to slowly watch the world pass you by! Two bedrooms and two bathrooms are also located on this main level.

The upstairs features 2 nearly identical bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms and a laundry room. Our upstairs bedrooms are known as our ‘Bunk Rooms’. Both feature 3 full size beds, one of which is stacked in a bunk formation. We do advise that although each of these rooms can accommodate sleeping 6, the upper bunk is designed to fit children only.
