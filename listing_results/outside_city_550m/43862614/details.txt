=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This charming, adorable tiny house sits on a 2 acre property behind a historic log cabin just outside of Nashville, TN. 
 
After a full day of exploring music city, guests can enjoy a relaxing and peaceful evening under the stars away from the city noise. Perfect for a solo artistic retreat or a great getaway with your favorite person!

The space
The tiny house is 168 square feet of glamping serenity nestled in the quiet, peaceful outskirts of Nashville, Tennessee. 

It is a lovely and charming, albeit very tiny experience, and I am excited to share it with you. 

Guests will sleep in a cozy loft with a comfortable full sized mattress (can sleep two). The daybed / sofa downstairs is also just shy of a twin sized mattress and can sleep one person. 

The kitchen offers a mini fridge, microwave, hot plate, and convection toaster oven for cooking simple meals. I provide coffee, tea, and a few simple snacks. There is a french press for coffee and a hot kettle for boiling water. 

The bathroom has a small shower and toilet. Towels and toiletries are provided, and if there's anything you forgot to pack on your trip, don't hesitate to ask! 

Wifi connection is available. The TV is set up for streaming Netflix, Prime Videos, etc.

The outside space has a picnic table and a hammock for lounging. Guests are welcome to cook out on the gas grill, and use the outdoor fire pit. S'mores anyone!? 

I hope you enjoy your tiny house experience. It's the perfect getaway for solo trips, friend trips, or a romantic weekend with your favorite person. 

If you are traveling with a sports rack on your vehicle, you are welcome to take the green kayaks out for the day (as long as they are secured and strapped safely to your vehicle).

Guest access
Guests have access to the back yard space including the fire pit and gas grill. Please enjoy the hammock and outdoor picnic area.
