Tastefully decorated throughout, this cabin will make the perfect getaway for groups wanting to spend some quality time together. You get the best of both worlds being both secluded and yet a short drive to all the entertainment Nashville and Hendersonville has to offer.

On the main level you will find our beautifully appointed kitchen stocked with everything you would need to cook a delicious dinner along with a small dining table and 2 bar stools at the countertop. A large living space featuring cathedral ceilings and a sleek fireplace surrounded by wood accent panelling is the perfect spot to relax in front of the fire while watching tv after a day exploring downtown. Additionally there are two bedrooms on this level, a full bathroom and laundry. If you are traveling with a toddler we have a 'pack n play' onsite for your use.

On the second floor, we have our 3rd bedroom with two full sized beds and an attached en-suite bathroom. There is also a loft area that contains a twin bunk bed along with a pull-out twin trundle beneath, providing 3 extra beds in this space. The loft also has a small couch and TV for the kids to enjoy along with a dedicated work space if anyone needs to hop on a laptop!

In addition to the previously mentioned upgraded outdoor spaces, we have added a variety of outdoor games to enjoy with family and friends such as corn hole, spike ball and kan jam. If the weather cooperates and you'd like to sit outside for a meal or drink then we have 2 outdoor tables and 10 chairs to gather together.

A detailed sleeping layout is as follows:

Bedroom #1 - 1 King size bed plus an additional Twin fold out bed. There is also a TV in this bedroom. (Sleeps up to 3 people in 2 beds).

Bedroom #3 - 2 Full size beds. This bedroom has an en-suite bathroom. (Sleeps up to 4 people in 2 beds).
Loft Area - 3 Twin beds in bunk formation along with a pull-out trundle beneath. (Sleeps up to 3 people in 3 beds).
