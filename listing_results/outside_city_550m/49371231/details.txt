=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This oasis in the trees is waiting to help you escape, renew and rejuvenate! We love to offer our guests not only a great place to stay but an experience they will talk about for years to come. We love providing lots of little extras to help make your time here really special. This is the perfect spot for a romantic getaway, family fun vacation, or solo retreat

The space
This stunning little house has the layout of a tiny house but a lot more space. It is all open with loft sleeping spaces. The bathroom is the only closed off area with a custom barn door. The jacuzzi tub is a great place to relax and the gas tankless water heater will give you all the hot water you could ever want! The kitchen has everything you need to cook anything you desire. Such a cozy and luxurious space to help you take a much needed break from the busyness of life.

Guest access
You have the whole house and surrounding area to yourself. You are welcome to hike our trails, splash in the creek and visit with the chickens, bunnies and cats.

Other things to note
We now have unlimited wifi! We also have a big flatscreen smart TV, DVD player, a lot of DVDs, a record player with a few records, a CD player and a few CDs, decks of cards and some board games and books to help keep you entertained!

We have plenty of firewood and try to always have fire starters and lighters to make it easy to get things going. 

As long as our chickens are laying, we provide fresh eggs to all of our guests.
