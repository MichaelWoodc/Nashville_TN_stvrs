=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This 5 bedroom traditional, log cabin is nestled serenely in the woods less than 30 minutes from downtown Nashville. The home sleeps up to 16 guests!  It features both wonderful indoor and outdoor spaces to gather with friends and family. Perfect environment for relaxing with friends and family in nature but just a short drive to shopping and restaurants.

The space
While outside, you will love the stunning wrap-around porch with many rocking chairs to enjoy a coffee or glass of wine. If relaxation is what you are looking for, then settling into the hot tub at the end of the day will melt those sore muscles away, or if you’d like to rustle up some s’mores with the kids then the large fire pit is just a few steps away. A couple of additional outdoor features include a basketball net, grill and covered rear porte-cache.

Moving inside you’ll find the rustic look of a Smokey Mountain Log Cabin. The kitchen, living and dining areas are all wide open spaces that flow together seamlessly with plenty of places to sit and relax. Additional rooms on the main floor include 2 large games rooms. One contains a pool table and a bar area, while the other features a foosball table, flat screen TV, cards/chess table, darts board, comfy futon and bookshelves with various games you are welcome to use during your stay. The laundry room and a full bathroom are also located on the ground floor.

Upstairs features 5 large bedrooms with a variety of beds to accommodate multiple sleeping arrangements. There are also 2 full bathrooms on this floor. The rooms contain the following bed types:

The master bedroom has a King sleigh bed and in it’s adjoining sitting room there are 2 day bed  trundles with 2 twin mattresses a piece.

Bedroom #2 is extremely large and has 2 Queen Beds

Bedroom #3 is also a very large room and features 2 full-size bunk beds next to each other.

Bedroom #4 is also on the enormous scale as bedroom sizes go and it features a King bed and a comfy couch.

Bedroom #5 is a quaint room with a Queen size bed.

All of our luxury beds and mattresses throughout the home were installed new in July 2021 along with all new linens and towels. In the past, the beds and mattresses had their quality questioned and we were happy to listen to the feedback. We hope you enjoy this wonderful upgrade!

Guest access
Guests will have access to the entire home, except for the basement where our cleaning crew stores their supplies.

Other things to note
The exterior of the cabin is monitored by security cameras. If any guests are found to have been walking on the roof, the security deposit will be forfeited. Yes, it happens! This is for your safety.

Note: The driveway is long and steep. Tennessee weather can be unpredictable. While rare, during snow & ice it has the potential to become impassable. We recommend parking at the end of the driveway to avoid any hazardous situations.
