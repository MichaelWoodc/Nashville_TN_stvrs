Other things to note
Please note: 

Being in the country has its perks—like spotting deer right outside your window! However, with a log home, you may occasionally see seasonal Asian beetles, carpenter bees, spiders or other small insects and pests inside or around the home.

We perform regular pest control and maintenance, but keeping windows and doors closed, not leaving food out and regularly taking garbage out helps ensure nature stays outside where it belongs. If anyone in your group is sensitive to insect stings, we recommend using a bit of extra caution when spending time outdoors.

To keep the property looking its best year-round, landscaping is scheduled on Fridays, either weekly or every other week. While we aim to schedule mowing during midday hours, there may be rare occasions—especially after periods of bad weather—when mowing or maintenance may shift schedule. We appreciate your understanding as we work to maintain a clean, beautiful, and welcoming space for all our guests.
