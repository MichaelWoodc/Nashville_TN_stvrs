Owl's House is perfectly designed for a unique getaway! The studio bedroom includes a flat-screen TV and windows that showcase the wooded views. There is also a stand for hanging clothes, a full-length mirror and an extra chair for seating. The cabin has a full bathroom with stand-in shower.

Outside, you'll love spending time in the private space enjoying the fresh air. There is a picnic table and extra seating around the firepit in the privacy of your yard. It is a great place to relax after a long day of adventure. There is also seating on the front porch to relax in the morning with a cup of coffee or unwind in the evening with a glass of wine. A BBQ grill is also available to take advantage of a cookout! You can also relax on the swing or in the hammock and enjoy the peaceful sounds of the woods just steps from the treehouse.
