=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Historic Log Cabin built from reclaimed Civil War era logs, it’s recent extensive renovation revealed county doc’s showing it was developed by architect to the stars, Braxton Dixon forJohnny Cash. Perfect for artists or musicians retreat. Full kitchen, bath, loft honeymoon suite w/half bath, king bed, living/dining room, Stone fireplace & laundry.  Sleeps 3 max. Deck overlooking treed acreage.  Just 30 mins to Nashville attractions, Grand Ol Opry & airport, a quick drive to local restaurants etc

The space
Approximately 1,000 square feet antique log cabin located in Hendersonville Tennessee on the “Cash Mountain” subdivision. Cabin built and decorated with nearly all reclaimed or repurposed furnishings. Complete remodel integrating modern conveniences with antique touches in keeping with the overall history of this unique space. Gorgeous honeymoon suite is upstairs, with sink and toilet. Full kitchen, living & dining room, full bath and laundry on main level. Stone fireplace adds to the warmth and ambiance. Wrap around deck with outdoor dining table and front porch rocker offer a gorgeous place for soaking in nature.

Guest access
There is a paved driveway to cabin, onsite parking for your car. Cabin is approximately 30 mins to Grand ol Oprey, Nashville and Airport. Restaurants and shopping are a 10 min drive to Hendersonville, White House or Goodlettsville.  Self check in available with electronic coded locks.

Other things to note
This cabin is one of 4 structures that originally existed on this property.  Much of the property that encompasses the entire Cash Mountain subdivision was developed by Architect to the Stars, Braxton Dixon for June and Johnny Cash, Roy Orbison, Tricia Yearwood and others. Two of the structures on this piece of property were destroyed by fire and all that remains are the fireplaces with the “heart” motif signifying development by Braxton Dixon. The second cabin on this property is currently under renovation to bring it back to better then it’s original state.
