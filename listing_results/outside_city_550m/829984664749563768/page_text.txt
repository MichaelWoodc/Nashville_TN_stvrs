"The Cottage Retreat" is located walking distance to Old Historic Nolensville with shops, coffee, food and more to enjoy. Going to Nashville for a night out? No problem, an easy 25 minute drive or uber ride. This home is 2,100 sq. ft. 3 bedrooms 2 bathrooms on 1.30 acres. Enjoy an evening poolside, yard games, or just a nice fire with some wine and smore's.

No dogs over 20 lbs and Absolutely no dogs on the pool deck. No cats sorry as people are highly allergic to.

Other things to note
This unique property boasts three short-term rentals. You can rent the entire property for family and friends, or you can rent an individual unit. If you choose to rent an individual unit, rest assured that the other areas will be unavailable for other renters. You’ll have exclusive access to your chosen space.
