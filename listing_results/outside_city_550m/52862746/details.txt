=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Tiny cabin getaway at its finest for couples, families and those who love the secluded forest but still close to everything! Less than 15 minutes to downtown Franklin, TN and 45 minutes to Nashville International Airport. This getaway retreat is situated on 13 acres at one of the highest points in Franklin, TN. With massive white oak, red oak, and maple trees surrounding the cabin, you will love being outside most of the day and evening breathing in the fresh air. There is also a beautiful pond!

The space
