=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Minutes from Nashville, TN. Reconnect with nature at this unforgettable alpaca escape.

The space
Create warm and fuzzy memories while staying in our well appointed, private camper on our mini alpaca farm in Middle Tennessee.  We offer a unique camping experience that combines the charm of rural life, though still close enough to Nashville to enjoy the thrill of the city.  Of course the City offers bars, honky-tonks with live music, Grand Ole Opry, tours, you name it, but why stay in the noisy downtown area after a long day?  Our farm offers a serene, peaceful getaway with amenities that are sure to make you relax such as enjoying the fire while making smores, stargazing, reading your favorite book, enjoying a picnic, or best of all -  feeding alpacas their favorite treats and snapping selfies that will make your friends wish they were you!

Your space is completely separated/fenced from all animals so that you only have to interact with them if you want to. The rental area includes the fully equipped RV, a small fenced yard with a picnic table and umbrella, firepit with Adirondack chairs, and a  charcoal grill. We also have kayaks if you'd like to rent those by the day.  If you'd like to make your arrival special with flowers, wine, or a charcuterie board, please let me know at least 72 hours prior to your stay to arrange that for you.
