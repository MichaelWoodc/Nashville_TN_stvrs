The space
The modern Farmhouse sits on 100 acres of pristine, tranquil land in Big East Fork Valley, site of many music videos (Black Keys, Gone West, Paramore, The Everly Brothers, Dan and Shay, Jack White) and music history (Steppenwolf, Janis Joplin, Leonard Cohen, Jimmy Buffet Band, Everly Brothers, Willie York of Big East Fork, Johnny Sheay).
