=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Come and relax on the outskirts of Nashville in this secluded spacious home. Only 20 minutes north of Nashville, Hilltop House is close enough to visit the city but far enough to enjoy nature. Boasting some of the best views in the Nashville area, Hilltop House is a newer home built in 2021. Come sit on our porch with a cup of coffee or drink, relax, unwind, and watch the deer, rabbits, and other wildlife. Our home is also  near the cities of Hendersonville, Goodlettsville, and Gallatin.

The space
Hilltop House is a four bedroom home that sleeps 14 guests. We have 3 full baths and a half bath. This house sits on 3 acres. We have plenty of space to host your group. Lots of windows and incredible mountain-like views makes this home one of the most unique homes in the Nashville area. You will not find a property like this one in all of North Nashville. We own 12 acres here and own all 3 homes on our hill. We are surrounded by hundreds of acres of heavy forest. We have lots of wildlife that visit like deer, rabbits, and groundhogs. Our location is in the Goodlettsville area, but we are also 10 minutes from Gallatin, Hendersonville, and White House. We are near Long Hollow pike and centrally located near several wedding venues and about 15 minutes from Drakes Creek park.   Our home is about 5 minutes from Long Hollow Church. We are on top of a large hill in the area.  We have a large front porch, a fire pit, and a patio in the back.
