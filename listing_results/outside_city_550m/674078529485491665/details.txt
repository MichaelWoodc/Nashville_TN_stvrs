=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Originally built in 1958 as a local grocery store, this property served the community until the late 1980s. After sitting abandoned for decades, it was renovated in 2021 into a Dolly Parton-themed Western retreat with lots of rustic charm!

Enjoy a cozy getaway, with live horses grazing in the backyard. Located in Middle Tennessee, this property offers easy access to major cities, with shopping, dining, and entertainment within 15 minutes. A perfect blend of comfort, character, and convenience

The space
Welcome to your ultimate Western-themed getaway with a touch of Dolly charm! This cozy retreat blends rustic style with modern comforts, perfect for a relaxing escape. Inside, you’ll find two inviting bedrooms, each with a queen-sized bed and TV, ensuring a restful stay. The spacious open floor plan connects the living room and fully equipped kitchen, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. The bathroom features a luxurious three-head shower, giving you a spa-like experience after a day of adventure.

But the magic doesn’t stop there—friendly horses in the back will be there to greet you as you pull up, adding to the charm of this countryside escape. With a little sparkle of Dolly-inspired flair, this one-of-a-kind stay is as cozy, fun, and fabulous as the queen of country herself!
