Decorated in vintage antiques and themes from 1967 ri 1978. Four bedrooms, 2.5 bathroom ranch house situated in a lovely valley. Several hiking trails lead through meadow to Timberlands Park, a lovely pond, and Big East Fork Creek. Bike (rentals available) upstream to Big East Fork.

The space
Big East Fork Farms is located 30 minutes from Nashville and 10 minutes from Franklin TN and historic Leipers Fork. The house is located on  a 2000 acre Land Conservation Easement adjacent to the Nachez Trace National Parkway. 
A rural and natural paradise within close  proximity to sushi and live music venues.
