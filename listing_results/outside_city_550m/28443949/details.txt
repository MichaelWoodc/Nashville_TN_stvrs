=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Private bedroom on 2nd floor of shared house farmhouse. Two queen beds and a sofa with a single bed in the large closet. It shares a full 2-sink bathroom with bedroom #6. Rural paradise with abundant wildlife in the protected Big East Fork Valley.  Shared space include front and back porches with rockers, kitchen, living room,  dining room, and views of amazing forest, meadow, and creek. Adjacent to horse barn. Horses usually in front yard. Hot tub - $50 setup fee. No smoking inside.

The space
The modern Farmhouse sits on 100 acres of pristine, tranquil land in Big East Fork Valley, site of many music videos (Black Keys, Gone West, Paramore, The Everly Brothers, Dan and Shay, Jack White) and music history (Steppenwolf, Janis Joplin, Leonard Cohen, Jimmy Buffet Band, Everly Brothers, Willie York of Big East Fork, Johnny Sheay).  Huge front and back meadows. Natural spring with delicious living water is accessible by a small walk. Abundant wildlife (turkey, deer, bobcat, red tail and Cooper's hawks). Babbling stream out front with "beach" for relaxing and staying cool. Hiking trail to sweet spring begins at back door

Guest access
Large kitchen, dining room, front and back porches, walking paths.  Living room available 9am until 9pm. Natural sweet water spring behind house. Short walk in front to natural creek and its wonders. Bring creek shoes!

Other things to note
All Fork Inn guests have access to the 7 acre Lake Gratitude Natural Area located 2 miles upstream.
