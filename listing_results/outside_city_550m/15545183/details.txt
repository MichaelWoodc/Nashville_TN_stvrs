The space
Welcome to our Franklin Farmhouse nestled on an acre of land in Franklin, TN. Surrounded by lush greenery, our space offers a comfortable country feel with all the amenities you need for a relaxing stay. Enjoy strolling through our vegetable garden where you can unwind and say hello and feed our friendly chickens.

We're a five minute drive to historic downtown Franklin, 10 minutes to cool springs, and 25 minutes to Nashville, ensuring easy access to all the attractions and amenities the area has to offer. 

Whether you're exploring the historic district, indulging in delicious dining options, shopping in unique boutiques, or enjoying a confection or ice cream cone, you'll find everything conveniently located nearby.

After a day of adventures, retreat to our quiet oasis for a restorative nights sleep. Whether you're sipping your morning coffee to a sunrise, unwinding watching fireflies in the evening, or enjoying smores at the firepit, our space offers the perfect blend of relaxation and convenience for your Franklin getaway.
