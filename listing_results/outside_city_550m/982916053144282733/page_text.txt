What do Taylor Swift, Conway Twitty, Johnny Cash, Kelly Clarkson, Marty Stuart, The Oak Ridge Boys, and Roy Orbison all have in common? They live or have lived in Hendersonville TN...just down the road from this completely renovated bungalow. Come see what drew the stars to our city. You can enjoy a quiet afternoon in the front porch rocking chairs, or a cool evening by the backyard fire pit. The home is situated in a quiet neighborhood just off Main St, and just 25 minutes north of Nashville.
