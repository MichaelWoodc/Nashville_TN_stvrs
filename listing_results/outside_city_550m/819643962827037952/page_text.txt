Listing highlights
In La Vergne, near Nashville
About 20 minutes by car to downtown Nashville's music venues and museums, with shopping and dining one exit away on Sam Ridley.
Open layout with two dining areas
An open floor plan with a dining table that seats eight and a kitchen table for five, so the whole group can eat together.
Spacious home, responsive host
Guests consistently call the home spacious and comfortable, and many praise Erin for quick, helpful communication.
This spacious home has room for everyone! Conveniently near Nashville, Murfreesboro, Nolensville, Franklin, and Percy Priest Lake on a quiet, family-friendly cul-de-sac.   Only 10 minutes to a movie theatre and a wide selection of shopping and dining.

The space
Open floor plan with plenty of room for everyone to relax together. Dining table that seats eight and a kitchen table that seats five, all with cushioned, comfortable seating because we love when family chooses to linger around the table together. Four upstairs bedrooms: two with king beds, one with a queen and the fourth with two twin over full beds. Full laundry room downstairs and jetted tub for soaking and relaxing in the master bathroom. Plenty of comfortable places to set up the laptop and work from home. Televisions in every bedroom and in the living room which can also be viewed from the kitchen. Whether you are traveling to relax, work or for entertainment, this is the place for you!

Guest access
Key pad entry. Code will be sent on day of check in.

Other things to note
Starter toilet paper, garbage bags, paper towels and dish washer pods are provided but not replaced once they run out. 

The driveway can comfortably hold 4 vehicles. If you have more vehicles, please consider carpooling as it is against a city ordinance to park on the street. Parking in the yard is not allowed.
