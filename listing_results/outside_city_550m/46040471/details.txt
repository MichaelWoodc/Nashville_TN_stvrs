=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Come stay in our secluded cabin retreat! Located just 30 minutes north of Nashville, this is the perfect place for a quick getaway. Fresh air, quiet neighborhood, and tons of wildlife. We regularly see deer, woodchucks, turkeys, raccoons, and many species of birds. We provide freshly ground coffee with a French Press, an assortment of teas, and hot chocolate.

The space
Our cabin over the garage has all the essentials with an updated vibe. Located on 6 wooded acres, you can relax in the main deck hot tub, have a picnic lunch in the woods, toast marshmallows by the campfire, or read a book in the hammock. The pool is open from May - September, and the hot tub is open year round.

Guest access
The hot tub and pool deck are attached to the main house about 240ft away. It is listed as shared space because it is not attached to the guest deck, however, we will not be making use of the hot tub during your stay. 

We recommend bringing some pool shoes or slippers to keep your feet clean and warm on the way there and back!
