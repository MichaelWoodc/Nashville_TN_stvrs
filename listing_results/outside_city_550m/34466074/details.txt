=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
25 minutes from downtown Nashville: Our tiny house has been converted from a utility shed into a one-of-a-kind cabin in the woods.  Tucked away among the trees, you’ll be able to relax & rejuvenate in our quaint little home. We are just far enough away from the hustle & bustle of city-life to rest your mind & body, but close enough to be able to enjoy all that Nashville has to offer! Rest on the covered front porch, play some corn-hole or enjoy the fire pit, just unwind, get away & refresh!!!

The space
NOT in a neighborhood & SECLUDED!! NO SHARED SPACES! This tiny house doesn’t seem very tiny @ all! The furnishings (vanity, cabinets, tables, shelves, kitchenette cabinet, dining table & steps/ladder) are all custom-made. This labor of love took 3 months & we’re so excited to offer this unique little cabin so others can enjoy the peace & tranquility! Full-sized, high-quality shampoo, conditioner & body wash are provided. SO many toiletries are made available to our guests, ie: disposable razors, body puffs, q-tips, cotton balls, lotions, toothbrushes & other oral hygiene needs. There will be a variety of snacks including a few breakfast items for guests’ enjoyment as well as a Keurig coffee pot with lots of varieties of K-cups, sweeteners and creamers! Kitchenette includes 2 burner stove top, toaster & microwave. Don’t be surprised if you see wild turkey, deer & you’ll definitely hear coyotes howling in the evening! It’s a perfect writer’s retreat, couple’s get-away or family backwoods adventure!

Guest access
Spacious, covered front porch, parking right beside the cabin, fire pit & corn hole!!!

Other things to note
We do NOT have WiFi or a tv! Cellular service is great though! This is NOT a hotel or motel, it is a HOME. NO PETS ALLOWED! No cats or dogs can be inside the property under any circumstances due to allergies! THERE ARE NO EXCEPTIONS! We take great pride in offering a clean, personal & comfortable stay. PLEASE familiarize yourself w/ the cabin in our house rules/notes both prior to & upon check-in; there is a folder provided in the cabin as well as on this site. Most things are common sense, but there are a few quirks like a faucet directions, etc. We anticipate every person that stays will take care of what we’ve worked so hard to provide. IF YOU USE OUR TOWELS/WASHCLOTHS TO REMOVE YOUR MAKEUP, you will be charged an additional fee!  Makeup remover wipes and ‘makeup’ washcloths are provided. We offer a variety of snacks & toiletries and expect our guests to use a sense of decorum, only consuming what they would enjoy during their stay and not taking snacks or toiletries beyond that. Kids are absolutely welcome, but we ask that you please advise during booking so that linens can be provided for EVERY person & when the cleaning service comes after your stay, they’ll be prepared w/ replacement sheets, towels, etc. (just because they’re little & don’t take up a lot of space, they must be counted in your number of guests @ registration/booking). Confirmed guests only! The presence of unconfirmed guests will result in immediate cancellation by AirBnB and termination of your stay. Our property is in the woods. While we do regular pest control and take measures to ensure that the cabin remains pest free, one or two may  get inside. There will be no refunds for nature. Always keep the door shut! PLEASE FOLLOW CHECKOUT INSTRUCTIONS! There is no cleaning fee, but failure to comply with checkout instructions will result in an additional $100 charge. Finally & most importantly, we are Christians & display scripture throughout our property.
