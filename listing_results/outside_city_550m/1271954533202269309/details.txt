=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Our Tent, Hazel, is 20ft, on a deck platform and nestled in the woods on our small, private campground and homestead. Complete with a queen sized bed and futon that doubles as a twin sleeping option; this tent can house up to a party of 5 spaciously. Non-electric, but provides you with the comforts and essentials to camping in all seasons!
Close to the shower house and restrooms.
Provides a chance to unplug and relax just 25min outside of Nashville. 
Luxury camping at its best!

The space
Our property stretches over seven acres, just 25 min north of Nashville. It is a homestead first and a guest experience second. You’ll see and experience our gardens and chicken coop. We have two RV slips, one bell tent and a camping meadow for those looking to set up for the weekend. We purchased the property two years ago and have been building our campground retreat ever since! Still under construction are our shower house and party barn! We have done so much, with so many amazing things to come!

Guest access
You are welcome to explore our entire property. It’s laid out with gathering areas and fir pits all around. We host events and writers rounds on certain nights and you are always welcome to participate! We hope you will take full advantage of all the spaces we have created for you to unwind and relax!
With the signing of our waiver forms, kids can take full advantage of our trampoline and ninja set as well!
