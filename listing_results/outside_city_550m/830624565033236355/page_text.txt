This 1,400 sq. ft. darling space is on 1.30 acres right down the street from Historic Nolensville and walking distance to coffee, food, dress shops and more. Want to kick your heels up in Nashville? No problem, only a 25 minute drive or a nice uber ride through some amazing scenery and beautiful homes along the way. Other things to note
This unique property boasts two short-term rentals. You can rent the entire property for family and friends, or you can rent an individual unit. If you choose to rent an individual unit, rest assured that the other areas will be unavailable for other renters. You’ll have exclusive access to your chosen space.

You'll have access to the large backyard where you can enjoy the pool, swing set, picnic tables, yard games, smokeless fire pit, and more. This rental also includes an indoor lounge area that can be used for our Airbnb’s guests.

Stayed with kids
Vey unique place to say with a small hotel/boutique feel. I would encourage anyone wanting to rent one of the units to read the full description of what’s included/not included. There are a total of 3 separate units plus the lounge area. So if you only rent one unit, your could be sharing the property with three other parties if the whole unit is rented.  That means sharing the pool and backyard space with people you don’t know. The descriptions for the units we stayed in stated “your rental includes access to the lounge area.“  We came to find out that the term “access” means “pay extra.” I would suggest the description of each unit be more specific of what’s included/not included in the rental. We had a large group (13 people), so there’s not a lot of space to congregate together if you don’t have access to the lounge or if there’s bad weather, ruling out the pool area as an option. Overall, a nice place but a little misleading in the description.
