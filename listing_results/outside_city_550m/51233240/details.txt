Pool Table. Go fishing in the stocked pond. There are trails on the 110 acres of property. Take in the beautiful rolling hill views where cattle roam. Perfect for large groups of 16+. The space
We take cleanliness seriously and sanitation is our number one priority. Our professional cleaner ensures that your safety comes first.

Enjoy the 15-ft heated swim spa or the large refreshing heated in-ground pool with slide. The home has an traditional farm house decor with great attention to detail. The kitchen is a chefs dream and stocked with everything you need. Outside by the pool you will find a BBQ grill and several tables to enjoy a meal by the pool and a fire pit to keep you warm. There are also several porches and a sunroom to enjoy morning coffee or afternoon sweet tea.

Weddings and events are allowed for an additional fee depending on number of guest. Plenty of parking for cars and service trucks.

In the main house, there is a primary bedroom with a king bed and on suite full bath. The 2nd bedroom has a queen bed and on suite full bath. The 3rd bedroom has 1 Queen Bunk (2 Queen Beds) and full bath off hallway.

In the cabana, there is a king bed downstairs with on suite full bath. Upstairs, there are 2 Queen Bunks (4 Queen Beds) with a half bath. Coffee and mini fridge station with microwave in the cabana as well.

The location of the home is approximately 25 miles from the Nashville honky-tonks, antiques and restaurants and sports arenas. Pleasant View shopping 5 minutes and Honey Suckle Farm is 10 minutes away.
