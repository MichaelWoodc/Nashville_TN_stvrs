=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Perfect getaway from the hustle and bustle of the city, yet still just a short drive away. Forget your worries in this spacious and serene cabin home on 4 acres while you enjoy the beautiful sights and sounds of nature right outside your door. Sip your morning coffee in a rocking chair on the wrap around porch, tell ghost stories around the big fire pit, & cool off in the pool during the hot summer months (seasonal). This cabin home is small enough to feel cozy, yet big & spacious enough for 10.

The space
The casita (extra fee) in the backyard of the property has a full bed and is used as the 4th room for sleeping 2 guests (guests 9 & 10). It also has air conditioning/heating, a table with seating for 2, mini fridge, coffee maker, TV with DVD player and a selection of DVD's. Super cute set up! There is no wifi in that unit so you cannot use streaming services. **There is an extra cleaning fee of $50, and an extra occupancy fee of $50 per person, per night to use the casita. 
*PLEASE NOTE, THERE IS NO RESTROOM IN THIS UNIT, SO IF YOU NEED TO USE THE RESTROOM IN THE MIDDLE OF THE NIGHT, YOU WILL HAVE TO USE THE PROVIDED FLASHLIGHT AND WALK TO THE MAIN  CABIN. PLEASE USE CAUTION AS THE TERRAIN IS NOT COMPLETELY LEVEL

**The pool is seasonal and open from May through September. It will be closed and winterized for the remainder of the time.

You are free to explore the property but please use caution as the nearly 2 acres of wooded area in the back has no marked trails and terrain can be unpredictable, so enter at your own risk! Also please wear pants, close toed shoes as there is poison ivy in some areas, and use bug spray to protect against bugs including mosquitos and ticks! Please use caution in general and watch your step when exploring any part of the property. We want you to enjoy your stay but safety is our number one priority.

PLEASE NOTE - GARAGE & BARN ARE OFF LIMITS. THANK YOU! :)

Other things to note
Our towels, blankets, pillows, and kitchen items are provided for your use during your stay. Please leave them in the home for future guests. If something is accidentally damaged or taken, just let us know, we appreciate your honesty.
