=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Forget your worries in our spacious and modern space. This beautifully done lakefront home is conveniently located on a quiet cove on Old Hickory Lake and only 30 miles from the hustle and bustle of downtown Nashville. Enjoy an evening cocktail on the covered deck while watching the sunset over the lake or spend time with the whole family in the huge rec room downstairs! Have a boat? The boat ramp is only 2 minutes away by car! Our home is fully equipped for a relaxing and fun lake trip!

The space
The house is located on a flat, lakefront lot that backs up to a quiet cove on Old Hickory Lake. There are neighbors to one side and a wooded lot on the other side. There is a large U-shaped driveway in front of the house and a straight driveway on the side perfect for parking a boat and trailer! The covered porch offers sunset views and the opportunity to watch the wildlife around you. Going inside, the main floor has 3 bedrooms (2 king rooms and 1 bunk room). You’ll also find a sitting room, family room with large tv, dining room, kitchen and the laundry room on the main floor. There are 2 full baths and a half bath all on the main floor as well. The downstairs has another very spacious king room that has a smaller room with a daybed off the side (perfect for a family). The large rec room is downstairs with a sofa large enough to fit the whole crew and another large tv. There is also a functional wood burning fireplace on each floor to keep it cozy in the colder months!

Guest access
Guests will have access to the whole house as well as the front and back yard. You will not have access to a dock. The docks that can be seen from the backyard are private property and belong to our neighbors. These are not to be used by our guests.

Other things to note
You will not have access to a dock. The docks in the area are private property and not for use by our guests.
