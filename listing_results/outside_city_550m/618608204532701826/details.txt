=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This newly renovated home is nestled on ¾ of an acre with a beautiful view of the lake and park-like backyard. Enjoy your morning coffee on the deck taking in the wonderful sunrise over the cove. This home is a short 25 minutes from Nashville, but far enough for that get away, country feel. This air-conditioned house is also located in the popular 2-foot cove on Old Hickory Lake.  Check out Dolly Parton’s vacation home as you kayak through the cove.

The space
This home features 3 newly furnished bedrooms and 2 ½ baths. The home also has a second living room that could be used as an additional bedroom with the pull out couch, or as a community area for fun, games, music, and relaxation.  There is a fully stocked kitchen, and a washer and drier available for your convenience. If you have a little one, we even have a high chair and pack-n-play crib available. The home has plenty of space and amenities for everyone!

We hope that you will make lifetime memories enjoying family, friends, and your pets, during your stay! 

Enjoy evenings barbecuing on the deck. Relax by the firepit roasting hot dogs and  marshmallows, or catching fireflies during the summer!  

This home is only 25 minutes to downtown Nashville and 15 minutes from the airport. Great home for families and friends to meet up, enjoy the water, and start sightseeing adventures in and around Nashville.

We have put together a detailed guide with local activities, restaurants and attractions in the area that we think you will enjoy.

Inside, there are smart TV's in the living room, game room, and master bedroom. You can utilize the available TV programming, or log on to your own subscription services. 

Bedroom 1: King Bed | Bedroom 2: Queen Bed | Bedroom 3: Full Bunk Bed with Twin Trundle Bed | Game Room/Second living room: Queen Sleeper Sofa

Most of the home is on the main level where you enter through the front door. Rooms on the lower level are the second living room/game room, one of the full baths, and the laundry area.  These can be accessed from outside if steps are an issue for one of your guests, but will need to be unlocked from inside. 

We have entertainment available inside and outside.  Enjoy the many inside board games, a Pac-Man arcade game, poker table with poker chips, and a ping pong table. Outside we have Smash Ball, horseshoes, Corn hole, a golf net (you will need your own clubs), a kayak, paddle board, and fishing poles.  Feel free to use the easy up, beach chairs and small dock for jet skis.

Perfect for kayakers and canoers, you can slip your boats in to the cove and enjoy direct access to the lake.  For larger boats you will need to put in at one of the 3 launch points within 5 minutes of the home. The middle of the cove is a bit shallow for larger boats, so do stay to the side as you navigate to and from the property. The cove depth generally ranges from 2-5' deep depending on where you are navigating and the height of the water as this can vary due to rain fall and other factors. 

*see additional info below for all the nearby access areas*  

-Cedar Creek Marina has a ship store, boat rentals, ramp, dock rental, and a restaurant; Scoreboards Bar & Grill. 

-Cedar Creek Recreation Area has a swim area, picnic tables, sand volleyball court, and boat ramp. They also have pontoon, yacht and kayak rentals.  The recreation area asks $5 to park, $2 to walk in, or if you will be using it a lot, $40 for an annual pass.

-Shutes Branch Recreation Area also has picnic sites, swimming areas, boat launch, and fishing platform.  There is also a biking trail that progressively gets more challenging.  Pets are allowed.

-Blue Turtle Bay is another marina a short 15 minutes away with pontoon boat rentals, and Sam's Sports Grill.

Guest access
You will have access to the entire property! The jet skis in the garage are the only thing "off limits." 
Please use proper water safety.  There is a large yard between the house and water, so you have some space to relax* while your children play close to the home, but still have easy access to the water. 
*There is unrestricted access to the water, so children and pets should always be monitored outdoors.
