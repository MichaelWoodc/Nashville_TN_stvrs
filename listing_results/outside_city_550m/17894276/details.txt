=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
After being off the STR market for 2 years, the Milk Barn is back!  

The Milk Barn at Artesian Springs is a fun and relaxing escape for couples or  families.  Located near Historic Nolensville TN, this renovated dairy barn features 2 bedrooms, one bath, a full kitchen, and beautiful outdoor spaces to enjoy.

The space
Decorated in simple Farmhouse style, the main living area has plenty of comfortable seating and features a flat screen  smart TV with Netflix and Amazon Prime movies. The dining table has seating for four.  The kitchen is fully stocked with all the essentials, including full size appliances, microwave and dishwasher as well as a Coffee Bar. Two private bedrooms are down the hall with luxury linens on the queen beds.  Outdoor areas include a creek side fire pit, picnic table and a charcoal grill (charcoal not included) . 
  
This cozy house is close to major attractions, but far enough away for rest and relaxation.  Explore Franklin's historic downtown area or shop to your heart's content at Cool Spring Galleria, both just 15 minutes away.  Spend a night at the Honky Tonks in Downtown Nashville (30 minute drive), or visit one of the many infamous music venues around town.  Stay close to home in Nolensville, strolling through antique and specialty shops, the Amish market, or the Saturday morning Farmer's Market. At the end of the day, kick you feet up around the bonfire and listen to the crickets and the bubbling creek!

Update:  The new Village Green is now open with a Publix grocery store and several new dining establishments,  just 2 miles down the road. Nolensville Town Square is coming soon!
