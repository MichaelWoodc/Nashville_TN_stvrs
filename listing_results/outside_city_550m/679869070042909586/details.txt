=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Relax at this tranquil hidden getaway, nestled among the trees, close to town center. Located on five acres and bordered by Stewarts Creek and the Sam Davis Home , guests have their own 590 sq ft suite (attached to the main house) with private entry door and access to the fenced/gated property. Only 30 minutes to BNA International Airport, Murfreesboro or downtown Nashville!

Extra guest charges are for adults only. Up to two children (0-15 years) may accompany parents for no extra charge.

The space
You'll enter your suite up four stairs close to parking. The area you enter first is a sitting room with a sofa bed (for guests #3-4), wall-mounted flat screen TV with Prime, Netflix and Hulu, small table and chairs and an area containing a Keurig, mini-fridge and microwave. To the left is a large bathroom with tub and walk-in shower (Full disclosure: the tub jets are currently out of order). The bedroom has a queen-size bed, walk-in closet and dresser space. It is clean and uncluttered. Just lovely, in our humble opinion! :)

Guest access
Guests may enjoy the entire five acres, including the front/back porches, deck and screened patio. We have muck boots and insect repellant for you to use if you're feeling adventurous! :)

Other things to note
We do have three super friendly dogs. We ask that you don't feed them, since one of them has a sensitive tummy and will leave us a surprise on our living rug if he gets people food. The dogs may greet you if they happen to be outside when you are coming/going, but we'll keep them away from you if you'd like.
