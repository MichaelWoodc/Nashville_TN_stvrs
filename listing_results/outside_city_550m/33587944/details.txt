A NOTE ABOUT THE SHORELINE

Old Hickory Lake is a real, working lake managed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. After rain or changes in current, natural debris — including driftwood, branches, or the occasional uprooted tree — can collect along the shoreline. We clear it regularly, but nature leads here. Water shoes are recommended any time you're in or near the water. If something needs attention during your stay, just let us know.

THE WILDLIFE

Deer move through the yard daily — often in herds of 8 to 20 — and geese wander past in the afternoons like they have somewhere important to be. Turtles sun on the logs, a fox slips through the meadow at dusk, and our resident skunk visits the water's edge most evenings, minding his own business entirely.

Please enjoy them from a distance. These are wild animals — not pets. Deer in particular can be unpredictable, especially in spring when does have fawns nearby and in fall during rutting season. Watch from the deck, take the photos, and let them do their thing. It's one of the best parts of staying here.
