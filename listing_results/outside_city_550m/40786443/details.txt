=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This welcoming home is ready for your next adventure blending vintage charm with modern convenience! Couples, friends and families can enjoy the screened back deck overlooking a large yard with string lights or cook a meal in our fully stocked kitchen. 

Visit historic downtown Franklin (4 miles) or Leipers Fork (11 miles) with local shops, great food, and charming small town feel. Or head north to Nashville (16 miles) for music, art, sports, honky-tonks and more! There’s something for everyone.

The space
The house has one bedroom with a queen size bed and one bedroom with a twin bed plus a "twin over full" bunkbed. 

There are two bathrooms with showers (one tub, one stall). 

The kitchen is fully stocked with appliances and spices for cooking. Food is not supplied. The coffee maker can make a full carafe or single cup. Two packs of Colombian coffee, some K-Cups and a variety of tea bags are available for your first morning! 

There are two main living areas with comfortable seating and smart TVs including a 65 inch TV in the bonus room. Feel free to login to your favorite streaming service while you are here and logout at checkout. 

The screened in back porch has string lighting, a large table and chairs, and porch swing. Backyard has string lighting with two play structures with swings and slides. 

A laundry room is available for guest use with large machines and fragrance free detergent. 

Accessibility: Please be aware that there are 3 steps up to the front door and 3 steps from the bonus room area up to the kitchen. There are no steps at the carport door. There is one step down into the living room. See photos for specifics.

Health/Cleaning: This house was built in 1962 and has been renovated and updated. The crawlspace has been disinfected, encapsulated, and a dehumidifier installed. All HVAC ducting is less than 5 years old. 

We do not use chemical based cleaners with artificial fragrances. We clean and provide for your use all natural cleaners and isopropyl alcohol, a safe non-chemical disinfectant.
