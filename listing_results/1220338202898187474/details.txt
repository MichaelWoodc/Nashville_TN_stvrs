=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Walk to the many coffee shops, local boutiques, music venues, bars, and top-rated restaurants in East Nashville’s Lockland Springs neighborhood. This charming private 1 Br, 1 Ba suite in a historic bungalow is perfect for exploring the area then relaxing in the private garden with hot tub. Plus, downtown Nashville is only a short drive away. This cute cozy space is perfect for everyone.

The space
Redbud Cottage is conveniently located in a quiet neighborhood in the heart of historic East Nashville. This one bedroom apartment is on the first floor of our 1920's bungalow. When you arrive, you will pull into off-street parking spot and use the private entrance to your gated outdoor patio. 

Guests will have the whole place to themselves, including a private entrance and no shared spaces. The outdoor space including the hot tub and patio are not shared and enclosed by a privacy fence. Hosts live in adjacent, (not shared) parts of the bungalow.   

Patio:
The garden patio is perfectly situated for relaxation with comfortable seating, a  hot tub, and an outdoor fireplace. Nice large beach towels are provided for the hot tub. From the patio, enter the apartment using your private door with smart lock for self check-in. 

Dining Room and Kitchenette:
The front door opens into the spacious dining room and kitchenette featuring a full size table with sink and small appliances and a 3/4 size fridge. The kitchenette is stocked with plates, bowls, cutlery, cups, wine glasses and coffee mugs for dining needs. There is also a Kurig, electric kettle, and french press with supplies for all your tea and coffee needs. Note that we have an air fryer, microwave and toaster but do not have a stove or oven. 

Living room:
The living room has a futon that can transform to a full size bed, a TV with Roku, and a dedicated work space. Relax on the futon with a book or playing one of the variety of games we provide in the natural light from the large windows.

Bedroom:
The bedroom has a queen bed and a twin daybed. There are blackout shades in the bedroom and all linens/ pillows are provided.

Bathroom:
The bathroom is fully stocked and equipped with essentials (hair dryer, shampoo, conditioner, hand soap, toilet paper, hand towels/ bath towels, etc). The shelves provide plenty of open storage with bathtub and shower.
