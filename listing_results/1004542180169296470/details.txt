=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Experience East Nashville’s fun vibe from this bright, artistic 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo for up to 4 guests. Enjoy mural-filled interiors, a fully equipped kitchen, dedicated workspace, in-unit laundry, free parking, and self check-in. Minutes from Broadway and the Grand Ole Opry, it’s a stylish Music City home base with pet-friendly accommodations.

The space
Settle into a tranquil second-floor condo in a quiet, secure building. Bright natural light, a massive colorful mural, bold artistic details, and a playful neon sign give this open-concept home a memorable Music City character.

The living area offers comfortable seating, a smart TV, board games, books and a vinyl record player. The fully equipped kitchen includes an oversized island with seating for four, stainless steel appliances, a dishwasher, microwave, blender, toaster, and a coffee bar with drip and Keurig single-serve coffee makers.

Sleeping arrangements:
Primary bedroom: king bed, smart TV, and private en-suite bathroom with a large double sink.
Second bedroom: queen bed, smart TV, and an attached oversized closet that doubles as a dedicated office with a desk.

A second full bathroom is accessible from the second bedroom’s closet/office area as well as the main living area. For added convenience, the condo also includes free in-unit washer and dryer, central air conditioning and heating, room-darkening shades, and a Pack ’n Play/travel crib.

Guest access
Guests have private, exclusive access to the entire 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo throughout their stay, including all rooms and the private entrance. Self check-in is available via smart lock.

Ample open parking spaces are available on the premises. The condo is on the second floor, and the building has an elevator for easy access.

Other things to note
Pet Policy: We are pet-friendly! However, our building's Homeowners Association (HOA) has certain limitations. To ensure a comfortable stay for everyone, you MUST contact us to discuss your pet PRIOR to booking.

Accessibility: The condo is conveniently located on the second floor of the building, which is fully equipped with an elevator for easy access.

The building has exterior security cameras on the property. Long-term stays are allowed.
