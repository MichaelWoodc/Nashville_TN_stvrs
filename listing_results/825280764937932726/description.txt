Welcome to our cozy 12 South Guesthouse! Located in the walkable 12 South Neighborhood of Nashville. Coffee shops, restaurants, bars and great shopping are just a short walk away.

Quick Lyft or Uber to all the great attractions Nashville has to offer like the Gulch, Downtown, Lower Broadway, Bridgestone Arena, Geodis Park & More.

Perfect stay for a couple visiting their children at Belmont University which is located up the street. Also a quick Lyft ride to Vanderbilt and Lipscomb.

The space
This guest house was constructed in 2023 and is located on the upstairs floor behind our main house in a detached building. 

This cozy space includes a private entrance and 1 queen sized bed, 1 bathroom with tub & shower, a full kitchen with dishwasher, microwave and oven, eating area, and futon.

The futon folds out and can sleep 1 person.

The house includes a Smart TV. Please note that cable is NOT provided, so you'll need to log in with your own streaming service accounts (Netflix, Hulu, etc.) to access content.

Includes Free Wifi

Guest access
This guest house is private and is detached from the main house. Note the Guest house is located on the top floor and includes a separate private entrance.
