=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Our carriage house is a private retreat in our East Nashville neighborhood.  We are close to downtown and a block from the city bus line.  It comfortably accommodates two and is next to a large city park.  Enjoy the private suite with full bath.

The space
Our guest suite is a private room and bath above our detached garage.  The garage is 2012 construction in our historic neighborhood.  We will be staying in the main home on our property while you enjoy the privacy of your separate space.  The accommodations are similar to a hotel room:  queen bed, a leather loveseat sofa, TV, sitting table, and bathroom with shower.  Parking is available on our driveway or on the street in front of our house.  

The suite has central air conditioning and heat and lots of windows.  The TV is digital over the air broadcast with approximately 30 channels.  The space is on the second floor up one flight of stairs.  A Keurig coffee maker and supplies are in the guest suite, and a refrigerator is in the lower garage.

Guest access
In addition to your private guest suite over the garage, you will have access to our backyard and screen porch. The porch is perfect for relaxing in front of a fire on a cool evening, watching TV, or just relaxing.

Other things to note
We generally do not provide any housekeeping service during your stay so you can enjoy your privacy.  If there is anything you need, please simply let us know.

Airbnb collects our state and county sales tax and hotel occupancy tax from you at booking.
