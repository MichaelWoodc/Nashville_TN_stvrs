=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Happy Camper is a 1971 fully restored camper with whimsical styling and all the comforts of home. It is close to the city (20 min. from downtown) but nestled down in a holler in the forest. With a queen bed in the single bedroom, a couch that converts to a double bed in the main living area, and bunk beds tucked into a corner, this is perfect for a family getaway or a group of friends out for the weekend. The deck overlooks a seasonal waterfall and the fire pit is perfect for stargazing.

The space
You have access to the yard around the camper and the fire pit. You can walk to Dollar General just past the fire station.

Guest access
It's all yours! Maybe shared with some opossums, bunnies, the neighbor's small dog named Sugar, some cats, a groundhog, a fox and some deer. So keep food put away and trash covered.

Other things to note
Please note, this is a 17 foot camper. It sleeps six but the inside space is really tight if you have more than 4 people inside hanging out. Keep in mind, there is a 180 square foot covered patio attached and ample outdoor space if the weather permits. It is in a natural area, so you may see small wildlife like cats, opossums, rabbits, ground hogs, bugs or the neighbor's dog, but none of them bite. Except the bugs. We provide bug spray and suggest you use it.
