=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to The Pie Town Emporium, a whimsical two-story penthouse in the heart of Nashville. Located in the Gulch’s Pie Town district, this unique retreat sleeps 8 and features 5 beds, 2.5 baths, and a nostalgic built-in library filled with games, books, and collectibles. Enjoy skyline views from the private balcony, and unwind in the soaking tub. Amenities including pool, gym, parking, and a prime location walking distance Broadway (just .7 miles), this is the ultimate Music City getaway.

The space
This spacious penthouse boasts 5 beds, 2 bedrooms, and 2.5 bathrooms, comfortably sleeping up to 8 guests. From the moment you walk through the fish and mermaid-themed entryway, you’ll know this isn’t your average vacation rental. Turn off the lights, switch on the blacklight, and enjoy a glowing surprise. A fully equipped kitchen awaits with new stainless steel appliances, plenty of cutlery, utensils, plates, salt/pepper, and more (sans cooking oil).

A Space That Tells a Story: The open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area is designed for entertaining and relaxation, with plenty of space to gather, unwind, and create unforgettable memories. But what truly sets this space apart is the built-in library, a treasure trove of nostalgia filled with classic novels, board games, vintage relics, and collectibles. If you love antiquing, storytelling, and the magic of the past, you’ll be enchanted by every carefully curated detail.

Upstairs, a Surprise Awaits: Head to the second floor and prepare to smile like a kid again as you step into the circus tent-themed bathroom—a whimsical touch that brings charm and fun to your stay.

Bedroom 1: King-size bed with an ensuite bathroom featuring a freestanding soaking tub—perfect for unwinding in style.
Bedroom 2 (Upstairs Loft): California King-size bed plus twin bunk beds, offering private balcony access with breathtaking city views.
Living Room: Queen-size sleeper sofa and a Smart TV for movie nights or catching up on your favorite shows.

Unbeatable Views & Resort-Style Amenities: Step outside onto your private balcony and soak in the panoramic downtown skyline, including Nashville’s iconic "Bat Building." Whether you're sipping morning coffee or toasting to an unforgettable night out, this view is unbeatable.

The Hyve building amenities are second to none:

Location, Location, Location: You’ll be just a short walk or quick Uber/Lyft ride from Broadway, the heartbeat of Nashville’s legendary live music scene and nightlife. Whether you're indulging in world-famous BBQ, catching a concert at the Ryman, bar-hopping at iconic honky-tonks, or discovering hidden gems in The Gulch, The Pie Town Emporium is the perfect home base for your Music City adventure.

This isn’t just a place to stay—it’s an experience you’ll never forget. Book now and get ready for an unforgettable Nashville getaway.
