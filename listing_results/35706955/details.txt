=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Boutique hotel housed in a beautiful historic church in East Nashville located less than 1.5 miles away from downtown.

Every stay at The Russell helps someone experiencing homelessness in Nashville through our Rooms for Rooms program! The average weekend stay provides 16 nights in a bed, 100 free showers, or 30 free meals!

The space
This 2nd floor suite has 2 king beds, 1 queen sleeper sofa, and is 485 SF. This room has unique features like a king bed in the old bell tower of the church and balcony overlooking the beautiful stained glass windows in the lobby below! Because of lack of shades, eye masks are provided for the king bed in the bell tower.
 
*Please Note: This room is on the 2nd floor and The Russell does not contain an elevator. However, guests may use the dumbwaiter to send luggage up to the floor they are staying on.

Guest access
Access to the building and guest rooms is through a unique key code that we send via email the day before arrival! There is not a check in desk or concierge but we are always a phone call away if needed.

Other things to note
There is a parking lot across the street exclusive for guests of The Russell.  Each room booked is allotted one parking space. Upon booking please enter the license plate number, color, make and model of your car into your booking form so we can register your car for the dates you will be in town. Cars that are not registered before arrival will be towed.

**Rates include housekeeping service upon departure only.
