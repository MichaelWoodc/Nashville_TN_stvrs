Welcome to our charming riverfront retreat in the heart of Nashville! Our Airbnb offers a unique and unforgettable experience for those seeking a perfect blend of city excitement and serene waterfront living. Located just a short drive  away from Nashville's iconic landmarks, renowned restaurants, and lively entertainment venues.

The space
LEVEL ONE
Picture a cozy retreat where a garage opens into a bedroom and bathroom on the same floor. The welcoming bedroom with a bunk bed for your adventures. The chic bath is perfect afteroom combines practicality with style, allowing for refreshing showers or soothing baths just steps from your bed.

LEVEL TWO
Welcome to our cozy home! Our inviting dining and living rooms offer a warm space with a half-bath. The dining area has a beautiful table and big windows that fill the room with sunlight. The fully equipped kitchen features high-quality appliances, a walk-in pantry, and ample counter space. Whether dining indoors or on the patio with scenic river views, you’ll find the perfect spot to gather and relax.

LEVEL THREE
The master bedroom is warm, inviting, and filled with natural light. Relax on the plush queen-size bed with luxurious linens. The furniture creates a calming space to unwind. The walk-in closet is spacious and organized. The ensuite features a stylish walk-in shower with a soothing water cascade, helping you relax.

LEVEL FOUR
Step onto your charming patio with river views, showcasing Nashville’s beauty. It's furnished with cozy comfort in mind, inviting you to unwind in plush seating, savor your favorite drink, and soak in the gentle sunset. Add some lighting to create a warm, inviting atmosphere for the evening.

Guest access
Guests will have full access to the entire unit during their stay in Music City!

Other things to note
Extra linens enhance your comfort. We prioritize your well-being with a luxurious, practical experience. Enjoy a well-equipped bathroom featuring essentials for a refreshing stay. Indulge with complimentary shampoo, conditioner, and body wash to elevate your routine. Relax in the shower or bath, knowing your comfort is our priority.

We commit to a superior stay through the thoughtful details in our bedrooms and bathrooms. Book now and immerse yourself in a world where luxury, comfort, and convenience converge for a restorative and indulgent experience.
