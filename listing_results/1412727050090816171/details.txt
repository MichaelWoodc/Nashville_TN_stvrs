=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Playful, punchy, and full of personality — this spacious SoBro 1BR brings big color and even bigger charm. With a bold rug, gold accents, a Crosley ready to spin, and a cozy sleeper sofa for extra guests, Cosmic Cowgirl is equal parts funk, flair, and comfort. A standout stay for those looking to do Nashville their own way.

The space
This 724 sq ft 1BR includes a king-size bed, full kitchen, open-plan living with a sleeper sofa, and thoughtful details like a Crosley record player and wall-mounted guitar. Designed with color and creativity in mind, it’s ideal for travelers who want space to stretch out and style that pops.

Guests have access to all our onsite amenities, including our restaurant, bar, gym, courtyards and dipping pool. Our community is made up of two towers — please note some amenities (including the pool) are located in our sister building, about a 3 minute walk from our entrance.

Guest access
You’ll have access to the entire apartment, including the in-unit washer/dryer and stocked kitchen.

Other things to note
LC SoBro is more than a place to stay — it’s a place to live well. As our guest, you’ll have access to incredible onsite amenities including:

🐐 The Goat – Our onsite restaurant and bar with great food, weekday happy hour specials, and noteworthy events.

☕ Morning Ritual – Specialty coffee and fresh pastries served daily, just steps from your door.

💪 Code Wellness – A two-story fitness studio equipped with everything you need for a great workout, whether you're lifting heavy or getting in a solid cardio session.

☀️  Rooftop Pool – Cool off or soak up the sun in our outdoor dipping pool, located around the corner in our sister building.

🌿 Outdoor Courtyards – Relax in open-air lounge spaces with fire pits, grills, string lights, and plenty of seating.

🏠 This apartment is part of a traditional residential community. Please be respectful of full-time residents.

🚭 No smoking, vaping, or drug use on the premises — a minimum $500 fine applies.

🎉 No parties. Authorized guests only.

☑️ The amenity spaces are open and access is provided to all full time residents and our registered guests. It’s worth noting that hours are controlled by the building and although very rare, the building sometimes closes amenities for maintenance and cleaning. This is unlikely but we like to be up front as we cannot issue refunds if the building closes the pool or other amenity spaces during your stay.

🅿️ Parking
Parking is not included with the listing. The closest parking lot is 705 3rd Ave S, at the corner of 3rd and Elm and just across the street from our community complex. Rates are controlled by the parking authority, averaging $25 for overnight parking, but sometimes fluctuate based on downtown event demand. Parking fees are paid via QR code in the parking lot.
