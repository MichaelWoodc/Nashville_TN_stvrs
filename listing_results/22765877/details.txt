Studio [B] is just blocks away from the RCA Studio B on Music Row where many of the greats, including Elvis, recorded.  You are also only 1 mile to the honky tonks of Lower Broad.  Enjoy a night on the town, then return to a quiet neighborhood for a good night's sleep.  Other nearby destinations include Vanderbilt and Belmont Universities, Centennial Park, and Hillsboro Village.

The space
Studio [B] is located in our backyard and accessible from the alley.   Parking is right at the door.  Keyless lockpad allows for self check in.  Inside you'll find an uncluttered studio apartment with touches of Nashville.  

It is suitable for a couple or a couple with a child if you don't mind being in the same room.  While children are welcome at Studio [B], it has not been child-proofed.  It also works well for a business professional or academic.  The second queen size bed is actually a futon that can be set up as a sofa or a bed.

The kitchenette is furnished with a refrigerator, Keurig coffee maker, Advantium micro/convection oven, toaster and electric skillet.
