The space
Four separate front doors under one reservation, so a reunion or wedding party never has to split between an Airbnb and a hotel. Quad Retreat gives every family or friend group their own house, then pulls everyone back together after dark. Each of the four homes is complete on its own: full kitchen, living room, and four bedrooms, so nobody is sharing a bathroom lineup with strangers.

Light wood floors, marble islands with bar stools, and sectional sofas with gallery walls run through every one of the four. The rooftop deck is the reunion point, with a hot tub, mini golf, a wet bar, string lights, and skyline views over the city.

Downstairs, a barrel sauna gives the group a place to unwind between rounds, and a game room keeps the night going with a pool table and arcade machine under record-wall art. One home tucks in a Bunk Room, built for the kids or the late sleepers who want their own corner away from the main bedrooms.

Spend the day at Geodis Park, catch sunset from the rooftop bar, run a mini golf tournament before the pool table takes over, soak in the sauna, then peel off to four separate homes to actually sleep.
