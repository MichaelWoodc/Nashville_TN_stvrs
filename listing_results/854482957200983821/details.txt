Eight minutes from Geodis Park, ten minutes from the airport, and fourteen from Broadway. Single-story layout, fully fenced yard, sleeps 6.

The space
Most Nashville short-term rentals are downtown condos. Space Cowboy is a single-story ranch house with a fenced backyard, a working firepit, and a front porch wide enough to actually sit on. It is the kind of place where the outdoor space is the reason you booked, not an afterthought.
