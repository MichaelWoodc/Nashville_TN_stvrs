=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Located just minutes from downtown Nashville, this brand new modern 1 bedroom condo is perfect for couples traveling together, families, or for larger groups (sleeps up to 6)! It's a quick 10 minute Uber ride to the Honky Tonks on Broadway, Bridgestone Arena, the Music City Center, and Nissan Stadium! It is walking distance fromthe legendary Swett's restaurant, Blackstone Brewery, the Merle Haggard mural, One City, Centennial Park, and HCA Healthcare! Wifi, cable, and parking included!

The space
PLEASE NOTE: Smoking (cigarettes or marijuana) is not allowed anywhere on this property (including balcony and common areas). Violation of this rule will result in your reservation being cancelled without and your eviction from the property.

A regular priced non-surge UberX ride to Broadway, our famous strip with live music and bars, will cost around $10-$12 and take less than 10 minutes (without traffic).

Inside the unit you will find everything you will need to relax and work on your trip. The building is brand new with all modern furnishings, including a full size washer and dryer. There are 4 total beds: There is a queen bed in the bedroom, a pull out queen sofa bed in the living room, a queen air mattress, a twin sized air mattress, and a twin sized floor mattress.

WiFi is included and there is a RokuTV where you can watch basic cable through SlingTV. I also have a subscription to HBO Max. Coffee and basic hotel amenities are also available. There is also a full set of kitchenware and pots and pans for cooking.

There is parking at the building and plenty of additional street parking available in the area. It is a quick 10 minute drive to all the neighborhoods in the downtown area including Lower Broadway and the Riverfront, Midtown, 12th South, Vanderbilt, the Gulch, Germantown, and the State Capitol. And it is a quick 15-20 minute ride from the airport. All metered street parking across the city is free after 6 pm and is usually pretty easy to find on weeknights. 

It is about a 10 minute walk to One City, an area with some restaurants and a coffee shop, and a 20 minute walk to the Parthenon at Centennial Park. It is also about a 13 minute walk to HCA Healthcare on Park Plaza. 

It is a residential building so please be courteous to the neighbors. I also have another one bedroom unit if you are traveling with a larger group, please see my other listings or message me for availability.
