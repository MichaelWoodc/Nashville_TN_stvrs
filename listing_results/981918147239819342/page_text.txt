Bee our guest at the Music City BeeHyve, a luxury condo within walking distance of Broadway and featuring a private balcony with a bee-utiful view of Nashville’s skyline. With its open layout and modern design, the condo employs a subtle, tasteful décor theme of bees and honey, balancing the splendor of a luxury hotel with the simple comforts of a log cabin. And true to its name, the BeeHyve can offer honey to its guests directly from a local beekeeper. Ask your hosts for availability!

The space
With 3 beds that can accommodate 6 guests, this unit is ideal for business travelers, couples, and families looking for comfortable bedding, high-speed WiFi, quick and easy access to downtown Nashville, or Hyve’s wide range of amenities, including a seasonal pool, on-site security, and a courtyard with fire pits, a grill, a ping pong table. The BeeHyve is also decked out with a fully-equipped kitchen, an in-unit washer/dryer set, and an ample amount of indoor and outdoor seating.

Right around the corner, you’ll find fun at Bad Axe Throwing, incredible city views from Fogg Street’s rooftop, delicious local beer at Tennessee Brew Works, live music at the Blue Room, intimate ambiance at the Blind Barber speakeasy, or tasty Bloody Marys at Two Hands!

★ Primary Bedroom has a queen-size bed.
★ Secondary Bedroom has a queen-size bed.
★ Living Room has a sofa with a queen-size pull-out mattress and a smart TV.
★ Full Bathroom with shower-tub and double vanity.
★ In-unit laundry room with washer/dryer set, pool towels, umbrellas, and pool tote bags. 

This condo is located on the 3rd floor of the 6-story Hyve building. At Hyve, you can enjoy the comforts of a luxury condo and its amenities – concierge, security presence, a seasonal pool, a 24/7 state-of-the-art gym, a co-working space, and more – while being positioned only four blocks away from Broadway. You can spend your stay exploring the riches of downtown Nashville without ever needing to step into a car, or you can gather friends and family together to enjoy Hyve’s lounge areas, community grills, and views of the city from the unit’s private balcony. This spacious unit also includes a fully equipped kitchen, stocked with coffee, tea, and condiments. Whether you leave or stay, Hyve has everything you need for an unforgettable trip to Music City.

Guest access
Guests may come and go as they please with seamless self-check-in via smart locks (utilizing the Latch App). Full access instructions are provided two days before arrival.

PARKING 
The building offers a free, secured parking garage on the upper level for guests and owners, with 34 spaces available on a first-come, first-serve basis (we kindly ask to limit to one vehicle per reservation). An on-site paid public parking garage can be found on the lower level. In this public garage, Hyve guests can park for a flat rate of $20. Free street parking is also available. EV Charging is now available in both garages.

If you arrive using a ridesharing service, you should be dropped off in front of the building on 7th Ave S. Use the ramp/walkway to access the lobby, then use the Latch App to open the lobby door. If we are not present to greet you, locate the elevator and take it to your unit for self check-in.

🧳 Complimentary luggage storage is available with our on-site concierge, Thursday to Monday. from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Cleaners cannot hold luggage inside units. No luggage storage is available Tuesday and Wednesday, however, our neighbor Tennessee Brew Work can hold luggage for free (if you buy a drink or food),  starting at 10AM for guests who need storage on these days.
