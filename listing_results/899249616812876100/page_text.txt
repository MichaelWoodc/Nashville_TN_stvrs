The space
• Riverside Roost is your peaceful Nashville getaway with beautiful riverfront views and quick access to the city's top attractions
• Enjoy your morning coffee on the covered balcony overlooking the Cumberland River
• Spend the day exploring Broadway, East Nashville, Music Row, and the Grand Ole Opry before returning to unwind by the water
• Prepare meals in the updated kitchen, relax with a movie, or cool off in the seasonal community pool
• Perfect for couples, small families, business travelers, and weekend getaways

🌅 Indoor & Outdoor Living
• Bright living room with large Smart TV
• Private covered balcony overlooking the Cumberland River
• Comfortable seating throughout the living area
• Community seasonal outdoor pool (open approximately late May through early September)
• Community fitness center

🛏️ Bedrooms & Sleeping Arrangements (Sleeps 4)
• This home includes 1 bedroom and 1 bathroom
• Bedroom 1: King bed, TV, ensuite bathroom; sleeps 2
• Living Room: Full-size sleeper sofa; sleeps 2
Bathroom:
• Ensuite Bathroom: Single vanity and shower/tub combo

🍳 Kitchen & Dining
• Fully equipped kitchen with updated appliances
• Tea table seating for 2
• Cookware, bakeware, dishes, and utensils are provided
• Salt, pepper, and cooking oil are not provided

🏖️ What's Included
• Fresh bed linens and bath towels
• Starter supply of toiletries and paper goods
• Free WiFi throughout the condo
• Access to the community pool and fitness center

📍 Location Highlights
• Riverfront location on the Cumberland River
• Minutes from Downtown Nashville and Broadway
• Close to Five Points in East Nashville, Music Row, and The Arts District
• Short drive to the Ryman Auditorium, Grand Ole Opry, Opryland Hotel, and Nashville's best dining, shopping, and entertainment

🚗 Parking, Safety & Notes
• Parking is available for up to 2 vehicles in the open community parking lot.
• This home is not pet friendly. If there is found evidence of an animal's presence during your stay or post check-out, guests will be charged an additional cleaning fee.
• The seasonal community pool is typically open from late May through early September.
• Check-in is 4:00 PM and checkout is 10:00 AM.
