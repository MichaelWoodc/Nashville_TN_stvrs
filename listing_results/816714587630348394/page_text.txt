The space
Welcome to The Vinyl — where Nashville’s music culture meets elevated group living. 🎶✨

This 4 bedroom, 4 bathroom home was designed to feel like your own private backstage lounge — blending music-inspired design, comfortable luxury, and a rooftop setup that turns every night into an event.

The highlight? Your rooftop deck — built for competition, cocktails, and skyline views that hit even better at sunset.

Inside, the home balances style with function:

• Open living space perfect for group hangouts
• Fully stocked kitchen + coffee station for meals or morning resets
• TVs in every bedroom for personal downtime
• In-unit washer/dryer for longer, seamless stays
Step outside and experience one of the best rooftop setups around — grill going, drinks flowing, music playing, and games keeping the energy up all night.

Located just minutes from Broadway, you’re close to the action while still having a space that feels like your own.

From the host
East Nashville is a vibrant and eclectic neighborhood located on the eastern side of the Cumberland River in Nashville, Tennessee. Known for its unique character, diverse culture, and artistic community, East Nashville has become a hub for creativity and individuality. The area is characterized by its historic homes, trendy boutiques, and an abundance of locally-owned restaurants, bars, and music venues.
