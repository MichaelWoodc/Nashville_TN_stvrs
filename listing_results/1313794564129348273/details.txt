=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to "On The Rocks", a one-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment designed with whiskey-themed decor, offering a blend of rustic charm and elegance. Nestled in the heart of Sylvan Park, this apartment is ideally located just one mile from Vanderbilt University and two miles from the lively nightlife of Broadway. Enjoy the convenience of free off-street parking, and take a stroll or jog to Centennial Park via the nearby Greenway.

The space
Welcome to this charming one-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment, thoughtfully designed with whiskey-themed decor that adds a touch of rustic elegance. The bedroom features a comfortable queen-size bed, room-darkening curtains, and a ceiling fan to ensure a restful night's sleep. The bathroom offers a combo tub/shower, perfect for a relaxing soak or a quick refresh.

The full kitchen is equipped for preparing your favorite meals, which you can enjoy in the stylish dining area. After a day of exploring, working, or studying, unwind in the bright, spacious living area, where the whiskey-inspired decor adds a warm and inviting ambiance.

Step outside to the community outdoor space, where Adirondack chairs sit under  café lights amidst tasteful landscaping, creating the perfect spot for evening  relaxation. Located at the end of the street, the Richland Greenway offers easy access for a bike ride to Vanderbilt or a walk/jog to Centennial Park. This apartment is the ideal blend of comfort, style, and convenience.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the private apartment and shared outdoor spaces.

Other things to note
Please be aware that a freight train runs behind the apartment complex, operating irregularly, including during the night. While some guests may not be bothered by the noise, others may find it disruptive. If you are sensitive to noise, we recommend not renting this property.

The train travels from Memphis to Nashville, but it does not blow its horn near the building, so you will mostly hear it as background noise. It is mostly obscured from view by vegetation. To help minimize disturbances, sound and light-reducing curtains and a sound machine are provided.

This apartment is located on the 2nd floor and requires stairs.
