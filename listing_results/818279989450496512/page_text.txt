Things we liked:
The place was clean. Linens were plentiful. Towels set out for everyone.  Complimentary shampoo, conditioner and body wash. Easy to find cutlery and dishes. Had a coffee pot, which was important to us. Located within walking distance of the Farmers Market, Krogers,  Popeye's Chicken and Marathon Village. In a cooler season it would have been walkable to Broadway. It was easy to find Ubers/Lyfts any time of day from this location. The price was comparable to two nice hotel rooms, which is what we would have needed if we went that route.
Things we wished were different:
The bunk bed was rickety. There was no broom! I would have swept the kitchen daily, crumbs build up, and no one likes to step on that. Also no mop,  if any liquids had spilled would have made for a sticky stay. The fridge ice maker was on the fritz. It was sooo slow to make ice. Like two ice waters a day slow.
Conclusion: worked out well for a short trip.
