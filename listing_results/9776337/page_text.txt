Enjoy everything great about Nashville by being in the heart of the city and staying in a beautifully remodeled vintage house “on” Music Row only 3 blocks from Studio B.  This property is great for Corporate Retreats and Family Reunions.  The space has 12 bedrooms and sleeps 32.

The area this stately home is located in has exploded with new and trendy restaurants, venues, and shops in the past few years. The property is close to everything, but it's very quiet, and very peaceful at night.

The space
This house sleeps 32.  The First floor has 4 bedrooms with 5 beds and sleeps 9.  The second floor has 4 bedrooms, 6 beds and sleeps 10.  The third floor has 4 bedrooms, with 7 beds (3 are bunk beds) and 1 small futon.  Third floor sleeps 13. 
Many of the very best restaurants in town are within a half to one mile radius. So, you can take a stroll or quick Uber ride for your favorite activities. It's fun and convenient never needing a car.  This property is on Nashville's trolley line.  The trolley will take you to many of the best restaurants.  

There is plenty of privacy as well as large gathering places.  The layout and size of this home allows you to have your own private bedroom and bathroom and yet easily get together with members of your group.  Each floor is permitted as a separate unit.  Metro Gov't Short Term Rental Permit numbers available upon request.   

You will be sent a message with door codes and other important information by 12pm on the arrival date. The door locks on this house are keyless entry with a touchpad where you will enter a code. You will not have to keep up with a key or key card during your stay.

This property has 9 newly tiled bathrooms, tiled showers, new vanities, fixtures etc. Each of the 12 bedrooms has a brand new queen or king sized bed. The bedrooms are fully equipped with dressers, clothes racks, mirrors, side tables and flat screen TVs.  New high end cabinets, quartz counter-tops, tiled back-splashes and stainless steel appliances in both kitchens.  This house is equipped with all new plumbing and tank-less water heaters for instant hot water.  A new high end washer and dryer is on each floor.  This property has a new geothermal heating and air conditioning system.    

Modern convenience meets old world charm.  The master bedroom has an en suite master bath.  The bathrooms have new large luxury tiled showers.  This home is setup to meet all your needs for both vacation and business trips. Includes Wireless internet and cable TV.  65 inch Flat Screen TV in Great Rooms.  50 inch flat screens in Bedrooms. There are 7 available parking spaces directly behind the house.  There is also street parking.  There is unlimited parking on the weekends. 

The Music City Mansion has a complete fire sprinkler system, fire alarm system, and smoke detector system which are all monitored by the fire department.  There is a security system, ever-safe non-breakable windows, fire extinguishers, emergency lights, lighted exit signs and other safety features.  This is the safest place to vacation anywhere.

Walk to weekly music festivals at Centennial Park or to The Pancake Pantry for the best breakfast in town. Attend summer camp, sporting contests, or other events at Vanderbilt which is a few blocks away. Or just fill the kitchen with groceries and enjoy watching movies or entertaining family & friends. There's plenty of room to socialize, eat, sleep, relax, etc.  Relax on the outside porch.

There is a world class recording studio directly behind the house. Let us know if you want to record a song or an entire album. It's the same recording studio Dolly Parton uses for all her music.

Guest access
You have access to the entire Mansion. Front door code will be sent on day of arrival.
