=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Perfect for Bachelorette or Bachelor Party Groups! Introducing Whiskey Glasses, a unique 4-bedroom condo at The Hyve in downtown Nashville, just .7 miles to Broadway. Blending modern style with a Country Music & UT-themed decor, this unit offers a distinctive Music City experience.
