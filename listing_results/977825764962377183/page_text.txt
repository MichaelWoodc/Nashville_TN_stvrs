Guest access

Other things to note
I am not responsible for any building mishaps out of my control such as amenities being out of service etc at the time of your stay. No refunds.

Please understand our cleaners are on tight schedules & in the case they can’t get in they will pass me by. Without prior approval for anything later your grace period will be 15 mins. After the grace period there will be a $15 fee per each 15 mins you stay late after that. If you prevent the scheduled check in for the next guest you will be subsequent to further charges.

Key replacement for the set is $4000 or $200 for one fob. Don’t lose the keys 😬.

Parking in our gated garage is $30 per night. Please let me know that you’ll need parking so it can be set up for you & instructions can be given.
