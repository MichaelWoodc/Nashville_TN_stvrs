★Welcome to the Nashville Star★ Tucked into East Nashville, this 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath, 3-level home sleeps up to 12 guests and blends stylish “Music City” theming with a spacious group-friendly layout. Featuring curated music memorabilia, a signature staircase mural, and multiple private suites, The Nashville Star offers a memorable stay just minutes from Germantown and Broadway’s downtown energy.

The space
What We Love About ★The Nashville Star★...
*Bold Music City theme with a signature staircase mural and curated memorabilia throughout the home
*Prime East Nashville location near top local favorites like Folk, Redheaded Stranger, and Audrey
*Quick 5–7 minute drive to Broadway and downtown Nashville’s iconic Honky Tonk scene
*Flexible multi-level layout designed for groups, families, and large gatherings
*Multiple ensuite bathrooms, balconies, and a third-floor bar area for elevated comfort and convenience

We welcome extended stays and are happy to accommodate guests looking for longer-term reservations, including stays of 30 nights or more!

Inside, the home is thoughtfully designed across three levels to maximize both privacy and shared gathering space. The first floor serves as the central hub with a comfortable living room, open kitchen and dining area, convenient laundry room, and a half bath—ideal for entertaining or unwinding after a day in the city.

The stunning staircase mural leads to the second floor that is comprised of two bedrooms.  The primary suite features a king bed, ensuite bathroom with double sinks and a walk-in shower, a spacious walk-in closet with vanity space, and a private balcony. The second bedroom is built for flexibility and comfort, offering a bunk bed with full mattresses on both levels plus a twin trundle, along with its own ensuite bathroom.

The third floor expands the sleeping arrangements even further with two additional bedrooms and a shared full bathroom. One bedroom includes a full bed and twin daybed alongside a mini bar area with sink and mini fridge, creating a cozy retreat-style space. The second bedroom offers a queen bed, built-in vanity, and a small loveseat for relaxing or getting ready. Two additional twin air mattresses are also available to accommodate larger groups.

Located in the heart of East Nashville, you’ll be surrounded by some of the city’s most creative energy. Trendy restaurants, coffee shops, and live music venues are just minutes away, while Germantown and downtown Nashville are both easily accessible for dining, nightlife, and entertainment.
Whether you’re here for a weekend getaway or a longer group stay, The Nashville Star delivers a vibrant, comfortable, and centrally located experience in true Music City style. 

*As an added courtesy, we wanted to inform you that the unit balcony may be closed off temporarily. 

★Neighborhood★
Located near so many vibrant East Nashville communities, businesses, and restaurants, you'll have the heart of its charm right at your fingertips! Eclectic, artistic, and evolving, this area lends itself to a character filled experience and homage to the dream of Music City. Here you'll be just minutes from the nearby Germantown neighborhood and your culinary senses will delight at the sheer volume of good restaurants that you will have nearby! For starters, Folk, Redheaded Stranger, and Audrey are 2 minutes down the road, and if you are willing to explore a few more short minutes, you'll find a plethora of other incredible dining options! Finally, for those of you wanting to hit up Nashville's famous Honky Tonk bars on Broadway, you are a quick 5-7 minute drive to downtown!

★Parking★
Please park in the side lot adjacent to the home. There is room for 3 cars. Please try to limit roadside parking for other townhouse owners/guests.

★House Rules★
During your stay, we ask that you remember to be respectful of your neighbors. Quiet hours are from 10pm- 7am. For real, take the party to the Honky Tonks on Broadway. Additionally, thank you for respecting our "no smoking" and "no pets" policy so that we can keep our properties in the best condition possible. Pets include but are not limited to cats, zebras, livestock, and/or spouses. There are also no firearms permitted within the home or on the surrounding property. Should you choose to ignore this, there will be a $300 fine assessed for breaking either of these rules. We thank you for refraining and being respectful of our homes - please don't make us hunt you down for fines as it cuts into our nap time.

*Please note that this home does have outdoor security cameras.

Other things to note
★Before You Arrive★
We want your arrival to be as easy and seamless as possible. After booking, you’ll receive a short Rental Agreement from our local management team to review and sign electronically before your stay. This quick step helps us make sure everything is in place for your arrival while protecting you, the home, and the homeowners who make your stay possible.

★Security Camera Disclaimer★
For your safety and the security of our properties, most of our rentals have a ring doorbell camera or an external camera located on the exterior of the home. No indoor cameras are present - your privacy is fully respected. 

★Damage Waiver★ 
You are renting a private home.  Please treat the property with the respect you would like shown to your own home.  We get that accidents happen – some more sober than others – and we want you to be aware of your Damage Waiver coverage should something get a little wild during your stay.  In an attempt to make this easier for you, the Damage Waiver fee is included in your cleaning fee.  Here’s how it works: By booking with Stay Local Nashville, Inc., you understand and accept that your booking fees include a Damage Waiver.  It is the Guests’ responsibility (that’s you!) to report damage that is caused to the Property during your stay.  The vacation rental Damage Waiver is not insurance, but instead an agreement by Stay Local Nashville, Inc., to replace or repair unintentional damages up to $1,000.00 (one thousand dollars) in total to the rental unit’s interior.  Damages that qualify for this agreement must occur during a Guest’s reservation, be disclosed to management prior to check out, and the Damage Waiver must have been paid in full prior to arrival (don’t worry, it’s wrapped into the cleaning fee!).  Any damages that either exceed $1,000.00 (one thousand dollars), are not disclosed prior to check out, or are deemed intentional, will be charged to the credit card on file, through the Airbnb resolution center. The Damage Waiver does not include theft or intentional or negligent damage and will not be covered by this fee and the Guest will be liable for all costs associated with intentional or negligent damage.  The Guest is liable for any and all costs and fees associated with the vacation rental, including reasonable collection costs and attorney’s fees that are in any way associated with the Guest’s and/or the Guest’s invitee’s breach of this agreement, including but not limited to, money owned under this agreement for damage to the premises, fees, and/or fines. 

★NoiseAware Disclaimer★
 Welcome to our respectful and responsible community. We are pleased to inform you that this property is committed to Rent Responsibly standards. This means we follow best-in-class guidelines to ensure you have a peaceful, safe, and memorable stay with us. Please be mindful of our neighbors during your stay. The property’s quiet hours are in place to ensure that you and our whole community can enjoy a restful night's sleep. We are dedicated to protecting our guests and neighbors from bothersome levels of noise. In an effort to do so, this property is equipped with NoiseAware technology. NoiseAware is a smart home device that measures volume levels throughout the property and allows us to respond to noise nuisances without disrupting your stay. NoiseAware is privacy compliant and is required on this property. NoiseAware is here for your benefit. It helps protect you from aggravating noise from nearby renters or neighbors, as well as proactively prevents noise complaints. If you have any questions please ask. We are committed to your comfort so please let us know if we can do anything during your stay to make it more enjoyable.
