Stay just off Nashville’s popular 12 South corridor in a home that offers comfortable gathering space, a practical layout, and easy access to one of the city’s most loved neighborhoods. With 4 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, tall ceilings, spacious living areas, and a screened porch, this home is well suited for families and groups looking for a convenient home base close to dining, shopping, and local attractions.

Other things to note
Primary renter must be at least 25 years of age. Guests may be asked to present their government issued IDs upon arrival or anytime during their stay. Do not rent this home for another party. The primary renter must be staying in the home.



About the Vacation Rental Unit:

This Nashville vacation rental offers a comfortable and functional setup in a location that makes it easy to enjoy the city. Just off the 12 South area, the home combines spacious interior living with thoughtful features that work well for families and groups. Tall ceilings and large common areas help the home feel open and inviting, while the screened back deck adds a relaxing outdoor space to enjoy Tennessee weather.



Inside, the layout provides flexibility for a variety of group types. The primary bedroom is located on the main floor and includes a king bed, offering convenience and privacy. Upstairs, guests will find three additional bedrooms, each with a queen bed, with one guest room also including a trundle for extra sleeping space. A sleeper sofa in the living room adds additional flexibility for the group.



The upstairs loft sitting area gives guests another place to spread out, whether for quiet time, conversation, or winding down after a day in the city. Recent improvements, including fresh interior paint, a new washer and dryer, and a new dishwasher, help make the home feel refreshed and well maintained.

This home stands out for its combination of space, comfort, and location. Guests can enjoy roomy interior living areas, a screened porch for relaxing outdoors, and a layout that gives everyone space to settle in comfortably. Whether the plan is to explore Nashville, enjoy the nearby restaurants and shops of 12 South, or simply spend time together at the home, this property offers a convenient and welcoming place to stay.



Parking:

Street parking may be limited directly in front of the home. The best free street parking option is on Lawrence Street. If space is not available there, 11th Street is a good alternative and typically has additional parking. Please do not park in the alleyway, as vehicles left there may be towed and may block access for neighboring garages.



About the Community/Property:

Located just off the 12 South area, this home places guests close to one of Nashville’s most popular neighborhoods for dining, shopping, and exploring. The area is known for its walkability, local restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and well-known murals. Guests can enjoy the convenience of being near the energy of the city while still having a comfortable residential home base to return to at the end of the day.



Other details to note:



The fireplace is not available for guest use and must remain off.



Please bag all trash securely and place it in the black garbage cans located inside the garage area before checkout. Do not place trash in the outside cans, as they are not designated for this property. Our cleaning team will handle garbage collection during turnover.



Upon booking, you’ll receive timely communications about your trip. Access to your professionally cleaned and sanitized home is also convenient with our custom keyless locks. And, if you need anything before, during or after your stay, our local team is ready to help!



Please be aware that there will be NO refunds for Wi-Fi issues as it’s not guaranteed. If reliable Wi-Fi is a must, you are welcome to bring your own internet hotspot.



Non-negotiable rules: No Smoking or Vaping, No Pets, No Events or Parties, No Firearms. Quiet hours are from 10pm to 7am (unless posted signage indicates otherwise).
