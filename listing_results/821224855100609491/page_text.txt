The space
From your first coffee to your last laugh of the night, Vibe 857 delivers the full Nashville experience.


Vibe 857 is a vibrant, four-bedroom condo in the heart of East Nashville—a hot spot for creativity, music, and bold local flavor. This 4BR/4BA retreat sleeps 10 with ease, and its layout is made for group trips where comfort and space matter. Whether you're toasting a birthday, planning a sister getaway, or just spending time with old friends, this stylish stay puts you right where you want to be: walkable to local gems, a quick ride to Broadway, and photo-ready from every angle.

MAJOR HOWEVER. The space was not properly cleaned. There was a SIGNIFICANT amount of dust throughout, including blinds, decor, baseboards, and behind beds/furniture. One baseboard even had old food particles on it. Several things were broken or damaged, including a bent bed frame, broken decor, and ripped pillows. 
The home did not have that clean smell and could have used air fresheners. Full-length mirrors would have also been helpful.
Overall, the home had great potential, but the lack of upkeep and care was disappointing.
