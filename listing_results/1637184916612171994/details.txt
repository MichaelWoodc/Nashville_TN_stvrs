=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Perfect for family, girls' trip, group getaway or music-filled weekend. This stylish East Nashville townhome is a short Uber/Lyft drive to Broadway, Grand Ole Opry, Bridgestone & Nissan Stadium - while giving you a quiet place to recharge & free parking in the private garage. 
Designed with a bold honky tonk vibe,  we provide coffee, shampoo, etc.. The home comfortably sleeps up to 9 and is close to coffeeshops. Experience ALL that Nashville has to offer without sacrificing comfort or style.

The space
Three spacious bedrooms, two with king size beds and on-suite bathrooms, one with a queen and twin  bed- all with comfy Nectar mattresses PLUS a queen sofa bed. Blackout curtains, full length mirrors. Each designed for comfort and relaxation.

Quick and easy access to Nashville's top attractions with an easy Uber/Lyft ride or walk to a nearby coffee shop, Iconic Grimmy's records or other East Nashville spots. Don't just go to Broadway, check out the East Nashville Vibe spots.

The airport is just 15 minutes away -  making it an easy trip.

Guest access
Guests will have complete access to the home and grounds with self-check in. Our garage fits two standard sized vehicle, no oversized vehicles. Additional paid, DAYTIME ONLY, parking is available within the complex visitor spots but is limited. 
The HOA is strict about enforcing this policy. Unfortunately, this rule is out of our hands.
If you need additional parking - it is 
available within the area (side streets - try Casper Ave.) but can be limited especially during peak seasons and weekends.

Free Parking: Private garage parking spots for 2 standard vehicles included for your convenience. Please note that large vehicles may not fit in the attached garage (18' 5"). There is additional free street parking nearby on Casper Ave, Zophi St or Dozier for additional overnight parking subject to availability.
