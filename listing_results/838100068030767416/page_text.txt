CozySuites proudly brings you the finest newly built condos in the famous Nashville Music Row and heart of Midtown. Steps away from THE best shopping, dining, and live music in Nashville, this locally inspired condo is perfect for leisure, business, and family. After a long day exploring the town, come back to the comforts of home like lightning-fast fiber internet, coffee, and complimentary streaming services.

The space
This CozySuites is suitable for a family, group of friends, or business travelers. It has all of the amenities necessary to enjoy your stay and all the comforts of home.

Great amenities await you!
- modern design infused with authentic Nashville inspired decor 
- a fully equipped kitchen 
- 100mbps+ internet, modern decor, a Smart-TV with streaming apps installed!
- garden area to lounge and relax with friends!
- complimentary coffee
- community garden with cabana, 2 outdoor TVs, gas grill, and fire pit!

Bedroom Configuration:
Studio: 1 queen bed and 1 sofa bed

Wheelchair accessible!

Please know that any sign of smoking is a violation of our stay agreement, which may result in immediate termination and recovery fees beginning at $500.
