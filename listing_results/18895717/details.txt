=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Modern tiny home in a hip urban setting. Don’t let the name fool you! It has more space than a typical 2 bed hotel room. Perfect for couples/families, or remote work. The space
Welcome to our space! Here you will find an open-concept tiny home, perfect for relaxing after a day of city fun! The most comfortable queen-sized bed awaits you in the custom-built, open loft! There is also a queen-sized sleeper sectional sofa in the living room area, with an optional memory foam topper for extra comfort. A small dining table is perfect for enjoying a meal in, or catching up on any work you may need to do. You will also find a fully stocked kitchenette, with an over-sized mini fridge, microwave, two burner cook top, coffeemaker, tea kettle, and mini blender. The spacious bathroom features a full size tub/shower combo and plenty of space for your luggage and belongings.

For your convenience, we also have a pack-and-play available for your little one (free of charge), and a Slumberpod (pack-and-play blackout tent) available for $10/night. Just let us know if you need these prior to your arrival and we will set them up for you!

Please note, we do not allow pets.

Guest access
The detached tiny house is located in our backyard, with its own private entrance and off-street, gated parking space! You have full access to the tiny house, and even a small section of yard that is fenced off for you. The luxury of complete privacy, with the benefit of having hosts right next door anytime you need!
