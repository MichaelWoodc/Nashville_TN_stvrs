The space
Welcome to Heading South — a design-forward Nashville retreat where art, architecture, and skyline views come together. 🎨✨

This thoughtfully curated 4 bedroom, 4 bathroom home was created for guests who appreciate elevated interiors, intentional design, and a space that feels as good as it looks.
From custom finishes to layered textures and bold details, every corner of the home is designed to feel collected, not cookie-cutter.

The centerpiece? A private rooftop deck with sweeping skyline views — a refined space for morning coffee, sunset cocktails, and quiet moments above the city.

Inside, the home balances form and function:

• Architecturally designed living spaces with natural light and clean lines
• Fully equipped kitchen + curated coffee setup for slow, elevated mornings
• Multiple lounge areas that feel both social and intimate
• In-unit laundry + seamless layout for longer, comfortable stays

This is a space where you can reset, host, and experience Nashville at your own pace.
