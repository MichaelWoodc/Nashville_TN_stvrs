=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
For 100 years, home to musicians and songwriters, now you can stay in this newly renovated piece of history on Nashville’s coveted Music Row! Located next door to the famous RCA Victor Studios and Warner Bros. Studios… see our photo of Elvis posing for the camera outside our building! Steps to Downtown’s favorite watering holes, restaurants, museums and shops. A 20 min. walk or $6 ride to Broadway!

The space
Built in 1915 and renovated in 2019, this space features historical pre-World War I details. The property represents one of the few remaining of its type still standing in Nashville. Decorated with high-end modern interiors and equipped with everything you'll need to live like a local. 

Featuring: original hardwood floors, quartz countertops, high-end fixtures and finishes, many windows with beautiful natural light, blackout wood blinds, 50" HDTVs with extensive channels in living and bedrooms, secure coded access, cookware, and luxury combed cotton-sateen linens, down-alternative duvets and pillow, and C.O. Bigelow shampoo, conditioner, and body wash. Shared front-load washer and dryer in the building.

Guest access
Guests are provided an access code to the building's secure entries on Music Square East and Roy Acuff Place and assigned a private access code to their Apartment. Secure, Private and Simple!
