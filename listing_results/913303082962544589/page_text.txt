LOCATION! LOCATION! LOCATION!!  All the charm & walkabilty of Nashville’s most coveted neighborhood combined with the luxury of staying in a NEW modern townhouse. Studio C at Harmony on Music Row is a 3 level property featuring open floor plan with bright, warm and inviting areas for gathering, entertaining, and relaxation. Every room in the house thoughtfully designed to provide guests with a Nashville Music Row experience. Perfect for your large group of friends & family!

The space
Studio C @ HMR is a beautifully constructed 4 Bd/4.5 bath home which offers guests 2,645 sq ft of space that features top-of-the-line finishes, oversized windows, and an abundance of natural light.  Offers a modern sophisticated kitchen that comes equipped with GE Monogram appliances, natural gas cook surface, quartz countertop island, sleek cabinetry, white oak hardwood flooring throughout, and luxurious bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms designed with mosaic tile and contemporary lighting. Home is equipped with the incredible scent of Hotel Collection diffuser. Each aspect of Studio C @ HMR is outfitted to inspire & reflect the beauty and history of Music City.

Harmony on Music Row is perfectly situated in the heart of Music Row. Centrally located between Belmont and Vanderbilt University and is also only 2 miles to Nashville's hottest offerings… Hillsboro Village, Edghill, The Gulch, 12 South, & Downtown / Broadway Honky-Tonks.

This space of perfect for family and friends, large groups, or business executives looking to enjoy Nashville in contemporary luxury with excitement and relaxation. 

**NO LOCAL BOOKINGS - we reserve the right to cancel anyone related to Nashville and within 60 miles.
