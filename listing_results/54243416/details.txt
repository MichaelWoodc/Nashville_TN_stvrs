=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Our guesthouse is the perfect space to chill after enjoying all that Nashville has to offer. We are centrally located to an abundance of delicious restaurants (Audrey, Folk, Redheaded Stranger), coffee shops, breweries, and just minutes (less than 3 miles) from downtown. East Nashville is one of the coolest neighborhoods in Nashville and has so much character and a special vibe. Take in everything this wonderful city has to offer and return back to a cozy retreat to relax and unwind.

The space
Our guesthouse is located at the back of our property above our detached garage. You will walk up the stairs and be greeted by the living room, kitchenette and dining area. Off the living room is the bedroom that offers a king size bed and full bathroom.

Guest access
The guesthouse is accessed from the alley behind our house.

Guests may park on the parking pad next to the guesthouse. This parking pad can fit up to 2 cars. Alternatively there is plentiful street parking but would require half a block walk down the alley.

Several restaurants (Audrey, Folk, Redheaded Stranger, Xiao Bao) and coffee shops (Forevermore, Momento, Retrograde) are within walking distance and we are centrally located to both downtown and East Nashville so Ubers/Lyfts are fairly inexpensive.
