=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome Nashville!  For all the comforts of home, you will find it here, in a brand new remodeled studio flat.  This space is in a quiet established neighborhood in Donelson, 3 miles from the airport and 15 minutes from all the action of downtown.

The space
If you are here on a vacation, you can enjoy the exciting sightseeing and nightlife Nashville has to offer and come home, relax and enjoy the solitude. If you are in town on business, this is a perfect place to regroup before your next day of meetings.

The kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances for you to make your favorite dish or just heat up a meal. There is a comfortable queen size bed and a pullout single bed.   
For your convince, there is a washer and dryer in the en-suite. 

***Weekly and monthly prices are also available by request!** 

We are conveniently close to several of Nashville’s favorite attractions,  The Grand Ole Opry House,  Gaylord Opryland Hotel, Opry Mills Mall,  and The Hermitage; home of President Andrew Jackson. There are also two water parks near by, Wave Country and Nashville Shores. The flat is short walk to the Stones River Greenway. We are also 15 minutes from all the action downtown.  

This Tranquil Studio Flat has a private entrance and is nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac and is located on the basement level of my home.  Through the French doors, the patio overlooks a private two-acre secluded yard.   The spacious back yard is also a visiting spot for deer living in the field beyond our property.

Guest access
There is plenty of off street parking on the private paved driveway.
