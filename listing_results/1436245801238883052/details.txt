=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Boots & Blush  — two West End homes under one address, built for groups that go big. 45 beds, 7 baths, and two private rooftops with skyline views make it the right fit for bachelor parties, bachelorette weekends, birthdays, and family trips. Kick back in the open-concept living space, head up to the rooftop, and ride 2 miles to Broadway. Steps from Vanderbilt, Centennial Park, and every Nashville hotspot

The space
Two connected homes under one roof, set up as a bachelor/bachelorette combo — one side for the bride's group, one side for the groom's. The layout also works well for wedding parties, birthday trips, family reunions, or any larger group that needs more space than a single home offers.

Inside, you'll find modern decor and accent walls throughout. Each side has an open main floor with a living area, a fully equipped kitchen

Each home has its own game room with a pool table and wet bar. Groups can use them separately or together, depending on how the weekend plays out.

Upstairs, each side opens to a private rooftop with lounge seating — a good spot to relax in the evening or gather the group before heading out.

You can book one side for a smaller group or the whole property for a larger one.
