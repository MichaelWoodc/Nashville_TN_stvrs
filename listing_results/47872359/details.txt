=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Relax in our cozy Southwest-meets-Nashville basement suite, on a quiet dead-end street just 15 minutes from downtown Broadway and the airport. You'll have a private entrance, peaceful patio, 65" TV, fast Wi-Fi, and a handy kitchenette. Ideal for couples, remote workers, and small families who want a calm, comfortable home base, not a party pad. Off-street parking and easy rideshare access, with great local and international restaurants nearby.

The space
You’ll enter the basement guest suite through your own private back entrance, stepping through a peaceful patio area. Inside, the open-concept living space features a comfy oversized sectional, a TV, and a DVD player—perfect for relaxing. The cozy dining nook and vintage-style kitchenette are stocked with a small fridge/freezer, hot/cold filtered water dispenser, Keurig, electric kettle, and microwave.

The full bathroom includes a shower/tub combo, a separate water closet, and plenty of storage in the linen closet. One bedroom features a queen bed, a small window for natural light, a hanging rack, and a mid-century-inspired dresser. The second bedroom is larger, also with a queen bed, and opens to the backyard with beautiful atrium doors. It gets great natural light (or can be fully dark with blackout curtains), and includes a full dresser and a vanity that’s perfect for getting ready or doing a little desk work.

Guest access
You're welcome to enjoy the patio, and entire basement guest suite. Locked doors to our storage room and stairway will have signage. You'll get a keypad code to come and go. Just one thing: please don't lock the bottom door handle when you head out; the keypad handles entry, and a locked handle means we'd have to come down from upstairs to let you back in.

Other things to note
Please note that this space is in the basement of our home.  We're a family with a young kid and a friendly dog, so you might occasionally hear some life happening in the house above. If you need total silence, this probably isn't the right fit, but we do our best to keep it peaceful. Quiet hours are 8pm to 7am, and we provide a white noise machine and earplugs if that helps.
