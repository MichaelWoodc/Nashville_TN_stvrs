=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Connect with nature at this unforgettable escape in Nashville's backyard. This treehouse is nestled in a Tennessee hardwood forest in a hollow. Close to the city, but away from it all, it's a perfect place to retreat from normal life. This isn't a tree fort. It's a tiny house with a loft in the trees over a trickling spring fed creek. It's private with all windows facing the forest. All the fun of being a kid w/ comforts of home like toilet, ac, electric fireplace, heater & 3 season hot shower.

The space
The treehouse is down a trail about fifty yards past the main house. You will climb stone steps up the side of the hill and enter the treehouse from the porch. Lots of glass means views of the forest all around, but the windows face away from the main property so it feels private inside. The main room has two comfy chairs, a kitchenette with glasses, cups, plates, etc. A mini fridge and microwave make heating things up easy and a coffee maker for those cozy mornings. The "outhouse" is on the front deck with a tankless toilet. On the back deck you can relax by the trickle of a natural spring. If you want to shower, we have a heated outdoor shower on the back deck as well. (unavailable Dec.-Feb. but we lowered the rates then by 20%) Washing inside is with a five gallon glass pitcher with a bowl for a sink. Slightly rustic, but exceptionally cute. You will sleep upstairs in a queen bed with lush linens and will climb a steep staircase ladder to the loft. It's not the best choice for people who need special assistance. It is certainly not ADA compatible. But for the adventurous and able bodied, it can't be beat!

Guest access
There are two other homes on the property that are occupied. Everyone has their own spaces and respects each other's privacy. No one will come up to the treehouse while you are there. The owners are onsite in the main house and can be reached if you need anything, but otherwise, will give you your space.

Other things to note
The path is lit to the treehouse at night, but if you have a flashlight, bring it. It gets pretty dark on our road. The road turns into a gravel driveway and you will go past the White House and turn left to park by the "parking" sign just behind the house. There is a gravel path to the treehouse.
