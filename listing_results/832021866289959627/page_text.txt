Lovely 1 Bedroom Condo close to Broadway! Approximately a 10 min Uber to all the exciting Broadway action but also located in a quiet area in Midtown to ensure a restful sleep. Self check-in by keypad entry. New complex built in 2019. Sleeps up to 6. There is a large bed and a sofa bed. In the closet, there is an extra mattress as well as pillows and sheets.

Includes one free parking space and complementary water and coffee. Washer and Dryer located in the unit. Detergent provided.

The space
1 bedroom condo.

Guest access
Entire condo. The condo is on the 2nd floor with access by stairs. I apologize but there is no elevator.

Other things to note
WELCOME TO NASHVILLE! Nashville has it all and it has something for everyone. 

Nashville attracts some of the biggest artists in the world! Come check out a sporting event: Tennessee Titans, Nashville Predators and Nashville FC and many other collegiate sporting events.

Other attractions include: Grand Ole Opry, Parthenon, Cumberland Park, Gaylord Opryland Report, Nashville Zoo, Tennessee Sports Hall of Fame and Museum, and Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum, among many others.

It is a perfect destination for a Girls' Weekend, Boys' Weekend, Romantic Weekend or Family Getaway!
