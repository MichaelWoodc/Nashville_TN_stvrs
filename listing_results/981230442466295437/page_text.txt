The space
Welcome to the Nash Bash Basecamp—an exclusive private space built for unforgettable group stays in Nashville! You’ll have all 8 stylish 1-bedroom units, side-by-side on the second floor with elevator access. And since these are the only units on the floor, your group will have the entire level completely to yourselves.

Each unit includes:
– A private bedroom with a queen bed
– A living room with a queen sleeper sofa
– A full bathroom
– A fully equipped kitchen with full-size appliances
– An in-unit washer and dryer
– Smart TV, fast Wi-Fi, and modern, cozy furnishings

In total, the space sleeps up to 32 guests, with:
– 8 queen beds
– 8 queen sleeper sofas

This setup gives your group the best of both worlds: personal space when you want it, and shared space just steps away when it’s time to gather. Host dinners, game nights, or just hang out across the units—all without ever leaving your private floor. It feels like your own boutique hotel, but with more comfort, space, and flexibility.

🅿️ Parking is easy and included:
– 8 reserved parking spots (one per unit)
– Additional spaces available upon request

🚪 Building Features:
– Elevator (and stair) access to your private floor
– The ground floor hosts a boutique bank and Studio Goddess—Nashville’s #1-rated bachelorette party experience

🍕 ☕ Just steps from the building:
– A pizzeria
– A Mexican restaurant
– Two local coffee shops

📍 Location:
– Just 2 miles from Broadway and downtown Nashville
– Quick, low-cost Uber or Lyft rides to all the action
– Set in a convenient area that offers breathing room without sacrificing access

Whether you're planning a bachelorette bash, family reunion, wedding weekend, or corporate retreat, the Nash Bash Basecamp is a rare gem—private, spacious, beautifully designed, and exclusively yours!
