=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to The Lofts at Noel Court - two side-by-side condos in the heart of downtown Nashville, steps from Broadway & Printer's Alley.

- Sleeps 12 | 2 bedrooms | 12 beds | 3 baths
- Two complete condos w/ private entries & keypad self check-in
- Full kitchens, dining & living areas in each unit
- WiFi, A/C & dedicated workspace
- Pet-friendly | Paid parking off premises
- Walk to Broadway, Bridgestone Arena & Music City Center

The space
The beat of Nashville pulses just outside your door. Perched two blocks from Broadway and across from the legendary Printer's Alley, The Lofts at Noel Court at 3rd Avenue North put you within walking distance of the honky-tonks, venues, and flavors that make Music City one of a kind. Whether you're here for a weekend of live music, a family gathering, or a group getaway, these two side-by-side condos offer the space and independence to make the trip memorable.

Sleeping arrangements
Each condo features one bedroom with a king bed and its own ensuite bath. Additional sleeping space includes two twin trundles and two sofa beds per unit - together accommodating up to 12 guests across both condos. The bedrooms sit in the interior rooms of each unit, keeping them quieter and better suited for a restful night after a long evening out on Broadway.

Living spaces
Both loft-style condos are laid out with generous living rooms, comfortable seating, and a dining area that flows naturally into the kitchen. A half bath on the main level of each unit adds everyday convenience. Extra pillows, blankets, and linens are on hand, and towels, bed sheets, and toiletries are provided so you can settle in without a trip to the store.

Kitchen & dining
Each full kitchen comes equipped with a refrigerator, freezer, stove, oven, microwave, and dishwasher - everything needed for a home-cooked meal or a quick breakfast before heading out. You'll find cooking basics, dishes, silverware, a coffee maker, wine glasses, a toaster, a blender, and a baking sheet. Pull up chairs around the dining table and share a meal before the night gets started.

Amenities & essentials
Both condos include WiFi, air conditioning, heating, a ceiling fan, and a dedicated workspace - handy if you're blending work with your trip. Each unit has its own private entrance and smart lock for keypad self check-in. On the safety side, you'll find smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher, and exterior and hallway security cameras (cameras are in common areas only - not inside the units). Paid parking is available off premises for added convenience.

Location
Sitting on 3rd Avenue North between Church Street and Union Street, you're steps from the Music City Center, Bridgestone Arena, and Nissan Stadium. East Nashville, West Nashville, and the city's green spaces and universities are all a short ride away. Everything that defines a Nashville visit is within easy reach.

The details
These condos are pet-friendly and available for long-term stays. Each unit operates independently with no shared interior door between them - giving your group the flexibility to stay connected while keeping your own space.
