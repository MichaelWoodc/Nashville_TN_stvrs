The space
Two front doors open onto one shared backyard built for a crowd, with a barrel sauna, giant chess set, and hammocks strung along a mural-lined fence between the homes. "The Gambler" has its own full kitchen and living room downstairs, bedrooms upstairs, and a rooftop deck with a hot tub, fire pit, and bar seating for sunset drinks over the skyline.

Inside, a poker table sits under guitar-print wallpaper for game night. "The Retreat" mirrors that setup with its own kitchen and living space, plus a rooftop hot tub and a mini golf course with a working putting green. Its game room centers on a pool table beneath a Johnny Cash mural, where the group tends to land after dark.

Spend the afternoon at Geodis Park, watch the skyline turn pink from either rooftop hot tub, putt a round of rooftop mini golf, trade shots at the poker or pool table once the sun's down, then split back to two full homes to actually sleep.
