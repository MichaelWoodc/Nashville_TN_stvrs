=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Lodge at Pilot Knob at The Pilot Knob Retreat i is a historic 1800's home that has been fully restored. This 3 bedroom cabin sleeps up to 13 guests and is one of three properties on a quiet, private 3 acre lakefront estate. Only 3.5 miles from Long Hollow Gardens, less than 1 mile from interstate access, ER, shopping, restaurants and all other needs.

The space
The home sits on the front of the property with a lakeview and is surrounded by trees that offer the ability to explore with a short hike that leads you to the water's edge so you and your guest can enjoy time & activities by the water.

The outdoor area features 3 covered porches and a side deck. The back covered porch is oversized and overlooks the in-ground salt water pool. The exterior also has ample outdoor seating, a fire pit, and grill—perfect for an evening under the stars!

The home is designed to provided spaces for connection and restoration for you and your guests. On the main floor, you can gather in the living room for great conversation or game night, enjoy lounge time in the library, and there's plenty of room for everyone to eat a family-style meal around the big table in the dining room.

At The Pilot Knob Retreat, we are proud to offer communal lake access for all the cabins. This means that every guest staying at our estate has the wonderful opportunity to enjoy and make unforgettable memories by the lake. Whether you're looking to swim, fish, or simply relax by the water, our communal lake access ensures that everyone can indulge in the natural beauty and serenity of the lake during their stay.

At The Pilot Knob Retreat, you'll find simplicity and sanctuary while being just minutes from Kroger Marketplace for all of your grocery needs, as well as Vietnam Veterans Blvd. for convenient access to nearby restaurants, stores, and activities. Downtown Nashville is only a 30 mile drive
