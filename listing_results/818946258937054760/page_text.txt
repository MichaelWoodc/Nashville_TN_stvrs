Get ready for an awesome trip to Music City in this side by side double listing MEGA HOUSE! REACH OUT TO OWNER! Place has everything you need for the perfect large group trip! 5 minute walk to the Heart of the Gulch, 5 minute walk to midtown and a 8 minute WALK TO DOWNTOWN. These homes offer it all!

The space
Brand new homes in the heart of Nashville and in the center of the Music. Reach out to the Owner and message if interested!!!!!!! You may not/likely do not need two homes and there is enough room likely in one because the homes are so big!!!! You'll only know if you ask and you can SAVE MONEY!!
