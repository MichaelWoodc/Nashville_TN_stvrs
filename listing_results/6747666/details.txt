=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Safe, modern, private, quiet neighborhood,  3 bedrooms each with TV and Blu-ray, 2 full baths, covered front porch, Spacious back deck with grill, Fireplace, outdoor fire bowl, Peace And Quiet yet Close To Everything.

The space
Cumberland Cottage is tucked on a quiet waterfront street that is steeped in history, with several famous old school Opry Stars living nearby. It's private and safe here. There is very little car traffic so it's great for walking around or riding bikes. We see deer, geese and turkey regularly, and enjoy fishing in the Cumberland River. Just down the street is a very quiet and historic park. It's a special neighborhood with friendly folks. Yet, it’s just minutes from the famous Gaylord Opryland Hotel, Opry Mills Mall and THE GRAND OLE OPRY. Within 4 miles there are over 2 dozen Restaurants & Nashville’s biggest Water Park. Downtown is only 15 minutes away, much closer than 95% of the waterfront homes in the greater Nashville Area. Two great golf courses are within 4 miles, and a taxi service is based only 1.5 miles away.
Once you get here, you may not feel the need to leave.  There's a fireplace in the living room and an outdoor fire bowl in the private back yard that's perfect for entertaining or relaxing.  If you like to cook, you’ll find a well equipped kitchen, with all the pots, pans, and all small appliances of home. Outside on the back deck there’s a gas grill with a side burner where you fishermen can fry up the day’s catch. 

Fresh, crispy linens are provided on each bed. White cotton towels are also provided. 

Each morning you’ll enjoy your breakfast and complimentary coffee in the sun filled dining room, or take a walk down the quiet lane and listen to the birds and the river.
In the evening… enjoy the peace and quiet on the front or back porch.  When it’s time to turn in each bedroom has an HD television, the first and second bedrooms sport new king beds and the third bedroom has a new queen bed.  If you have a party of eight,  two deluxe inflatable twin mattresses are available.
****Take note that before new guests arrive, we wash all our linens and blankets INCLUDING the duvet covers, unlike many other rentals.****
Other amenities include Washer & Dryer, , Cable TV with HBO, Blu-Ray players and board games, and High speed WiFi.
All you need to bring is your clothes and your food. We provide coffee and some kitchen amenities. We go the extra mile for our guests and also provide eco -friendly bath soaps , shampoo and conditioners.
When you arrive, we can provide a greeter to help you settle in to the property, or just leave you the door code prior to your stay. Just let us know what you need and we'll do our best to make it happen.
