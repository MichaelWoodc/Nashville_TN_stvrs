Bring the whole group to Nashville! These two side-by-side homes are thoughtfully designed to sleep 20+ guests, making them ideal for family vacations, reunions, friends' trips, and celebrations of every kind. Spacious living areas give everyone room to gather and unwind — while still having their own space when they need it.

The space
Just 20 minutes from Nashville International Airport and a short Uber or Lyft ride from Broadway, you're minutes from the honky-tonks, live music, dining, and nightlife that make this city famous. Come home to a comfortable, spacious retreat built with large groups in mind.

Book your stay and discover why Nashville keeps drawing people back — for the music, the energy, and the memories that last long after checkout.

NOTE: Expect some noise due to active construction happening right across the street.

About Us:
At King Rentals, we’re committed to making your stay as comfortable, easy, and enjoyable as possible. We’re here to help you relax, enjoy your time together, and create wonderful memories with your family and friends.

Other things to note
IMPORTANT – PLEASE READ BEFORE BOOKING:
• Maximum Occupancy: Maximum of 24 guests, including adults, children, and infants. No additional guests are permitted due to fire safety and insurance requirements.
• Age Requirement: All guests must be 18 or older, except children staying with their parents or guardians.
• No Parties: Parties and unauthorized gatherings are strictly prohibited. Noise complaints or law enforcement calls may result in immediate termination of the stay without a refund.
• No Smoking: Smoking is strictly prohibited.
• No Pets: This property has a strict no-pet policy.
• Check-In/Check-Out: Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available for an additional fee starting at $60. Please message us in advance to check availability.
• Cancellations & Travel Insurance: We highly recommend purchasing travel insurance for protection against unexpected cancellations, weather, natural disasters, power outages, and other unforeseen events. We’re unable to offer refunds or rescheduled stays outside Airbnb’s Extenuating Circumstances Policy.
• Starter Supplies: We provide a starter supply of essential items. Please bring your personal toiletries and plan to purchase additional supplies if needed.
• Trash: At checkout, kindly take out the trash and place it in the cans near the garage or in the dumpster.
• Stay Connected: Follow us at @kingrentalspropertymgmt for updates.

We had a total of 14 in town for a Vanderbilt football game. The accommodations were perfect for this size of group and it's an easy walk to the stadium. There is ample street parking and the neighborhood is pretty quiet. We had no issue with the houses themselves, really clean, convenient and a neat setup. Wi-fi was hit or miss, but that could be due to the number of devices. 
The only complaint is that on the day of our reservation we got a call from the host asking that we change our booking. Instead of the adjoining units, we were asked to move to other units within the small townhome community. Not only was this a headache because it was hours before 14 of us were traveling to town, but it also seemed rather dishonest as all communication was done via text or phone call (presumably to not include on the Airbnb app) and the reasoning changed throughout this drama. In the end we held strong, and the host provided the unit, as described. Just a hassle to deal with on check-in day.
