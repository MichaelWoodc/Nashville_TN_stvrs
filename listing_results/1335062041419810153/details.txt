Get ready for a Nashville adventure with your besties! This stylish and centrally located suite is the perfect base for your girls' trip. Just steps away from the vibrant Honky Tonk Highway, you'll be immersed in the heart of the city's music scene.

The space
Here's what awaits you:

•	Prime Location: Walk to legendary bars, honky tonks, live music venues, and delicious restaurants. Explore the city's iconic landmarks and hidden gems from this convenient home base.

- Broadway, Honky Tonk Highway.

- Nissan Stadium.
- The Gulch.
- Music Row.
- Johnny Cash Museum.

- Tennessee Performing Arts Center.

•	Stylish Decor: Enjoy a chic and modern space with a lush king bed, two queen beds, and unique seating and dining areas. Don't miss our vibrant neon selfie wall for the perfect Insta-worthy photo op!

Enjoy luxury drinks at the touch of a button during your stay. Capsules provided, bring your own spirit and your bartender! 

•	Fully Equipped Kitchen: Cook up some pre-party snacks or whip up a late-night feast. Enjoy tea or freshly brewed coffee with complimentary ground coffee, K-cups, and a variety of creamers and Torani syrup. 

•	Relaxing Oasis: Unwind after a long day of exploring Music City sipping your craft cocktail or specialty coffee on the gorgeous courtyard balcony with a pool view. Binge hits on the 4K UHD 65-inch Smart TV, stream on high speed Wi-Fi,  or have an exciting game-night in!

Don't miss out on the ultimate Nashville girls' trip. Book your stay today!
