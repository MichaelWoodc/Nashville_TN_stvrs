Welcome to Espresso House, your cozy luxury retreat in the heart of Nashville. Walk 5 min to Belmont, 8 min to the cafés, restaurants, and boutiques of 12 South, and reach Broadway in just 8 min. 

Wake up to a premium coffee bar with espresso, pour-over, French press, drip, pods, and tea. Unwind with a spa-inspired bathroom, vaulted ceilings, luxurious design, and a peaceful night’s sleep thanks to blackout shades and sound-deadening. Enjoy your luxurious retreat in the heart of Music City.

Other things to note
Espresso House was designed to be more than a place to stay—it was designed to be an experience of your trip.

Nestled in one of Nashville’s most desirable neighborhoods, you’re just a 5-minute walk to Belmont University, an 8-minute walk to the shops, restaurants, cafés, and murals of 12 South, and only an 8-minute drive to Broadway, Bridgestone Arena, Nissan Stadium, and downtown. Explore the city during the day, then return to a peaceful retreat that feels worlds away from the crowds.
