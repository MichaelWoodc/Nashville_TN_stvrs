The space
This three-story East Nashville townhome is built for groups who want room to spread out without sacrificing style.

The main level is an open-concept kitchen and living room with a fully equipped kitchen (island seating for 4, Keurig and drip coffee makers, blender, and toaster), two sofas - one a sleeper for 2 additional guests - leather armchairs, smart TVs, a window-side desk nook for remote work, a half bath, and a private balcony.

On the ground floor, a bunk room with a twin-over-full bed (sleeps 3) sits just steps from its own full bath with a pedestal sink and tiled standing shower - ideal for kids or anyone who'd rather skip the stairs to bed.

Heating and air conditioning run on separate smart-thermostat-controlled zones for upstairs and downstairs, so everyone stays comfortable.
