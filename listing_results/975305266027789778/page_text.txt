Minimalist, modern and cozy, this home sits just minutes from downtown. Its tranquil vibe will ensure that you are rested for a night out in Music City! Sleep soundly in the comfortable beds, enjoy a leisurely morning coffee or tea on the back deck, and get your groove on while listening to records before exploring our beautiful city! This is a friendly, residential neighborhood with 2 walkable coffee shops and 1 restaurant.

This is our home. No parties. No eccessive noise between 11pm - 7am.

The space
Park in the private driveway and enter the house through the front door using a personalized keypad code. You'll step into a serene and spacious living/dining room combo that looks out onto the back deck which is begging to be used for early morning contemplation and late-night conversation. The open-concept kitchen is fully equipped with anything you might need to make a meal. Walk past the coffee bar into the den wherein lies a workspace, floor to ceiling bookshelves, a television, and piano. The hallway is your pathway to 3 alluring bedrooms, and is also where you'll find the half-bath. The first bedroom has a queen size bed, desk, television, and a large, walk through closet leading to a Jack and Jill bathroom. The bunk-bed room has a closet and bookshelf with games. The bunk-bed has a twin mattress on the top and a full mattress on the bottom. The primary bedroom has a king size mattress, a sitting corner, a large walk-in closet with drawers, and an ensuite bathroom.

Guest access
You will have the entire house to yourself during your stay.

Deirdre's place was a wonderful stay, conveniently located just outside the hustle and bustle of downtown.
