=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to The Woodbright designed to make guests feel right at home. This 1960s ranch (884 sq ft) sits on a quiet street and offers peaceful refuge while you explore Nashville. We welcome pets and have a fenced backyard for them to enjoy. You will be a short drive to some great hole-in-the-wall restaurants serving cuisine from around the world. We are 1/2 mile to Dozen Bakery, Primitive Coffee, and Peachtree Grill. From our home you can Uber to BNA, Broadway, or Geodis in about 10 minutes.

The space
Bedrooms
2 Bedrooms have Queen beds and 1 has a full bed. There are extra linens/blankets available should you want a little more warmth. 

Living Area and Kitchen
There are two loveseats and an open view of the kitchen. The dining area is a countertop with stools that is built into a half wall between the kitchen and living area. The kitchen has a gas stove, microwave, refrigerator, and a coffee station with a small drip coffee maker, a French press, and a blade grinder. 

Bathroom
The 30x60 shower is equipped with basic supplies such as soap, shampoo, hair dryer, small first aid kit, etc.

Parking
Multiple spots available off the street including behind the gate

Yard
The entire backyard is fenced and is separated into 2 sections. There is a garage (not accessible) behind the house, and I have the area behind the garage separated by a gate. If you want to always be able to see your dogs, you can keep that gate closed. Or if you want your dogs to roam the entire backyard, you can leave the gate open. I do store a small amount of lumber behind the garage.

Guest access
The entire home is accessible to guests. The garage is not accessible.
