WestWood Shed • A Vacation & Work Stay: Suite #3 is an Accessible Home Office. Four Private Apartment Suites—each with a front living room and open kitchen for entertaining your visitors, with a keyed separate bedroom and laundry—all within your private Suite. Suite #3 is a favorite—an ideal place to work, recover, and vacation. 

Off 8th & Walk to GEODIS. The renowned City of Berry Hill (inside Nashville) has over 60 music agencies & recording studios, and an efficient police department.

The space
Suite #3 has a large private deck, an office-living room & open kitchen with the most ADA features for wheelchair accessibility throughout the apartment. All three lower Suites have a "Visitable" space, which means your visitors with handicap limitations can access your front office-living room area—zero-step entrance & walkways, 36" doors, & wheelchair accessible restroom. In addition to Suite #3's Visitable office-living room—there are 5-foot turning spaces, a lower 23" high bed, an open closet/vanity, a roll-in shower, side-by-side washer/dryer, & raised/lowered electrical outlets. There is a dedicated van-accessible parking spot, a wheelchair ramp that leads to the 1st-floor walkway & an accessible shared patio in front. 

Lock away your private area—each Suite has a keypad on the front office-living room door, with interior keyed doors to lock off the bedroom and shower/laundry area. This allows you to share your office-living room with your visitors and co-workers while blocking access to your personal space. You may deadbolt lock the front door at night for added security, blocking access to the keypad if you've shared your private code with a co-worker or visitor.

Drip coffee makers are provided with 2 flavors of sealed coffee packages—Maxwell House medium dark and dark roast Cafe Bustelo. You may take home whichever package you open if you'd like; open packages will be thrown out. There is also a kettle with green and black tea bags and a few hot chocolates. We also provide 4 organic milk boxes and some munchies.

Every kitchen is well stocked with baking and cookware, both casual and nice plates, stemware, and just about everything you'll need to feel at home. Below the kitchen sink, you'll find dish-cleaning supplies, including dish soap and dishwasher pods. When you leave, the dish brush, if used, goes into the dishwasher, and the sponge is replaced.

Your apartment has a washer and dryer with fragrance-free washer soap. We provide towels and linens, a hair dryer, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, and hand soaps. The linens for the 2nd bed do not remain in the Suite—during the booking process you can arrange for additional guests and the 2nd bed will be completely setup with your prompted request.

Guest access
Each Suite has a keypad on the front office-living room door, and interior keyed doors to lock off the bedroom and shower/laundry area. For added security, you can deadbolt lock the front door at night and block access to the keypad if you've shared your private code with a co-worker or visitor.

If you need additional parking, we're here to assist. Parties over 8 must be prearranged with us for appropriate parking and to ensure no overlapping events with other guests in outdoor spaces.
