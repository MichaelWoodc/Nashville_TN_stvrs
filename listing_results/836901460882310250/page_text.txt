Our home located in the heart of East Nashville is perfect for guests who want a cozy oasis in a quiet neighborhood.  This gorgeous and unique east Nashville home was built in 1935 as a General Store where horses and carriages would line up along waiting for the McGavock Pike Barge which crossed the Cumberland River. We heard that they used to serve "beers and beans." The front living and dining room is what stands from that original General Store.

The space
This home is conveniently located within a short drive to everything East Nashville, Grand Ole Opry,  Opry Mills Mall and only 10 minutes to downtown, Broadway, Top golf, Nissan stadium, and much more!  The home offers 2 beds & 2 baths, comfortably occupying 4 people. If you have 6 in your group, we do have an air mattress that can be provided. You will enter in through the backdoor and be welcomed in by the great fully stocked kitchen with complimentary Starbucks coffee. This home also includes a large porch and backyard perfect for a relaxing or barbecuing with friends or family. The garage will not be available for guest use, the driveway can easily fit 6 cars! If you're looking for a memorable lodging experience while visiting Music City, this is it!

*PETS: Up to 2 dogs are allowed with prior host approval. A $75 fee per pet applies, and breed restrictions may apply. Cats are not allowed. Pets must be kept off all furniture at all times and kenneled when left unattended. Please clean up after your pets. Additional fees may apply for excess pet hair, damage, or extra cleaning.

All properties are contactless checkin. Please be sure to arrive to checkin between 4pm & 8pm. If you do arrive later, you will still be able to checkin but may not have assistance if needed. Checkin time is always 4pm unless approved in writing by management. If you book and edit your checkin time to be earlier than 4pm, you will not be able to checkin until 4pm.

If making a reservation with a same day checkin, checkin time is subject to change depending on cleaning schedule for that day.

Sorry, we do not host locals. Any local that books this property without contacting management first for approval will have the reservation canceled without refund.
