=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Come Hideout in our cute little apartment with one large bedroom, one smaller bedroom (perfect for a second couple or kiddos), a kitchen and a HUGE backyard! This apartment is situated within walking distance to some great East Nashville bars, restaurants, coffee and brunch spots. Just five miles from downtown Nashville, this space makes a great home base for all of your Nashville adventures. Hot Tub is open year round!
We also have a 19year old daughter available for babysitting!

The space
A private entrance around back will lead you directly into a spacious kitchen with full sized refrigerator, microwave and washer/dryer. The two bedrooms and bathroom have all been recently remodeled. Come get cozy!

The hot tub is exclusively yours during your stay and is open year round.

Other things to note
We have a large backyard with a trampoline, massive tree swing and a great patio overlooking it all! Please be warned that due to our cold winters, the backyard doesn't look as lush during the winter months as it does during the spring, summer, and early fall. On a similar note the indoor space might feel a bit small with 4 adults if it's super cold outside. During the warmer months there is much more room to spread out as the outdoor spaces become an extension of the indoors and the gazebo acts as your living room! Just something to think about if you are planning a winter trip. 
Speaking of the warmer months, we participate in NO MOW MAY which means we don't mow the wild flowers and grasses until around June 1st. We will start mowing again when the wild flowers die back around the first week in June.

Please note, we have decided not to put up the pool for summer of 2026 but the hot tub is open all year!
