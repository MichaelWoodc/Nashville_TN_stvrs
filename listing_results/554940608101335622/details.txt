The space
Two full groups, two full homes, one rooftop when everyone wants back together. That is the whole idea behind this Airbnb double: split off during the day, then climb up as the East Nashville skyline turns pink. Home 1 keeps it classic: a king bedroom, hardwood floors, and rooms of stacked beds under abstract wall art.

The open kitchen spills into the living room, where an island with bar seating keeps the cook in the conversation. Home 2 leans harder into the city, with a cowboy hat wall decal, guitar art, and neon Nashville signs glowing over the bedrooms, plus a HOWDY-themed curtain in one bath.

Claim the egg chair under the rooftop hedge wall, grab a spot at the outdoor bar seating, or fire up the rooftop grill while the neon overhead reads "Nashville has my heart." Spend the day near Nissan Stadium and the East Nashville scene, grill up top as the sun drops, trade a few ping pong volleys in the garage, then split back to your own home to actually sleep.

The building's exterior "LOVE" mural makes an easy photo stop on the way in or out.
