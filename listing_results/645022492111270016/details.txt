=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
This property has it all! Walkable to Edgehill Village, Hillsboro Village, Music Row, Belmont, Vandy, 12th South and Midtown, private backyard with heated saltwater pool and killer outdoor entertaining space-complete with roll up garage door which opens between the living room to custom outdoor kitchen/bar and patio area.

The space
{B A C K Y A R D}
The backyard is gated and offers the optimal private oasis! There's a patio/gas firepit area, heated saltwater plunge pool, pool deck area for sunbathing, covered patio area with natural gas grill, custom outdoor kitchen/bar and 65” smart TV. The half bath has dual access from the outdoor kitchen area and the living room. 

{I N T E R I O R}
The entire first floor is open concept, making it a breeze for entertaining. The roll up garage door opens between the living room and the covered patio/kitchen area, combining the outdoor and indoor spaces seamlessly. The gourmet kitchen is fully stocked with all the comforts of home.  

All 3 bedrooms are located upstairs and have full en suite bathrooms. Two of the bedrooms have king beds and the third has a queen bed (all high end mattresses and very comfortable). There is also a second floor deck which is great place for a cup of coffee or to read a few chapters before the day gets started.

Guest access
The property is gated. Guests can comfortably park two vehicles in the gated driveway, a third can also fit if needed.  For additional parking, guests can park on 14th Ave S. 

The back door will be guests main point of entry during their stay as it is directly accessible from the gated driveway and the direction you will most often walk to enjoy all the nearby sites.

Other things to note
The plunge pool is about 4' 2" deep and typically stays heated to 80+ degrees March-October depending on our weather. If the temps drop to below 55 degrees multiple days/nights in a row, it will not heat.

Our fire pit is gas with lava rock. Under no circumstances should wood be used in it.

Gatherings are not to exceed the number of guests in your reservation.

Please note our bedroom/bed accommodations are for 6. We also offer an air mattress if this better suits your sleeping accomodations. Intex brand air mattress is queen sized, elevated 16 in off of the floor with built in air pump for self-inflating.

Please be advised that during peak, summer months we will not be able to accommodate early check ins. We ask that you plan your travel and arrival accordingly-thank you!

Pool service is unfortunately not optional. Typically it is scheduled for Fridays but occasionally it does switch and could fall on a Saturday. It only takes about 30 minutes to service and guests will not have to wait to enjoy the pool since it’s salt water. If service is refused the pool visit fee will be passed on/charged to the guest.

It will be assumed that with an accepted reservation confirmation that guests have read, understood and agree to abide by our house rules. Failure to do so could result in additional fines.
