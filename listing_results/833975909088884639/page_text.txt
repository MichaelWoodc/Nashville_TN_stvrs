Stay in a dog-friendly East Nashville condo with central air conditioning and access to the building’s shared rooftop deck. The home sleeps four with one queen bed and one sofa bed and includes a full kitchen, Wi-Fi, and comfortable living space. Walk to neighborhood dining and entertainment, with downtown Nashville just a short trip away.

The space
Call hip, vibrant East Nashville home at this luxurious, modern condo, sitting pretty right in the middle of the action. Step out your front door and walk to bars, restaurants, cafés, shops, music venues, and more. Nissan Stadium, home of the Tennessee Titans, is just over a mile away, and downtown Nashville is around two miles away across the Cumberland River.

At home, you’ll revel in the sleek design, stylish decor, and open floor plan. If you feel like cooking, the full, well-equipped kitchen features everything you need, and there’s room for everyone to grab a meal at the high-top kitchen table. After a day of exploring Music City, relax at home in the central AC while the washer/dryer takes care of any laundry, or head upstairs to the rooftop deck for sunset. Watch a bit of TV or use the free WiFi to post the day’s pics, or step out for a show at any of the city's countless venues. The Nashville getaway you’ve been dreaming of awaits - all that’s left to do is book.

Guest access
As our guest, you'll have full access to the entire property, except for a few areas reserved for house supplies.
