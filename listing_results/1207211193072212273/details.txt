OUTSIDE HIGHLIGHTS
Lakefront Access:

Private boat dock. If you are bringing your own boat or renting one, you can put into the water at the Shute’s Branch recreation area just down the road. It is just past the intersection of Needmore and Saundersville Rd. There is a boat launch there. You can put your boat into the water there and then tie up at our private dock during your stay. Do note that the water level in our cove does vary and can be shallow if there has not been much rain (even if the rest of the lake is at normal level). It usually sits between 3-4 feet but can get as low as 18 inches- 2 feet. If you have a large boat, parking it at our dock may not work. We've found boats with outboard motors have no issues.

Guest access
The entire house and everything in it is for your use during your stay. The only exception is the locked owner's closet on the main floor to the right of the 1/2 bath.

Other things to note
We are lakefront and there is no barrier between the yard and the water. Please be aware of this when booking with small children or others who cannot swim. 

There is fiber internet at the house, however, cell service is not great (1-2 bars). Keep this in mind if you anticipate needing to make a lot of phone calls (texts go through just fine but calls can be a little difficult). 

Also, this should go without saying, but we are a house in the middle of a wooded area directly on the water. This means that despite our best pest control efforts, you might see a bug. This is to be expected and is no cause for alarm. If seeing a bug or two will ruin your stay, this isn't the property for you. 

We are about 30 minutes from downtown Nashville. If you are visiting the area and want to be very close to downtown, then this isn’t the right fit. We have given you our exact address so that you can map the distance from the lake house to anywhere else you might want to go. Please do not book and then mark us down in your review for the location when you knew in advance exactly where we are located. We can’t (and don't want to) change the location! We think it's pretty special.

We do allow commercial shoots on the property. However, they need to be approved and we require a fee of $500 in addition to the nightly rate. Please inquire if this is of interest.
