=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Made from reclaimed materials, this charming and newly constructed cabin has a vintage style that sits perfectly amongst the forest. It features a gorgeous open plan space and floor-to-ceiling windows that provide a 180-degree view of nature outside.

Secluded on its own quiet 42 acres, the cabin lets you feel alone with nature. Further out, there's easy access to stores and restaurants with a few lovely places for antique shopping. Nashville itself is just a short drive away.

Other things to note
The driveway is gravel and could possibly have ruts and washouts if we’ve had major rain in the recent weeks. It’s drive-able by small car, but you’ll need to take precaution so you don’t bottom out. 4x4 is not required.

Also, you might see a few stink bugs from time to time. They’re impossible to keep out. They only live for 1 day once they come inside (according to the pest control guy). They have definitely been cleaned up before your arrival! They are completely harmless, but definitely a nuisance! If this is a deal breaker please don’t stay here.
