📍 RESTAURANTS, BARS & LOCAL ATTRACTIONS:

🚗 Just 5–10 minutes to Downtown Broadway, Nissan Stadium, and the Country Music Hall of Fame — close to the action, without the noise.

🌮 LOCAL DINING FAVES:

🌯 Redheaded Stranger (Walkable)
A beloved local Tex-Mex spot known for legendary crunch wraps, bold flavors, and their must-try Dreamweaver sauce. Don’t sleep on the burger either — locals swear by it.

🥟 Xiao Bao (Walkable)
One of the best Chinese-fusion spots in town with pork belly bao buns, hand-pulled noodles, and lemongrass pork dumplings that hit every time.

🍕 Folk (Walkable)
An upscale neighborhood favorite serving pizza, seafood, and meats — standout dishes include beef tartare, smoked trout spread, and their surprisingly incredible fresh clam pizza.

🍽 Audrey (Walkable)
Chef Sean Brock’s celebrated restaurant offering an innovative, elevated take on Appalachian cuisine in a refined, intimate setting.

🌮 Mas Tacos Por Favor (Short Drive)
A no-frills Nashville classic serving authentic tacos, tamales, and elote. Locals rave about the Baja fish and fried avocado tacos.

🍔 Dino’s (Short Drive)
Nashville’s oldest dive bar — famous for greasy-perfect burgers, late-night bites, and an effortlessly cool, laid-back vibe.

☕ LOCAL COFFEE SHOPS:

☕ Forevermore (Walkable)
A stylish neighborhood gem serving excellent specialty coffee alongside curated vintage clothing and goods. Grab a latte, hit the photo booth, maybe leave with a new outfit.

🥐 Frothy Monkey (Short Drive)
A go-to spot for all-day breakfast, specialty lattes, and relaxed brunch vibes.

☕ Dose Coffee (Short Drive)
Known for rotating selections from top roasters, this East Nashville staple offers great coffee by day and a bar by night.

🥑 Café Roze (Short Drive)
A minimalist café serving breakfast bowls, smoothies, and cocktails — loved for its aesthetic and all-day menu.

🎶 ATTRACTIONS & THINGS TO DO:

🎸 Downtown Broadway (5–10 minute drive)
The heart of Nashville’s music scene and nightlife. Broadway is lined with legendary honky-tonks, live music pouring out onto the street from morning to late night, rooftop bars, and iconic venues like Tootsie’s, Robert’s Western World, and Ryman Auditorium. Whether you’re bar-hopping, catching a show, or just soaking up the energy, it’s a must-see — and close enough to enjoy, then retreat back to a quieter, more local neighborhood.

🏟 Nissan Stadium (5–10 minute drive)
Home of the Tennessee Titans and Nashville’s biggest concerts and events. Whether you’re catching a game, a stadium show, or a festival weekend, it’s an easy ride away — close enough for convenience, far enough to skip the post-event chaos.

🎼 Country Music Hall of Fame (5–10 minute drive)
A must-visit for music lovers. Explore iconic exhibits, legendary artifacts, and rotating showcases that tell the story of Nashville’s musical legacy — from classic country roots to modern hits. A great daytime stop before heading out for live music at night.

🌿 Shelby Bottoms Greenway & Nature Center (Short Drive)
A massive urban park with walking and biking trails, picnic spots, and nature paths — a peaceful escape minutes from the house.

🎨 Five Points Neighborhood (Short Drive)
The heart of East Nashville’s arts scene, packed with unique shops, live music venues, and top-tier dining.
