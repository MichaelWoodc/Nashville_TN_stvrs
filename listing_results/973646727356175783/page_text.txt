The space
🎸 B-Nashy - Your Luxe Nashville Escape! ✨

A stylish retreat with 5 sleeping spaces, 8 queen/king beds, and 4 en-suite bathrooms is designed for comfort, style, and Insta-worthy moments! Whether you’re in town for a family or friends' getaway, here to celebrate a milestone or bachelorette/bachelor, B-Nashy blends modern luxury with Music City charm and is just 2–3 miles from all the Nashville action!

🌟 Why You’ll Love B-Nashy:

• 5 sleeping spaces - 8 queen/king beds (no bunks!) • 4 full en-suite bathrooms with premium products
• Fully stocked kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances
• Large walk-in closets with luggage caddy and wooden hangars
• Full-size, top of the line washer/dryer + hypoallergenic detergent
• Private driveway parking for 3-4 cars + ample free street parking
• Blazing fast fiber internet WiFi + 65" Smart TV
• Dedicated workspaces
• Luxurious bedding & spa-quality bath products
• Chic, artistic interiors perfect for photos
• Prime location - only 2–3 miles to Broadway, Nissan Stadium & top Nashville hot spots


🛏️ Sleeping Arrangement + Layout Breakdown 🛏️

1st Floor
• Bedroom 1: Sleeps 2 — Queen bed + ensuite bathroom with shower/tub combo (suitable for guests looking to avoid most stairs)
• Cozy living room with plush seating + 65 inch Smart TV
• Island Bar Dining Seating adjacent to a fully equipped spacious kitchen
• Coffee bar for your morning pick-me-up
• Laundry room with full-size washer/dryer + utility rack
• Front & back doors with easy access to driveway (some steps required)

2nd Floor
• Bedroom 2: Sleeps 2 - Queen bed + private ensuite bathroom with a soaking tub!
• Bedroom 3 : Sleeps 2  - Primary King Suite: Spacious retreat with large walk-in closet + luxurious ensuite bathroom featuring dual sinks + walk-in shower

3rd Floor
• Bedroom 4: Sleeps 2 — Queen bed + access to full bathroom
• Bonus Living Room 2: Sleeps 8 — Four queen beds + dedicated workspace with fun swivel chair
• Selfie wall for capturing your Nashville memories 📸
• Shared full bathroom with tub/shower combo

💤 Need an extra bed setup? Don't feel like sharing a bed?

If anyone in your group needs an air mattress and linen set, or rollaway bed just let us know a week before your arrival so we can have one placed in the closet ready for your group! There’s a small setup fee—typically per option and we can arrange more than one option depending on what's needed.

Guest access
This chic music-themed oasis is exclusively yours, without interruption for the duration of your stay, so relax, unwind, and enjoy your stay!

Our check-in process is designed for seamless convenience. You'll receive an automatic door code shortly before your check-in time, granting you access throughout your stay. Additionally, we will also provide a house book link 24 hours before check-in, offering essential information about the home as well as some great food and activities recommendations!
