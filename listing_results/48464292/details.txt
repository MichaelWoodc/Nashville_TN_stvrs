=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Featured in Dwell Magazine 2022. Flatrock House is a handcrafted, dog friendly, slow living retreat just a 10-15 minute drive to all of Nashville's main attractions. It's perfect for the guest seeking an authentic, restful retreat from busy tourist areas while still being close to all Nashville has to offer. Remote workers will love the high speed internet and plentiful outlets.

The space
Welcome to Flatrock House! Start your day with music on the Tivoli radio or bluetooth speaker. Brew fresh coffee in the French press and enjoy the morning light on the deck. When you return from a day of fun in Nashville, spin some vinyl and luxuriate with a hot bath in the large soaking tub. Put a movie on the projector or enjoy a final cocktail on the porch under the 80 year-old Virginia pine before drifting off to sleep in the queen sized memory foam bed.

THE LAYOUT
The upstairs loft has one queen-sized bed, extra blankets, full closet, and record player. The daybed in the living room converts into a twin bed for an extra guest. The kitchen contains essentials for cooking simple meals, a French press for coffee, plus complimentary filtered water, Perrier, coffee, and snacks. Guests control the temperature with the newly refurbished minisplit. Dedicated fans and and a space heater are provided for extra comfort. 

WALKABILITY
The neighborhood is suitable for walks, dog walks, and jogs, though sidewalks in the area are inconsistent.

LOCATION
Please note that the house is in a residential area outside of tourist zones, so you will live like a local! The neighborhood is close to the zoo and the new soccer stadium. It is a 10 minute drive to downtown Nashville, 15 minutes to the airport, 20 minutes to East Nashville, 5 minutes to the Wedgewood-Houston arts district, and about 15 minutes to Vanderbilt and Belmont. For nature lovers, Flatrock House is a 15 minute drive to the best trails in town, including The Two Rivers Park, Heartland Trail, and Radnor Lake.  For those looking for a unique Nashville experience, explore Woodbine, our neighborhood, and its dozens of affordable international restaurants, locals-only coffee spots, and Black Abbey, one of the our favorite breweries.

PETS
We love our four-legged guests. If you have questions or more than 2 pets, drop us a line before booking. We recommend pets remain crated. 

NOTES
The space gets a lot of natural light, and sleepers who are sensitive to light may want to bring a sleeping mask. The loft tends to be warmer than the ground floor. In extreme heat, the loft may not get below 65 degrees, and fans are provided for sleepers who like it cooler. Flatrock House is a guest house built as an addition to my original 1937 cottage and shares one back lower wall with the original cottage. That wall is soundproofed for your privacy. The loft ceilings in are low, please watch your head. The kitchen has cast iron burners and a stand-alone convection oven instead of a traditional range.  

We look forward to having you!
