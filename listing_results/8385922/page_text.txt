The space
This cozy and comfortable town home features 2 upstairs bedrooms, each with a queen size bed and I full bath. The main level is for entertaining. The living room features a sectional sofa, fireplace and LG Smart LED TV with YouTubeTV, AT&T Fiber wireless internet. Games, playing cards and various books are located in the bookshelf for your enjoyment.

There is a half bath and laundry area with full sized washer/dryer and eat-in kitchen equipped with a stainless side-by-side refrigerator with ice/water dispenser, Keurig coffee maker and Ninja Blender for mixing up those margaritas and/or smoothies. Salt & Pepper, pots, pans, dishes & utinsils provided for 4 persons. The fenced back yard and deck with seating is perfect for morning coffee and evening gatherings.

Upstairs: Bedroom 1 is a stylish master suite with a queen sized bed, double closets (hangers included) and upholstered chair.

Upstairs: Bedroom 2, known as "The Beatles White Room" features a queen size bed and larger closet with hangers included.

Upstairs bath has guest towels, shampoo & conditioner, soap, tissues. We provide initial toilet paper, shampoo & conditioner however, if more is necessary during your stay you will need to purchase more.

Guest access
Storage closet under stairwell and outdoor storage room are not for guest use. You may use the backyard but the gate to fenced back yard is to remained locked.

Other things to note
NO PARTIES. NO EVENTS. NO SMOKING. 

Bring your bikes and bike (or walk) the Greenway from Percy Priest Dam to all the way downtown! Bring your kayak or boat! Percy Priest Lake and Elm Hill Marina and 0.8 miles away!
