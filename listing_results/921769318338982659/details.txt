=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to our newer (2023 built) corner unit downtown condo! At a spacious 1,500 square feet, this is one of the largest units in the building. Sleeps 8 on super soft new beds. Building amenities (all same floor as our unit) include a pool, cabanas, fire pits, grill, gym, lounge & free secure parking garage. Just 7 blocks from Broadway Ave & 4 blocks from the Convention Center. Less than 1 mile to Bridgestone Arena, Nissan Stadium, Ryman Auditorium, Ascend Amphitheater, Music Row & The Gulch.

The space
Nashville Vibes welcomes you with a dramatic entry hallway leading to the living area, highlighted by a large neon Nashville Vibes sign set on a 7 foot by 7 foot tall juniper greenery wall. We've included a selfie stand so you can take fun group photos during your stay. Beyond that are black and white print wall art candids of Nashville's country music legends, as you come into the living room and kitchen from the hallway. The living room is huge, with room to seat everyone on the sectional sofa, side chairs, barstools and kitchen table chairs.  Head outside to our wraparound balcony to enjoy a Nashville sunset!  
Both bedrooms have a queen bed and two twin beds (six total beds that sleep 8 comfortably).  Nobody in your group has to slum it on a pull out couch!

Guest access
With a stay in our unit, you will have access to all of the common spaces in the building.  We use the Latch app so everyone in your group can have access to the unit, pool, gym, parking garage and front lobby door via the downloaded app on their phones.  Your phone is your key!

Other things to note
Like most major downtown areas, Nashville does have a homeless population in its downtown core.  Our building is walkable to Broadway Ave, the Music City Center, Ryman Auditorium and other downtown entertainment spots, and generally fine to do so with groups during the day, but like all downtown areas, it is cheap, easy and safe to take the plentiful $5-$8 Uber/Lyfts to and from in the evenings.
