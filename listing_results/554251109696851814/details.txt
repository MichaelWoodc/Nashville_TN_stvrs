=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
You will love our location! Built in 2021, our private guesthouse apartment shares a corner with East Nashville’s Urban Cowboy (where you can get amazing pizza and drinks) as well as Lockeland Table. Our cozy space is modernly furnished and full of beautiful, natural sunlight. Located in the heart of Lockeland Springs, we are also within walking distance of amazing coffee shops, restaurants, retail shopping, concert venues and Shelby Bottoms Park, where you can bike or walk trails.

The space
Our apartment is on the second floor of our detached garage with it’s own private entrance (green door) and keypad. Street parking is available just a few steps away. 

The bedroom features a queen sized bed, walk-in closet, white noise machine, two touch lamps with USB charging ports and a TV with Roku (Netflix included). 

The living room includes a queen, pull-out sofa bed and the marble coffee tables conveniently become nightstands when the bed is in use. 

The bathroom features a newly tiled shower, deep soaking bathtub, hair dryer and towels. Shampoo, conditioner, body soap and hand soap are also provided. (These products all come from a local East Nashville shop called The Goodfill that focuses on zero-waste, plant-based refillable products.) 

The marble table and comfy leather barstools are the perfect place for sipping coffee, catching up on emails, or relaxing with a glass of wine as you put together one of our Tennessee puzzles. From here you can enjoy the view of our beloved Magnolia trees. 

The full kitchen has an electric range, fridge with ice and filtered water, dishwasher, microwave, toaster, electric tea kettle and Keurig coffee maker with K-cups provided. Other kitchen essentials also include a mixing bowl, knives, cutting board, strainer, small set of pots and pans, wine opener and a baking sheet. 

Includes fast and reliable Fiber WIFI.
