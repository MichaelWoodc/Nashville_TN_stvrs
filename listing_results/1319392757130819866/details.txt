Stay at the Honky Tonk Angels Suite: A Slice of Music History

The space
Welcome to the Honky Tonk Angels Suite, a unique studio retreat that celebrates the rich musical heritage of Nashville. Nestled in the iconic Spence Manor, this suite invites you to experience life where legends like Elvis Presley, Dolly Parton, and the Beatles once stayed. This historic location, steps away from Music Row, is the perfect base for soaking in the city’s vibrant rhythm.

Located at 11 Music Square East, this studio puts you at the epicenter of Nashville’s legendary music scene. RCA Studio B, Columbia Studio A, and the Hideout Recording Studio are just a short walk away. Venture beyond music landmarks to discover local favorites like Hattie B’s Hot Chicken, The Patterson House, and Winners & Losers Bar.

Inside the Honky Tonk Angels Suite, you'll find the perfect mix of modern comfort and vintage charm. Prepare your meals in a kitchen equipped with all the essentials, and enjoy the convenience of an in-unit washer and dryer.

Whether you’re in town for a romantic escape, a solo journey, or a fun group getaway, this studio is your backstage pass to Nashville. Unwind in the guitar-shaped pool or let the history of Music Row inspire you. The Honky Tonk Angels Suite offers a one-of-a-kind stay in the heart of Music City.
