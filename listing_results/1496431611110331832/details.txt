Come stay at the Parthenon Pad in Midtown / Downtown Nashville! A 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom condo apartment ideally located within walking distance of Centennial Park and many shops / restaurants to explore in the West End corridor of downtown Nashville. You'll love being just minutes from downtown Nashville, West End, Vanderbilt, and all things Middle Tennessee!

This is a first floor unit inside of a condo building located on 30th Ave N. Easy covered garage parking. No pets allowed, sorry.

The space
The 30th Ave Lofts building is made up of about 40 condo units, all privately owned and mostly used as short term rentals. Guest parking for booked reservations is available on the street out front of the main entry, or in either of two covered parking garages accessible from alleys surrounding the building. Communal lobby with comfy seating to wait on your Uber / Lyft ride :) -- mailbox access available for longer term stays.
