The space
Three floors of living space, a glowing rooftop terrace, and a front door that opens straight into one of Nashville's most walkable neighborhoods - this townhome is built for groups who want more than a place to crash. Whether it's a milestone birthday, a long-overdue reunion, or a weekend of live music and hot chicken, this home keeps the energy going long after you've made it back from a night out in Music City.

Sleeping arrangements
The home sleeps 9 across three distinct levels. On the ground floor, a King Suite with an attached bath and direct access to the 2-car garage offers a naturally private retreat for anyone who wants a little separation from the group. Up on the second floor, a Queen Murphy bed tucks neatly away when not in use, keeping the space open and flexible during the day. The third floor holds two ensuite bedrooms - a King Primary Suite with two rollaway memory foam cots available for flexible configurations, and a Queen Suite. Every guest has somewhere comfortable to land.

Living spaces
The second floor is where the group naturally gathers. Open seating, pop art details, and wide windows fill the main living area with good natural light from morning through afternoon - equally suited to slow starts and lively pre-dinner hangs. Air conditioning, ceiling fans, and full heating keep things comfortable through whatever Nashville's weather throws your way this time of year.

Kitchen & dining
The full kitchen is set up for real cooking. A refrigerator, freezer, stove, oven, microwave, and dishwasher are all in place, with cooking basics, dishes and silverware, wine glasses, a coffee maker, toaster, baking sheet, and barbecue utensils rounding things out. A dining table anchors the space for sit-down meals, and coffee is also on hand to start the morning right.

Outdoor living
The rooftop terrace is the clear showstopper. String lighting, generous group seating, and a BBQ grill make it an easy gathering point - crisp autumn evenings in Nashville are exactly the kind of nights this space was made for. Set up a game of cornhole before heading out, or let the evening run long over a grilled meal under the lights. Down below, a backyard with outdoor furniture and a dedicated outdoor dining area gives the group another option to step outside and settle in.

Bathrooms
The home has 3.5 bathrooms, including ensuite baths connected to the King Suite and both third-floor bedrooms. Each is stocked with shampoo, conditioner, body soap, and a hair dryer, with hot water throughout. A bathtub is available for anyone looking to slow the pace between outings.

Amenities & essentials
This pet-friendly home includes Wi-Fi, a dedicated workspace, washer and dryer, TV, board games, iron, extra pillows and blankets, hangers, clothing storage, and all standard essentials. A private entrance, self check-in via smart lock, free parking on premises, and free street parking make arrival and day-to-day logistics simple. Safety features include exterior security cameras, a smoke alarm, and a carbon monoxide alarm. Long-term stays are welcome.

Neighborhood
Step outside and you're in one of Nashville's most walkable stretches. Hattie B's Hot Chicken and the shops and restaurants of 12 South are right there, and 8th Ave South spots are close by. Downtown Nashville sits less than two miles away - near enough for a quick Uber, easy enough to make every evening feel effortless.
