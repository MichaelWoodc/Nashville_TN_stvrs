=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Bright, fresh, thoughtfully furnished, 2 bedroom with high ceilings & front porch. Comfortable space to relax or use as HQ while you explore Five Points, walk or rideshare to the stadium, music venues, downtown, and more. We've recently upgraded the WiFi.

The space
Furnished, renovated '50's townhouse. Sweet and petite with a small front porch that overlooks a charming, historic neighborhood with sidewalks. 

We are happy to be able to offer our Historic Edgefield/East Nashville home to people visiting Music City. We know what it's like to be AirBnB guests (see how other hosts reviewed us during our travels!). 

We are a legit mom and pop operation - Fire Marshall approved, tax paying, and fully licensed. 

There are some details that just don't come through in the photos: ceiling fans in each room (central air/heat too!); extra blankets, sheets & towels; and phone charging stations in each bedroom. If there is a need for a third bed, a queen, raised, air mattress is provided and can be made up in the living room to accommodate one or two people. We prefer to host groups of two to four people but families or groups of up to five can be accommodated with the air mattress. 

Essential cooking and dining supplies are in the kitchen along with takeout menus and restaurant recommendations.

Our teenie townhome is the only one on the row that faces Fatherland Street, which means extra privacy, and easy access to free street parking. There is also a single spot reserved on the parking pad behind the row.

-Single full bathroom with tub/shower (NOTE: bathroom is connected to the bedrooms with doors on both sides for direct, private en-suite access. There's no access from the common room);

-Third, optional bed is a queen-sized air mattress;
