A contact-free, no frills motel just 4 miles south of downtown.

• Free WiFi & cable 
• Free on-site parking
• Mini-fridge and dual drip/Keurig coffee maker
• Private bath
• Contact-free check-in
• Located off of I-65 & Thompson Lane; mins by car to downtown, Nissan Stadium, Music City Center, Bridgestone Arena, Ascend Amphitheater, Vanderbilt, Belmont, Opry,  the Airport, & more
• Walking distance to recording studios, coffee, & local restaurants

Please read our listing fully before booking.

The space
This listing is for a King Room. The room features 1 king bed and 1 full bathroom, comfortably sleeping up to 2 guests. There is a Smart TV equipped with cable and streaming apps. The room has a mini fridge and dual/keurig coffee maker. 

The communal lobby has a shared microwave, couch and a small dedicated work space. Keep in mind, there is no chair or dedicated workspace inside the room. The couch featured in the photos is in the lobby, not the motel room. 

The Iris Motel is a great fit for:
• Independent budget travelers looking to stay close to downtown without downtown rates 
• Travelers passing through; the iris motel is just off the interstate 
• Musicians recording in Berry Hill
• Anyone looking to explore a classic but less touristy neighborhood of Music City. 

Our motel takes on a modern minimalist concept to travel. If you’re looking for a stay with all the fancy amenities our location might not be the fit. However, if you’re looking to stay in a comfy bed with a modern aesthetic and to avoid the touristy areas of Nashville, this hidden gem may be for you. 

*There is no front desk onsite. Our local team is available 9 am to 5 pm and by phone for any emergency 24/7.*
