"The Dolly Loft on Music Row" is the perfect homebase for the ultimate Music City experience!

This unique Dolly inspired property, consists of 3 bedrooms and sleeps up to 10 guests!  It's located directly on MUSIC ROW, surrounded by Country Music's top recording studios and walking distance to dozens of bars and restaurants!  

Experience Nashville in an unforgettable way!!

Gated Parking + Walkable and Historic Location + 6 Makeup Stations + More!

The space
This top floor loft consists of 3 bedrooms and each one has a story inspired by The Queen of Country herself.  You're going to have a hard time deciding which room you want to claim!

Layout:

Three bedrooms, a cozy living room, full kitchen, full bath, and upstairs balcony.

"The White Limozeen" • Queen bed with double makeup station
"The Rainbow Room" • Three Queen beds with double makeup station
"The Library Room" • Queen bed with double makeup station

Perks:

▶︎ A+++ Walkable Location
▶︎ Makeup Stations in every bedroom
▶︎ 2 Outdoor areas 
▶︎ Free  gated parking (5 gated spaces + 3 in alley)(shared with downstairs unit)
▶︎ Coffee + Creamer + Sugar provided
▶︎ Hi-speed Internet
▶︎ Soft, clean linens for each bed
▶︎ Fresh towels, shampoo, body wash and hair dryer provided
▶︎ 5 minute uber to Broadway hotspots

Guest access
Guests will have access to the entire upstairs unit.  There is another Airbnb downstairs that shares the gated parking and common area.

Other things to note
If you have a larger group and would like to book this unit plus the 4 bedroom unit downstairs, please find the "combo" listing on our account!
