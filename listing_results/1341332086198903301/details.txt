=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Rhythm & Rooftop Penthouse at The Hyve — a luxe 2-story penthouse in the heart of Downtown Nashville. Sleeping up to 10, this stylish retreat features soaring ceilings, designer decor, and an incredible 6th-floor wraparound balcony with sweeping city views. Relax at the heated resort-style pool, fire pits, and Peloton-equipped gym, all just .7 miles from Broadway — close enough to walk, or a super quick Uber. Perfect for groups, bachelorette parties, and more!

The space
Designed with groups in mind, this penthouse blends elevated style with all the comforts of home. The fully equipped kitchen features stainless steel appliances, cookware, and everything needed to prepare meals during your stay (just bring your favorite cooking oil). Start your mornings with the Keurig coffee bar stocked with k-cups, then unwind after a night out with Smart TVs, cozy lounge spaces, and plenty of room to spread out across two spacious levels.

During your stay, enjoy thoughtful extras throughout the condo, including complimentary toiletries like shampoo, conditioner, and body wash, a hairdryer, iron and ironing board, extra bedding, pool towels, and a walk-in closet for added convenience. A full-size washer and dryer with detergent included makes longer stays easy and stress-free.

The Hyve offers some of the best resort-style amenities in Nashville. Spend the day at the heated outdoor pool with cabanas and loungers (open March–October from 7am–10pm), gather around the fire pits, grill out in the outdoor kitchen area, or challenge your group to ping pong on the pool deck. You’ll also have access to a state-of-the-art fitness center with Peloton equipment, a coworking space, community lounge, and on-site security for extra peace of mind.

You’ll have access to the entire penthouse except for the owner’s supply closet. Whether you’re visiting for a bachelorette trip, birthday celebration, concert weekend, or Nashville getaway with friends and family, Rhythm & Rooftop Penthouse delivers the perfect blend of luxury, location, and unforgettable city vibes.
