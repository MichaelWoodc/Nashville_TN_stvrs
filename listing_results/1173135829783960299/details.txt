=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Nestled on a quiet street in the serene neighborhood of Inglewood, this newly renovated basement suite provides a reprieve from the bustle of downtown Nashville. Conveniently located, we are just a 20-minute walk from several breakfast options, lunch spots, chic restaurants, and a beer garden. Ideal for the remote worker, the space is equipped with ultra-fast fiber internet as well as a comfortable workstation with an external monitor. Come experience the laid-back local life of East Nashville!

The space
The open floor plan apartment is located on the basement level of a nineteen thirties brick cottage.

Living room with seating for 4 and 32” smart TV. Playing cards and games are also provided.

Full bathroom with a shower boasting strong water pressure; plus provided linens, shampoo, conditioner, & body wash.

Sleeping area with comfortable queen-sized bed, nightstands with reading lights and built-in USB charging, and white noise machine. Dresser and hanging rack with wood hangers provided for clothing storage, as well as an iron and ironing board.

Dedicated Office Space with a spacious 2’x5’ desk equipped with an external monitor (HDMI) available for your use, as well as ultra-fast fiber internet on your own dedicated network – video calls are crisp and clear.

Kitchenette with microwave, Keurig coffee station, and mini fridge with freezer. Disposable plates, cups, and utensils are also provided.

Unit has a dedicated A/C and heating unit.

Unit has a free dedicated off-street parking spot.

Unit is professionally cleaned before each stay.

If you need to get downtown, it’s just a short 15-minute drive.

Excellent local food & drink options:
Breakfast - Dose Coffee, Ladybird Taco
Lunch - Mitchell Deli, Castrillo’s Pizza
Dinner - Lou, Sabell’s, Bite a Bit
Drinks - Village Pub & Beer Garden, Inglewood Lounge

Guest access
Private entrance accessed via an external concrete staircase, through a set of double wood doors, on the back right corner of the house.

Other things to note
This basement suite is below our family's private home so please be respectful of us and our neighbors. We will also do our best to honor quiet hours and keep noise transfer through the floor to a minimum at all times.
