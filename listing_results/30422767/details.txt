Chateau on the Row - The Only True Studio Apartment on Music Row.

Nashville s most unique vacation rental is situated smack dab in the middle of historic Music Row. Nestled in the same building that houses Jays Place recording studio,

Chateau on the Row offers a music themed vacation experience that is second to none. Owner Jay Vernali welcomes you to be part of the “mix” that includes a free recording studio tour.

The space
Situated within walking distance of Nashville s ultra hip Hillsboro Village, Vanderbilt University and Belmont University, Chateau on the Row is located within seconds of some of the top restaurants, night spots, museums and the historic independent movie theater The Belcourt

Some of the artists that have graced the halls of this building include Garth Brooks, Trisha Yearwood, Frankie Ballard, Jewel, Dobie Gray, Chet Atkins, Sam Moore and Shel Silverstein to name a few.

Because Chateau on the Row is housed over a working recording studio you will occasionally be privileged to hear some musical magic as it is being born! No worries: at night The Row transforms into a quiet and peaceful neighborhood.

Chateau on the Row has all the comforts of home plus many added amenities.
