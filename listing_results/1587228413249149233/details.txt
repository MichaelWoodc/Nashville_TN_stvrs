=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to your own private retreat in the heart of East Nashville! Tucked behind our home, this newly built detached guest house (DADU) offers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience. Whether you’re visiting for live music, incredible food, sporting events, or a weekend getaway, you’ll have a peaceful place to relax while staying just minutes from everything Nashville has to offer.

The space
Designed with comfort and thoughtful details throughout, our private guest house comfortably accommodates up to four guests and features:

* Spacious bedroom with king-size bed
* Queen sleeper sofa in the living room
* Full bathroom
* Fully equipped kitchen 
* Open living area with Smart TV
* Wi-Fi and designated work space 
* In-unit washer and dryer
* Self check-in with a personalized keypad
* Free on-site parking

Whether you’re traveling as a couple, with friends, for business, or with family, you’ll have everything you need to feel at home.

The Neighborhood

One of the best parts of staying here is the location. Our guest house is nestled in one of East Nashville’s most walkable neighborhoods, just a short stroll from Five Points and the heart of East Nashville.

From your front door, you can easily walk to:

* Five Points dining, shopping, and nightlife
* Local coffee shops and cafés
* Award-winning restaurants
* Craft breweries and cocktail bars
* Boutique shopping and local markets
* Live music venues
* Shelby Park and Shelby Bottoms Greenway for walking, running, and biking

When you’re ready to explore beyond the neighborhood, you’ll be just:

* 10 minutes to Broadway & Downtown Nashville
* 10 minutes to Nissan Stadium
* 12 minutes to The Gulch
* 15 minutes to Vanderbilt University
* 15 minutes to Nashville International Airport (BNA)
