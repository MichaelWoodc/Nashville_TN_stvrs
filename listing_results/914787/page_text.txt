4 Bed Condo stylishly decorated with FREE parking. This condo complex has a Pool w/ updated patio furniture, gym and a laundry facility. Walk to downtown Nashville and all the Broadway bars. Next to Germantown -a hip & popular neighborhood with lots of great restaurants, coffee shops & bars. Also near Brooklyn Bowl, Sounds Baseball Stadium, Ryman, Titans Stadium, Country Music Hall of Fame plus so much more! Fully equipped kitchen, TV with Cable, plus a fun Neon sign to take photos in front of!

After all the fun, come back to relax in this quiet, spacious, comfy condo. Although parking is free & easy, you don't need a car for this condo. Many things are within walking distance! There is public transportation and it's only 15 mins from the airport. There are plenty of Ubers, Lyft and taxis available. Our condo is in a great location to enjoy all the things that Nashville has to offer.
