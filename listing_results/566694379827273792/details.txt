=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Experience the heart of Music City from The Guitar Shack, a comfortable 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home in Nashville, Tennessee, boasting great city views and an unbeatable location just 7 minutes from Broadway!

A private 1-car garage offers a fun bonus space with a movie theater, pool table / ping pong table combo, and a chalkboard wall. The true draw is the stunning view of the city. Get around easily to the live music, restaurants, and iconic honky-tonks of Broadway in just minutes

This is a 3 story, 3 bedroom townhouse (approx. 1500 square feet) we have 1 king bed, 2 queen beds, 2 full beds with twins on top.
