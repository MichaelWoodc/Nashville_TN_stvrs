The space
There are two bedrooms. One with a king bed and the other has a full bed with a single loft bed. Guests may also use the couch in the living room as an additional place to sleep. 

We do not allow parties at this property. We use a noise alert monitoring system in order to ensure that neighborhood noise levels are respected. There is no electronic video surveillance or audio recording. It simply notifies guests and us when the noise levels has reached a volume that would disturb the neighbors.

Guest access
We are a pet-friendly home. If you are bringing a pet, please let us know in advance and select the number of pets you are bringing. We allow up to two dogs. If you have any other type of pet or more than two dogs, please reach out before booking. These are approved on a case-by-case basis. Our standard pet fee is $75, which covers 2 dogs for up to 4 nights. Anything beyond that, you will be charged a separate fee after booking of $15 per night per pet. 

After booking, we will also send you a pet agreement that will need to be signed before your arrival. It basically asks guests to follow these guidelines:

- Pick up after dog droppings
- Do not allow the dog to bark excessively
- Do not allow dogs on furniture or beds
- Don’t leave animals unsupervised, unless they are in a crate.
- Put pet sheets on furniture
- Pets must be housebroken
- Crate the dog if left unattended indoors for a long period of time.
- Groom the dog before the trip and vacuum after your pet if it sheds excessively
- The dog must be up-to-date on immunizations

*If these guidelines are not followed, guests will be charged the extra fee for cleaning.
