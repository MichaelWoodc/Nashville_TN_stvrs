=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Escape to our newly remodeled Nashville condo, perfect for groups of up to 8! This modern retreat features 2 bedrooms and 2 full baths. Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen and a quiet back deck. Ideally located just 5-7 mins from BNA airport and 10-12 mins from the vibrant energy of downtown Nashville and Opryland. Your perfect Music City home base awaits!

The space
Welcome to your newly remodeled, single-level Nashville retreat! This clean and modern condo offers a comfortable and inviting space for your group.

The open-concept layout features a spacious living area with a large sectional sofa, perfect for relaxing after a day of exploring Music City. The living area flows seamlessly into a dining space and a fully equipped, modern white kitchen, ready for you to prepare your favorite meals.

Step out onto the back deck to enjoy your morning coffee with views of the quiet wooded area.

Sleeping arrangements comfortably accommodate up to 8 guests:
* Bedroom 1: Queen Bed with an en-suite bathroom and closet space.
* Bedroom 2: Queen Bed, a Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, and a Twin Trundle Bed, with a walk-in closet and an en-suite bathroom featuring a modern walk-in shower.

Need more space? We own two other units in the same subdivision. Please inquire about booking multiple condos for groups of up to 24!

Guest access
Guests will have full and private access to the entire condo during their stay. This includes both bedrooms, both bathrooms, the living area, the kitchen, and the back porch. Make yourself at home and enjoy all the comforts our space has to offer.
