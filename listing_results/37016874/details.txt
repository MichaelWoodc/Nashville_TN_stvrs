You'll love having your own Quiet Oasis to relax at but you'll also love how quickly you can get to the action packed nightlife downtown on Broadway!

The space
The home's cozy living room allows for relaxed gatherings on a comfy sectional sofa that faces a gas fireplace - perfect for those chilly months.   The well stocked kitchen and dining area overlooking the pool, is a wonderful option for those eat-in nights.  All 3 bedrooms  on the 2nd floor feature calming color tones and will be a welcome retreat after busy days. Guest access
