Enjoy this beautiful, cozy space, located centrally, in the heart of East Nashville. Fully stocked, two bedroom, 1.5 bath town-home perfect for a quick get away or extended stay.

Nestled in the vibrant neighborhood of East Nashville, this stunning condo is within walking distance to hundreds of local restaurants, breweries, and some of Nashville's HOTTEST attractions. Only a 5-minute Uber/Lyft ride to Nissan Stadium! The ultimate Nashville getaway awaits you.

The space
After your jam-packed days exploring Music City, come home to relax in any of the comfortable and spacious 2 bedrooms with queen-size beds (sofa bed in living room also converts to queen sofa sleeper). Prep delicious home-cooked meals in the fully stocked kitchen with all your cooking essentials. End your night by heading to the living room to switch on your favorite show on the large smart TV while enjoying popcorn and relaxing peacefully.
