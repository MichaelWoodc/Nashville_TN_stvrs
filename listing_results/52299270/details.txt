=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to Studio One Lofts. This unit sits on the first floor of a 2-story 1950's restored motel. The building and condo may bear its age, but the space has character & is the perfect little spot for anyone looking to stay close to downtown on a budget.

• Walkable to restaurants & bars
• Budget friendly
• Keypad entry
• Free WiFi
• Washer & Dryer on-site
• Free unassigned parking on-site
• Pool on site! Open seasonally
• Check in at 3 pm / Check out at 11 am

The space
This condo is a great option for those looking for a spot near downtown and wanting to stay on a budget. The studio mimics the size of a motel room but has the amenities of a small apartment. There is a queen size bed, small table, smart TV and queen sofa bed. The kitchen has been renovated with stainless steal appliances added (no dishwasher so please no food down the kitchen drain; please use the food stopper when cooking). 

The complex is a bit older so don't expect a fancy building. What makes the studio desirable are two main features: the pool and location. The pool is literally outside your doorstep and the studio itself is in the heart of the Melrose/8th Ave neighborhood; central to where ever you want to be in Nashville. It's only 3 miles from Honky Tonk Row, a 7-10 minute Lyft ride.

**The pool is open seasonally, roughly Memorial Day Weekend thru Labor Day.**

Guest access
You'll have access to the entire unit, laundry facility, & the pool during open season (roughly Memorial Day thru September). 

There is a parking lot on site with enough spots for 1 car per unit. Additional parking can be found up the street from the complex.

Other things to note
→ The complex – the studio complex is old. It was built in the 1950s and it's age is starting to show. While we've taken measures to improve our condo, keep this in mind when booking. You won't be arriving to a fancy condo complex; it's a modest 2-story building sitting among trendy new construction. You will be booking this unit for the location and, if in the summer, the pool. 

→ Please note before booking – while we have our quiet hours of 10 pm to 8 am, there is potential for noise as the complex is located in an urban area and you will have tenants above and beside you. We provide a sound machine for the unit but please be aware of this detail to make sure this condo is the right fit for you! 

→ As soon as you step foot in Nashville, you'll notice that the skyline is peppered with cranes and other signs of the booming construction that is happening.  Because of the construction, at times there are limited sidewalks to use. Please be conscious of your surroundings exploring about town.

Otherwise, to be considerate to other guests, we cannot guarantee that an early check in or late check out request will be accommodated. Feel free to arrive anytime after 4 pm.

→ The pool is open seasonally - roughly Memorial Day thru September
