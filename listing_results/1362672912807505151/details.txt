=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Whether you’re touching down at BNA and need a quick commute, heading to the Grand Ole Opry for an unforgettable night, or need a launch pad for a weekend on Broadway, our private lower-level suite with its own entrance is your perfect Nashville home base! Skip the airport hotels and enjoy a space designed for the modern traveler. Located in a tranquil neighborhood, our suite provides a quiet escape without sacrificing proximity to the airport, local favorites, and the heart of Nashville.

The space
🌿 Living & Dining

Relax on a comfortable futon that converts into a full sized sleeper for two!  The living area is perfect for movie nights in and cozy downtime.  The dining table seats four and is great for meals, games, or remote work.

🍳 Fully Equipped Kitchen

Cook with ease in a complete kitchen featuring full-size appliances, a Keurig, dishes and cookware, pantry essentials, and plenty of countertop space. Perfect for long stays or quick meals.  Coffee is supplied!

🛏 Two Comfortable Bedrooms

Bedroom 1: Features a memory foam queen sized bed with soft bedding, clothing storage and simple modern decor - perfect for unwinding after a long day.

Bedroom 2: Includes two memory foam twin beds with cozy bedding, a full length mirror and nature-themed wall art, ideal for larger group stays.

🛁 Clean & Modern Bathroom

Enjoy a fresh, updated bathroom with a tile shower, bright lighting and a clean vanity.  For your convenience towels, soap, a hairdryer and bath essentials are provided.

☀️ Spacious Screened Porch

Get some fresh air while avoiding insects!  This shared space offers bistro seating for two and is the ideal place to have your morning cup all year long.  The string lights provide a warm ambience for when you want to kick back with a glass of wine in the evening before heading in for the night.

Guest access
Guests may access the carport for parking, the entire lower level suite including the shared vestibule, and the screened porch.

Guests are not permitted to use the laundry facilities in the vestibule.

Guests may enjoy the backyard, but should not go into the wooded areas beyond the property line.

Other things to note
While we do live in the upstairs portion of the house, we are mindful of our guests and minimize noise during your stay.  To enter the private suite, guests must pass through an enclosed back porch and a laundry room vestibule, which are both shared spaces.  During a guest visit we do not utilize the porch at all.  We may access the vestibule to do laundry on any given day from the hours of 9am-6pm.   Notice will be given beforehand.

The maximum number of guests permitted in the Airbnb is 6.  

The 5th and 6th guest on a booking is an extra $25 per night each.

There is a small dog that lives on the premises - guests with severe dog allergies should take note.  The dog is never permitted into the guest suite or vestibule .
