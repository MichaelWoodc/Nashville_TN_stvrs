The space
Before anyone even heads out for the night, the great room sets the mood: a deep blue velvet sectional, orange armchairs, and a neon sign glowing "Sail Away With Me" on the wall. It's the kind of room people gather in before Broadway, not just pass through. The kitchen opens right into that living space, island and stainless appliances facing the sectional, so whoever's cooking is still part of the conversation.

A separate dining area handles group breakfasts the next morning. Tucked past the kitchen is the real surprise: a workspace that doubles as a mini lounge, with cassette-tape wallpaper, a framed Dolly Parton print, a gold Nashville map, and a Marshall speaker over a stocked bar cart.

It's a small room that carries the whole retro-Music-City concept through the house, and it's a great spot for a slower morning with coffee before the day gets going. Picture the arc of the trip: getting ready at the primary bedroom's full-length mirror, cocktails at the wet bar, a playlist on the sectional, then back out for a night on Broadway before everyone crashes on memory foam.
