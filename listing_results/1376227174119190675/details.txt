The space
The Gambler keeps the energy going from the second you walk in. A poker table and pool table sit right off the kitchen, so a hand can start before the bags are even unpacked. The open kitchen and living room keep the group in one flow all day, with island seating for the cook and a sectional facing the TV for whoever's watching the game.

Four bedrooms give everyone real separation at night without losing that shared common space. Up top, the rooftop deck strings lights over skyline views and the hot tub, the kind of spot where the whole trip ends up happening after dark.

Spend the afternoon on chess and cornhole in the backyard, head to Geodis Park or Broadway for the evening, then wind down in the rooftop hot tub before the game room takes over for a late poker night.
