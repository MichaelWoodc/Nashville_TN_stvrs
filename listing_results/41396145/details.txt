=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Russell House is a CHIC and COZY retreat in the heart of East Nashville's hip 5 Points neighborhood (home to the famed Tomato Art Festival). This apartment is located at the top of our backyard carriage house,  just steps away from a bustling neighborhood center…more than 30 restaurants and 50 shops are within a 10-minute walking distance! We also have free, gated, off-street parking for one car up to the size of an F-150.

10 MINS drive to Broadway, Nissan Stadium, Bridgestone!

The space
The Russell House is a passion project for me and my wife, Katherine. After managing properties for the last 5 years, we decided it was time to build one of our own! We tried to think of everything we love when staying in an Airbnb and deliver it to you, our guests. Katherine did all of the interior design. We have used only the highest quality materials and goods to offer a truly luxurious experience. The Russell House features hand-designed cabinetry/shelving by Bloomfield Originals; furnishings and decor from West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, and the Citizenry; and artwork by Emily Leonard. We commissioned 1767 Designs to handcraft the one-of-a-kind barn door...it features rescued wood from the historic Russell Street Church of Christ, located just down the street. We also provide our guests with Barista Parlor coffee to enjoy!

We are 2 miles (9-min drive) from Broadway.
We are a 1.5 mile walk from Nissan Stadium.

Guest access
Guests will have full access of the Russell House. The garage below is for the owners only. We park a car there and store belongings, but do not live, work, or sleep there.

Other things to note
The Russell House is a completely private space with its own entrance on the top floor of our carriage house. We do park a car in the garage on the bottom floor, but the door is designed to be quiet.

*We highly recommend purchasing travel insurance as there are no exceptions made to our cancellation policy.
