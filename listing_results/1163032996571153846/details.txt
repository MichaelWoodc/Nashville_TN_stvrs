=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Our vintage 2 bedroom, 1 bath apartment is located on Main Street on the 2nd floor of a historic building built in 1904 in downtown Pleasant View, TN. As the original telephone switchboard that connected family members and friends across the miles, we love that it now provides a clean, cozy, and peaceful space to make new memories and chat in person!

The space
The upstairs unit where you will be staying has a vibrant, relaxing, and laid back vibe. You'll discover an eclectic mixture of memorabilia and decor from the 70's & 80's mixed with modern amenities we are sure you'll enjoy. 

The renovated space below is the perfect place to purchase a variety of items you'll love. Gifts, apparel and locally made treats are now available! You can also host small events and attend some thrown by us, the owners! From photo sessions with themes to paint and sip nights, bridal showers to birthday parties, we plan to make the building one that brings joy to our guests, customers, and the community. The first level meets today's standards while keeping the vintage charm of days gone by.

You will recognize that all of your comfy beds, linens, and  living room furniture are in excellent condition. You can stay caught up on your favorite shows or enjoy a movie night on one of the 3 Smart TVs with DIRECTV along with fast fiber internet.

You may find yourself intrigued by some of our old school treasures such as the TV console, NES & SNES, silver-faced stereo receiver, magazines, books, and photos! Get your groove on as you spin a few records or show off your singing skills with a little late night karaoke. Play a great assortment of old and new board games, jam on the guitar or sit out on the porch as you plan each fun filled day.

In addition to all of this, we provide fluffy spa bath robes for two, a designated office area, a laundry room, and a private cafe style balcony to enjoy complimentary coffee with some breakfast snacks!

What really sets us apart is that you're being hosted by a team that truly cares about serving our community and the guests we get to share it with!
