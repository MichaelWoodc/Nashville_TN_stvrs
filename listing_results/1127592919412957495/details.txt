The space
Your group takes over four floors that all funnel back to the same common spaces, which is exactly the point. This is the kind of Airbnb where the house itself becomes part of the celebration, not just the place you sleep. Downstairs opens up around a neon "Taylor can't come to the phone right now" sign in the dining area and a glowing "FEARLESS" sign on a dark accent wall in the living room.

Sofas and yellow armchairs give everyone a spot to land, and the kitchen island with bar stools keeps whoever's cooking in on the conversation. The Green Room is where the night picks up: ping pong, a bar, a photo-op area, and lounge seating for anyone waiting their turn, while the Luxury Lounge gives the group another spot to spread out and relax.

One upstairs bedroom goes all in on the theme with a makeup bar under Hollywood-style lighting, a vanity, and patterned wallpaper built for getting ready as a group. Spend the day downtown, come back to soak in the hot tub in the Outdoor Lounge as evening sets in, run a ping pong tournament after dinner, then scatter to four bedrooms to actually sleep.
