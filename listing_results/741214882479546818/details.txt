=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Most downtown condos look out at a parking deck. This one looks at the river. Step onto your own balcony with trees and open water in front of you, then walk the pedestrian bridge straight into Broadway when the night calls for it. A king bed in the bedroom, a sofa bed in the living room, a kitchen stocked for real cooking, and a pool for the hot part of the day. Titans games and Topgolf sit around the corner. Four years hosting, nearly 200 stays, and still the quiet option on the block.

The space
The kitchen is fully stocked for cooking in — pots, pans, and bakeware all provided. Smart TVs in the living room and bedroom let you log in to your own streaming accounts.
The bedroom has a king bed, a smart TV, a clothing rack, and a dresser. In the living room, the sofa pulls out to a queen — so the unit comfortably sleeps four.
The balcony is the quiet counterweight to downtown: a morning cup of coffee or an evening glass of wine over the water. In cooler months, the fireplace does the same job inside.

Guest access
Guest has full access to the entire condo and all shared amenities offered at our complex.Guests have the entire condo to themselves — bedroom, bathroom, full kitchen, living room, and the private balcony. You'll also have access to the building's shared amenities, including the pool, grills, and fitness room.
Entry is keyless through an E-lock, with a unique code that's reset after each stay. Parking is free and open directly in front of the unit — no assigned spots, just pick whatever's most convenient.
