=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Nestled in the woods just 15 minutes from Downtown Nashville, this 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath custom-built log home offers 1000 sq ft of wrap-around porch living and luxury interior decor. You can enjoy the lights and sounds of Music City and rest well at night to nothing but the sounds of the forest.

The space
The cabin is 2 stories, featuring the primary bedroom on the first floor with an en-suite bathroom and 2 additional bedrooms on the second floor. There is a full bathroom directly in the middle of the 2 upstairs bedrooms. On the first floor, along with the primary bedroom, you will find an inviting living room with Smart TV, 8-person dining room table, fully equipped kitchen (everything you'd need to make home cooked meals, please note no cooking oil provided), and laundry room. There are 3 different entry points out on to the wrap around porch with additional seating and views of 2 seasonal ponds, a small bridge, and even a pear orchard!
