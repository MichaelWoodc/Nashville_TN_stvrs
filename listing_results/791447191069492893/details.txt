=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Location, comfort, convenient. Our fully finished basement suite is a great place to crash for a weekend, or call home for a longer stay. This is our home, and the private basement suite has its own parking, entrance, kitchen, bathroom and one bedroom (KING). Full laundry on site. 

I lived in the suite for 4 months while I renovated the kitchen above. It is very comfortable, and perfect for what it is... a great place to call home for a temporary stay.

The home is also fully fenced for dog.

The space

There is a bedroom, living/kitchen area, and a full bathroom. The ceiling height of the bathroom is 6’6” just in case you are very tall! The yard is fenced in with lots of space for a dog to run! If you bring your dog, we provide a dog bed, food and water bowls, and some treats. The dog must be on the reservation.
We have two golden retrievers that live with us.
