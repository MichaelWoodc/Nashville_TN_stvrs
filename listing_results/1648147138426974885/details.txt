=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Monroe House #8 is the 3-bedroom option in the Monroe community, with the same shared-courtyard energy as the larger homes. Step out to two pickleball courts, a fire pit, BBQs, cornhole, and foosball, then retreat to your own private house at night. Sleeps 8 across 3 beds and 2 baths; no garage, with free street parking on Monroe St. Germantown is across the street, and Broadway and the Ryman are about 10 minutes away. Designed, built, and hosted by the local owners.

The space
Welcome to Monroe House #8, the smaller 3-bedroom option inside the 16-house Monroe community on the Buena Vista / Germantown edge. You still get the main event: a private house that opens into the shared social courtyard with two pickleball courts, fire pit seating, BBQ grills, outdoor dining tables, cornhole, foosball, and giant Jenga.

Inside, the house is a three-story townhome built in 2020 with modern finishes, a full kitchen, living area, fast Wi-Fi, central AC, smart TV, and in-unit washer/dryer. The setup is simple for a smaller group: 3 bedrooms, 3 beds, and 2 baths across three floors.

House #8 does not have a garage. Park free on Monroe St, then use the courtyard as your hangout space.

Guest access
Full access to the entire house: kitchen, living room, bedrooms, bathrooms, and laundry. House #8 does not have a garage, but free street parking on Monroe St is usually easy.

Outside, you have access to the shared Monroe courtyard, which is the reason groups book here. It has two pickleball courts, fire pit with seating, BBQ grills, outdoor dining tables, cornhole, foosball, and giant Jenga. The courtyard is shared with the other Monroe Houses guests, which works especially well for groups booking multiple homes together.
