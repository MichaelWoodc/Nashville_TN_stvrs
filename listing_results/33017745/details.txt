The space
We are AirBnB Super Hosts and own two other condos in the same building. This condo is  located on the fourth floor of the historic Spence Manor  building, which was originally built as a five-star hotel for Elvis to stay when he recorded at the  RCA Studio B.  Other notables who stayed at Spence Manor include The Beatles, Johnny Cash, Willie Nelson, Frank Sinatra and many more. The building has been converted into individually-owned condos.
