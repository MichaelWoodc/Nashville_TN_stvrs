=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Gallatin is the newest boutique hotel in East Nashville, brought to you by the owners of The Russell Hotel! Explore some of our favorite neighborhood spots on the East Side, or take the good times downtown with just a short 3 mile trip to Broadway. 

Every stay at The Gallatin helps someone experiencing homelessness in Nashville through our Rooms for Rooms program! The average weekend stay provides 16 nights in a bed, 100 free showers, or 30 free meals!

The space
Cozy Room 211 has one King size bed, a full bathroom, and is 175 square feet. Designed for the traveler on a budget, this lodging option proves you don’t have to compromise on price in order to have the unique ‘boutique hotel’ experience.

Guest access
Access to the building and guest rooms is through a unique key code that we send via email the day before arrival! There is not a check in desk or concierge but we are always a phone call away if needed.

Other things to note
