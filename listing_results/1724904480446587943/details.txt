The space
🏡 The Space
Step into an authentic mid-century experience. Built in 1963, our character-rich, 2-level home features stunning original hardwood floors and a layout with 3 private bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms.
As our guest, you will enjoy full access to the welcoming main floor. This level features our beautiful, original knotty pine kitchen and a perfectly preserved vintage blue bathroom—complete with its original blue sink, tub, and classic tilework. For your complete peace of mind, every private guest bedroom is equipped with its own secure door lock and personal key.
🌳 The Neighborhood & Grounds
We are nestled at a quiet T-intersection in a historic neighborhood built on the scenic outskirts of the original Andrew Jackson plantation. The property features both a front and back garden. Guests are warmly welcome to relax and enjoy the fresh air within the defined boundaries of our beautiful front and side yard spaces (please note the back garden area remains private).

During your stay

🗺️ Leave Your Mark: Interactive Traditions
We invite you to interact with our home and share a piece of your story! In the dining room, you will find two large wall maps—one of the United States and one of the World.

The Pin Tradition: We love tracking where our global community comes from. To keep your exact travels private, we invite you to place a pin marking your birthplace using the pin box resting on the US map.

Our Numismatic Collection: You will also notice a unique display of international paper bills and coins arranged across the world map. These are special currency souvenirs left behind by our previous global guests.

🕊️ A Gift For You (Origami Crane Project): As a special token of our appreciation, we are happy to fold a custom origami crane for you upon request. This crane serves as a token of your participation in a decade-long public art project solo-run by one of your hosts, who has lovingly crafted and gifted over 5,000 origami cranes to people all over the world!
