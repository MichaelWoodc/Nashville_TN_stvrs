=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Step into a warm, whimsical stay inspired by all things chocolate. Completed in 2025, this thoughtfully designed condo blends playful charm with modern comfort—perfect for couples, friends, or solo travelers.

Located just a few doors down from local favorites like Poppy & Peep Chocolate, Matryoshka Coffee, and Gladly Gather -  you’ll be staying in one of Nashville’s most creative and flavorful pockets.

The space
Our thoughtfully designed one-bedroom space offers everything you need for a cozy, comfortable stay whether you’re here to explore, unwind, or work remotely.

The Bedroom
The serene bedroom features a comfy queen bed, closet or hanging space for your clothes and suitcase, and blackout curtains with roller shades to ensure a restful night’s sleep. You’ll also find nightstands with lamps and earplugs tucked inside for added comfort.

Bathroom
The clean, modern bathroom includes a shower, fresh towels, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, and a hair dryer for your convenience.

Kitchen
The fully equipped kitchen has everything you need to prepare a meal or reheat your favorite takeout. It includes a Smeg fridge, stove, microwave, coffee maker, and essential cookware. We also provide coffee, tea, olive oil, and basic spices.

Living Area
The cozy living space includes a queen sleeper sofa, a smart TV with streaming access, and a dining table that’s great for meals or remote work.

Outdoor Space
Guests have access to picnic benches just outside—perfect for your morning coffee or a casual meal with friends. (Fun fact: I’m on the HOA board and helped make those happen.)

Extras
Enjoy fast WiFi for streaming or remote work, as well as heating and air conditioning to keep you comfortable year-round.

Guest access
Guests have full access to the entire apartment during their stay. This includes the bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, living area, and any outdoor shared spaces.

The only off-limits area is the small utility closet where the water heater is located. Otherwise, the space is all yours to relax and enjoy!

Other things to note
Designed by Evane Stoner Interiors, a host & local expert who brings Nashville charm and chocolate dreams together in this one-of-a-kind space. As a native to the area, thoughtful touches blend modern comfort with the rich history of chocolate, reflected in unique artifacts from Projet Chocolat, a true curator of chocolate.
