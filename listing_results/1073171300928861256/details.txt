=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
We live in a great area of East Nashville, just 4 miles from downtown and a short 2.5 miles from the super fun East Nashville Five Points area! We are within walking distance of many local businesses and the beautiful Shelby Park, which has lovely trails, e-bikes, and scenery. Our house provides parking and an outdoor patio to hang out on, with yard access. You will be tucked away from downtown's bustle but still in a convenient, cute, quiet location. Come and enjoy our cozy basement apartment!

The space
Our space has a comfortable queen bed and a sectional couch, the sectional couch is not a pull out. It does NOT have a full kitchen, but does have a coffee maker, mini fridge with ice tray and microwave. It is nice and dark for sleeping and has new carpets! You will find Nashville curated art and musical themed finds throughout the space. We live upstairs, you might see us or hear us, we will be quiet, chill and respectful and ask you to do the same. This is not a party house, just a comfortable place to lay your head in an adorable neighborhood close to the city and many East Nashville gems.
