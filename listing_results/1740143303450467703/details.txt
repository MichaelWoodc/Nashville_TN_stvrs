=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===

The space
Welcome to **Boots & Bourbon**, your Nashville home away from home! This spacious 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom home is designed for groups and comfortably accommodates up to 12 guests.

The home features a modern open-concept living area, fully equipped kitchen, comfortable bedrooms, and plenty of space for everyone to relax.

One of the highlights is the **private rooftop**, complete with a hot tub, lounge seating, outdoor dining area, BBQ, and views of the Nashville skyline.

You’ll also have access to a **private 2-car garage**, making parking easy during your stay.

Located near **The Gulch, Music Row, SoBro, and Broadway**, you’ll be close to Nashville’s restaurants, nightlife, live music, and top attractions while still having a comfortable private space to come home to.

## Close to Boots & Bourbon

### The Gulch

A lively, walkable neighbourhood filled with restaurants, rooftop bars, boutiques, murals and live-music spots. Perfect for brunch, dinner or a night out.

### Music Row

Explore Nashville’s famous recording district, home to historic studios, music-industry landmarks and the legendary RCA Studio B.

### 12South

A trendy, walkable neighbourhood known for local boutiques, cafés, restaurants, colourful murals and relaxed bars.

## Music & Nightlife

### Lower Broadway & Honky Tonk Highway

Experience the heart of Nashville with famous honky-tonks, rooftop bars and live country music. Many venues offer live music throughout the day and well into the evening.

### Ryman Auditorium

Known as the **“Mother Church of Country Music,”** this historic Nashville landmark hosts concerts and offers daytime tours.

### Grand Ole Opry

Experience one of Nashville’s most iconic live country-music shows. Check the performance schedule and book tickets in advance.

### The Bluebird Café

An intimate and legendary venue known for singer-songwriter performances. Reservations are strongly recommended.

## Museums & Nashville History

### Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum

Explore costumes, instruments, recordings and exhibits celebrating the artists and history of country music.

### National Museum of African American Music

An interactive museum celebrating the influence of African American artists across gospel, blues, jazz, R&B, hip-hop and many other genres.

### Frist Art Museum

A beautiful downtown art museum featuring rotating exhibitions inside Nashville’s historic former post-office building.

### Johnny Cash Museum

Discover memorabilia, costumes, awards and personal items celebrating the life and career of Johnny Cash.

## Parks & Sightseeing

### Centennial Park & The Parthenon

Enjoy a walk through Centennial Park and visit Nashville’s impressive full-scale replica of the ancient Greek Parthenon, which also houses an art museum.

### John Seigenthaler Pedestrian Bridge

Walk across the Cumberland River for beautiful views of the Nashville skyline. Sunset is an especially great time to visit.

### Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park

Enjoy walking paths, Tennessee history displays and views of the Tennessee State Capitol.

## Neighbourhoods Worth Exploring

### SoBro

Located just south of Broadway, SoBro is home to restaurants, live music, museums, the Music City Center and many downtown attractions.

### Germantown

A charming historic neighbourhood known for brunch spots, restaurants, boutique shopping, the Nashville Farmers’ Market and nearby museums.

### East Nashville

A creative and energetic neighbourhood filled with local restaurants, coffee shops, vintage stores, breweries and live-music venues.
