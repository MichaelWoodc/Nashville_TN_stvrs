You'll have a great time at this comfortable place to stay. This townhome is a modern townhouse on the shores of the Nations neighborhood. You're under a mile to some of the hottest restaurants and bars in town and only a ten minute drive to Broadway. Walkable to excellent coffee, Hattie B's hot chicken, ML Rose burgers, Coco's Italian, Midnight Oil pizza and Nashville original breweries.

The space
* 1st Floor: Your room with a keypad lock on it
*  2nd Floor: Full Kitchen, Living Room, Full Bathroom, Washer/Dryer & Big Screen, Roku TV (shared)
* 2nd Floor: Your Bathroom will be in the shared living room. No one else will use the shower during your stay.
