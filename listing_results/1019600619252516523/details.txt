The space
Serendipity House features a 1000 sq ft private roof deck with views of downtown Nashville--a great place to view the 4th of July and New Year's Eve fireworks! The roof deck is complete with a dining area, festive string lights, and a whimsical giant chess set.

The attached private two-car garage space is yours to enjoy. Ample additional street parking is convenient and free.

With an open floor plan in the main living area, Serendipity House is ideal for traveling with family and friends. Also, please note that the bedrooms are equipped with memory foam mattresses, luxurious bedding, HD Smart TVs, en suite bathrooms, comfortable robes, and a generous amount of closet space. 

The living room contains a 75" Roku 4K Smart TV, as well as several board games and books. For the full Nashville experience, colorful guitars with picks are on display. To top it all off, there is a vintage-style record player complete with country music LPs, such as Classic Country Gold, for your listening pleasure.

The open kitchen is fully equipped and stocked with the essentials and more. In addition, a free washer and a free dryer are provided for your use.

Access your inner child in the dining room, where you'll find a table for eight, an arcade game, game table games such as mini pool, foosball, and ping pong, an oversized connect 4 game, and a vintage piano.

Guest access
Cleaning closets are locked and reserved for host use, and a very small part of the garage is used for storage. The rest of the space is reserved for guests, including two garage parking spaces in the private attached garage. In addition, street parking is ample, convenient, and free. 

Guests will enjoy a generous amount of closet space, drawer space, and cabinet space. The guest space, including all three floors of the townhouse plus the roof deck, is 100% private for guests with private entrances and a smart lock code unique to each reservation.

Other things to note
