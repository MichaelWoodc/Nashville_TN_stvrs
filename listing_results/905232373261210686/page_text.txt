Bedroom
This is a true one bedroom apartment with a door separating the bedroom from the living room. The serene bedroom has a new king size Article bed and a new memory foam mattress with hotel-quality pillows and beautiful white bedding. Our guests rave about our mattress and often ask me for the link to purchase one for their own homes! 

We clean all the bedding before each stay, including the mattress protector, duvet cover, blankets and of course all the towels and sheets! In the living room our Queen sized sofa bed is comfortable too with a brand new upgraded foam mattress! Technically, our permit allows up to 6 guests but we furnished the Airbnb with only two beds to sleep up to 4 comfortably. 

Amazing Hidden Gem Location:
Our Melrose Woodland-in-Waverly location is a hidden gem - our tree lined street has sidewalks and is full of families, young professionals and retirees. It's ideal for leisure walks and for checking out the assortment of restaurants and shops within a couple of blocks. It's also only 1.8 miles south of Downtown Nashville and 1.4 miles to Geodis Park! One mile to 12 South! Near Music Row, Belmont, Vandy, Green Hills, Wedgewood Houston and more.

We are two blocks to the ever popular Hattie B's Hot Chicken, a full size Publix Grocery Store and various shops including Gruhn Guitars, 8th and Roast, Love Peace & Pho, Urban Juicer, Smiling Elephant, an organic market and butcher shop and more. A short walk to First Watch, Ladybird Tacos, Cilantro Mexican Grill, Maple Street Biscuit Company, Zanies Comedy Club and more. Our neighborhood is filled with families, retirees, artists, hobby gardeners and is great for dog walking too!

Expand the distance to about one mile (you can take sidewalks the whole way-ask me how!) to shop 12 South's many shops and restaurants. We're also about a half mile from other fun restaurants in Melrose such as Sinema, Pancho & Lefty's Cantina, M.L Rose and Chick-fil-A.

There's quick and easy access to I-65, a bus stop that goes right downtown on route #8 one block away and it's a quick Uber ride or scooter ride 2 miles to downtown.

Guest access
You'll have full use of this apartment and have your own private entrance. We have a few locked storage closets and one door to our garage that will be locked (on both sides) to restrict access to the garage.

Other things to note
Please note that the apartment is above our detached garage. It was thoughtfully constructed in 2018 with foam insulation and an extra quiet belt. So pros/cons: You'll have no other apartments sharing a wall or floor above, below or beside you and can listen to TV and music indoors as loud as you want without bothering anyone! You also get windows on all four walls for tons of natural light! The downside is you will hear the garage open and close a couple of times a day and you will probably hear our young kids playing outside in the daytime. A sound machine is provided in the bedroom for light sleepers.

Also this is extra important - guests MUST be very comfortable with stairs as there is a flight of stairs inside the door to walk up to the living area of the apartment.

We do have a Ring camera outside the front door to help us ensure a smooth check in experience. Upon request, we are happy to remove it during your stay!

From the host
We're just off 8th Ave S. in a beautiful tree-line historic district called Woodland In Waverly filled with sidewalks, easy parking, friendly families, retirees and neighbors walking their pets. It's the absolute best location for anyone who wants to be extremely close to downtown and in a quiet Nashville neighborhood that is walkable to dozens of restaurants and a full sized grocery store.

Within two blocks grab Thai at the Smiling Elephant, coffee from Nashville's best coffee shop 8th & Roast, shop Starland Vintage & Unusual for vintage goods, juice from Urban Juicer, sweets from Baked on Little Gourmand, music at The Eighth Room then catch a comedy show at Zanies. YTwo blocks to a full size Publix super market, Hattie B's Hot Chicken or check out the local organic market and butcher shop Smokin' Oats.

Woodland in Waverly is a walker's paradise that is "central to everything in Nashville and features unique historical architecture, beautiful gardens and a truly authentic Nashville experience."
We are just outside of downtown Nashville and it's so easy to get around from here (it's literally one road get to downtown!)

If you're up for walking three or four blocks be sure to check out First Watch, Ladybird Tacos, Cilantro Mexican Grill, Maple Street Biscuit Company and more. Expand the distance about one mile (you can take sidewalks the whole way-ask me!) to reach 12 South shops and restaurants. We're also about a half mile from other fun restaurants in Melrose such as Sinema, Pancho & Lefty's Cantina, M.L Rose and Chick-fil-A.

There's quick and easy access to i-65, a bus stop that goes right downtown on route #8 one block from the apartment and it's a quick Uber ride 2 miles to downtown.
