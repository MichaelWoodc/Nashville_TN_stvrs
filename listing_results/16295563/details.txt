=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Escape to our spacious and eco-friendly oasis in the heart of East Nashville, professionally designed by J & K Design Studio to make your stay truly memorable. This stylish retreat is perfect for families, friends, and business travelers alike.

The space
Step into a bright, airy, open-concept main floor where a modern living room flows into a dining area and a stunning kitchen. With two separate living rooms, your group will have plenty of space to relax. Unwind with a movie on the 65-inch TV, or step out onto the deck for an evening barbecue.

We blend the luxury of a high-end hotel with the comforts of home. The kitchen is fully stocked for your culinary adventures, featuring beautiful marble countertops perfect for entertaining. We are proud to offer an eco-friendly experience with biodegradable toiletries, sustainable paper products, and soft organic sheets.
