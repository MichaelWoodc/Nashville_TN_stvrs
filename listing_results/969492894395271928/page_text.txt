This home can sleep 10 people.

The space
LEVEL ONE
The first floor features one bedroom w/ Queen bed and its own private 3pc ensuite bathroom. In the garage you will find our Large Movie Theater room w/ comfy reclined seating for ten people, Flat Screen SMART TV with Surround sound.

LEVEL TWO
The second floor contains the kitchen, living room, balcony, trundle daybed (two single beds) and half bath. 

LEVEL THREE
The third floor features two bedrooms, one Master Bedroom with King Bed, and the secondary bedroom with two Queen sized beds. Both these bedrooms have their own dedicated walk-in closet and private ensuite bathroom. Conveniently located on this floor is also the Laundry room (washer & dryer) and Make-up Room w/ Hollywood Mirror. 

The property has high-speed WiFi and TVs in each bedroom.

SMART TVs - We provide complimentary YouTube TV for all guests. Guests have the ability to login to their own streaming service profiles at their discretion.

PARKING
There is room for two spots directly behind the garage. Please see the picture with the highlighted areas showcasing the free parking available to guests. 

Additionally, we provide guests with standard kitchen appliances, k-cups, pots and pans, shampoo, conditioner, soap, hair dryers, iron and iron board, cleaning supplies, laundry detergent, toilet paper, makeup towels, paper towels and other home/travel essentials. We have you covered!

Guest access
Guests will have access to the entire home except for a locked owner's closet. They'll receive a four-digit access code before arrival to enter the unit through our Schlage Encode smart lock. Additionally, there will be an onsite lockbox accessible in case of a smart lock failure. We provide 24/7 customer support to ensure you never get locked out. We also enhance property security with a Ring doorbell.

Other things to note
We are the following minutes away from these destinations by car:
- 6 minutes from Broadway, Ryman Auditorium, Nissan Stadium, Country Music Hall of Fame & Bridgestone Arena
- 14 minutes from Grand Ole Opry & Opry Mills Mall
- 10 minutes from the Nashville International Airport (BNA)
- 2 minutes from Geodis Park (Home to Nashville SC)
- 5 minutes from Belmont University
- 6 minutes from Vanderbilt University

PET FRIENDLY - We are pet-friendly hosts; however, we must be informed of all pets occupying the property prior to your stay. An additional $100/pet cleaning fee will be administered for your stay. Any evidence of unregistered pets in the house or on the property will result in a $250 fine. Service animals are not considered pets and are not subject to the pet fee (with appropriate documentation); however, emotional support animals are considered pets.

The home is located in the Wedgewood-Houston neighborhood of Nashville and steps away from Geodis Park, the Fairgrounds, and the Expo Center. Located a few blocks south of Downtown, the Wedgewood-Houston (or “WeHo”) neighborhood is a creative hub on the cultural rise with artists and entrepreneurs opening art galleries, studios and workshops, restaurants, distilleries and breweries in the area.
