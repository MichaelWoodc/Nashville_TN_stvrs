The space
Welcome to the Above Nashville Starlight at Sylvan Park!

Set in one of Nashville's most beloved residential pockets, the Above Starlight blends the easy, tree-lined charm of Sylvan Park with the design-forward polish ABOVE is known for. This is the version of Nashville that locals keep to themselves — walkable, unhurried, and minutes from everything that brings you to Music City — wrapped in a home built for both quiet mornings and the kind of nights you remember.

The home spans ~4,000 square feet across 2 levels, with 5 bedrooms with 7 beds, 4 full bathrooms, 2 half baths, and sleeping up to 12 guests. The heart of the house is its open main level, where a chef-caliber gourmet kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining and living areas — an effortless layout for a morning coffee crowd or a dinner with the family. 

This home is built for groups who never want to leave. Out back, a private pool with pool basketball anchors a true backyard resort — soak in the hot tub, unwind in the sauna, and cook without missing a beat thanks to a full outdoor kitchen. When the group gets competitive, there's a dual pickleball court / basketball court waiting, and a fully equipped workout room in the back house keeps your routine intact. Inside, multiple dedicated workspaces make it easy to log a few focused hours before switching off completely — a natural fit for remote-working stays and longer visits.

Step outside and Sylvan Park is yours: locally owned cafés, neighborhood restaurants, and easy strolls along the Richland Creek Greenway and McCabe Park. You're a short drive from Centennial Park, Vanderbilt, The Nations' breweries and dining, and 10 minutes to the energy of downtown Broadway — close enough to be in the middle of it all, far enough to actually rest.
