The space
Elvis gets the second floor. This 1-bedroom, 1-bath suite sleeps 6 across 3 beds - more than the floor plan suggests, thanks to a clever flex room.

The bedroom has a queen bed, a queen sleeper sofa and a flat-screen TV. The flex room converts from a dining room with table and 6 bar stools into a third sleeping space with a queen bed courtesy of a custom Murphy Bed and its own TV. Set it up as dinner-party mode or dorm mode, your call.

The full kitchen features a gas range and includes a full-size fridge/freezer, dishwasher, oven, microwave, cookware, and coffee machines. There's no separate living room but a combination of the flex room and the bedroom having a sleepers sofa does double duty.
