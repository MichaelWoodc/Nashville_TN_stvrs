=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Experience Nashville luxury at our brand new 4BR/3.5BA retreat with a massive rooftop boasting panoramic views and chic modern decor. Situated only a block away from the trendy boutiques and distilleries of Marathon Village, and a mere 5-minute ride with Uber or Lyft from the lively entertainment districts of Broadway, Germantown, and the Gulch, this location puts the best of Nashville right at your fingertips.  Book now for an unforgettable stay in Music City!

The space
**Big full length mirrors and closets for each bedroom**
**Black-out blinds in each bedroom for late night revelers**
**75 inch 4K TV with Sonos for pregame and gameday**
**2 car parking garage is available for off-street parking**

Our home is designed for large groups that want to be a stone’s throw away from all the fun and memory-making experiences Nashville has to offer.  The space provides a vibrant atmosphere matched with beautiful artistic touches to let your imagination run wild.  The crown jewel of course is the rooftop - an expansive opening that offers unmatched panoramic views of downtown.  Settle in for sunset cocktails and get ready for a trip to remember!

1st Floor

Upon entering, you’ll find the first bedroom down the hall and to your left.  Cozy queen mattresses supported by Francis Lofts await you for a relaxing night of sleep.  A full bath is attached to this bedroom.

2nd Floor

Ascend the staircase to unveil a one-of-a-kind surprise that will take your breath away - a mesmerizing Vistafolia plant wall, thoughtfully designed by award-winning horticulturalist Paul Adler. Immerse yourself in a surreal, imaginative atmosphere that promises to elevate your stay with friends or family as you prepare for an unforgettable day or night on the town.

The kitchen is a chef's dream, equipped with top-of-the-line appliances and everything you need to savor homemade breakfast, lunch, or dinner with your loved ones. We understand the importance of a morning coffee ritual, which is why we have a stocked coffee machine ready to satisfy your caffeine cravings.

Recharge in the sumptuous master bedroom, furnished with a king-size Softframe Designs bed that promises a restful slumber. Adjacent to the bedroom is a spacious bathroom featuring his and hers sinks, and an expansive shower complete with a bench - the perfect spot for indulging in a traditional shower beer while last night's escapades melt away.

3rd Floor

Ascend to the third floor where local artwork showcasing Nashville's iconic landmarks adorns the hallway leading to bedrooms three and four. Bedroom three features two comfortable queen beds with Thuma frames, while bedroom four boasts two sets of custom queen-over-queen bunk beds with individual cubbies for phone-charging and electronics.

Both bedrooms have easy access to the third bathroom, complete with a spacious shower and his-and-hers sinks.

Rooftop

Saunter out to the rooftop and be transported to one of the best views the city has to offer!  We’re locals here in town so we’ve explored just about every inch of the city.  We have a tough time finding a view that matches this one!  With ample seating available, you’ll be able to settle in with your friends and beverage(s) of choice to take in all the sights of downtown Music City.

Guest access
Ubers and Lyfts are plentiful!  For guests who are driving, there is a two-car garage available for off-street parking.  There is also ample, free street parking available.
