The space
The light-filled Guest Suite has tall, lofty ceilings and extra large windows overlooking the Cumberland River - 2 Beds (1 King & 1 Queen), small office area and a dining table that seats 4.  
The kitchenette is equipped with microwave, toaster oven, Keurig coffee maker, tea kettle, Brita water filter and a mini fridge with freezer, along with a full-sized washer and dryer to use at your convenience.  
The bathroom has a large soaking tub, oversized shower and a double vanity.  
We provide sheets, towels, shampoo, body wash, blowdryer, clothing iron, WiFi, Cable TV, Netflix and games.
We are centrally located in a safe and well-established neighborhood in West Nashville - quiet and away from the hustle and bustle of downtown.
