=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Step into a restored 1899 loft on 2nd Avenue with skyline & river views - steps from Broadway, the Ryman & Bridgestone Arena.

- Sleeps 12 | 4 bedrooms | 8 beds | 5 baths
- Skyline & river views through tall loft windows
- Pool table & sound system for nights in
- Full kitchen w/ dishwasher, coffee maker, blender & more
- Washer & dryer, Wi-Fi & keypad self check-in
- Steps from Broadway * Ryman Auditorium

The space
The 1899 bones of this 2nd Avenue loft do more than set the scene - they frame it. Tall windows pull Nashville's skyline and the Cumberland River straight into the living space, and the hum of Broadway is never more than a short walk away. For a group of twelve looking for a base that earns its place on the itinerary, this is it.

The space
One open floor plan keeps all twelve guests together without the usual disconnect of a multi-story rental. Living and dining run into each other naturally - practical for a full group powering through a packed Nashville schedule, and just as comfortable when the night wraps up and everyone finds a corner to settle into. A private entrance with keypad self check-in and elevator access mean the group arrives and leaves on its own terms.

Sleeping arrangements
Four bedrooms sleep up to twelve across eight beds. Each bedroom has its own ensuite bathroom, which means mornings stay smooth no matter how the previous night ended. A bunk area adds sleeping flexibility across the full group, and every bedroom has its own TV for quieter downtime away from the main space.

Living & entertainment
The pool table anchors the entertainment side of the loft - set up for a round before heading to Bridgestone Arena or a full night in with the sound system running. Whether the group is pregaming or winding down, the space handles both without effort.

Kitchen & dining
The full kitchen covers serious cooking for twelve - refrigerator, freezer, stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, coffee maker, blender, toaster, baking sheet, wine glasses, cooking basics, dishes and silverware, and coffee are all in place. The open layout keeps the kitchen connected to the rest of the group rather than tucked off to the side.

Bathrooms
Five bathrooms serve the group - four ensuite and one additional. Each comes supplied with a hair dryer, shampoo, conditioner, body soap, cleaning products, and hot water.

Amenities & comfort
A washer and dryer handle laundry across a longer stay or a run of back-to-back nights out. Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace are available for anything that can't wait - note the workspace area does not have a private door. Air conditioning, heating, and portable fans keep the loft comfortable regardless of the season. Safety features include exterior security cameras on the building, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and a fire extinguisher. Essentials - towels, bed sheets, soap, and toilet paper - are provided throughout, along with hangers, bed linens, extra pillows and blankets, an iron, and clothing storage.

Location
Sitting on 2nd Avenue near Banker's Alley, the loft puts Broadway's live music strip, the Ryman Auditorium, Bridgestone Arena, and the Country Music Hall of Fame all within walking distance. Restaurants, bars, and cafés fill the surrounding streets. Paid parking is available off premises.

Guest access
Exclusive Access and secure entry ensure a safe and comfortable stay. As our guest, you will have keyless building entry and keyless elevator access to the penthouse. This means that you can easily and securely enter the building and access the penthouse at any time during your stay without having to worry about a physical key. The access codes will be provided to you prior to your arrival and will be unique to your stay. We hope this added convenience will make your stay more enjoyable and stress-free.

Other things to note
Please inquire about our additional properties in the building should you require accommodations for a larger group or if your dates aren't available.
