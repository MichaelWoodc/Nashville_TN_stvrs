=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Hachland Hill's Main Inn is a large cabin/lodge with logs over 200 years old. Situated on 90 acres of stunning, natural beauty (flowers, shrubs, spring fed creek, abundant wildlife) + hiking trails for your enjoyment. The Main Inn is perfect for solo-travelers, couples looking for a serene escape or a family getaway. You'll be at home in the country, and  just a short drive from downtown Nashville.

The space
Poplar #4 bedroom has two queen sized beds. This bedroom has a private bathroom. The common areas of the Main Inn include a living room and large dining room with a beautiful view. There is a rec room with ping pong/pool and a large outdoor terrace with plenty of seating and a fire pit. The inn is appointed with primitive antiques, yet has modern comforts in the sleeping and living spaces.

Guest access
The main inn has over 10,000 square feet. Make yourselves at home indoors and out, on our beautiful terrace overlooking the woods, and comfortable porches.  
There is a big Rec room with ping pong/pool. Hike and explore the 90 acres of wooded hills and open meadows.

Other things to note
Designed and appointed with love by Tennessee Treasure, Phila Hach (chef, author, TV personality). Pictures just don't do the setting justice. Eight additional private rooms are available as well as our small inn, Spring Creek (5BR/5BA) in its entirety for groups of up to 16pp..
