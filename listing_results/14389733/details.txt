=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to the Treetop Loft — a secluded, private suite just 10–15 mins from downtown Nashville. Relax in a king-size bed, enjoy nature views through a stunning 5x7 window, and unwind by the fire pit and waterfall. The suite includes a private entrance, full bath, and kitchenette stocked with snacks and drinks. Set on over half an acre, it’s perfect for anniversaries, weekend escapes, or a peaceful base to explore Nashville together. Fast Wi-Fi and parking included for a stress-free stay.

The space
This bright and spacious suite is a private addition to the main house, thoughtfully designed for your comfort and complete privacy. Inside, you’ll find everything you need for a relaxing stay:

A coffee maker and Keurig for your perfect morning brew

Complimentary snacks to satisfy those little cravings

A microwave and toaster for quick meals or snacks

A mini fridge and freezer stocked with complimentary drinks and plenty of room for your groceries
