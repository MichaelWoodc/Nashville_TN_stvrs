The space
Welcome to The Swiftie Shangri-La: The ultimate Taylor Swift-inspired escape in the heart of Nashville!
This luxurious and thoughtfully designed loft blends high-end style with whimsical touches that capture the magic of Music City and the essence of Taylor herself.

Step inside and be instantly charmed by the cozy yet chic interior, filled with Taylor Swift Easter Eggs. The lofted bedroom offers two plush full-size beds and streams of natural light, creating a warm, inviting retreat. The open living area features soaring 20’ vaulted ceilings, a large Smart TV, a custom “Sparkle Guitar” coffee table, an iconic red lips sofa, and a queen-size pull-out couch for extra guests.

The Folklore & Evermore inspired bathroom doesn’t disappoint with elegant marble countertops, a spacious walk-in shower, and a custom glass shower door featuring none other than Taylor’s beloved cat, Olivia.

But the crown jewel of this space? The oversized private deck, inspired by the “You Need to Calm Down” music video. Unwind with a drink in hand as you soak in stunning city views, bask in the sunshine, or head down to the pool for a refreshing dip!

Centrally located, you’re just minutes away from Nashville’s top restaurants, bars, boutiques, and of course—legendary live music venues. Uber and Lyft rides are a breeze, typically arriving within 3–5 minutes.

Whether you're a die-hard Swiftie or simply looking for a unique and stylish stay in Nashville, The Swiftie Shangri-La is the perfect place to make magical, unforgettable memories.

From the host
Welcome to The Gulch, Nashville's trendiest and most vibrant neighborhood, offering an unparalleled urban experience. This hip district, known for its modern architecture and bustling atmosphere, has become a favorite destination for locals and visitors alike.

Nestled between Downtown Nashville and Music Row, The Gulch is an eclectic blend of stylish high-rises, chic boutiques, and a lively mix of restaurants and bars. With its trendy, upscale vibe, the neighborhood beckons both fashion-forward shoppers and food enthusiasts seeking a culinary adventure.

During the day, explore the fashion boutiques and art galleries, or indulge in some retail therapy at the upscale stores and specialty shops. The Gulch is also home to a diverse array of dining options, catering to every palate. From farm-to-table eateries and international cuisines to rooftop bars with sweeping city views, you're sure to find a dining experience that satisfies your cravings.

As the sun sets, The Gulch comes alive with its vibrant nightlife scene. Sip cocktails at rooftop lounges, groove to live music in trendy clubs, or simply take a leisurely stroll under the twinkling lights, soaking in the lively ambiance.

For those seeking a taste of Nashville's creative side, The Gulch offers a thriving art scene, with numerous art installations and murals scattered throughout the neighborhood. The famous "What Lifts You" mural by Kelsey Montague is just one of the photo-worthy spots that add to The Gulch's allure.

Easily accessible by foot or rideshare services, The Gulch is a must-visit destination for anyone looking to immerse themselves in Nashville's urban chic lifestyle. Experience the pulse of the city, savor the artistic flair, and embrace the energy of The Gulch, a neighborhood that captures the essence of Music City's modern charm.
