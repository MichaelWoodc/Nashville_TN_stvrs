=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Welcome to our urban oasis! Our location cannot be beat. We are in a beautiful, historical, residential neighborhood in Nashville and walkable to some of Nashville's  best neighborhoods for shopping and eating. We are just over a mile from Vanderbilt and Belmont. We are a 10 minute drive from the airport or downtown. Your room is spacious and bright, overlooking our wild backyard. You will have your own private entrance through our cedar screened in porch. Driveway is yours! LGBTQIA+ friendly!

The space
Enjoy the king size bed and private bathroom. Feel free to lounge in the hammock on the screened porch. There is a little house/studio in our back yard that you can see from your room. It is currently used for storage and no one lives in it. We do live on the other side of the walls and this is an older house. This is NOT a hotel with insulated walls. We try to be as quiet as we can but you will likely hear us (or our dogs) at some point. This is a private room in a house with a private entrance and a private bathroom though it is still a bedroom in someone's home and not an entire house or apartment. We keep our backyard pest free and wild for the local wildlife. We are both volunteers at a local wildlife rehab.

Guest access
You will have your own entry and access to a large room in the back of the house with walk in closet and a private bathroom with european shower (rain head and regular). You may also use the screen porch which is shared with  your airbnb hosts.

During your stay
We live in the house but won't be in your room or bathroom. You will not have access to the main house but you will have access to the screen porch. We are close by and available if you need anything during your stay. You also may hear us from time to time though we do try to be quiet. If you are looking for a hotel experience our place probably isn’t for you. If you are looking for a bright cozy room with a private bathroom in a residential neighborhood, our place is perfect.

Other things to note
Pets are allowed with pre approval.
