=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Josephine is a luxurious, Parisian-inspired retreat in vibrant East Nashville, Music City. This chic boutique home comfortably sleeps 6 guests across 3 Queen beds, offering a unique blend of vintage charm and modern luxury. Features include a soft water system, gourmet kitchen, luxury linens, and an EV charger. Perfect for sophisticated travelers seeking a stylish, peaceful base near Nashville's dynamic attractions.

The space
Welcome to The Josephine, a truly unique, Parisian-inspired short-term rental designed for sophisticated travelers. It blends vintage charm with modern luxury, offering a warm, moody, and luxurious ambiance for your perfect stay.

Key features and amenities include:
   Water System: Soft water system throughout, transforming municipal water into clean, gentle water for your hair and skin.
   Kitchen: Thoughtfully outfitted with elevated dinnerware, glassware, flatware, and barware, perfect for intimate celebrations.
   Entertainment: Living room with a 65-inch flat-screen TV, complete with Apple TV for your favorite apps. A curated collection of classic games and puzzles is also available. Inquire about our exclusive in-house live music offering.
   Lighting: Automatic lighting sets a delightful mood in common areas, complemented by dimmer switches in all living spaces and bedrooms.
   Bedding: Each bed is adorned with luxury linens, Certipur-US memory foam mattresses, and down feather pillows to ensure a restful night's sleep.
   Comfort: For personalized comfort, sound machines, portable adjustable fans, and blackout curtains or shades are provided in every bedroom.
   Bathrooms: We prioritize your well-being with paraben-free, synthetic fragrance-free, sulfate-free, phthalate-free, and formaldehyde-sourced chemical-free soaps, including dishwasher and laundry detergents, and personal care items. Sheets and duvet covers are crafted from 100% organic cotton or 100% organic bamboo, while bath towels are made from 100% rare Suprima cotton. Both third-floor bathrooms feature rain shower heads, and all full baths include toilet stools for added comfort. The master bath also offers a towel warmer.

Property Layout:
   First Floor: Features a front porch, entry foyer, mudroom nook, and one ensuite bedroom with a Queen bed, tub/shower, and walk-in closet.
   Second Floor: Hosts the living room, dining room, kitchen with an island bar (seating for 3), walk-in pantry, powder bath, and an outdoor balcony.
   Third Floor: Includes a second ensuite bedroom with a Queen bed, tub/shower, and walk-in closet, plus the primary ensuite bedroom with a Queen bed, double sinks, walk-in shower, and walk-in closet. A laundry room is also located on this floor.

Please note: The garage is unavailable to guests. This property includes both interior and exterior stairs.

Guest access
Guests will have seamless and secure entry to the entire home with a smart lock keyless door lock; your entry code will be provided before your arrival. You'll have one dedicated parking space in the driveway behind the house, conveniently located next to the EV Rivian charging station. Additional guest parking is available nearby. A paved sidewalk connects the shared driveway to the front entrance.

Other things to note

This property is in compliance with all city ordinances regulating short term rentals. If requested, a copy of the active, short-term rental permit will be provided to booked guests.

Parking
This property comes with one dedicated parking space in the driveway behind the house. (See photo with the 80s Mercedes for reference.) A paved sidewalk connects the shared driveway to the front entrance of the property. 

East Nashville is home to the creative class (aka musicians and artists) so be advised that you may hear and/or see music happening nearby, either at Nelson’s Drum Shop or potentially a neighboring property. This is sporadic and very minimally disruptive, but worth noting for those extremely sensitive to sound. 

East Nashville also has multiple train lines that run through the neighborhood. This is just a part of living in the city and this property is located near one of those railways. Trains go by periodically and may or may not use their horn at street crossings. We have no control over the schedule, safety protocols, or behavior of conductors.

In general, this is a very peaceful area with minimal noise disruptions beyond typical, city living (cars driving by, people walking their dogs, etc). From time to time, you might even hear or catch a glimpse of fireworks from Nissan Stadium when they are used at the end of a Titans game, music performance, or the infamous 4th of July fireworks display.
