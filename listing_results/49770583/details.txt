=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
WALK TO MUSIC ROW, The Gulch, Midtown & Downtown!

Enjoy nearly 2500 sq. ft of professionally cleaned space. Groups will love the air hockey, foosball, and arcade in the game room. Families will enjoy playgrounds located a few doors down in one direction and a few blocks away the other way. This gorgeous, oversized 4 bedroom, 3 bath townhome is the perfect place to stay, right off Music Row, in the heart of Nashville.

Need to accommodate more than 12 guests? Ask about our next-door rental!

The space
As you enter, you’ll find the open-concept dining & kitchen space. The stylish dining area is first, with room for six at the table. The chef's kitchen includes granite countertops, gas range, stainless steel appliances, and large kitchen island with additional seating for four.

Under the staircase in the kitchen is a cozy desk area, loaded with games and books for all ages.

Just past the kitchen, step down into the living room. With stylish furnishings (including sofa and loveseat), flat-screen cable TV, prop musical instruments perfect for social media photos, and access to the outdoor patio via French doors.

Outside you will find a sunny space for you to relax, cook out on the grill, or enjoy drinks under the shade of the patio umbrella and pergola.

The upstairs game room has an air hockey table, foosball, and full size old-school upright arcade game with Pac-Man, Donkey Kong, Galaga, Frogger Dig Dug, Space Invaders, and much more!

House sleeps 12 guests:

The primary bedroom has a king-size bed and full bed, cable TV, plus luxury ensuite bathroom with dual quartz vanity and tile, oversized walk-in shower with a rain showerhead, seat, and skylight. It also features a study nook with a desk, great for getting some remote work done.

In the second bedroom, you will find two full-size beds. The third bedroom has a twin over full-size bunk bed with cable tv. Finally, the fourth bedroom is located on the main floor and has two queen beds. (There are also two rollaway beds and air mattresses available.)

Guest access
Guests have full access to the entire home (apart from two locked supply closets) and the fenced-in yard/patio.
