Illume 412

The space
Experience Music City from an elevated perspective at this one-bedroom condo in Illume - a fresh new Nashville community. Set in Gulch View - a prime locale near downtown and The Gulch - this urban escape is minutes from the city’s incredible music scene, fantastic dining, local shopping, and nightlife hotspots. Luxe amenities include a beautifully landscaped courtyard, a fitness center, and a seasonal pool in the heart of the building. Pairing contemporary style with a sustainable design, this forward-thinking retreat includes features like energy-efficient systems and a green roof. Perched on the top floor, your condo promises stunning sunset views from the private, western-facing balcony above the pool. You’ll also love the chic accents and high-end furnishings throughout.

The sunlit living area is furnished with a comfy love seat and a sectional that converts to a queen-size sleeper. Stream new Netflix releases on the 65-inch TV with Roku. Other highlights include high ceilings and large windows facing Music Row. Stainless steel appliances bring a sleek aesthetic to the well-appointed kitchen. Find an array of helpful gadgets, including a Keurig coffee maker. When it’s time to eat, enjoy your culinary creations at the table for two in the dining area.

This rental sleeps four guests between one bedroom and a queen-size sleeper sofa. A plush queen-size bed with hotel-quality linens promises restful nights in the bedroom. A 50-inch TV with Roku sets the scene for quiet movie nights. The en suite bathroom features a tub/shower combination.

The private balcony is appointed with a chaise lounge and bistro table for two, providing a serene perch above the pool. Situated towards the west, your balcony is perfectly positioned for the best views of Tennessee sunsets.

Walk to The Gulch and explore local boutiques, a vibrant nightlife, and one of the hottest food scenes in the city. You’re also near other sought-after areas, including Music Row and Edgehill Village. Or, take a quick rideshare to downtown to visit the convention center and iconic Nashville attractions like the Country Music Hall of Fame and Ryman Auditorium. Bridgestone Arena and Nissan Stadium are also a short drive away.
