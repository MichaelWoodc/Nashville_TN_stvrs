=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Inspired by Dolly Parton's Tennessee Mountain Home, our cabin is nested 20 min from the heart of Nashville on 4 acres of secluded woods. Our cabin takes you back in time with the comforts of beautiful quilts, handcrafted pottery and authentic hardwood floors. Sit in the hot tub and see the stars thru a canopy of trees or listen to Dolly's old records on the patio. Whether you are planning a romantic weekend, holiday w/ family or a girls trip, our Tennessee Mountain Home is your perfect getaway.

The space
Our Tennessee Mountain Home is central to Nashville and all it offers as it is directly off of HWY 24. Just a 25 minute drive to BNA Airport so you can settle in quickly upon arrival and only 15 minutes to downtown Nashville. The neighborhood is extremely safe and kid friendly, we even have the renowned Becks Farmhouse Coffee just 1.5 miles up the road. You will have a beautiful 700sqft deck to sit and sip coffee and watch the sunrise or let the kids and dog play as the yard is safely fenced in. There is a 6-8 person hot tub situated on the deck, a grill and a propane fire pit for cold winter nights. There is also an indoor wood burning fireplace available for use during the winter. Whether you want to spend the day on Nashville's Broadway or visiting historic local towns, this location makes it easy and practical for both. The woods are free for use to explore and there is a gravel path down to the running creek to stroll down to with family or loved ones. Everything you can think of is provided to make your stay as comfortable as possible! 

The property has two buildings. The garage has been converted into a bunkhouse with its own separate private bathroom and laundry. The main cabin has a bedroom downstairs and a private loft upstairs.

Downstairs - 1 king room and play nook for kids. 

Upstairs - 1 king bed and a pack-n-play available for use. 

Bunkhouse - 1 twin over twin bunk, 1 twin of full bunk and a twin size couch.
