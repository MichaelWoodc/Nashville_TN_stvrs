=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Gallatin is the newest boutique hotel in East Nashville, brought to you by the owners of The Russell Hotel! Explore some of our favorite neighborhood spots on the East Side, or take the good times downtown with just a short 3 mile trip to Broadway. 

Every stay at The Gallatin helps someone experiencing homelessness in Nashville through our Rooms for Rooms program! The average weekend stay provides 16 nights in a bed, 100 free showers, or 30 free meals!

The space
Cozy Room 318 has one full-size bed, a full bathroom, and is 150 square feet (10ft x 15ft). Designed for the solo traveler on a budget, this compact lodging option proves you don’t have to compromise on price in order to have the unique ‘boutique hotel’ experience. *While this compact room is designed for one guest, it can sleep two if desired.* A window with a courtyard view illuminates the vibrant color block walls and bathroom. 

*Please note: This room is on the 3rd floor and The Gallatin does not contain an elevator for guest use. We do have a luggage lift to help you get your bags up and down to your room. Guests will have to go up one flight of stairs.*

Guest access
Access to the building and guest rooms is through a unique key code that we send via email the day before arrival! There is not a check in desk or concierge but we are always a phone call away if needed.

Other things to note
There is a parking lot across the street exclusive for guests of The Gallatin.  Each room booked is allotted one free parking space!

**Rates include housekeeping service upon departure only.
