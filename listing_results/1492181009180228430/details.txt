=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Brick, beams, and a queen bed you won't rush out of. This 1865 factory building has been a lot of things over 160 years; now it's the quietest place downtown to spend a weekend as two. Coffee on the sky deck at sunrise, a pool for the hot part of the afternoon, and Marathon Village at street level when you want espresso, whiskey or a live set. Five minutes to The Gulch, seven to Broadway, and a door you can close on all of it. Better than a hotel, and not remotely the same.

The space
Step into Revelery at 1865, where modern design meets Nashville charm. This thoughtfully styled condo sleeps up to 4 guests and features an open living area with cozy seating, a sleek kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and a comfortable dining space. Both the bedroom and pull-out accommodations are designed for rest after a night out on Broadway. High ceilings, chic décor, and large windows create a bright, inviting atmosphere. Guests also enjoy access to a resort-style pool, fitness center, and the exclusive 1865 Club—making it the perfect home base for your Nashville adventure.

Guest access
Guests at Revelery at 1865 enjoy full access to all community amenities, including the sparkling outdoor pool, modern fitness center, and the exclusive 1865 Club. The condo features secure entry and elevator access for convenience. Free parking is available on-site, and you’ll have the entire unit to yourself for a private, comfortable stay. Whether you’re relaxing poolside or heading out to Broadway, everything you need is right at your fingertips.
