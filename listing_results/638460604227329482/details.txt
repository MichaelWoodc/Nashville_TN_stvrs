=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Comfortable, convenient and beautiful, this modern 1 bedroom free standing guest house is an extraordinary value. Located in the heart of the bustling Belmont/Hillsboro 12th South district you will be centrally located to see the best Nashville has to offer.

The space
It is the perfect location for music city visitors, Belmont parents, or couples who want to explore the many shops and restaurants in the Hillsboro Village/12th South area. The building is completely freestanding and private, and amazingly quiet due to extra insulation in walls and floors. One car parking is free and 2 car parking is available. The house has hardwood floors, 10 foot ceilings, and large full length windows with built in adjustable shades. The house has a modern kitchen with a gas stove, microwave, half size refrigerator, ice maker, dishwasher, lots of cabinet space and all cooking and dining utensils for longer stays. Large living room with new 55' LED television and fast/free wifi. Brand new Ethan Allen queen sized pull out couch. The bedroom is separated from the living area for privacy, and has a large comfy queen sized bed (top of the line mattress), and has a large walk-in closet. The bathroom is exceptional with a honeymoon tub, and a steam shower. All towels, soap, etc provided. A cleaning/laundry can be arranged for your stay.

Guest access
There is an upstairs loft that is used as an office by the owner but the entrance is separate and privacy respected.
