The space
If you want a front-row seat to everything Nashville does best, SoBro Guest House puts you right in the middle of it. Set a few blocks south of Broadway in the heart of the SoBro neighborhood, these boutique-style suites keep world-famous live music, buzzed-about restaurants, and the glow of the city's entertainment district within an easy walk. Broadway itself is only about an 8-minute walk—close enough to be in the middle of it all, far enough that you're not sleeping in the noise, and near enough that you won't need a Lyft to get home.

When you're ready to slow down, you'll come back to a space that actually feels like home—just with better sheets. Each apartment-style suite comes with a full kitchen, a comfortable living area, a private bedroom, and a whole lot of character. Thoughtful touches like luxe linens, a smart TV, indulgent bath products, and locally roasted coffee smooth out the everyday moments, and every suite is finished with original work from Nashville muralist Nathan Brown—so you get a splash of local color without leaving the room.

Step outside and the neighborhood takes over. Bridgestone Arena is an easy walk for a concert or a Predators hockey game, Music City Center sits a short stroll away, and Ascend Amphitheater lines up shows along the water—the same grounds that fill with music each summer for CMA Festival. Nissan Stadium waits just across the Cumberland for NFL Sundays and stadium concerts. Closer to home, Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint is practically around the corner and The Listening Room Café draws crowds for intimate songwriter sets just down the street, with a doughnut stop and the shops of The Gulch a short walk away when you want them. Between outings, the building's shared outdoor lounge is an easy place to regroup or catch a quiet moment.

Bringing the whole crew? Let us know if you'd like to book more than one suite, and we'll help coordinate the details so your group stays close while everyone still has room to spread out.

Inside, however, we've created a restful retreat where comfort and calm are always part of the playlist.

SoBro, short for South of Broadway, is one of Nashville's most exciting and walkable neighborhoods. Just steps from Broadway's legendary honky-tonks, Bridgestone Arena, and the Country Music Hall of Fame, it's a place where music, food, and local culture collide. You'll find some of the city's best restaurants, rooftop bars, and coffee shops nestled alongside art galleries, boutique shopping, and vibrant street murals. With downtown and The Gulch just minutes away, SoBro is the perfect home base for exploring the very best of Nashville.
