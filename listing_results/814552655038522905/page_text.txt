The space
Enjoy your time in Pecan Dreams with your group of friends or family by spreading out in our spacious new-construction home.  Just moments from downtown, this home is the perfect place to get ready for your night out on Broadway, or to relax as a group while using our ample open-space social area and fully equipped kitchen. Wind down your day in our generous sleeping arrangements or cozy up on the couch to watch your favorite show. Our home is close to restaurants, nightlife, shopping, parks and much more.

Other things to note
