The Neighborhood
Berry Hill is a small but vibrant pocket of Nashville known for its creative energy and welcoming feel. With more than 70 recording studios tucked into colorful cottages, the area has a unique artistic character you’ll notice as you explore.

Tree-lined streets give the neighborhood a relaxed, easygoing vibe while still placing you just minutes from many of Nashville’s most popular districts. Murals and public art add charm throughout the area.

Though you’re close to Geodis Park, 12 South, The Gulch, Music Row, and downtown, Berry Hill stays pleasantly quiet in the evenings, making it a peaceful home base after a day in the city.

Its central location provides quick access to Broadway, Vanderbilt, Belmont, the Nashville Zoo, Opryland, and the airport.

From the host
Berry Hill is a small, creative pocket just south of downtown Nashville, known for its welcoming, residential feel and strong artistic roots. With more than 70 recording studios tucked into colorful cottages, music and creativity are woven into everyday life here.

The neighborhood is relaxed, with tree-lined streets, murals, and friendly neighbors. Several nearby parks offer green space for picnics, playgrounds, and time outdoors. Ride shares like Uber and Lyft are easy to catch, making it simple to get around without a car.

As one of Nashville’s six historic “satellite cities,” Berry Hill was originally named after the W.W. Berry family, whose farm once occupied the area. Today, it blends its rich history with a lively creative culture, offering a peaceful home base that’s still close to the energy of the city.
