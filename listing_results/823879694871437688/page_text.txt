The space
We designed this apartment to optimize the airbnb experience for all travelers! The space has high speed internet, 55 inch ROKU  TVs, fully stocked kitchen for cooking, free on and off street parking, and best of all, fresh linens and towels provided each stay including four separate beds. 

 We have provided the essentials like towels, linens, soaps, and more for your stay. This space is great for couples, adventurers, work, family and friends. Located in the North Gulch, it’s incredibly convenient to Broadway, Bridgestone Arena, Downtown, All Concert Venues, Museums, Food, Entertainment and more.

Guest access
Guests will have full access and use of the place 24 hours a day. You can come and go as you like and there’s an electronic lock, so there are no keys to keep up with! I live nearby and can help with anything you need.
