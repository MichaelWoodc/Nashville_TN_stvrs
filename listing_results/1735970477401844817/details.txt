=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Walk the line at our Johnny Cash Jail House Suite. A quiet queen studio on historic Music Row, a short walk from RCA Studio B, where Johnny Cash himself recorded. Kitchenette with induction cooktop & dishwasher, walk-in shower, table for two, and a furnished balcony with gas fire pit & views of Belmont Mansion & Music Row.

Free parking spot behind the home. Hillsboro Village is 5 minutes on foot; Broadway is a 5-7 minute drive. Pack 'n' play in the closet if you're traveling with a little one.

The space
The Man in Black keeps it simple. This second-floor studio has a queen bed, flat-screen TV, and a small table and chairs for two - a hideout for couples, solo writers, or anyone road-tripping through Music City.

The kitchenette covers the essentials: mini fridge/freezer, 2-burner induction cooktop, microwave, and dishwasher, plus cookware and a coffee machine. No full oven - plan for stovetop meals or one of the dozen restaurants within walking distance. The full bathroom has a walk-in shower. A pack 'n' play waits in the closet.

Step out onto your own private second-story balcony: outdoor furniture, a gas fire pit, evening lighting, and Belmont Mansion in view. Booked together with either the Backwoods Barbie Suite or the Elvis Heartbreak Hotel next door, and two or three suites can be combined - handy when the group takes the larger suites and you want the quiet room attached.

**ACCESS**

The full studio is yours, plus the balcony, one allocated parking space behind the home, and the shared basement laundry.

**NOTES**

Second-floor studio reached by stairs; no elevator. Kitchenette has an induction cooktop and microwave but no full oven.

You're on 17th Avenue South - the real Music Row, where the labels, publishers, and studios still work. RCA Studio B, where Elvis recorded over 200 songs, is a short walk from the front door. Belmont University and Belmont Mansion are just up the hill; Vanderbilt is less than a mile north.

Hillsboro Village is a 5 minute walk for coffee, brunch, and the Belcourt Theatre. Edgehill Village and 12South are minutes away. Lower Broadway's honky-tonks are about a 5-7 minute drive - close enough for a night out, far enough that you'll actually sleep. Nashville International Airport is roughly 15 minutes by car.

This is one suite within a 98 year-old home divided into five individually themed rentals, so other guests may be staying in neighboring suites. One rear parking space is allocated per suite; free street parking on 17th Ave is first-come, first-served and easiest after business hours. Plan ahead if you're bringing more than one vehicle.

The basement laundry (two full-size washers and dryers, pods and dryer sheets included) is shared with guests in other suites and accessed from outside. Luxury linens, towels, shampoo, conditioner, and shower gel are provided.

Legends on the Row is independently owned and operated. Each suite is a fan tribute celebrating an artist's legacy and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any artist, estate, or their management.
