=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
The Shirley Loft 203 pays homage to Shirley Harvey the late wife of Fred Harvey Jr. of Harvey’s Department Store that existed at 506 Church Street from the 1940s to the 1980s. After attending Vanderbilt University, Shirley stayed in Nashville to work and serve her community.

Every stay at 506 Lofts helps someone experiencing homelessness in Nashville through our Rooms for Rooms program! The average weekend stay provides 16 nights in a bed, 100 free showers, or 30 free meals!

The space
This 415 SF interior studio loft features a hand-painted mural, original brick walls, and custom built furniture with modern finishes that has amazingly married the aesthetics of this 130 year old building!  **Note- this is an interior loft so it does NOT have exterior windows but it has plenty of lighting and is one of our most popular lofts!

Guest access
Prior to your arrival, you will receive a unique key code that will give you access to the property.  This code will unlock the main door to the building, your unit, as well as the stairwell door and elevator.

Although there is no on-site parking, there is a wide array of parking options close to the building.  Upon booking your stay, we will send you a detailed parking guide.

Other things to note
[Please Note: This is an interior room so it does not have exterior windows.] 

We require Friday and Saturday nights to be booked in the same reservation unless the reservation is for 5 nights or longer. Under certain scenarios we can make exceptions to this, so please ask if you are looking to check-in or out on Saturday.

We are also neighbors to a very cool and elegant cigar bar called The Ariston. They have a full bar and sell amazing cigars! The smell is occasionally noticeable in the halls but almost never in the rooms, and is rarely an issue with guests. We always like to address it upfront just to be safe (and to make sure y'all stop by the cigar bar if you're interested!).

*Acceptance of reservations for local residents (residing within 60 miles of Nashville) is at the discretion of the property.
