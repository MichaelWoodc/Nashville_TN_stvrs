=== ABOUT THIS SPACE ===
Stay inside The 1865, a tobacco warehouse rebuilt into a handful of condos. Original brick, exposed beams and pipes, big windows, and bold art, with a full kitchen and room to settle in. You're in Midtown — a short walk to Vanderbilt and Centennial Park, minutes from The Gulch and Marathon Village, and a 7-minute drive to Broadway when you want it. Sleeps 4: a bedroom plus a sofa bed. Fast wifi and a spot to work. Gated building, free parking, gym, and a heated pool open in the warmer months.

The space
The unit is on the first floor, so no stairs or elevator to deal with. Inside, the original exposed brick and wood beams run through the whole space, paired with a full kitchen, a comfortable living area, and a sofa bed that sleeps two more.
The building itself is part of the draw. Gated and secure with free parking, a fitness center, and the 1865 Club — a speakeasy-style cocktail lounge right on site. The heated pool and outdoor bar are open in the warmer months.
