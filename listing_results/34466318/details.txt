Welcome to The Cottage — Your Midtown Nashville Hideaway

Location, convenience, and a little extra privacy come together at The Cottage, a charming one-bedroom retreat tucked away behind a small 12-unit apartment building in the heart of Midtown Nashville.

Located off the alley and away from the street, The Cottage gives you your own private place to relax while putting you close to some of Nashville's best restaurants, nightlife, universities, hospitals, and attractions.

With a large bedroom featuring two queen beds, accommodations for up to 4 guests, a full kitchen, washer & dryer, and FREE designated parking, The Cottage is a great alternative to a hotel for couples, families, friends, medical visitors, and longer stays.

SLEEPS UP TO 4

One of the highlights of The Cottage is its extra-large bedroom with two queen beds, comfortably accommodating up to four guests.

FULL KITCHEN

The Cottage includes a full kitchen, giving you the flexibility to prepare breakfast before heading out, make a meal at home, store drinks and snacks, or enjoy leftovers from one of Midtown's many excellent restaurants.
