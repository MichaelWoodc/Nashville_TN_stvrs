The space
This four bedroom, two bath craftsman was built in the 1920's and still radiates its original charm while offering you all the modern amenities you could want. 

You'll love waking up to the birds chirping in our backyard, making yourself some fresh local coffee in our country comfort kitchen, and having breakfast in the Nashville morning sun. 

You can spend your day strolling through the shops and restaurants of East Nashville, just a few minutes down the road, or head 15 minutes downtown to Broadway to see shows (music and people watching).

Southern living at its best!
