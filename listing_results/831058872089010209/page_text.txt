Enjoy a Music City experience at this brand-new construction, contemporary residential condo with an industrial flare centrally located just nine minutes from downtown Nashville and twelve minutes from BNA Airport. It has everything you need from a short stay to a long stay!

The space
This brand new contemporary residential condo with an industrial flare sleeps four comfortably.  The private bedroom has a queen bed with a 43-inch smart tv in it along with a walk-in closet and black out shades.  The living room space has all sorts of Music City decor with a queen-size pull-out bed from the couch with a 55-inch smart tv and black out shades.  The kitchen is spacious with everything you need to cook with.  There are two bar stools at the kitchen island for eating and a table with two bar stools that can be used as a dedicated workspace or another place to dine.  The bathroom is stylishly designed and spacious with a stand-up shower.  The laundry room has a brand-new washer and dryer along with plenty of shelves for storing things if you are staying for a while.

Guest access
Guests will have access to the entire condo.

Other things to note
Free parking conveniently located in between downtown Nashville and the airport.  Easy drive or uber/lyft ride to everything Nashville has to offer.
